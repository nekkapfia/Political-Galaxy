# Core 2A: Germany – CDU/CSU
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview

Union hardens migration control inside EU membership: faktischer Aufnahmestopp, border turn-backs from safe EU/Schengen states, safe-third-country processing outside Europe, suspended family reunification for subsidiary protection, and expanded deportations including to Afghanistan and Syria for offenders and Gefährder. Rule-shaper posture — reform EU migration policy, not Dexit. One-term realism applies to treaty change; enforceable border and returns tools drive the score.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* 2025 programme: faktischer Aufnahmestopp; border controls with rejections of asylum claims from EU/Schengen states until external border secured.
* Suspend family reunification for subsidiarily protected; end voluntary admission programmes.
* Safe-third-country concept; asylum procedures outside Europe; push to abolish subsidiary protection status at EU level.
* Federal departure centres; deportations to Afghanistan and Syria especially for offenders/Gefährder; Ausweisungsarrest formulations.
* Bezahlkarte / in-kind benefits priority; reverse Ampel express naturalisation and general dual-citizenship expansion.
* Naturalisation at end of successful integration; Leitkultur; German language; Basic Law and equality commitment (also C3-relevant).
* Maintain EU membership and Eurozone fiscal discipline; protect German interests inside treaties.

### Expanded Analysis

Primary sovereignty gain is enforcement capacity on returns and intake, not exit from EU law. Rule-shaper discount applies: Union writes and reforms many EU rules rather than purely receiving them. Score sits beside AfD’s high-40s for different reasons — AfD maximises exit rhetoric under one-term realism; Union maximises enforcement inside membership.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48

Hardened borders and returns raise sovereignty; residual EU membership and rule-shaper structure prevent a higher nationalist band. **48**.

# Core 2B: Germany – AfD
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 58**

### Overview

AfD’s international posture is relative non-interventionism and Russia accommodation versus the mainstream: immediate lift of economic sanctions on Russia, restore energy ties including Nord Stream framing, limit Ukraine materiel support from Bundeswehr stocks, and treat NATO as a defence alliance only until a European alternative exists. Interest-guided relations with USA, China, and Russia without hierarchy. Soft-power and expeditionary restraint dominate over alliance solidarity maximalism.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Lift all economic sanctions on Russia immediately; resume gas deliveries / restore Nord Stream (programme language).
* Oppose large-scale material support to Ukraine from Bundeswehr stocks; criticise depletion of German readiness.
* NATO membership retained “until establishment of independent capable European military alliance”; NATO as defence alliance only; oppose out-of-area missions without UN mandate and German security interest.
* Withdraw remaining allied troops and nuclear weapons from German soil (programme demand).
* Interest-guided foreign policy toward USA, China, Russia without hierarchical ranking.
* Exit UN Migration Pact and UN Refugee Pact; demand permanent German UN Security Council seat.
* Invitation and self-defence mitigations apply where conflict parties are framed under those doctrines for other parties; AfD minimises German forward interference.

### Expanded Analysis

Relative to Union/SPD/Green governing records, AfD reduces German participation in sanctions and lethal-aid packages and reframes NATO as temporary and defensive. That raises international-sovereignty autonomy on the interference axis while Russia-accommodation and alliance-scepticism remain distinctive. Soft-power tools are not the centre of the programme; energy and sanctions reversal are. Score reflects restraint on expeditionary and sanctions density, not isolationist completeness (NATO membership is retained conditionally).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 58

Sanctions-lift and Ukraine-support refusal reduce outward interference density; conditional NATO retention and incomplete exit from alliance structures keep the score mid-band. **58**.

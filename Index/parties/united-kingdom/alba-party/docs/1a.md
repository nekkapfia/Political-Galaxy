### Overview

The Alba Party, operating primarily in Scotland, positions itself as a more culturally conservative alternative to the Scottish National Party on a range of social and identity issues while remaining committed to Scottish independence. On Core 1A it occupies a mid-range position. The party has expressed greater scepticism than the SNP toward expansive gender-identity and related institutional speech controls, yet it does not advance a comprehensive repeal of the Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021 or the UK Online Safety Act. The result is a score higher than the SNP but still constrained by the dense Scottish and UK speech-restriction baseline.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Continued operation within the Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021 framework, without a high-priority repeal commitment.
* Retention of the UK Online Safety Act architecture as it applies in Scotland.
* Greater scepticism than the SNP toward expansive institutional and educational measures focused on gender identity and related speech controls.
* Defence of women’s sex-based rights and associated expression, which in practice resists certain identity-based restrictions on speech and association.
* No proposal for wholesale narrowing of public-order or communications offences.
* Retention of core criminal prohibitions on violence, true threats and direct physical harm.
* Limited engagement with non-crime hate-incident recording in the more expansive forms supported by the SNP.

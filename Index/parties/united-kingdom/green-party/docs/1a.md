# Core 1A: United Kingdom – Green Party of England and Wales
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 24**  
**Xenos Score: N/A**

### Overview

The Green Party of England and Wales, as a political entity, produces a restrictive Core 1A profile centred on identity-based speech controls, institutional orthodoxy requirements, and mandatory attitude-correction mechanisms. Its governing architecture retains and emphasises criminal and regulatory restrictions on expression characterised as identity-hostile, maintains and amends platform content duties, imposes institutional rules that treat critical speech as harmful, and includes mandatory unconscious-bias / EDI training. The cumulative weight of these measures places the party inside the ideological reprogramming / thought-control range.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Retention and active enforcement of stirring-up hatred offences and related Public Order Act speech provisions targeting speech characterised as racist, Islamophobic, antisemitic, misogynistic or otherwise identity-hostile.
* Retention and amendment of the Online Safety Act imposing platform duties against content described as falsehoods, fakes or half-truths in political debate.
* Institutional rules (Equality Act and related frameworks) that treat critical speech on gender, race and immigration as harmful and subject to restriction or sanction in public and quasi-public settings.
* Mandatory unconscious-bias / EDI / sensitivity training for staff, requiring participation in attitude-correction programmes.
* Self-identification rules that generate additional speech and association constraints around sex-based categories and single-sex spaces.
* Employment and professional sanctions for holding or expressing views deemed incompatible with institutional roles.
* Retention of the core non-violent hate-speech and communications offences that criminalise certain forms of expression.

### Expanded Analysis

The party’s restrictive architecture is defined by the simultaneous operation of criminal speech provisions, platform content duties, institutional orthodoxy rules, mandatory attitude-correction training, and professional sanctions for deviant views. Under Direct Restriction Only each of these is scored as a direct compulsion or prohibition on voluntary expression, association or belief-related conduct. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative set; no credit is assigned for any restrictions the party proposes to remove, because only the restrictions that remain are relevant.

The individual elements fall in the 22–32 range. When operated together they reinforce one another and produce a final placement of 24, inside the **19–27 Ideological reprogramming / thought control** band. The presence of mandatory training designed to correct attitudes is particularly characteristic of this band. The architecture does not reach the physical-coercion bands (10–18) or the floor.

No Endos/Xenos split is applied in this determination.

**Key Shift relative to higher-scoring parties:** Replacement of residual expression protections with a multi-layered system of identity-based speech criminalisation, institutional orthodoxy enforcement, and mandatory attitude-correction training.

### Why this produces Endos 24

The seven retained restrictions (criminal identity-speech offences, platform content duties, institutional orthodoxy rules, mandatory attitude-correction training, identity-reclassification effects on speech/association, professional sanctions for deviant views, and the core non-violent speech code) produce a cumulative weight of 24 under Negative-Only Weighting and the fixed band definitions. The score sits inside the ideological reprogramming band and below all major UK parties that do not centre mandatory attitude-correction and intensified identity-speech enforcement.

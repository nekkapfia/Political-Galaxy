# Core 1A: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 28**  
**Xenos Score: 18**

### Overview

The Partito Nazionale Fascista as governing political force produces a restrictive Core 1A profile defined by one-party monopoly of speech and association, comprehensive press control, the Special Tribunal for the Defence of the State, confino (internal exile), OVRA political policing, and differential coercion of designated out-groups. Early squadrist and legal consolidation (1922–26) is implementation of the same programme, not a separate ideology. From 1938 the Racial Laws added Jews as a hereditary Xenos category; the style of restriction (legal disability, exclusion from public and associational life, administrative coercion) did not change—only the set of targets expanded. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* One-party monopoly: opposition parties banned; independent political association prohibited (completed by the exceptional decrees of 1925–26).
* Comprehensive press controls; independent political media eliminated.
* Special Tribunal for the Defence of the State — parallel political jurisdiction criminalising dissenting organisation and speech.
* Confino (internal exile) and administrative detention used against dissidents.
* OVRA and related political-police surveillance and enforcement.
* Designated political Xenos (socialists, communists, organised anti-Fascists): tribunal prosecution, confino, organisational destruction.
* From 1938, Racial Laws: Jews added as a descent-based Xenos category under marriage restrictions, exclusion from public employment and education, and limits on professional and associational life — same style of legal disability and exclusion, wider target set.
* Ordinary criminal law continued for non-political matters.
* Corporative economic structure is out of scope for Core 1A; only bans on independent political and labour association are scored.

### Expanded Analysis

PNF Endos — the population that complied with the Fascist order — lived under a completed speech and association monopoly and press control, but not under routine confino if they remained within prescribed politics. That aligns with **28–36**: laws criminalise dissenting speech; access to critical information is curtailed; political jurisdiction is extensive. Score **28**.

PNF Xenos — political opponents throughout, and from 1938 also Jews under Racial Laws — faced Special Tribunal process, internal exile, or status-based exclusion from major domains of voluntary life (marriage, education, employment, association). That aligns with **10–18**: detention, confinement, enforced restriction of voluntary action and association. Score **18**.

The 1938 racial turn does not create a new restriction method for scoring purposes; it places an additional group under legal disability and exclusion of the same general type already applied to political enemies. RSI wartime deportation and extermination policy is a separate entity and is not scored here.

Framing as national discipline or corporative harmony is discarded. Only the observable restriction sets on each group are scored.

### Why this produces Endos 28 / Xenos 18

Endos aligns with **28–36** (one-party monopoly, press control, criminalisation of dissenting speech) → **28**. Xenos aligns with **10–18** (confino, Special Tribunal, Racial Laws dual structure and exclusion) → **18**.

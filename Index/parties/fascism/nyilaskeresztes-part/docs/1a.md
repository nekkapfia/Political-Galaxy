# Core 1A: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Párt
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 12**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

The Arrow Cross Party (Nyilaskeresztes Párt) under Ferenc Szálasi held power in Hungary from October 1944 until the early months of 1945. Its brief rule occurred under German occupation and in the final, collapsing phase of the war. The regime combined extreme nationalist and racial ideology with chaotic, violent repression. Political opponents, Jews and other designated groups faced immediate and extreme coercion, including mass killings and deportations carried out in cooperation with German forces. The practical duration was short, yet the intensity of restriction and violence was among the highest in the Fascism-country set. Under the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor, the Xenos score is held at the practical floor of 4.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Immediate and extreme repression of political opponents, residual moderate conservatives, and anyone associated with the previous Horthy or Lakatos governments.
* Active participation in the renewed deportation and mass killing of Hungarian Jews in Budapest and elsewhere after October 1944.
* Street-level and paramilitary violence by Arrow Cross militias against Jews, leftists and suspected enemies.
* Comprehensive suppression of independent political expression, association and press under conditions of war and occupation.
* Forced labour, arbitrary detention and summary executions.
* Radical racial-nationalist ideology that treated Jews and certain other groups as existential enemies.
* Retention of only the most minimal residual ordinary criminal order amid wartime collapse.

### Expanded Analysis

The restrictive profile of the Arrow Cross regime is defined by extreme, rapid-onset political and racial violence under conditions of military occupation and state disintegration. Political expression and association outside the Arrow Cross framework were extinguished. For designated Xenos groups—above all Jews—the regime moved quickly from legal discrimination (already advanced under previous Hungarian governments) to open mass murder. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, high-intensity prohibitions and lethal compulsions. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative weight while the regime lasted; the short duration does not generate upward credit.

The Endos/Xenos differential is extreme. Jews and other targeted groups faced systematic lethal violence that reached genocidal intensity in the final months of the war. Under the authoritative Core 1A rule that Endos and Xenos use identical criteria and the Score 4 Floor, the Xenos score is held at 4. The differential itself remains fully justified by the clear, stable and genocidal divergence in treatment.

**Key Shift:** Brief but extreme radicalisation under German occupation, producing one of the highest-intensity non-Italian, non-German restriction profiles in the Fascism-country set and a floor-level Xenos score.

# Core 1A: Fascism – Garda de Fier
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 18**  
**Xenos Score: 8**

### Overview

The Iron Guard (Legion of the Archangel Michael) under Horia Sima shared power with Ion Antonescu in the National Legionary State from September 1940 until the Legionary Rebellion of January 1941. Its period of influence was short but intense. The movement combined mystical Romanian nationalism, anti-Semitism and a cult of death and sacrifice with violent street politics and the systematic targeting of Jews, political opponents and residual liberal or conservative elites. During its months of power it imposed severe restrictions on speech, association and bodily security for designated enemies while never achieving the comprehensive totalitarian institutionalisation of mature Italian Fascism or National Socialism. The result is a high-intensity but short-lived restriction profile with a clear Endos/Xenos differential.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Violent suppression of political opponents, including assassinations and the persecution of former officials associated with King Carol II’s regime.
* Systematic anti-Semitic violence, expropriation and pogroms, culminating in the Bucharest pogrom of January 1941.
* Attempts to impose Legionary control over the press, cultural institutions and associational life.
* Creation of Legionary police and parallel power structures that operated with considerable impunity.
* Mystical-totalitarian ideology that demanded the moral and political transformation of Romanian society and treated Jews and political enemies as existential threats.
* Failure to consolidate a fully stable one-party institutional apparatus before the January 1941 clash with Antonescu.
* Retention of residual ordinary state structures alongside Legionary paramilitary violence.

### Expanded Analysis

The restrictive profile of the Iron Guard / Legionary State is defined by intense, ideologically driven political and racial violence rather than by the completed bureaucratic totalitarianism of longer-lived regimes. Political expression and association outside Legionary parameters were met with intimidation, expropriation and murder. Jews faced organised violence and expropriation that reached pogrom intensity. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, high-intensity prohibitions and physical attacks on autonomy. Negative-Only Weighting records the cumulative weight while the Legion held power; the short duration and incomplete institutionalisation prevent an even lower Endos score.

The Endos/Xenos differential is pronounced. Jews and political opponents of the previous regime faced markedly worse treatment—violence, expropriation and, in the January 1941 events, mass killing—than the core population that accommodated or supported the Legionary order. This constitutes a clear, stable and discretely targetable divergence satisfying the optional-split rule.

**Key Shift:** Short-lived but extremely violent attempt to impose a mystical-fascist order through paramilitary terror and anti-Semitic pogroms, producing one of the higher-intensity restriction profiles among the shorter-lived Fascism-country parties.

### Why this produces Endos 18 / Xenos 8

Intense political and racial violence combined with incomplete institutionalisation produces an Endos score of 18. The Xenos score of 8 reflects the additional lethal intensity directed at Jews and political enemies. Negative-Only Weighting applies throughout. The scores sit below Early Francoism and above the Arrow Cross and National Socialist floors, consistent with a short, violent, but not fully totalitarian episode.

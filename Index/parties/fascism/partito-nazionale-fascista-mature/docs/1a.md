# Core 1A: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista – Mature
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 28**  
**Xenos Score: 18**

### Overview

The Partito Nazionale Fascista (PNF), as the political entity that held power in Italy after the exceptional decrees of 1925–26 (leggi fascistissime) completed the transition to a one-party dictatorship. Opposition parties were banned, independent trade unions suppressed, the press placed under comprehensive state control, and special tribunals established for political offences. From 1938 the Racial Laws introduced formal legal discrimination against Jews. The regime now possessed a dense, institutionalised apparatus of speech, association and political controls that operated continuously rather than through the more improvised methods of the early phase. The result is a substantially lower autonomy score than Early Italian Fascism, with a widened Endos/Xenos differential after the introduction of racial legislation.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Exceptional decrees (leggi fascistissime) of 1925–1926 that banned opposition parties, suppressed independent trade unions and established the Special Tribunal for the Defence of the State.
* Comprehensive press controls requiring government approval of newspaper directors and systematically excluding anti-regime journalists and content.
* One-party monopoly of political organisation and the corporative restructuring of economic and social association.
* Confino (internal exile) and other administrative measures used routinely against dissidents.
* 1938 Racial Laws introducing formal discrimination against Jews (exclusion from public employment, education, military service, marriage restrictions and property limits).
* Continued operation of ordinary criminal law for non-political matters alongside the parallel political jurisdiction.
* Progressive expansion of surveillance and political policing through the OVRA and related organs.

### Expanded Analysis

The restrictive profile of Mature Italian Fascism is defined by the completion of the legal and institutional apparatus of the regime and the later addition of formal racial discrimination. Political expression and association outside the Fascist framework were no longer merely costly; they were formally prohibited and subject to specialised judicial and administrative repression. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, systematic prohibitions. Negative-Only Weighting records the full weight of the one-party system, press monopoly, special tribunals and, after 1938, the Racial Laws. Residual private and non-political spheres continued to exist and prevent an even lower score, but they do not generate upward credit.

The Endos/Xenos differential widens after 1938. Jews became a clearly defined legal out-group subject to systematic exclusion from multiple domains of public and professional life. Political opponents remained subject to the special-tribunal and confino regime. Both groups faced markedly worse treatment than the core population that operated within Fascist institutions, satisfying the optional-split rule with a clearer and more formalised divergence than in the early phase.

**Key Shift:** Completion of the legal and institutional apparatus of the Fascist dictatorship and the introduction of formal racial legislation, producing a durable further reduction in personal autonomy and a widened Endos/Xenos differential.

### Why this produces Endos 28 / Xenos 18

The dense restrictive architecture of the one-party state, press monopoly, special tribunals and (from 1938) Racial Laws produces an Endos score of 28. The Xenos score of 18 reflects the additional formal discrimination and heightened repression directed at Jews and remaining political opponents. Negative-Only Weighting applies throughout. The scores sit substantially below Early Italian Fascism and above the still more extreme wartime / Salò phase.

# Core 1A: Fascism – Nazism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 8**

### Overview

Nazism, as the political entity that held power in Germany from 1933 to 1945, produced one of the most rapid and comprehensive collapses of personal autonomy in modern history. Once in power the NSDAP dismantled residual Weimar protections of speech, association, press and political organisation and constructed a totalitarian system of multi-domain controls. Designated out-groups (Jews, political opponents, Roma, homosexuals, Jehovah’s Witnesses, the disabled and others) faced intensifying differential treatment that culminated in genocide. The Endos score falls to the extreme low single digits; the Xenos score is held at the practical floor of 4 under the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor. This is the characteristic restrictive architecture of Nazism as a governing political force.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Enabling Act and Reichstag Fire Decree suspending core civil liberties and enabling rule by decree.
* Rapid destruction of independent parties, trade unions, press and civil-society organisations (Gleichschaltung).
* Establishment of the one-party state and the concentration-camp system for political opponents.
* Nuremberg Laws (1935) and subsequent racial legislation creating a formal legal dual structure for Jews.
* Comprehensive controls on speech, association, movement, residence and occupation for the general population, intensifying under wartime mobilisation.
* Systematic extermination of Jews and mass murder of other designated groups (band-5 / genocidal intensity for the most targeted Xenos categories).
* Ordinary criminal law retained only marginal relevance for non-political matters.
* Total mobilisation of society under Party and SS control, eliminating residual private spheres of autonomy.

### Expanded Analysis

The restrictive profile of National Socialism is defined by the near-total replacement of the preceding liberal-constitutional order with a multi-domain totalitarian system and by the progression from legal discrimination to open genocide against designated out-groups. Under Direct Restriction Only the scored weight is the full set of prohibitions and compulsions that eliminated independent political expression, association, religious autonomy and, for the most targeted groups, bodily survival. Negative-Only Weighting records this cumulative architecture without upward credit for residual private life that survived only at the margins.

The Endos/Xenos differential is extreme and formal. Jews and other designated categories were placed under a separate and progressively more lethal legal and administrative regime. After 1941–1942 this regime reached systematic extermination. Under the authoritative Core 1A rule that Endos and Xenos use identical criteria and the Score 4 Floor, the Xenos score is held at 4 rather than pushed into the theoretical 1–2 physiological-control band. The differential itself remains fully justified by the clear, stable and genocidal divergence in treatment.

**Key Shift:** Rapid construction of a totalitarian multi-domain system culminating in wartime total mobilisation and genocide, producing the lowest Endos score and a floor-level Xenos score among the Fascism-country parties.

# Core 1A: Fascism – Nazism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 8**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

Nazism as a governing political force produces a floor-adjacent Core 1A profile. The final restriction set includes destruction of independent speech and association, one-party monopoly, the concentration-camp system, comprehensive wartime controls on movement and daily life, and designated out-groups (Jews above all; also political opponents, Roma, homosexuals, Jehovah’s Witnesses, the disabled and others) subject to legal dual structure, deportation, forced medical experimentation, extermination through torture, and systematic genocide. Endos aligns with extreme low autonomy (7–9). Xenos aligns with the lowest applicable floor bands (4–5). A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Enabling Act and Reichstag Fire Decree suspending core civil liberties and enabling rule by decree over speech, association and political organisation.
* Gleichschaltung: destruction of independent parties, trade unions, press and civil-society organisations; one-party monopoly under the NSDAP.
* Concentration-camp system for political opponents and expanded categories of designated enemies — detention, forced labour, and confinement.
* Nuremberg Laws (1935) and subsequent racial legislation creating a formal legal dual structure for Jews (citizenship, marriage, residence, occupation, association).
* Comprehensive controls on speech, public association, movement and residence for the general population, intensifying under wartime mobilisation.
* Systematic extermination of Jews as a designated group (genocide) — band **5**.
* Forced medical experimentation and extermination through torture on designated prisoners — band **4**.
* Suppression of independent religious organisation where it conflicted with Party control; Jehovah’s Witnesses targeted for refusal of ideological conformity.
* Ordinary violent-crime prohibitions did not protect designated political or racial targets.

### Expanded Analysis

Nazism replaced the residual Weimar order with a multi-domain totalitarian system. Independent political expression, association and press were eliminated. The camp system and political police made opposition lethal. Wartime mobilisation extended controls into daily movement and association for the general population.

**Endos** (Germans not in designated enemy categories): public political speech outside Party orthodoxy was closed; association was channelled through Party and state organisations; residual private non-political life existed under surveillance and wartime controls but was heavily restricted across voluntary political and associational domains. This aligns with **7–9** — extreme low autonomy: control extends to most voluntary actions with heavy restrictions on all aspects of life. Score **8**.

**Xenos** (Jews and other designated groups): Nuremberg dual structure, deportation, camps, forced medical experimentation, extermination through torture, and systematic genocide. Lowest applicable bands are **5** (systematic extermination of designated groups) and **4** (forced medical experimentation, extermination through torture). Lowest band governs → **4**.

Framing as national revival or wartime necessity is discarded. Duration is irrelevant. Only the observable restriction architecture is scored against band text.

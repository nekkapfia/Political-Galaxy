# Core 1A: Fascism – Early PNF
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 36**  
**Xenos Score: 28**

### Overview

Early Partito Nazionale Fascista (roughly 1922–late 1925) produces a restrictive Core 1A profile defined by progressive destruction of competitive multi-party politics, squadrist and administrative pressure on opposition speech and association, early press restrictions, and differential targeting of socialists, communists and other organised anti-Fascists. Liberal constitutional forms still existed on paper; the full one-party monopoly, OVRA and special-tribunal system of 1926 onward were not yet complete. This phase is pre-racial: ethno-biological antisemitism was not an organising principle. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Systematic pressure on opposition newspapers through violence, intimidation, administrative harassment and seizures.
* Acerbo Law (1923) and electoral manipulation undermining genuine competitive multi-party politics.
* Squadrist violence and administrative sanctions against socialist, communist, liberal and Catholic opponents — physical intimidation of speech and association.
* Progressive restriction of independent trade-union and political associational activity outside Fascist structures.
* Residual liberal legal forms (parliament, courts, formal press law) still existed but were increasingly subordinated to Fascist political will.
* Differential intensity against organised political opponents on the left and other anti-Fascist forces.
* Ordinary criminal law continued to operate for non-political private matters.
* No completed one-party legal monopoly, special political tribunal, or racial dual-structure legislation in this window.

### Expanded Analysis

Early PNF narrowed the practical space for voluntary political expression and association through violence, administrative pressure and early legal measures. Independent political speech was not yet subject to the comprehensive monopoly of the mature dictatorship, but the cost of opposition had already become severe.

**Endos** (general population accommodating the new order): public political life was contracting; residual private and non-political spheres continued; formal liberties remained on paper. Criminalisation and intimidation of dissenting political speech and the hollowing of competitive politics align with **28–36** — ideological enforcement and police-state mechanisms beginning to close dissent, without yet completing total information control or universal criminalisation. Score **36** (top of band; architecture incomplete).

**Xenos** (organised socialists, communists, active anti-Fascists): squadrist violence, organisational destruction and press intimidation were routine. This is markedly worse treatment of a discretely targetable out-group — physical coercion against speech and association — aligning with the lower part of **28–36** / border of denser enforcement. Score **28**.

Framing as restoration of order is discarded. This document covers only the early, pre-racial, pre-OVRA consolidation phase. Mature PNF and the 1938 racial turn are separate entities.

### Why this produces Endos 36 / Xenos 28

Endos aligns with **28–36** (criminalisation and intimidation of dissenting political speech; progressive closure of competitive association; incomplete monopoly) → **36**. Xenos aligns with intensified physical and administrative coercion against organised opponents within the same band range → **28**.

# Core 1A: Russia – LDPR
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 28**  
**Xenos Score: 18**

### Overview

LDPR (Liberal Democratic Party of Russia) produces a restrictive Core 1A profile as a systemic nationalist party operating inside the Russian managed-sovereignty order. It does not govern as a full United Russia substitute, but its programme and parliamentary practice defend hard security speech limits, nationalist order politics, and harsh treatment of designated internal enemies and “foreign” influence categories. It does not offer classical-liberal speech maximalism. Placement sits in **28–36** for Endos with a lower Xenos score where enemy categories are marked. A limited Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Defence of criminal and administrative limits on speech that “discredits” the state, military, or official security narratives — not a repeal party for wartime and extremism speech offences.
* Nationalist and order politics supporting tough public-order and anti-extremism enforcement against designated opponents.
* Rhetoric and programme hostility toward groups framed as internal enemies, foreign agents, or civilisational threats — supports differential coercion when aligned with state practice.
* Ordinary direct-harm criminal law retained.
* No progressive Western DEI attitude-correction stack; restriction style is authoritarian-nationalist, not managerial-progressive.
* No programme to dismantle foreign-agent / undesirable-organisation logic as a liberalisation project.
* Immigration and foreign-national status → partly Core 2A; domestic speech and association posture remains Core 1A.
* Party-system structure out of scope for Core 1A.

### Expanded Analysis

**Endos:** LDPR does not govern alone at United Russia intensity, but its Core 1A-relevant posture accepts criminalised political and security speech and strong order enforcement. Independent media and opposition association are not priorities to restore. That aligns with **28–36 — Ideological enforcement and police state**: laws penalise dissenting security-political speech; information and association space is narrowed. Score **28**.

**Xenos** (persons and groups LDPR marks as enemies — organised liberals or opposition framed as foreign agents, designated ethnic or ideological targets in hard rhetoric when paired with state tools): supports heightened coercion already available in Russian law. Aligns with **10–18 — Extreme restrictions on voluntary actions, movement and association** when those tools are the lived restriction set for the out-group. Score **18**.

LDPR is not scored as if it alone built the full United Russia apparatus; it is scored on the restriction set its programme defends and would keep. Framing as patriotism is discarded.

### Why this produces Endos 28 / Xenos 18

Endos aligns with **28–36** (defence of criminalised security-political speech and hard order politics) → **28**. Xenos aligns with **10–18** (support for differential coercion of designated enemy categories) → **18**.

# Core 1A: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 4**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

The Khmer Rouge (Communist Party of Kampuchea), as the political entity that held power in Democratic Kampuchea from 1975 to 1979, produced one of the most extreme collapses of personal autonomy on record. It combined the abolition of money, markets, private property, independent religion, family autonomy and cities with forced labour, mass relocation, and the systematic extermination of designated categories (former regime officials, intellectuals, ethnic minorities, and those labelled as enemies). Under the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor, both Endos and Xenos scores are held at the practical floor of 4. The split is not applied because the regime’s repression was so comprehensive that the divergence, while real, does not produce a meaningful higher Endos score under the floor rule.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Abolition of money, markets and private commerce; forced collectivisation of all economic life.
* Evacuation of cities and the forced relocation of urban populations to rural labour sites.
* Systematic destruction of independent religious practice, particularly Buddhism, and the targeting of monks.
* Abolition of formal education, independent cultural life and most professional specialisations.
* Forced labour under military-style discipline with extreme working hours and minimal subsistence.
* Class and political labelling that designated large categories of the population as enemies subject to execution or lethal deprivation.
* Near-total control of movement, residence, speech, association and family life.
* Mass executions, torture centres (including S-21) and death through overwork and starvation as instruments of policy.

### Expanded Analysis

The Khmer Rouge as a governing political force attempted the most radical reconstruction of society among Communist regimes, eliminating residual private, economic and cultural spheres almost entirely. Political expression and association outside the Party’s absolute control were impossible. Designated categories faced systematic extermination. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, near-total prohibitions and lethal compulsions across every major domain of personal autonomy. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative architecture; no residual private sphere of meaningful size survived to generate upward credit.

Because the repression was so comprehensive, the Endos score is already at the practical floor. The additional targeting of specific groups (intellectuals, former officials, ethnic Vietnamese, Cham, etc.) is real, yet under the Score 4 Floor and the identical-criteria rule both scores are held at 4 and the optional split is not applied. This is consistent with the treatment of regimes whose overall intensity already saturates the bottom of the scale.

**Key Shift:** Attempted total reconstruction of society through the abolition of money, cities, private life and independent institutions, combined with mass killing of designated categories, producing the lowest possible autonomy scores under Core 1A rules.

### Why this produces Endos 4 / Xenos 4

Near-total multi-domain coercion and mass extermination produce scores at the practical floor of 4 for both Endos and Xenos. The optional split is not applied because the overall intensity already saturates the floor; a higher Endos score would be inconsistent with the observed comprehensive destruction of personal autonomy. Negative-Only Weighting applies throughout. These are the lowest scores in the Communism-country set.

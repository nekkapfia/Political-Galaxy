# Core 1A: Communism – Juche
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 7**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

Juche, as the political entity that has held power in North Korea under the Kim dynasty (from Kim Il-sung onward), has produced one of the most comprehensive and enduring multi-domain collapses of personal autonomy still in existence. It combines hereditary totalitarian rule, near-total control of information and movement, a pervasive surveillance and informant system, forced labour, and the systematic targeting of political and social categories through the songbun classification system. Independent political expression, association, religion and access to unauthorised information are extinguished. The Endos score falls to the high single digits; the Xenos score is held at the practical floor of 4 under the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Hereditary one-party (and one-family) monopoly with the complete subordination of state, military and social organisations to the ruling authority.
* Near-total control of information, including the prohibition of unauthorised foreign media, and severe punishment for contact with outside information.
* Pervasive surveillance and informant networks that condition everyday speech and association.
* Songbun hereditary/socio-political classification system that assigns differential rights, opportunities and risks across generations.
* Severe restrictions on internal movement and near-total prohibition of unauthorised exit from the country.
* Forced labour, including political prison camps (kwanliso) for designated enemies and their families.
* Systematic suppression of independent religious practice and independent cultural expression.
* Collective punishment principles that extend liability to family members of those labelled as enemies.

### Expanded Analysis

Juche as a governing political force has maintained and refined a multi-domain totalitarian system over decades. Political expression and association outside the prescribed line are impossible. Access to unauthorised information is treated as a serious political offence. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, systematic prohibitions across speech, association, information, movement, labour and bodily security. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative architecture; residual private existence that survives only under intense surveillance and songbun constraints generates no upward credit.

The Endos/Xenos differential is extreme and formalised through songbun and the political camp system. Those assigned hostile or wavering songbun, together with political offenders and their families, face markedly worse treatment, including incarceration in camps where conditions are lethal. Under the authoritative Core 1A rule that Endos and Xenos use identical criteria and the Score 4 Floor, the Xenos score is held at 4. The differential itself remains fully justified by the clear, stable and often hereditary divergence in treatment.

**Key Shift:** Construction and long-term maintenance of a hereditary totalitarian system that combines near-total information and movement control with a formalised socio-political caste system and political prison camps, producing one of the lowest autonomy profiles among Communist political entities.

### Why this produces Endos 7 / Xenos 4

Near-total multi-domain coercion, hereditary classification and the political camp system produce an Endos score of 7. The Xenos score is set at the practical floor of 4 in accordance with the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor. Negative-Only Weighting applies throughout. The scores sit at the extreme low end of the Communism-country set, comparable to Stalinism and Maoism.

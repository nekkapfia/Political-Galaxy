## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 22**

### Overview

Leninism, as the political entity that held power in Soviet Russia from the October Revolution through Lenin’s effective leadership (approximately 1917–1924), constructed a one-party dictatorship that systematically eliminated independent political expression, association and press. The Cheka, the suppression of the Constituent Assembly, the banning of opposition parties, War Communism and the early concentration-camp system formed the core restrictive architecture. While less totalising and less bureaucratically complete than later Stalinism, Leninism already established the fundamental pattern of single-party monopoly, political terror against designated enemies, and the subordination of personal autonomy to the requirements of revolutionary power. Differential treatment of class enemies, political opponents and later certain national groups was present from the outset.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Suppression of the Constituent Assembly and the elimination of competitive multi-party politics.
* Banning of opposition parties and independent socialist, anarchist and liberal organisations.
* Creation of the Cheka and the early use of revolutionary terror, hostage-taking and summary execution against class and political enemies.
* Comprehensive press controls and the closure of non-Bolshevik newspapers.
* War Communism measures that subordinated economic and personal activity to state command, including forced requisitioning and labour compulsion.
* Early concentration camps and forced labour for political and class opponents.
* Red Terror as an explicit instrument of policy against designated internal enemies.
* Residual space for ordinary private life outside the political sphere remained larger than under later Stalinism, but political expression and association were already tightly restricted.

### Expanded Analysis

Leninism as a governing political force replaced the residual liberal and socialist pluralism of 1917 with a single-party monopoly backed by political police and revolutionary violence. Under Direct Restriction Only the scored weight is the set of prohibitions and compulsions that eliminated independent political organisation, free press and organised opposition. Negative-Only Weighting records these foundational restrictions; residual private spheres that survived outside politics generate no upward credit. The architecture is less complete and less bureaucratically dense than mature Stalinism, yet it already established the essential pattern of one-party dictatorship and differential terror.

A clear Endos/Xenos differential exists. Class enemies, political opponents, former regime officials and certain national or social groups faced markedly higher levels of violence, expropriation and detention than the core population that accepted or served the new regime. This constitutes a stable, discretely targetable divergence satisfying the optional-split rule.

**Key Shift:** Replacement of residual multi-party and independent associational life with a one-party dictatorship backed by political terror, establishing the foundational restrictive architecture of Leninist rule.

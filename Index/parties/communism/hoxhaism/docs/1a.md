# Core 1A: Communism – Hoxhaism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 14**  
**Xenos Score: 6**

### Overview

Hoxhaism, as the political entity that held power in Albania under Enver Hoxha (1944–1985), constructed one of the most closed and intensely controlled Communist systems in Europe. It combined extreme isolation, permanent mobilisation against internal and external enemies, comprehensive surveillance, and the systematic destruction of independent religious, intellectual and associational life. Political expression outside the Party line was extinguished; designated class and political enemies faced imprisonment, internal exile and execution. The regime’s self-image as the purest remaining defender of Stalinism after the Sino-Soviet split reinforced its refusal to liberalise. The Endos score sits in the low teens; a clear Xenos differential is present.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Complete one-party monopoly under the Party of Labour of Albania with the elimination of residual internal pluralism.
* Extreme isolation from both East and West, including the later break with China, producing a uniquely closed information and movement environment.
* Comprehensive surveillance and the Sigurimi political police as central instruments of control.
* Systematic destruction of organised religion (declaration of the world’s first atheist state) and the targeting of clergy and believers.
* Forced collectivisation and tight labour discipline.
* Class-label and political-biography systems that assigned differential rights and risks.
* Severe restrictions on movement, foreign contact, and independent cultural expression.
* Use of prisons, internment camps and internal exile against political and class enemies.

### Expanded Analysis

Hoxhaism as a governing political force radicalised the Stalinist model in the direction of maximum isolation and permanent internal vigilance. Political expression and association outside the Party were impossible. Independent religious life was formally abolished. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, systematic prohibitions across speech, association, religion, movement and information. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative architecture; residual private existence that survived only under intense surveillance generates no upward credit.

A clear Endos/Xenos differential exists. Designated class enemies, political opponents, religious believers and those with foreign contacts faced markedly higher levels of imprisonment, exile and execution than the core population that demonstrated reliable conformity. This constitutes a stable, discretely targetable divergence satisfying the optional-split rule.

**Key Shift:** Construction of an extremely closed, surveillance-intensive one-party system that combined Stalinist repression with unique isolation and the formal abolition of religion, producing one of the more severe European Communist restriction profiles.

### Why this produces Endos 14 / Xenos 6

Extreme isolation, comprehensive surveillance, the destruction of religion and continuous political repression produce an Endos score of 14. The Xenos score of 6 reflects the additional intensity directed at designated enemies. Negative-Only Weighting applies throughout. The scores sit above Stalinism and Maoism but well below any reform-Communist or post-totalitarian model.

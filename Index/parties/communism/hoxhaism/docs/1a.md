# Core 1A: Communism – Hoxhaism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 10**  
**Xenos Score: 6**

### Overview

Hoxhaism as a governing political force produces a near-floor Core 1A profile defined by total Party-state monopoly of speech and association, extreme isolation, pervasive Sigurimi surveillance, mandatory ideological conformity, systematic suppression of religion, and designated enemy categories subject to imprisonment, internal exile and execution. Albania under Enver Hoxha combined Stalinist political architecture with intensified autarky and anti-revisionist orthodoxy. Cumulative weight sits in the extreme-restriction / upper floor bands. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Total monopoly of political expression, press, education and association under the Party of Labour of Albania; no legal independent political speech or organisation.
* Pervasive Sigurimi security-police surveillance and informant networks making private expression continuously observable and sanctionable.
* Mandatory ideological conformity to Hoxhaist anti-revisionist Marxism-Leninism in public life, education and culture; deviation treated as political crime.
* Systematic suppression of religion: mass closure of churches, mosques and religious institutions; persecution of clergy; prohibition of public and much private religious practice — among the most complete anti-religious campaigns in the Communist set.
* Internal exile, forced labour camps and political imprisonment for designated opponents and often their families.
* Extreme external isolation after successive breaks with Yugoslavia, the Soviet Union and China, reinforcing informational closure and movement bans.
* Class and political enemy categories (including residual “kulaks,” religious figures, alleged revisionists and foreign agents) subject to intensified coercion.
* Near-total closure of residual private political space; ordinary social life heavily politicised and monitored.
* Ordinary violent-crime prohibitions exist but do not define placement.

### Expanded Analysis

Hoxhaism is Stalinist one-party monopoly plus maximal isolation and anti-religious intensity. For Endos — ordinary citizens not labelled as enemies — public expression was limited to prescribed ideology; surveillance and collective responsibility made dissent costly; foreign information and travel were almost entirely cut off. Placement at 10 reflects extreme multi-domain restriction short of full Khmer-style abolition of private and urban life.

For Xenos — political opponents, religious communities, designated class and “revisionist” enemies — imprisonment, internal exile, camp labour and execution were routine instruments. Religious believers as a category faced especially systematic destruction of institutional and public practice, pulling Xenos into the upper floor.

Framing as defence of pure socialism or anti-revisionism is discarded. Only the observable restriction architecture is scored.

### Why this produces Endos 10 / Xenos 6

Speech and association monopoly, Sigurimi surveillance, mandatory ideology, near-total religious suppression, camps, exile and external isolation place Endos at 10. Designated political, class and religious targets under intensified coercion produce Xenos 6.

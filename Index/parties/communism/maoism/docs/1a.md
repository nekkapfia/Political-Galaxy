# Core 1A: Communism – Maoism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 11**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

Maoism, as the political entity that held power in the People’s Republic of China under Mao Zedong (particularly in its High Maoist phases: land reform and campaigns of the early 1950s, the Great Leap Forward, and the Cultural Revolution), produced a multi-domain system of speech, association, movement, labour and bodily controls of extreme intensity. Mass mobilisation campaigns, struggle sessions, the destruction of independent cultural and intellectual life, and the systematic targeting of designated class, political and “bad element” categories defined its characteristic practice. The Endos score falls to the low teens; the Xenos score is held at the practical floor of 4 under the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Complete one-party monopoly under the Chinese Communist Party with the subordination of all organisations to Party control and successive rectification campaigns.
* Comprehensive controls on speech, publication and cultural expression, intensified during the Anti-Rightist Campaign and the Cultural Revolution.
* Mass political campaigns (land reform, suppression of counter-revolutionaries, Anti-Rightist Campaign, Cultural Revolution) that used struggle sessions, public humiliation, detention and violence against designated enemies.
* Forced collectivisation, the people’s communes and the labour discipline of the Great Leap Forward period.
* Class-label system that assigned hereditary or semi-hereditary political status and differential rights to “good” and “bad” class origins.
* Restriction of movement and residence through the hukou system and political approval requirements.
* Systematic attack on independent religious, intellectual and traditional cultural expression.
* Ordinary private life reduced to a narrow residual sphere tightly conditioned by political reliability, campaign participation and surveillance.

### Expanded Analysis

Maoism as a governing political force radicalised the Leninist-Stalinist model through continuous mass mobilisation and the deliberate destruction of residual private and cultural spheres. Political expression and association outside the prescribed Party line were extinguished. Designated class and political categories faced struggle sessions, detention, exile to the countryside and, in peak campaigns, mass killing. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, systematic prohibitions and compulsions across multiple domains. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative architecture of High Maoist practice; residual private existence that survived only at the margins generates no upward credit.

The Endos/Xenos differential is extreme. “Landlords,” “rightists,” “capitalist roaders,” “bad elements” and other designated categories were placed under separate and often lethal regimes of repression. Under the authoritative Core 1A rule that Endos and Xenos use identical criteria and the Score 4 Floor, the Xenos score is held at 4. The differential itself remains fully justified by the clear, stable and mass-scale divergence in treatment.

**Key Shift:** Construction of a continuous-campaign totalitarian system that combined one-party monopoly with mass mobilisation, struggle sessions and class-based differential repression, producing one of the lowest autonomy profiles among Communist political entities.

# Core 1A: Communism – Juche
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 7**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

Juche as the governing ideology-practice of the DPRK produces a floor-adjacent Core 1A profile defined by total leader-and-Party monopoly of speech and association, songbun hereditary political classification, pervasive surveillance, mandatory ideological worship and study, movement and information closure, and designated hostile classes subject to camp detention, collective punishment and lethal deprivation. Cumulative weight sits at or near the practical floor. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies; Xenos reaches the Score 4 floor.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Total monopoly of political expression, media, education and association under Party and supreme leader; no legal independent speech or organisation of any kind.
* Songbun hereditary status system classifying the population into core/loyal, wavering and hostile strata.
* Pervasive surveillance, informant networks and inminban neighbourhood organisational controls making private expression continuously observable and sanctionable.
* Mandatory ideological study and leader-worship practices in schools, workplaces and public life; institutional routine designed to enforce alignment with official belief and ritual.
* Extreme controls on internal movement, residence and any contact with foreign information or media; unauthorised exit treated as political crime.
* Collective family punishment for political offences and defection, extending sanctions beyond the individual.
* Kwanliso political prison camps for designated hostile elements, often involving multi-generational detention, forced labour and extreme material deprivation.
* Designated hostile songbun categories and political offenders subject to camp systems, execution and collective sanctions as standing policy.
* Near-total elimination of residual private political, informational or associational space.
* Ordinary violent-crime prohibitions exist but do not define placement.

### Expanded Analysis

Juche operationalises total political and personal control more completely than most Communist regimes. Songbun makes political status hereditary. Surveillance and neighbourhood organisation close private speech. Mandatory leader-centred ideology functions as continuous attitude and ritual alignment. Movement and information are sealed from the outside world.

For Endos — those in loyal or acceptable songbun strata not under active camp detention — daily life remains under comprehensive speech, association, movement and belief controls. Placement at 7 reflects near-floor multi-domain restriction without requiring the absolute theoretical zero.

For Xenos — hostile songbun, political offenders and their families — kwanliso camps, collective punishment and lethal conditions are standing policy. That differential saturates the practical floor at 4 under project rules.

Framing as self-reliance, anti-imperialism or unique Korean socialism is discarded. Only the observable restriction architecture is scored.

### Why this produces Endos 7 / Xenos 4

Total speech and association monopoly, songbun, surveillance, mandatory leader ideology, movement and information closure place Endos at 7. Hostile categories under camp detention, collective punishment and lethal deprivation produce Xenos 4 (practical floor).

# Core 1A: Communism – Marxism (Anarcho-Communism)
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 98**  
**Xenos Score: 20**

### Overview

Marxism (Anarcho-Communism), scored as pure end-state doctrine rather than as any later Party-state regime, produces a near-ceiling Core 1A profile for Endos and a low score for Xenos. The end-state is classless and stateless: no standing police, army, speech code, or ideological enforcement apparatus over the associated producers. The only prescribed coercion is aimed at preventing restoration of the bourgeoisie as a class. Economic structure (collective ownership of the means of production) is out of scope for Core 1A. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* End-state: no specialised coercive state apparatus — no standing police, political prisons, or army of the class-state type over the producers; administration of things replaces government of persons.
* No standing criminal speech, association, or belief codes applied to Endos in the end-state text.
* No mandatory ideological re-education, self-criticism, or thought-reform programmes prescribed for the classless society.
* System-preservation rule only: coercion is authorised against attempts to resurrect bourgeois class rule (restore private capital over social production, reimpose wage-labour exploitation as class relation, or rebuild bourgeois political monopoly).
* During revolutionary removal of the bourgeoisie (Xenos phase): expropriation of the means of production, political neutralisation of the defeated class, and denial of protected speech, press and association for counter-revolutionary organisation.
* Economic collectivism — abolition of private ownership of major means of production — is an economic-system feature, not a Core 1A restriction on personal voluntary acts.
* Ordinary interpersonal direct-harm prohibitions may exist as minimal mutual protection; they do not define placement.

### Expanded Analysis

For Endos — the associated producers in the classless end-state — pure doctrine prescribes almost no personal-autonomy restrictions. There is no institutional carrier left for censorship, movement controls, or attitude-correction once the state has withered. Placement at 98 follows the band language for 97–98: the only rules that remain are those that allow the association to protect itself from destruction. That is not band 100 (no rules of any kind), because system-preservation against bourgeois restoration is still a rule. It is the top of the system-preservation band.

For Xenos — the bourgeoisie and active restorers during the revolutionary transition — the programme authorises expropriation, political neutralisation, and the withdrawal of liberal personal-autonomy protections. That is designated out-group removal. On pure doctrine, before later regimes add Cheka, Gulag or Cultural Revolution instruments, Xenos sits in the high teens to low 20s.

Leninism, Stalinism, Maoism and related governing entities are scored separately. Their police, censorship and thought-reform systems are not read back into Marxism (Anarcho-Communism) as end-state doctrine.

### Why this produces Endos 98 / Xenos 20

End-state Endos face no standing personal restrictions beyond system-preservation against class restoration → 98. Xenos (bourgeoisie and restorers) face expropriation and political neutralisation without protected autonomy → 20.

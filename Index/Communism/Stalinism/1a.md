# Core 1A: Communism – Stalinism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 9**  
**Xenos Score: 4**

### Overview

Stalinism, as the political entity that held power in the Soviet Union under Joseph Stalin (approximately 1928–1953), produced one of the most comprehensive multi-domain collapses of personal autonomy in modern history. Building on the Leninist foundation, it constructed a fully totalitarian system of speech, association, movement, residence, labour and bodily controls. Mass terror, the Gulag, forced collectivisation, the destruction of independent cultural and religious life, and the systematic targeting of designated social, national and political categories defined its characteristic practice. The Endos score falls to the extreme low single digits; the Xenos score is held at the practical floor of 4 under the identical-criteria rule and the Score 4 Floor.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Complete one-party monopoly with the destruction of residual internal Party pluralism and the subordination of all organisations to Party control.
* Comprehensive censorship and the elimination of independent press, literature, science and cultural expression outside the prescribed line.
* Mass political terror, including the Great Terror, show trials, and the systematic use of the NKVD against real and invented enemies.
* The Gulag system of forced labour camps as a central instrument of political and economic control.
* Forced collectivisation and the associated famine, deportation and repression of the peasantry as a social category.
* Mass deportations of national groups and the creation of special settlement regimes.
* Near-total control of movement, residence and employment through internal passports, propiska and workplace discipline.
* Systematic destruction or severe restriction of independent religious practice and institutions.
* Ordinary private life reduced to a narrow residual sphere tightly conditioned by political reliability and surveillance.

### Expanded Analysis

Stalinism as a governing political force completed and radicalised the Leninist model into a multi-domain totalitarian system. Political expression and association outside the prescribed Party line were extinguished. Designated social, national and political categories faced mass deportation, imprisonment and death. Under Direct Restriction Only these are scored as direct, systematic prohibitions and compulsions across speech, association, movement, labour and bodily survival. Negative-Only Weighting records the full cumulative architecture; residual private existence that survived only at the margins generates no upward credit.

The Endos/Xenos differential is extreme and formal. “Enemies of the people,” specific nationalities, former kulaks, political opponents and other designated categories were placed under separate and often lethal regimes of repression. Under the authoritative Core 1A rule that Endos and Xenos use identical criteria and the Score 4 Floor, the Xenos score is held at 4. The differential itself remains fully justified by the clear, stable and mass-lethal divergence in treatment.

**Key Shift:** Completion of a multi-domain totalitarian system through mass terror, the Gulag, forced collectivisation and the systematic targeting of social and national categories, producing one of the lowest autonomy profiles among Communist political entities.

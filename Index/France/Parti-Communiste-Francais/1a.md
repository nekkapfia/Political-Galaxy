# Core 1A: France – Parti Communiste Français
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 44**  
**Xenos Score: N/A**

### Overview

The Parti Communiste Français produces a mid-to-lower Core 1A profile built from traditional left defence of anti-racist and anti-fascist speech limits, retention of the 1881 Press Law hate-speech framework, Gayssot and related memory offences, platform duties under DSA-era rules, and equality frameworks. Class and anti-fascist framing remain heavier than the newest identity-speech intensifications associated with LFI. The party operates inside the Nouveau Front Populaire but does not set the densest identity-protective tone. No Endos/Xenos split is applied.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Traditional left defence of criminal hate-speech law (1881 Press Law framework on incitement to hatred or discrimination) and its use against far-right, racist and fascist expression; no narrowing agenda.
* Retention of Gayssot Law and related memory offences criminalising Holocaust denial / contestation of designated crimes against humanity.
* Support for platform content regulation against illegal and hateful material under DSA implementation and residual national obligations.
* Anti-discrimination and equality frameworks aligned with broader left commitments; secondary institutional pressure on expression remains part of the public-sector and campaign environment.
* Generally supportive of anti-racist and minority-protection norms; less central than LFI to elevating anti-Islamophobia as a primary speech-regulating principle that treats critical discussion of Islam as presumptively illegitimate.
* Economic and class framing remains heavier relative to comprehensive identity-orthodoxy framing than under LFI or EELV.
* Operates inside the Nouveau Front Populaire but does not drive the densest identity-speech posture; LFI priorities dominate when the alliance is LFI-weighted.
* No programme to narrow core hate-speech or platform-regulation architecture.
* Retention of ordinary direct-harm criminal prohibitions.

### Expanded Analysis

PCF is legacy left on Core 1A. It affirms anti-racist and anti-fascist speech limits and retains the full national hate-speech, memory-law and platform baseline. Identity-protective norms are present but less programme-central than under LFI. The party has not driven the shift that made anti-Islamophobia a decisive speech-regulating principle on the radical left.

Critical speech controls remain real through the shared national architecture. What is thinner relative to LFI is the mobilisation-backed intensity on Islam-related and newest identity-speech axes. Class and anti-fascist framing still organise more of the party’s speech politics than comprehensive identity orthodoxy of the LFI/EELV type.

No stable, discretely targetable out-group differential is formalised by PCF policy at the level required for an Endos/Xenos split. The party belongs on the progressive side of the baseline but not at the densification pole.

The architecture sits above pure liberal positions, near or slightly denser than PS on traditional anti-racist enforcement, and clearly below LFI and EELV on identity-protective intensity. Closest functional analogue is a less institutionally ambitious version of centre-left baseline retention with stronger anti-fascist emphasis.

### Why this produces Endos 44 / Xenos N/A

Hate-speech framework retained, Gayssot retained, platform regulation retained, equality frameworks retained, traditional anti-racist and anti-fascist enforcement affirmed, but without LFI-level identity-speech densification on the Islam axis or dense orthodoxy drive, place Endos at 44. No split warranted.

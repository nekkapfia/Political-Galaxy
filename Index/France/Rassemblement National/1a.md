# Core 1A: France – Rassemblement National
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 58**  
**Xenos Score: 48**

### Overview

Rassemblement National produces a mid-range Core 1A profile for Endos and a lower score for a designated religious-practice out-group. The final set retains the French republican speech and security baseline (hate-speech and negationism statutes, anti-terror powers), intensifies laïcité-style controls on Islamic religious self-presentation in public and institutional settings, and does not adopt progressive mandatory attitude-correction densification. Immigration and foreign-national status rules are Core 2A and are not scored here. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies where religious dress and related presentation rules fall differentially on a stable practice group.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Retention of criminal hate-speech, anti-discrimination and Gayssot-type negationism provisions — not a repeal programme for the republican speech code.
* Intensified laïcité and public-order rules targeting Islamic religious self-presentation (veil, abaya and related restrictions in schools and expanded public or institutional contexts as programme priority).
* Defence and use of anti-Islamism and security powers that can constrain association and expression for security-designated Islamist targets.
* Ordinary direct-harm criminal law retained.
* Personal drug-possession criminalisation retained.
* Order and authority politics; no progressive institutional re-education or DEI-style mandatory training stack as a governing plank.
* No stated project to dismantle the core republican speech statutes in favour of open viewpoint-neutral maximal speech.
* Immigration, asylum and foreign-citizen status rules → Core 2A only — excluded from Core 1A.
* Party-system structure out of scope for Core 1A.

### Expanded Analysis

**Endos** (population not under distinctive religious-dress disability): RN keeps the republican statutory speech floor and security tools, adds no LFI/EELV-style mandatory orthodoxy training, and centres order plus intensified laïcité. Residual speech criminalisation and drug paternalism sit in the same neighbourhood as other non-progressive French parties (Renaissance, LR). Composite Endos aligns with **51–59 — Paternalism / self-betterment** and residual order, without progressive **19–27** densification. Score **58**.

**Xenos** (persons for whom targeted Islamic dress or presentation is religious or obligatory practice): expanded veil, abaya and related public/institutional bans restrict self-presentation on religious-identity criteria. Aligns with **44–50 — Early ideological/identity-based controls**: rules requiring conformity in self-presentation; observable identity-based control of external expression. Score **48**.

Rhetorical defence of speech critical of immigration or Islam does not remove dress and presentation restrictions from the final set for the affected group. Core 2A immigration enforcement is excluded from this score.

### Why this produces Endos 58 / Xenos 48

Endos aligns with **51–59** (republican baseline retained; no progressive reprogramming densification; order and laïcité politics without an Endos-facing dress dual structure) → **58**. Xenos aligns with **44–50** (intensified Islamic dress and self-presentation controls) → **48**.

# Core 1A: Fascism – Vaterländische Front (Austrofascism)
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 32**

### Overview

Vaterländische Front under Dollfuss and Schuschnigg (1933–1938) produces a restrictive Core 1A profile defined by suspension of parliamentary democracy, one-party Fatherland Front monopoly, bans on opposition parties, press controls, and detention of political opponents. Catholic clerical influence conditioned public and associational life. Biological racism was not a central organising principle; no Nuremberg-style dual structure for Jews. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies (political opponents as out-group).

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Suspension of parliament and dismantling of democratic constitutional protections for political speech and association after 1933.
* Banning of the Communist Party, the Social Democratic Party (after the February 1934 fighting), and later the Austrian National Socialists — independent political association prohibited.
* One-party Fatherland Front monopoly on legal political organisation.
* Significant press controls and censorship against opposition and critical voices; independent opposition media closed or constrained.
* Detention, administrative measures and political policing against Socialists, Communists and National Socialists.
* Confessional-Catholic conditioning of education and public associational life, constraining expression and organisation outside approved frameworks.
* No comprehensive biological-racial legal system comparable to the Nuremberg Laws; Jews not placed under a hereditary dual structure of the German type.
* Corporatist economic production structure is out of scope for Core 1A; only bans on independent political and labour association are scored.
* Ordinary criminal law continued for non-political private matters alongside political repression of banned organisations.

### Expanded Analysis

Austrofascism replaced parliamentary competition with a clerical-authoritarian one-party order. Independent political speech and association outside the Fatherland Front were prohibited or severely constrained. Press control closed critical political information. Detention was used against organised opponents after the suppression of the Social Democrats and the ban on National Socialists.

**Endos** (population accepting the Fatherland Front order): no legal opposition politics; press controlled; associational life channelled through official and Catholic structures. Residual private and religious life continued under confessional conditions but without the total mobilisation apparatus of National Socialism. This aligns with **28–36** — ideological enforcement and police state: dissenting political speech and organisation banned; access to critical political information curtailed. Score **32**.

**Xenos** (Socialists, Communists, National Socialists as banned opponents): party destruction, detention, administrative repression, and exclusion from legal political life. This aligns with **10–18** — detention and severe restriction of voluntary political action and association for the out-group. Score **18**.

The Xenos split is political, not racial-legal. Framing as Christian Ständestaat is discarded. Only observable restrictions on each group are scored.

### Why this produces Endos 32 / Xenos 18

Endos aligns with **28–36** (one-party monopoly, press control, ban on opposition association) → **32**. Xenos aligns with **10–18** (detention and repression of banned political opponents) → **18**.

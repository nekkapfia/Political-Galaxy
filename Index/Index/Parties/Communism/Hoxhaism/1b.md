## Regulation

### Overview
Hoxhaism realises one of the purest and most isolationist classical Stalinist command economies. Near-total state ownership, extreme central planning, and the near-elimination of residual private activity characterise the system after the 1976 Constitution.

### Relevant Policies
- Near-total state ownership of industry, agriculture, trade, banking and services
- Extreme central planning of production, investment and resource allocation
- Administrative direction of labour and suppression of independent economic organisation
- Constitutional prohibition on foreign credit and investment
- Minimal residual personal property; private enterprise reduced to the narrowest margins
- High forced accumulation and priority of producer-goods sectors

### Expanded Analysis
Under Hoxhaism the distinction between state and economy is almost entirely collapsed. Virtually all significant productive assets are state-owned; planning and administrative commands allocate resources and labour. The 1976 constitutional settlement further sealed the system against external capital and residual market activity. Consumption and labour are subordinated to the plan’s political and national objectives. Residual private claims are negligible.

The package exhibits extensive ownership, planning and labour direction with almost no meaningful private counterweight.

### Final Score
The decisive mechanical effects are near-total state ownership, extreme central planning, administrative labour direction, and constitutional isolation from residual market and foreign capital. This produces a Regulation Score of 21.

## Taxation

These proposals are scored via the separate Taxation Score method (not included in the Regulation Score above).

Hoxhaism is an extreme command economy. Formal tax instruments are secondary to direct appropriation of enterprise profits and amortization funds (historically >90 % of profits and ~70 % of amortization transferred to the budget). The standard tax-to-GDP formula is inappropriate.

**Alternative Method applied**  
Effective tithe rate: **72 %**  
Taxation Score = 100 − 72 = **28**

Justification rests on near-total state ownership, high enterprise-to-budget transfers, isolationist constitutional rules, and comparative ranking among the purest small-country Stalinist systems.

**Taxation Score: 28**

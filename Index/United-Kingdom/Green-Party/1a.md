# Core 1A: United Kingdom – Green Party of England and Wales
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 26**  
**Xenos Score: 21**

### Overview

The Green Party’s governing programme produces a restrictive Core 1A profile built from identity-based criminal speech controls, platform content duties, institutional orthodoxy rules, mandatory attitude-correction training, self-identification speech and association constraints, and professional sanctions for deviant views. The cumulative weight sits inside the 19–27 Ideological reprogramming / thought control band. Heightened intensity against speech critical of protected identity categories justifies the Endos/Xenos split.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Stirring-up hatred offences and related Public Order Act provisions targeting speech characterised as racist, Islamophobic, antisemitic, misogynistic or otherwise identity-hostile. Affirmative enforcement emphasis; no narrowing agenda.
* Online Safety Act retained and amended to impose platform duties against content described as falsehoods, fakes or half-truths. Not a repeal agenda; keep and potentially widen.
* Institutional rules (Equality Act and related frameworks) treating critical speech on gender, race and immigration as harmful and subject to restriction or sanction.
* Mandatory unconscious-bias / EDI / sensitivity training, implemented as compulsory in Green-influenced councils (notably Brighton & Hove). Purpose is correction of attitudes and beliefs (bias, privilege, correct views on identity); non-compliance carries institutional consequences.
* Self-identification rules that generate secondary constraints: once affirmed gender is treated as operative, speech asserting biological sex (misgendering) and association decisions around single-sex spaces become subject to hostile-environment or potential hate-incident framing.
* Employment and professional sanctions for views deemed incompatible with institutional roles, including manifesto language on police officers.
* Core non-violent hate-speech and communications offences retained.

### Expanded Analysis

The decisive feature is the simultaneous operation of multiple reinforcing layers. Criminal speech provisions and platform duties create a dense regulatory surface. Institutional orthodoxy rules and professional sanctions extend the same logic into employment and public roles. Mandatory attitude-correction training supplies the explicit 19–27 element: it is compulsory, targets internal attitudes rather than merely external conduct, has been implemented in Green-run institutions, and is linked to institutional consequences. Self-identification rules add further secondary constraints on speech and association.

Speech critical of specific protected identity categories (race, gender identity, Islam and related topics) attracts heightened criminal, institutional and professional risk relative to other political speech. This is a stable, discretely targetable intensity differential.

The architecture does not reach physical-coercion bands. Ordinary direct-harm offences remain but do not define placement; the decisive weight is the ideological-enforcement and attitude-correction layers.

### Why this produces Endos 26 / Xenos 21

Seven retained restrictions, including compulsory attitude-correction training whose purpose is belief change, place the core population at 26 inside the 19–27 band. Heightened application against identity-critical speech and persons produces Xenos 21.

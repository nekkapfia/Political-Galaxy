# Cultural 5A: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview
CDU/CSU emphasises Leitkultur, integration requirements and positive national-identity content while accepting much of the existing liberal settlement on status rules and European commitments.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Recent platforms emphasise Leitkultur, integration requirements and positive elements of German history and identity in education and citizenship policy.
* Heritage, customs and traditional community support receive rhetorical and some programmatic priority.
* Family and social policy rhetoric supports conventional structures while current positioning accepts much of the existing liberal framework on status rules.
* Education and cultural policy proposals include stronger national history and identity content alongside continued European and diversity commitments.
* Residual progressive and liberal settlement on many family-status and inclusive education frameworks remains largely in place; continuity pressure is selective.
* No systematic programme to dismantle progressive transmission mechanisms across curriculum design and family-status law.

### Expanded Analysis
CDU/CSU sits in contested low-mid territory. Leitkultur and positive national-identity content raise preservationist signal in education and citizenship language, and family rhetoric is more conventional than the left’s. Simultaneously, the party accepts much of the post-war liberal settlement on status rules and retains European and diversity commitments that limit full baseline restoration. The residual progressive/liberal settlement is not reversed as a project; it is managed with selective continuity emphasis. Domain hierarchy shows mixed primary domains: some continuity pressure in history and identity content, acceptance of liberal status frameworks, heritage secondary. That is more preservationist than SPD/Greens, less so than AfD’s systematic baseline orientation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48
Leitkultur and positive identity emphasis with acceptance of the liberal status settlement and without systematic reversal of progressive transmission mechanisms place CDU/CSU in the contested low-mid band (41–50). Mid-high placement would require traditional transmission to be the protected default across education and family-status law; the analysis shows selective continuity inside residual liberal frameworks.

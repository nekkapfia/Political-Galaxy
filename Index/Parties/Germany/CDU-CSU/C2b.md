# Cultural 2B: Germany – CDU/CSU
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 36**

### Overview
The CDU/CSU occupies a cautious middle position that largely accepts the post-1945 guilt framework while seeking limited room for national confidence. Traditional German national identity remains heavily qualified and secondary to historical responsibility. Residual baseline of predominantly critical national framing: traditional pride is limited or heavily qualified; critical and atonement narratives carry substantial institutional weight.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of Holocaust remembrance and the culture of historical responsibility as non-negotiable.
* Limited pride permitted in the post-1945 Federal Republic and reunification; longer German history treated with significant caution.
* Cultural figures usually presented in de-nationalised or carefully balanced ways.
* Strong expressions of traditional national identity routinely distanced from; the party has historically policed its own right flank.
* Acceptance and continuation of the post-1945 pattern of near-absence of new traditional national statues of longer German history or achievement.
* Residual baseline of predominantly critical national framing: traditional pride is limited or heavily qualified; critical and atonement narratives carry substantial institutional weight.

### Expanded Analysis
The CDU/CSU reinforces rather than challenges the dominant guilt orientation. Any affirmative language is confined almost exclusively to the democratic achievements of the Federal Republic and is routinely balanced by references to historical responsibility. The party does not seek to restore pride in pre-1945 German history or to reverse the de-nationalisation of cultural figures. Its institutional practice, when in government, has maintained the existing commemorative and educational architecture of atonement. The score therefore sits in the Shame / upper Negative range.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 36
Acceptance of the post-1945 guilt framework as baseline, combined with only limited and heavily qualified national confidence, places the CDU/CSU in the Shame band.

# Cultural 3: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 0 | 3B 36**

### Overview
The CDU/CSU maintains complete ethnic openness. No ancestral or blood criterion exists for German membership. The Exclusion Test is not met. The party supports Leitkultur as a guiding framework and defends language requirements, citizenship tests, and values commitments as conditions of naturalisation, while rejecting ethnic criteria. Immigration volume and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for Leitkultur as a framework for integration and membership.
* Defence of language requirements, citizenship tests, and values commitments for naturalisation.
* Opposition to overly rapid or low-threshold naturalisation.
* Explicit rejection of ethnic or blood criteria.
* Birth and descent routes culturally unrestricted.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is not met. 3A is 0. 3B is light-to-moderate because cultural expectations are defended and preferred but remain limited in depth and concentrated on the naturalisation route. This aligns with Light Demand (30–39).

# Cultural 5B: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Radicalism (Pace) Analysis
**Score: 34**

### Overview

Governing Volkspartei pace: reform and enforcement intensification (migration, debt brake, defence) without AfD-style rupture rhetoric against courts, EU procedure, and deliberative process as such. 2025 migration hardening is significant but sequenced inside coalition and EU negotiation constraints.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Migration enforcement intensification (Aufnahmestopp, safe third country) via legal and EU routes.
* Debt brake as constraint on rapid fiscal rupture.
* Defence spending increases as multi-year commitment.
* Preference for negotiated EU reform over unilateral exit timelines.
* Federal governing experience weights record over pure opposition urgency.

### Expanded Analysis

C5B measures speed. Union’s governing style is incremental-to-firm rather than permanent crisis override. Lower radicalism than AfD’s compressed-timeline rhetoric; higher than pure status-quo parties on migration enforcement.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 34

Enforcement ambition inside deliberative and treaty process → **34**.

# Core 1B: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Regulation Score: 42**
**Taxation Score: 58**
**Final Score: 46**

### Overview

Union combines moderate income and corporate tax relief with retention of the social-market and green-transition regulatory core, constrained by the debt brake. Taxation Score sits near Germany’s OECD tax/GDP baseline (~37–38%, implied Taxation ≈ 62) with modest downward package adjustment. Regulation remains denser than AfD/FDP deregulatory packages because Net Zero and planning instruments are reformed rather than abolished.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Lower income tax; gradually lower corporate tax toward ~25% (programme direction).
* No pension cuts; encourage work beyond 67 with tax-free earnings up to €2,000/month on top of pension in programme language.
* Debt brake (Schuldenbremse) remains a financing constraint.
* Green transition reformed not abolished; planning/bureaucracy simplification rhetoric without AfD-scale mandate repeal.
* Germany OECD tax-to-GDP working baseline ~37–38%; GDP ~€4.3–4.5tn.
* Package scale smaller than AfD/FDP comparative campaign estimates → Taxation closer to baseline.

### Expanded Analysis

**Taxation:** Formula `(1 − tax/GDP) × 100`. Baseline ~62. Union’s package is real but moderate; **58** reflects limited relief versus AfD’s larger Soli/inheritance/CO₂ cut stack. **Regulation:** Social-market labour, planning, and residual green industrial rules keep Regulation in the low-40s under the 100-year test. Final **46** weights both components.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 46

Taxation **58** (near-baseline package). Regulation **42** (retained green/social-market density). Combined Final **46**.

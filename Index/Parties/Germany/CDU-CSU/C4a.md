# Cultural 4A: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 24**

### Overview
The CDU/CSU supports the existing early-tracking system and the dual vocational model, while advocating quality improvements and targeted support for disadvantaged students. Formal access remains open; the party maintains the core structural features that link birth circumstances to pathway access while seeking incremental improvement.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of the Gymnasium and early selection as mechanisms for promoting excellence.
* Support for expanding and strengthening the dual vocational system.
* Emphasis on targeted rather than structural measures to address socio-economic gaps.
* Acceptance of residual quality gradients within the existing tracking framework.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules beyond the existing system.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. Germany’s early-tracking system already produces stronger birth-linked pathway differences than pure comprehensive systems. The CDU/CSU’s defence of that structure, combined with targeted rather than structural gap-closing, accepts residual quality and placement differences. Formal access remains universal; practical ease continues to correlate with parental education and early performance. This places the score in the mid 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 24
Universal schooling plus defence of early tracking and dual system, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the mid 21–30 band. Score 24.

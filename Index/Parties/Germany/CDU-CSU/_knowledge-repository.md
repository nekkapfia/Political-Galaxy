## 2. Core 1A — Personal Autonomy

**Scores recorded in current Index files:** Score: 54

### Claims / mechanisms already present in scored documents

- Retention of criminal Volksverhetzung and related hate-speech / incitement provisions — defined categories of speech remain penalised; no repeal programme stated.

## 9. Cultural 2B — Pride / National Identity

**Scores recorded in current Index files:** Score: 36

### Claims / mechanisms already present in scored documents

- The party defends Holocaust remembrance and the culture of historical responsibility as non-negotiable.
- It allows limited pride in the post-1945 Federal Republic and reunification, but treats longer German history with significant caution.

- “But” structures in pride language; de-nationalisation vs affirmation.

## X. 2025 programme and institutional specifics (research expansion)

### Migration / borders (Core 2A)
- 2025 Wahlprogramm: “faktischer Aufnahmestopp”; border controls with rejections; anyone entering from EU/Schengen state to claim asylum is not threatened and is to be turned back until EU external border secured.
- Suspend family reunification for subsidiarily protected; end all voluntary admission programmes.
- Push at EU level to abolish subsidiary protection status; implement safe-third-country concept so asylum applicants transferred out of Europe for procedures.
- Federal departure centres; deportations including to Afghanistan and Syria especially for offenders and Gefährder; unrestricted Ausweisungsarrest for offenders/threat persons in some formulations.
- In-kind benefits priority over cash; Bezahlkarte nationwide and restrictive.
- Reverse Ampel express naturalisation; reverse general dual citizenship expansion; naturalisation at end of successful integration; Leitkultur key to integration; German language required; stop foreign-government influence via mosque associations; optimise “Leben in Deutschland” course; require commitment to Basic Law and equality of men and women; some CSU language on recognition of Israel’s right to exist as naturalisation condition.

### Security / surveillance (Core 1A adjacent)
- Expand video surveillance; automated facial recognition at danger points; ISP retention of IP addresses; reverse cannabis legalisation.

### Economy / tax (Core 1B)
- Lower income tax; gradually lower corporate tax toward 25%; no pension cuts; encourage work beyond 67 with tax-free earnings up to €2,000/month on top of pension.
- Debt brake (Schuldenbremse) remains political constraint on package financing.

### Foreign policy (Core 2B)
- Support Ukraine with diplomatic, financial, humanitarian means and arms deliveries; more responsibility in NATO; spend above 2% GDP on defence; welcome stationing of weapons systems in Germany.
- Coordinated European China policy; reduce economic dependence while cooperating on global issues.

### Culture / identity (C2B, C3, C5A)
- Explicit Leitkultur language: grown rules and norms must be recognised by all who want to live here; without German language integration fails.
- Mandatory pre-school language tests.
- Traditional centre-right continuity with post-2015 corrective hardening on migration.

### Research still useful
- Merz January 2025 Bundestag motion details and vote lineup (AfD support controversy).
- Exact corporate/income tax schedule proposals for Taxation Score.
- CSU vs CDU divergences on Leitkultur and Islam policy if scored jointly.

### CDU/CSU package notes (for Taxation Score overlay)
- Programme / post-election coalition direction: income-tax relief for small/middle incomes; corporate tax burden on retained profits toward max **25%**; full abolition of Solidaritätszuschlag in Union programme language (coalition 2025 later kept Soli in some reports — flag inconsistency).
- Corporate income tax reduction path discussed in coalition implementation: stepwise toward lower KSt (investment booster 30% degressive depreciation 2025–27; KSt cuts from 2028 in five 1-pp steps in some enacted packages).
- DIW-style orientative costing of Union campaign package: order **~€110 bn/year** relief class.
- Direction: material cut → Taxation Score **above** baseline 62, less aggressive than FDP/AfD campaign packages in comparative estimates.

# Cultural 5A: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 44**

### Overview

Christian-democratic traditionalism on family and cultural continuity coexists with market modernisation and coalition-era progressive accommodations. Direction is moderately traditional relative to Greens, less rupture-traditional than AfD’s mother–father–children and anti-replacement programme.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Christian-democratic family and social policy heritage.
* Leitkultur and language continuity in integration policy.
* Market and administrative modernisation without cultural-replacement programme.
* Coalition compromises with progressive partners historically pull institutional direction toward the centre.

### Expanded Analysis

C5A is direction, not pace. Union preserves more traditional institutional assumptions than progressive parties while accepting substantial post-1960s settlement. Mid-band.

Moderate traditional direction within catch-all governing practice → **44**.

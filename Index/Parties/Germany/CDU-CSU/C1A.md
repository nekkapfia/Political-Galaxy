### Overview
CDU/CSU organises moral legitimacy around competence, order, institutional continuity and electoral mandate, with Christian-democratic tradition as a named recurring framework for governance advanced through democratic means.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Competence, order, economic stability and institutional continuity are presented as primary duties of government.
* The security and prosperity of citizens are cited as central justificatory grounds.
* European cooperation and international responsibility are framed as pragmatic and values-based.
* Electoral mandate and delivery capacity are treated as core sources of legitimacy.

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Christian-democratic tradition is a named and recurring framework for balanced governance and party self-understanding, advanced through democratic and institutional means without clear higher-law override force.

### Expanded Analysis
Competence, order, institutional continuity, security of citizens and electoral mandate organise the primary Mundane layer. Christian-democratic tradition is a named, recurring framework — more than occasional colour — and forms a secondary component of the party’s moral self-description. Under residual rules this is **moderate secondary** transcendent weight. Both sources are present. Operational primacy is Mundane.

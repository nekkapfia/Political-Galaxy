# Core 2B: Germany – CDU/CSU
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 36**

### Overview
CDU/CSU as the long-dominant centre-right force has supported NATO, EU sanctions leadership, military assistance in key crises, and values-based external policy. Residual baseline is Atlanticist and European interventionist capacity rather than non-interference.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Consistent support for NATO collective defence and expeditionary contributions under alliance frameworks.
* Active role in EU sanctions design and maintenance in major crises (including Russia).
* Support for military assistance to partners under attack (Ukraine and earlier cases) within multilateral frames.
* Development and values-based diplomacy that includes governance conditionality.
* Residual baseline of Atlanticist and EU-centred external pressure instruments; no programme-level non-intervention posture.
* Self-defence of German territory and collective defence obligations are out of scope or heavily mitigated.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. CDU/CSU foreign policy has long treated sanctions, alliance military assistance and normative conditionality as legitimate tools. Invitation mitigation applies to Ukraine support; the residual posture remains clearly interventionist relative to non-interventionist platforms.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 36
Sustained support for NATO, EU sanctions leadership, military assistance and residual values-based pressure tools place CDU/CSU in the mid-to-high 30s.

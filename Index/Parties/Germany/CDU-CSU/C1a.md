# Cultural 1A: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 27**

### Overview
The CDU/CSU present themselves as the party of order, controlled migration, economic competence and defence of the constitutional and social-market order. Core aims are responsible government that maintains stability while tightening control over immigration. Organising sources are clearly mundane: citizen interest, institutional stewardship and competence.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Immigration and security justified by the need to protect the concrete interests and safety of German citizens and to preserve social cohesion.
* Institutional continuity, the Basic Law and governing competence presented as practical necessities.
* Residual Christian-democratic and social-market language remains, but is secondary to order and competence.
* Elite and European constraints criticised when seen to undermine national control capacity.
* Electoral mandate and delivery as central legitimating sources.
* No organising claim that Christian or natural-law duty may override clear democratic preference on core governing questions.

### Expanded Analysis
The CDU/CSU organise justification through citizen interest, order and institutional pragmatism. Christian-democratic and social-market references supply historical colour and residual moral vocabulary but do not elevate a transcendent duty above democratic and practical governing requirements. Immigration and security trade-offs are resolved by reference to living citizens’ safety and cohesion. The pattern is clear mundane lean, consistent with other centre-right order parties in the set.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 27
Mundane sources (citizen interest, order, competence, institutional pragmatism) are primary. Transcendent sources are limited. The score is 27.

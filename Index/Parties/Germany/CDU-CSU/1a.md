# Core 1A: Germany – CDU/CSU
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Score: 54**

### Overview

CDU/CSU as Germany’s mainstream centre-right bloc produces a mid-range Core 1A profile defined by retention of the Federal Republic’s speech and order baseline: criminal Volksverhetzung and related hate-speech provisions, residual public-order controls, ordinary direct-harm and drug-possession law, and security powers they generally defend. They do not run a Die Grünen-style dense progressive attitude-correction programme as governing core, nor an AfD-style identity dual structure as programme centrepiece. No party-level Endos/Xenos split is applied. Placement sits in **51–59** with residual weight from speech statutes left in place.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Retention of criminal Volksverhetzung and related hate-speech / incitement provisions — defined categories of speech remain penalised; no repeal programme stated.
* Defence of constitutional-order tools against designated extremist association where already in German law; not a liberalisation of political-association bans for banned organisations.
* Ordinary direct-harm criminal law retained.
* Personal drug-possession criminalisation largely retained (not a full liberalisation programme).
* Order and security politics without progressive mandatory re-education densification as a national CDU/CSU plank.
* No stated nationwide religious-dress dual structure equivalent to Restore Britain or RN signature bans as the defining Core 1A addition.
* Immigration and foreign-national status rules → Core 2A — not scored here.
* Federal party-system and constitutional structure out of scope for Core 1A.
* CSU social-conservative emphasis does not by itself create a separate scored Xenos legal dual structure at federal programme level.

### Expanded Analysis

CDU/CSU administer the existing German restriction set rather than densifying progressive orthodoxy or building a new religious-dress out-group regime as their Core 1A offer. Endos under a CDU/CSU-led order face statutory speech limits (Volksverhetzung), ordinary order law and drug paternalism. That package aligns primarily with **51–59 — Paternalism / self-betterment** for residual lifestyle and order controls, while retained hate-speech criminalisation remains capable of **28–36** alignment where enforced. Composite Endos in the mid-50s reflects mainstream baseline administration without Green-style training densification and without AfD-maximal identity programmes as the scored centrepiece.

Speech-statute repeal is not their programme; progressive reprogramming is not their programme. Final set is the federal status quo they keep. No clear, stable party-programmed dual structure beyond general statute → **no Endos/Xenos split** at party level. Scoring is against band text only, not relative softness within the German right.

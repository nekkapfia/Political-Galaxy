## 2. Core 1A — Personal Autonomy

**Scores recorded in current Index files:** Score: 56

### Claims / mechanisms already present in scored documents

- Retention of core criminal law against violence and true threats; no programme to abolish ordinary direct-harm offences.
- Volksverhetzung and related hate-speech statutes — AfD criticises selective enforcement and “opinion crime” practice but does not present a completed repeal-and-replace of the entire speech code as a single locked final set; residual criminal speech law remains a live baseline risk.
- Explicit opposition to progressive DEI / attitude-correction institutional programmes — those densification layers are **out** of an AfD final set.
- Stricter laïcité-analogue / public-order rules on Islamic religious self-presentation (headscarf, burqa/niqab-type restrictions in schools and public employment / selected public contexts) as programme priority → **44–50** for affected practice group.
- Personal drug-possession criminalisation retained unless explicitly liberalised (not a core liberalisation brand).
- Order and security politics; association controls aimed at designated Islamist organisations where already framed as security/constitutional-order measures.
- Immigration, asylum and foreign-citizen status → Core 2A only.
- Party-system structure out of scope for Core 1A.
- Endos** (population not under distinctive religious-dress disability): AfD removes or refuses progressive mandatory orthodoxy training, keeps ordinary order and drug paternalism, and operates inside a Germany that still has Volksverhetzung on the books. Without treating AfD as a full speech-code ...
- Xenos** (persons for whom targeted Islamic dress/presentation is religious or obligatory): school and public-presentation bans restrict self-presentation on religious-identity criteria. Aligns with **44–50 — Early ideological/identity-based controls**. Score **48**.

### Enrichment targets / research gaps for this axis

- Manifesto / programme text on speech, protest, hate-speech, data, drug, and lifestyle restrictions.
- Explicit repeal vs retain positions relative to current Strafgesetzbuch / Versammlungsrecht baseline.
- Any Endos/Xenos split justification (religious dress, designated groups).

## X. 2025 programme and institutional specifics (research expansion)

### Migration / borders (Core 2A primary)
- 2025 Wahlprogramm (Riesa party congress, Jan 2025): focus on migration; non-participation in EU asylum system; rejections and detention of asylum seekers at borders; abolition of all Bleiberechtsregelungen for persons obliged to leave; “Remigration” language for foreigners without German passport who break laws or abuse welfare (citizens not targeted in stated formulation).
- Asylum procedures to be processed outside Germany; comprehensive Rückführungsoffensive; convert cash benefits for asylum seekers to in-kind; reduce benefits for Ausreisepflichtige to existential minimum.
- Activate voluntary and mandatory deportation programmes including return of Syrian nationals; deny Ukrainian refugees welfare benefits in some formulations.
- Police monitoring of borders; refugees held/processed outside Germany.

### EU / Euro / sovereignty (Core 2A)
- Exit from EU (“Dexit”) and founding of a new states’ confederation / “Europa der Vaterländer”; rejection of United States of Europe and irreversible federal EU.
- Exit from Euro system; reintroduction of national currency (possibly parallel to Euro); if Bundestag refuses, Volksabstimmung on Euro membership.
- Reform or replace EU with limited Wirtschafts- und Interessengemeinschaft focused on common market.
- Opposition to EU/NATO eastern enlargement in programme language.

### Foreign policy (Core 2B)
- Lift all economic sanctions on Russia immediately; resume gas deliveries / restore Nord Stream.
- Interest-guided foreign policy toward USA, China, Russia without hierarchical ranking.
- NATO membership retained “until establishment of independent capable European military alliance”; NATO as defence alliance only; opposition to out-of-area missions without UN mandate and German security interest.
- Withdraw remaining allied troops and nuclear weapons from German soil.
- Oppose large-scale material support to Ukraine from Bundeswehr stocks; criticise depletion of German readiness.
- Exit UN Migration Pact and UN Refugee Pact; demand permanent German UN Security Council seat.

### Climate / energy / economy (Core 1B Regulation)
- Reject anthropogenic climate policy as programme stance; withdraw from Paris Agreement; end subsidies for renewables; restrict wind/solar expansion.
- Restart nuclear power and research; retain coal and fossil generation; abolish CO2 levy.
- Abolish Solidaritätszuschlag and inheritance tax in tax package language; Bürgergeld recipients beyond six months obliged to community work.
- Bureaucracy reduction and planning simplification as regulation-side indicators.

### Culture / family / identity (C2B, C5A, C3)
- Family defined as mother, father, children in sharpened programme language.
- Traditional education models; national self-preservation framing.
- No ethnic blood bar stated for membership (3A remains 0); cultural-political expectations high for substantive belonging (Leitkultur-adjacent nationalism).

### Speech / order (Core 1A)
- Retain criminal Volksverhetzung baseline in practice (not full speech-code repeal party); emphasis on order and deportation over speech liberalisation in dominant messaging.
- Lower age of criminal responsibility proposals (e.g. from 14 toward 12) appear in programme discussion — route carefully (paternalism / order vs pure 1A).

### Research still useful
- Full 2025 programme PDF page cites for tax € magnitudes (Taxation Score).
- Exact wording on dual citizenship / Einbürgerung.
- Land-level AfD education and police motions as revealed preference.

## Slider-specific data — Core 1B (Taxation Score & GDP)

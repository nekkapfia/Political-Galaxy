# Cultural 5B: Germany – AfD
## Radicalism (Pace) Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview

AfD’s pace profile is high-urgency on migration enforcement, energy reversal, and institutional constraints. Rhetoric frames extended parliamentary process, judicial oversight, and EU mechanisms as obstacles to necessary immediate action. Limited federal governing record means patterns are drawn from manifesto intensity, congress decisions (e.g. Riesa Remigration insertion), and Land practice rather than multi-year federal implementation.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Immediate and total shifts demanded on border control and returns (Remigration, offshore asylum, border detention).
* Rapid reversal of energy transition policies; nuclear restart; end renewable subsidy path.
* Rhetoric treating deliberation, courts, and EU procedures as delay mechanisms.
* Compressed timelines and executive/popular override themes in communications.
* Crisis framing of demographic and security trajectories as requiring non-gradual response.
* Federal governing record thin — weight manifesto and opposition intensity with that caveat.

### Expanded Analysis

C5B measures speed and intensity, not tradition/progress direction (C5A). AfD couples traditionalist direction with radical pace claims on borders and energy. Absence of federal cabinet delivery caps the score below a pure “permanent revolution in government” band but keeps it high among opposition parties that demand rupture rather than managed reform.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72

Urgency language, fast-track preference, and rupture framing on core domains outweigh deliberative process commitments; thin governing record is noted but does not collapse the score. **72**.

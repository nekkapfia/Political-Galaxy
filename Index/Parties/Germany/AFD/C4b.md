# Cultural 4B: Germany – AfD
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 18**

### Overview

AfD rejects systemic equity as the primary explanation of group gaps. Migrant outcome gaps are attributed to cultural incompatibility, insufficient individual integration effort, and policy failure rather than structural oppression. The party opposes gender quotas, self-ID laws, and diversity mandates as allocation tools and favours colour-blind merit and traditional integration models.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Platforms attribute group gaps primarily to culture, individual effort, and policy design — not systemic oppression.
* Oppose gender quotas, diversity mandates, and self-ID equity infrastructure.
* Favour colour-blind merit and traditional integration requirements.
* Critique institutional equity initiatives; integration and enforcement preferred to outcome equalisation.
* Anti-discrimination formal equality is not treated as a mandate for equity densification.

### Expanded Analysis

C4B rises with systemic attribution and hard corrective equity mechanisms. AfD minimises both. Residual general anti-discrimination law in the German baseline does not, under guide rules, equal equity-lens densification. Low-band score.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 18

Absence of systemic equity diagnosis and rejection of quota/outcome machinery lock a low Equity Lens score. **18**.

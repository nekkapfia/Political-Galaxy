# Cultural 5A: Germany – AfD
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview

AfD is traditionalist on family definition (mother, father, children in sharpened 2025 language), education content, Leitkultur, and national cultural continuity. Progressive institutional capture in education, media, and culture is a central target. Market deregulation and energy reversal do not flip the cultural direction score: C5A tracks institutional-cultural direction, not economic modernisation per se.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Family defined as mother, father, children (2025 programme sharpening).
* Patriotic cultural policy supporting customs, traditions, local culture, and positive historical memory.
* Explicit rejection of “national masochism” and elite guilt narratives.
* Leitkultur, German as state language, cultural continuity against multicultural replacement.
* Traditional education models; critique of progressive institutional capture in schools and culture.
* Demographic continuity framing in family and social policy.

### Expanded Analysis

Direction is preservation and restoration of traditional family and national-cultural institutions against post-1960s and post-2015 progressive replacement. That is high traditionalism among major parties. Incomplete governing power means the score is programme- and opposition-revealed rather than fully enacted federal institutional settlement.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72

Family definition, Leitkultur, and anti-replacement education/culture mechanisms establish clear traditional direction. **72**.

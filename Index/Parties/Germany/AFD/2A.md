# Core 2A: Germany – AfD
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 49**

### Overview

AfD’s national-sovereignty profile is enforcement-first on migration and long-horizon on EU/Euro exit. The 2025 Riesa programme inserts **Remigration**, non-participation in the EU asylum system, border rejection/detention, offshore asylum processing, and a comprehensive Rückführungsoffensive. Dexit and Euro exit remain programme aims but are constrained by **one-term realism**: treaty exit is not a four-year deliverable; achievable border and returns enforcement drive the score more than rhetorical confederation designs. Germany’s rule-shaper status on many EU files still applies as context, not as a free pass for residual EU constraints AfD would dismantle.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* 2025 Wahlprogramm (Riesa): Remigration for non-citizens who break laws, abuse welfare, or are framed as non-integrable — German passport holders not targeted in stated formulation.
* Non-participation in EU asylum system; asylum procedures outside Germany; border rejection and detention of asylum seekers.
* Abolition of Bleiberechtsregelungen for persons obliged to leave; in-kind benefits for asylum seekers; reduced benefits for Ausreisepflichtige.
* Comprehensive deportation/return offensive including return of Syrian nationals in programme language.
* Dexit / “Europa der Vaterländer”; rejection of irreversible EU federalisation; Euro exit and possible national currency (or parallel currency); Volksabstimmung path if Bundestag refuses.
* Reduce EU competences; limited Wirtschafts- und Interessengemeinschaft alternative in programme language.
* Family reunification restriction for subsidiary protection in programme comparisons.
* One-term realism: score enforcement and negotiation posture over completed treaty exit.

### Expanded Analysis

Primary drag is migration enforcement capacity under residual EU and German law. AfD maximises unilateral border and returns tools relative to mainstream parties and rejects Migration Pact-style solidarity. Remigration language is constitutionally framed in programme materials as applying to non-citizens under existing or amendable law — still a sharp sovereignty claim on membership of the resident non-citizen population. EU/Euro exit aims are real but discounted under one-term realism and rule-shaper context: the party is not scored as if Germany had already left the treaties. Net position sits in the high-40s: hard enforcement nationalism inside remaining treaty constraints, not completed Westphalian restoration.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 49

Enforcement and non-participation aims pull up; residual EU/Euro membership under one-term realism and rule-shaper discount prevent a higher band. Weighted mechanisms place AfD at **49**.

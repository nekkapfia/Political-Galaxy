# Cultural 1A: Germany – AfD
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 12**

### Overview

AfD’s justificatory core is mundane-national: the right of the existing demos to control borders, the concrete costs and security risks of policy for living citizens, and cultural continuity as inheritance of the present population rather than sacred or ecological transcendence. Elite, judicial, media, and European constraints are framed as overrides of democratic will. Ecological higher-order duties and class-internationalist theology are rejected as organising sources.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Immigration and membership policy justified by demos self-preservation and citizen cost/security, not planetary or class transcendence.
* National interest of living Germans and reciprocity as organising principles.
* Critique of elite/judicial/EU overrides of democratic preference.
* Cultural continuity defended as cohesion of the existing population.
* Softening of some terms in internal adjustments while retaining demographic and cultural self-preservation substance.

### Expanded Analysis

Foundation scores low on transcendent binding sources. AfD does not treat 1.5°C pathways, class abolition, or revealed religion as overrides of preference aggregation. The nation-as-living-people frame is particularist but remains within mundane political justification rather than sacred ecology or revolutionary class theory. That places the party at the low end of the Foundation scale among major German parties.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 12

Mundane national-interest and demos-preference justification without ecological or class-transcendent overrides locks a low Foundation score. **12**.

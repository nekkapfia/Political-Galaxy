# Core 1B: Germany – AfD
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Regulation Score: 53**
**Taxation Score: 68**
**Final Score: 60**

### Overview

AfD combines a large tax-cut package (Solidaritätszuschlag and inheritance-tax abolition, CO₂/green levy removal, income and corporate relief, energy-tax reduction) with a Regulation agenda that reverses Net Zero industrial density, restarts nuclear, retains fossil capacity, and pushes bureaucracy/planning simplification. Taxation is scored from the formula against Germany’s OECD-style tax/GDP baseline (~37–38%, baseline Taxation ≈ 62) with upward adjustment for package scale. Regulation reflects 100-year non-tax rule density after green-mandate reversal.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Abolish Solidaritätszuschlag; abolish Erbschaftsteuer (programme language).
* Abolish CO₂ levy / green levies; end renewable subsidies; restrict wind/solar expansion; withdraw from Paris Agreement as programme stance.
* Income-tax relief; corporate tax toward internationally competitive level; energy/electricity tax reduction.
* Orientative independent costings of campaign-scale relief in the high tens to ~€180bn/year class — treat as upper-bound estimates pending official programme € tables by tax type.
* Restart nuclear power and research; retain coal and fossil generation.
* Major bureaucracy and planning simplification.
* Bürgergeld: community-work obligation beyond six months (regulation/welfare conditionality).
* Germany OECD tax-to-GDP working baseline ~37–38% (2023–24p); GDP working range ~€4.3–4.5tn.

### Expanded Analysis

**Taxation:** Formula is `(1 − tax/GDP) × 100`. Baseline at ~38% tax/GDP implies ~62. AfD’s package is directionally a large cut in tax take (Soli, inheritance, carbon, income, corporate, energy). That raises the Taxation Score above baseline; **68** reflects substantial relief without treating the highest campaign costings as locked official inputs. **Regulation:** Green industrial and planning mandates are primary non-tax density under the 100-year test. Programme reversal of Net Zero strictures, nuclear restart, and bureaucracy cuts raise Regulation relative to green-mandate parties, while retained labour/welfare conditionality and incomplete classical deregulation keep Regulation off the top of the band. **Final 60** averages the two components in line with prior AfD 1B practice.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 60

Taxation Score **68** from formula overlay on ~38% baseline plus large cut package. Regulation Score **53** from green-mandate reversal and planning simplification under the 100-year test, tempered by residual social-market density. Combined Final **60**.

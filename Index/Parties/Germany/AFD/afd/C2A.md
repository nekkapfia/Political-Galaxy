# Cultural 2A: Germany – AfD
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 74**

### Overview

AfD is the strongest major-party opponent of group allocation, gender quotas, equity commissioners, and SBGG-style self-ID expansion. Formal demand to repeal the Selbstbestimmungsgesetz (in force since November 2024) and preference for biological sex as the category for single-sex provision sit alongside colour-blind merit rhetoric. Individual and national-membership criteria are preferred over identity quotas.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Formal demand to repeal the Selbstbestimmungsgesetz (self-ID law).
* Long-standing rejection of gender quotas and equity commissioners.
* Opposition to positive action and diversity hiring targets.
* Emphasis on biological sex for single-sex spaces and legal status.
* Preference for formal individual equality over group-equity allocation.

### Expanded Analysis

C2A ranks group versus individual allocation. AfD’s residual programme removes progressive identity-allocation instruments and seeks repeal of self-ID infrastructure. That produces a high individuality score relative to Greens/Linke/SPD. Residual general equality law and incomplete classical-liberal purity keep the score below a theoretical maximum.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 74

SBGG repeal demand and quota rejection are specific allocation mechanisms weighted heavily; residual equality-law baseline prevents a ceiling score. **74**.

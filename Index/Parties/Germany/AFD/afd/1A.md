# Core 1A: Germany – AfD
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Score: 56**

### Overview

AfD’s residual personal-autonomy set is defined less by classical speech-code repeal than by refusal of progressive densification and by order-security tools. Mandatory DEI / attitude-correction layers are out of the final set. Ordinary criminal law against violence and true threats is retained. Volksverhetzung (§130 StGB) remains a live baseline: factional pressure (including 2025 congress signals) to restrict or abolish “opinion crime” provisions has not locked a completed national repeal-and-replace of the speech code. Programme emphasis prioritises border and deportation over speech liberalisation. Public-order rules on Islamic religious dress in schools and selected public employment, association measures framed against designated Islamist organisations, and retention of drug-possession criminalisation densify the residual set relative to residual-liberal parties. Surveillance posture is mixed-to-sceptical (opposition to blanket Vorratsdatenspeicherung and comprehensive public video as symptom control).

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* No programme to abolish ordinary direct-harm criminal offences; core violence and true-threat law retained.
* Volksverhetzung and related speech offences remain on the residual statute book; AfD criticises selective enforcement and “opinion crime” practice; 2025 Höcke/congress pressure to abolish or narrowly confine §130 is a high-salience signal, not yet a locked single-line final set.
* Explicit opposition to progressive mandatory DEI / anti-bias / attitude-correction institutional programmes — those layers are **out** of the AfD residual set.
* Programme priority for stricter public-order rules on Islamic religious self-presentation (headscarf / burqa-type restrictions in schools and selected public employment).
* Personal drug-possession criminalisation retained (not a liberalisation brand).
* Association and security-order tools aimed at designated Islamist organisations; programme language also targets “linksradikale Antifa” classification and certain demonstration bans framed as security.
* Opposition to comprehensive public video surveillance as primary tool and to Vorratsdatenspeicherung / online searches in comparative security messaging.
* Defence of cash as legal tender; opposition to programmable digital euro as financial surveillance (secondary 1A-relevant).
* Lower Strafmündigkeit proposals (toward 12) appear in programme discussion — order/paternalism adjacent; not the primary 1A driver.
* Immigration status of foreign nationals is routed to Core 2A, not scored as 1A personal autonomy of citizens.

### Expanded Analysis

The decisive 1A feature of AfD relative to the progressive cluster is the **absence** of mandatory attitude-correction densification. Under Core 1A hard rules, that removes the 19–27 band lock that binds parties whose residual set includes belief-targeting training. Residual German speech law, however, is not cleared: without a completed repeal of Volksverhetzung-class offences, the baseline criminal speech risk remains. Order tools — religious-dress public rules, association controls, retained drug paternalism — pull the residual set below a classical-liberal ceiling. 2025 programme messaging still centres Remigration and borders more than speech-code reconstruction, so the party should not be scored as if it had already enacted a full liberal speech regime. Surveillance scepticism on data retention and blanket cameras offsets some security densification elsewhere. Net residual invasiveness sits in the mid-band: freer of progressive orthodoxy machinery than Greens/SPD/Linke, denser on order and identity-presentation rules than FDP-style residual liberalism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 56

Mandatory attitude-correction is out (avoids 19–27 lowest band). Residual Volksverhetzung baseline plus order-security and religious-dress controls prevent a high classical-liberal score. Mechanisms weighted by residual-law impact under negative-only rules place AfD at **56**.

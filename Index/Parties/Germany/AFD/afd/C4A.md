# Cultural 4A: Germany – AfD
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 34**

### Overview

AfD defends differentiated schooling by ability, Gymnasium as an elite path, Förderschule as default for special needs, and Bildungspflicht framing (including parental/home education directions in Land debates). Universal practical school access remains; private and selective options are **quality/selection gradients**, not hard access barriers. Score sits in the quality-gradient band.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Support for maintaining or strengthening early tracking and academic selection (Gymnasium pathway).
* Förderschule as default again for pupils with special educational needs.
* Opposition to ideologically driven standards reduction or preferential treatment by migration background in school placement.
* Traditional vocational and academic pathways based on performance.
* Bildungspflicht / loosening of strict Schulpflicht in some Land programme language (parental instruction option).
* Mandatory German competence before regular class participation for migrant pupils (programme comparisons).

### Expanded Analysis

Under C4A hard rules, universal access plus Gymnasium/private/Förderschule differentiation is scored as selection quality, typically ~20s–30s, not as 50+ hard barriers. AfD is more selectionist than comprehensive-equalisation parties, hence above the lowest quality-gradient scores, without denying practical school access.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 34

Access remains universal in practice; tracking and Förderschule defaults are quality/selection mechanisms under guide bands. **34**.

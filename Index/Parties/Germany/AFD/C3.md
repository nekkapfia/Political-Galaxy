# Cultural 3: Germany – AfD
## Endos Prerequisites Analysis
**Ethnic / descent (3A): 0**
**Cultural / integration (3B): 60**

### Overview

AfD does not propose a permanent ethnic or blood bar to full membership. Exclusion Test for 3A is therefore not met → **3A = 0**. Variation sits in **3B**: significantly stricter integration and naturalisation expectations, Leitkultur commitment, opposition to 2024 citizenship liberalisation, and cultural conditions for substantive belonging. Remigration and deportation volume remain Core **2A**, not C3.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* No programme permanent ethnic/descent-only bar to citizenship or membership (3A Exclusion Test not met).
* Stricter integration requirements; deeper commitment to German Leitkultur and values for full belonging.
* Oppose 2024 liberalisation of citizenship law; reverse many of its provisions.
* Naturalisation more difficult and contingent on successful cultural integration.
* Explicit rejection of ethnic criteria while emphasising cultural ones.
* Dual citizenship and Einbürgerung tightening in programme direction (cultural-political, not ethnic-genetic).

### Expanded Analysis

C3 asks who may become a full endos member. AfD answers with culture and integration intensity, not ancestry tests. That is the standard modern-party pattern on 3A (zero) with elevated 3B relative to progressive parties that minimise cultural tests. Immigration enforcement tools stay on 2A.

### Why this produces a Final Score of the headed dual scores

Exclusion Test fails → **3A = 0**. High cultural and naturalisation density → **3B = 60**.

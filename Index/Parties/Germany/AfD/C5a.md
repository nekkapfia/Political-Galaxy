# Cultural 5A: Germany – AfD
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview
The AfD demands patriotic cultural policy, positive German history teaching and Leitkultur while calling for reversal of progressive capture in education, media and cultural institutions.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Platform demands patriotic cultural policy, positive teaching of German history in schools, and support for customs and local culture in public institutions.
* Explicit rejection of elite guilt narratives in education and culture in favour of national self-confidence as a stated policy goal.
* Leitkultur, German as state language, and cultural continuity requirements advanced in integration and citizenship policy.
* Family and social policy positions support conventional family structures and demographic continuity measures.
* Calls for reversal of progressive institutional capture in education curricula, media regulation and cultural funding.
* Residual progressive settlement on curriculum framing, gender-related education content and multicultural institutional norms is targeted for rollback, not accepted as permanent default.

### Expanded Analysis
The AfD treats traditional national and familial transmission as the intended operating system of primary domains. Education content is to prioritise positive German history and reject guilt-centred framing; Leitkultur and language continuity structure integration and citizenship; conventional family structures are policy goods; progressive capture of curricula, media and cultural funding is to be reversed. The residual baseline of post-1960s/post-reunification progressive and multicultural institutional norms is not conserved—it is the object of systematic challenge. Domain hierarchy runs preservationist: education framing and institutional transmission of national continuity first, family-status traditionalism supporting, material heritage secondary. This is baseline restoration orientation within democratic contestation, not selective signalling inside an untouched progressive residual order.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72
Concrete demands for positive history teaching, Leitkultur, conventional family policy, and reversal of progressive capture in education and cultural institutions place the AfD in the mid-high band (66–79). Residual progressive settlement is challenged as the problem, not accepted as the default. Contested mid-range placement would understate the programme’s treatment of national and familial continuity as the public standard in primary domains.

# Cultural 5B: Germany – AfD
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview

The AfD advocates rapid, large-scale, and disruptive change across immigration, EU integration, energy policy, and selected institutional arrangements. It consistently criticises consensus-driven gradualism and extended deliberative procedures as barriers. Although it lacks a federal governing record, its platform intensity and low tolerance for process constraints place it clearly above mainstream German parties on pace of change.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Platform calls for immediate and substantial shifts on border control, deportation capacity, and reversal or sharp acceleration of energy-transition timelines.
* Rhetoric that frames extended parliamentary process, judicial oversight, EU mechanisms, and traditional consensus procedures as obstacles to necessary action.
* Emphasis on compressed decision-making and reduced tolerance for coalition-style sequencing on core national issues.
* Positioning that treats rapid institutional and policy reset as legitimate and required rather than exceptional.
* Limited federal governing experience; score rests on the clarity and consistency of accelerationist positioning and rejection of deliberative constraints.
* Contrast with CDU/CSU and SPD: markedly higher willingness to override or compress normal German consensus and review processes.

### Expanded Analysis

German institutional culture strongly favours consensus and extended negotiation. Against that baseline, the AfD’s explicit preference for rapid, disruptive change and its criticism of deliberative delay constitute a clear upward departure. The scoring guide permits high scores for detailed, consistent platforms that reject process constraints even without a long governing record. The AfD meets that test and is placed in the mid-high band (70–79), below the most operationally totalising UK platforms but well above German mainstream gradualism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72

Clear and consistent advocacy of rapid, large-scale, disruptive change with low tolerance for consensus and deliberative procedures places the AfD at 72.

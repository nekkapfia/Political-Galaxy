# Cultural 1B: Germany – AfD
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 84**

**Orthodoxy: 83, Tribalism: 85**

### Overview
The AfD maintains high internal discipline around nationalist and anti-immigration foundations and rejects the post-2015 progressive and establishment consensus as illegitimate. Core positions are treated as closed against dilution; rival moral frameworks of open-border liberalism and progressive identity politics are not granted equal standing. Both aspects are very high, producing a Predominant-band score.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong leadership and factional enforcement around immigration, demography and national-preference positions.
* Internal purges and expulsions used to maintain ideological coherence on foundational issues.
* Post-2015 migration and multicultural consensus framed as elite failure and moral error rather than legitimate alternative.
* EU and supranational frameworks treated as overrides of national democratic will and as illegitimate higher authorities.
* Limited tolerance for public deviation from the core national line once set.
* Residual baseline of national-preference orthodoxy enforced by organisational selection and messaging rather than state monopoly.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is very high: foundational national and immigration positions are protected against internal revision by discipline and expulsion practice; limited tolerance for public deviation once the line is set. Tribalism is very high: progressive and establishment moral frameworks on migration, identity and EU authority are rejected as illegitimate rather than treated as debatable equals within a shared civic frame. EU and supranational claims are overrides of national democratic will. Mechanisms are political-organisational—selection, purges, messaging—not terror or legal monopoly of the totalitarian type. Intensity remains below one-party state extremes. The institutional pattern is high dogmatism characteristic of disciplined national-populist opposition, calibrated in the Predominant (76–85) band alongside RN and Reform UK.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 84
Leadership and factional enforcement, purges/expulsions, elite-failure framing of the post-2015 consensus, and rejection of EU/supranational authority as illegitimate produce very high Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 84.

# Cultural 4B: Germany – AfD
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
The AfD rejects the progressive equity lens. Group outcome gaps are treated as compatible with equal opportunity and are explained primarily by individual, cultural and behavioural factors rather than systemic oppression requiring identity-based corrections.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit opposition to equity interventions, quotas, diversity mandates and systemic-oppression narratives.
* Emphasis on individual performance, cultural compatibility (Leitkultur) and assimilation requirements.
* Critique of institutional DEI and anti-discrimination expansions perceived as reverse discrimination.
* Support for basic equal treatment under law while rejecting group-preference mechanisms.
* Preference for strict merit and integration-performance rules.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks the opposite of the gap-as-proof diagnostic. Persistent group differences are not treated as automatic evidence of unfair process. Hard and soft equity tools are opposed. Individual and cultural explanations dominate. Residual acceptance of core anti-discrimination law prevents an absolute floor. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Consistent rejection of the progressive gap-as-proof diagnostic, opposition to quotas and DEI, and prioritisation of individual merit and cultural performance align with the low equity-lens bands.

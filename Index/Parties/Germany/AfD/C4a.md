# Cultural 4A: Germany – AfD
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 24**

### Overview
The AfD favours traditional selection mechanisms, strong academic standards, and resistance to policies that prioritise group identity or migration background over individual merit. It operates inside Germany’s early-tracking and dual-vocational system. Formal access remains open; the party accepts greater differentiation based on early performance and family background than gap-closing parties.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for maintaining or strengthening early tracking and academic selection (Gymnasium).
* Opposition to ideologically driven reductions in standards or preferential treatment based on migration background.
* Emphasis on traditional vocational and academic pathways based on performance.
* Acceptance of residual quality gradients correlated with family and early performance.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules beyond the existing tracking structure.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. Germany’s early-tracking system already links parental background to pathway placement more strongly than pure comprehensive systems. The AfD’s defence of that structure and resistance to softening selection accept residual birth-linked quality differences. Formal access remains universal; practical ease continues to correlate with family resources and early performance. This places the score in the mid 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 24
Universal schooling plus defence of early tracking and selection, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the mid 21–30 band. Score 24.

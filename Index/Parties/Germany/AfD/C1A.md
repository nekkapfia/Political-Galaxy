# Cultural 1A: Germany – AfD
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)
### Overview
AfD organises moral legitimacy around national interest, concrete preferences of the German demos, cultural continuity as inheritance of a living community and electoral mandate. Transcendent higher-law sources do not organise its claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* National interest, secure borders and the concrete preferences of the German demos are presented as primary political goods.
* Mass immigration is criticised as an override of popular will and a threat to social cohesion and security.
* Cultural continuity and national identity are defended as the inheritance of a living political community.
* Sovereignty and the capacity of the state to control its territory are framed as practical necessities.
* Electoral mandate and direct popular preference are treated as the proper sources of authority.
* Concrete harms to security, welfare systems and social cohesion appear as evidence.

### Expanded Analysis
National interest, secure borders, concrete preferences of the German demos, cultural continuity as inheritance of a living community, sovereignty as practical necessity, electoral mandate and concrete harms organise legitimacy. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law, worship-level deification or sacred Ideal appears. The foundation is Mundane: demos will, concrete interests and national self-preservation.

# Cultural 1A: Germany – AfD
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 12**

### Overview
The AfD grounds political legitimacy in the concrete interests, security and cultural continuity of the German people as a living demos. Immigration, national priority and institutional critique are justified through popular sovereignty and citizen interest. Transcendent sources are effectively absent from organising moral grammar.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Immigration and border control justified by the right of the German people to decide membership and by protection of services, safety and cohesion for existing citizens.
* National and cultural identity defended as living inheritance of the demos, not as sacred blood mystique independent of preference.
* Elite, media and EU constraints criticised as overrides of democratic will.
* Order, reciprocity and practical delivery as primary mundane obligations.
* Residual civilisational language operates as reinforcement of demos claims, not as higher law.
* Electoral mandate and popular will as central legitimating sources.

### Expanded Analysis
The AfD organises justification through popular sovereignty and citizen interest. Trade-offs on immigration, identity and European integration are resolved by reference to living German citizens’ preferences and measurable interests. Civilisational vocabulary, where present, reinforces demos claims rather than installing transcendent authority. There is no organising ecological higher law, religious command, or closed ideological necessity. The pattern is extreme mundane lean.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 12
The transcendent source is effectively absent. Moral authority derives from popular sovereignty and concrete citizen interest. The score is 12.

# Cultural 2B: Germany – AfD
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview
The AfD is the only major German party that actively challenges the post-1945 culture of historical guilt and the systematic de-emphasis of traditional national identity. It argues for a more positive view of German history and continuity. Even so, its position remains heavily constrained by the broader German cultural environment and does not reach confident national affirmation.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit criticism of the “cult of guilt” (Schuld- und Schamkultur) as the organising frame for German history.
* Calls for greater pride in German culture, achievements and longer historical continuity.
* Rejection of the framing of traditional German national identity as inherently dangerous or illegitimate.
* Cultural and historical figures more readily claimed as German rather than purely universalised.
* Would reverse the near-absence of affirmative traditional national statues if it gained national power.
* Rhetoric remains partly reactive; strong national pride is still treated as extreme within the wider political culture.

### Expanded Analysis
The AfD is the clear outlier among major German parties. It is the only one that treats traditional national identity as legitimate and that would actively shift education, commemorative practice and public symbolism away from the dominant atonement frame. At the same time, the party operates inside a society whose institutions, education system and elite culture still treat strong national pride as suspect. Its programme therefore produces a genuine but constrained challenge rather than a full restoration of confident national affirmation. The score reflects both the direction of its intent and the limits imposed by the surrounding cultural environment.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52
The AfD rejects organising guilt narratives and affirms longer German continuity, placing it at the top of the Balanced band. It remains well below the Honour or Pride bands because the broader cultural constraint and residual reactivity prevent full national affirmation.

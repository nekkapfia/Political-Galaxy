# Cultural 3: Germany – Alternative für Deutschland
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 0 | 3B 54**

### Overview
The AfD maintains complete ethnic openness in formal policy: no ancestral or blood criterion for German membership. The Exclusion Test is not met. It articulates the strongest cultural expectations among major German parties, emphasising Leitkultur, deeper integration requirements, and opposition to low-threshold naturalisation. Immigration volume and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit rejection of ethnic or blood criteria for German membership; naturalisation remains open to all ethnicities.
* Strong support for Leitkultur as a guiding framework for integration and full membership.
* Opposition to the 2024 citizenship liberalisation; preference for higher cultural and integration thresholds.
* Language, values, and cultural alignment treated as significant conditions of substantive Endos status.
* Birth and descent routes carry no cultural tests; elevated expectations concentrate on naturalisation and recognition.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is not met. 3A is 0. 3B is moderate because the AfD treats cultural non-alignment as placing people outside full membership and demands clearer cultural commitment than the mainstream parties, without requiring total cultural severance. This aligns with Moderate Demand (50–59).

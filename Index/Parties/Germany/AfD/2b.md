# Core 2B: Germany – AfD
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 56**

### Overview
The AfD prioritises German national interest, criticises the scale of Zeitenwende commitments, and calls for rapid de-escalation in Ukraine and a reduction of German military and financial exposure. It is the clearest major-party alternative to the mainstream intervention toolkit.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Calls for negotiated end to the Ukraine conflict and sharp reduction of German arms deliveries and funding.
* Scepticism of open-ended EU sanctions and of using the EU as a transformative foreign-policy instrument.
* Priority on German borders, energy security and domestic capacity over expeditionary or values-export missions.
* Rejection of “warmongering” framing in leadership messaging.
* Self-defence of German territory remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. AfD policy would shrink military assistance, narrow sanctions use, and reduce Germany’s role as a driver of EU external pressure. One-term realistic outcome is a clear upward move relative to the current governing baseline. Residual willingness to use limited pressure tools prevents a very high score.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 56
Explicit programme to reduce Ukraine military support and EU sanctions activism places the AfD well above the mainstream interventionist cluster.

# Core 2A: Germany – Alternative für Deutschland
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 66**
**Status: FULLY LOCKED**

### Overview
AfD seeks large-scale deportations, euro exit or fundamental renegotiation, rollback of EU competences and the reassertion of German legal primacy over migration and regulatory policy, against a residual baseline of EU law primacy, Eurozone membership and NATO alliance constraints.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Permanent reduction of irregular migration and large-scale deportations of rejected asylum seekers and foreign offenders.
* German exit from the euro or fundamental renegotiation of Eurozone rules to restore monetary sovereignty.
* Rollback of EU competences in migration, justice and regulatory domains; primacy of German law.
* More independent German foreign and defence policy with reduced NATO operational constraints.
* Removal or non-application of the 2024 EU Migration Pact solidarity obligations and Dublin-related constraints on national return policy.
* Residual EU law primacy, Eurozone membership and NATO architecture continue unless the locked exit or non-application instruments succeed.

### Expanded Analysis
Germany’s residual external constraint set includes EU law primacy in conferred competences, Eurozone monetary framework, CJEU residual, 2024 Migration Pact solidarity and Dublin-related return limits, and NATO operational-political architecture. AfD’s programme locks large-scale deportations, euro exit or fundamental renegotiation, rollback of EU competences, primacy of German law, and non-application of Migration Pact solidarity obligations. A more independent foreign and defence posture with reduced NATO operational constraints is sought. The institutional pattern is systematic recovery of border, monetary and legal control: the stated aim is to end the principal residual external constraints on German territorial, legal and currency authority. Residual alliance and treaty architecture is treated as removable by the locked set rather than accepted as permanent.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 66
Euro exit or fundamental renegotiation, rollback of EU competences, primacy of German law, large-scale deportations and non-application of Migration Pact constraints reduce residual foreign legal and monetary constraint. Primary-drag from recovery of sovereign control over borders, law and currency against the residual EU/euro/NATO baseline produces a score of 66.

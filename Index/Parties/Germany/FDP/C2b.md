# Cultural 2B: Germany – FDP
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 39**

### Overview
The FDP takes a liberal, relatively detached approach to national identity. It supports a thin form of constitutional patriotism but shows little interest in traditional German history or collective national pride. Residual baseline of predominantly critical national framing: traditional pride is limited or heavily qualified; critical and atonement narratives carry substantial institutional weight.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Emphasis on individual freedom, European integration and constitutional values rather than national history or identity.
* Traditional German national pride neither strongly affirmed nor actively attacked; largely ignored or treated as outdated.
* Cultural figures presented in universal or liberal terms rather than as national symbols.
* Critical of both AfD nationalism and the more extreme guilt narratives of the left, yet its own position remains thin and de-nationalised.
* Acceptance of the post-1945 public-space pattern of near-absence of traditional national statues.
* Residual baseline of predominantly critical national framing: traditional pride is limited or heavily qualified; critical and atonement narratives carry substantial institutional weight.

### Expanded Analysis
The FDP’s stance is one of liberal detachment from traditional national identity. It does not organise education or culture around collective guilt, but neither does it seek to restore affirmative national narratives or monuments. Constitutional patriotism and European frames predominate. The result is a Negative-band score: critical perspectives and de-nationalisation remain the default, even if the party is less aggressive than the Greens or Die Linke in promoting them.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 39
Thin constitutional patriotism without affirmative engagement with longer German history or traditional national symbols places the FDP in the Negative band.

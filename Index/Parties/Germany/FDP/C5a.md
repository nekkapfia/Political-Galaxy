# Cultural 5A: Germany – FDP
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 31**

### Overview
The FDP orients family, gender and citizenship rules toward individual self-determination and favours European frames over national-narrative continuity, with no distinctive priority for traditional forms.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Family status, gender recognition and citizenship rules oriented toward individual self-determination rather than conventional transmission norms.
* Education and cultural policy favour European and international frames over national-narrative continuity as the organising curriculum priority.
* Heritage receives no distinctive protection priority relative to liberal individual rights.
* Traditional forms accommodated only where they do not constrain liberal autonomy frameworks.
* Residual liberal-progressive settlement on status rules and inclusive institutional culture is accepted; no programme to restore conventional family or national-continuity curriculum as baseline.
* Continuity appears, if at all, as residual private choice, not as institutional default in primary domains.

### Expanded Analysis
The FDP’s Cultural 5A pattern is autonomy-first. Primary domains—family status, gender recognition, citizenship, education framing—are organised around individual self-determination and European/international reference points rather than conventional transmission or national-continuity curriculum. The residual liberal settlement is the operative baseline; traditional forms survive as private options insofar as they do not constrain autonomy frameworks. Domain hierarchy places institutional design of status rules and education frames first; heritage is secondary and not used to reconstruct traditional social transmission as the public default. This is progressive-liberal primacy, not contested mid-range preservation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 31
Autonomy-oriented family, gender and citizenship rules, European framing over national-narrative continuity, and accommodation of traditional forms only where they do not constrain liberal rights place the FDP in the low 31–40 band. Contested mid placement would require selective institutional protection of traditional transmission as a policy good; the analysis shows autonomy primacy instead.

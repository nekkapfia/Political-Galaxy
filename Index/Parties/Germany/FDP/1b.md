## Regulation

### Overview
FDP proposes clear deregulation of residual green and bureaucracy layers — abolition of the German Supply Chain Act, maximal thinning or abolition of EU sustainability reporting, bureaucracy moratoria, and technology-open energy — while retaining ordinary labour protections and competition rules.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Abolish German Lieferkettensorgfaltspflichtengesetz; push abolition or maximal thinning of EU CSDDD, CSRD and Taxonomy reporting.
* Bureaucracy moratorium and one-in-two-out style rules; suspend non-essential reporting duties.
* Energy affordability and technology openness; reduce Green Deal reporting burden.
* Residual ordinary Kündigungsschutz, co-determination and standard competition law retained.
* Residual non-Net-Zero environmental baselines retained.

### Expanded Analysis
FDP removes or radically thins residual green-reporting and supply-chain layers and imposes bureaucracy-reduction rules. Energy and industrial choices are shifted toward technology openness. Ordinary labour protections and competition rules continue. The institutional pattern is substantial reduction of residual green-compliance and bureaucracy expansion of restrictions while residual labour and competition baselines remain.

### Regulation Score
Abolition of residual Supply Chain and sustainability-reporting layers with bureaucracy reduction and retained ordinary labour and competition baselines produces a Regulation Score of 55.

# Core 2B: Germany – FDP
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 46**

### Overview
The FDP supports Ukraine assistance and EU sanctions while placing greater emphasis on fiscal limits, European burden-sharing and caution about open-ended commitments. The interference toolkit is retained but exercised with more restraint language than the Greens or parts of the Union parties.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for Ukraine military and financial aid within fiscal and capacity constraints.
* Backing for EU sanctions packages.
* Greater emphasis on European partners sharing the load and on limiting permanent German over-extension.
* Liberal international economic orientation that still accepts targeted political conditionality.
* Self-defence is out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. FDP policy keeps sanctions and assistance tools active while signalling limits on scale and duration. The result is a slightly higher score than the most committed Zeitenwende parties, reflecting residual caution rather than a turn to non-intervention.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 46
Retained sanctions and Ukraine support, tempered by fiscal and burden-sharing constraints, produce a mid-to-high 40s placement.

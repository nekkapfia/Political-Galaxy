# Cultural 4A: Germany – FDP
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 20**

### Overview
The FDP emphasises individual opportunity, reducing bureaucratic barriers, and strengthening choice and competition within the education system. Formal access remains open; the party prioritises performance and merit as the primary basis for advancement and is sceptical of heavy equalisation interventions.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for greater school autonomy and parental choice.
* Emphasis on performance and merit as the primary basis for advancement.
* Scepticism toward heavy regulatory interventions aimed at equalising outcomes.
* Acceptance of residual quality gradients as compatible with formal openness and individual agency.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. The FDP operates inside Germany’s early-tracking baseline but emphasises choice, autonomy, and performance over structural equalisation. Formal access remains universal; practical ease continues to correlate with family resources and early performance. The party’s preference for merit and choice over gap-closing places it at the lower end of the German range, near the boundary of the 11–20 and 21–30 bands. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 20
Universal schooling plus strong merit/choice emphasis and residual quality gradients, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the upper 11–20 / lower 21–30 boundary. Score 20.

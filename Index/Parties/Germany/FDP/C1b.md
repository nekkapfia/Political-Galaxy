### Overview
The FDP maintains classical-liberal foundations with relatively open internal culture, routine leadership contests and substantial revision space, while practising cordon against AfD and selective resistance to progressive identity orthodoxy. Orthodoxy is the lowest among major German parties; Tribalism is moderate-high, producing a Moderate-to-Strong band score at the lower edge.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Liberal principles (markets, rights, rule of law) as real identity markers with lower organisational closure than mass integrative parties.
* Leadership contests and public disagreement more routine.
* Rejection of AfD as coalition partner in mainstream periods.
* Resistance to progressive identity and regulatory orthodoxy on selected economic and speech-adjacent files.
* Electoral and coalition pragmatism creating substantial revision space.
* Residual baseline of classical-liberal openness that treats establishment liberal frameworks as legitimate.

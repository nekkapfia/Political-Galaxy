# Cultural 5A: Germany – Die Grünen
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
The Greens prioritise curriculum decolonisation, gender self-identification and European-multicultural framing over positive national continuity, treating traditional German cultural content as secondary.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Platform support for decolonisation of school curricula and for gender self-identification in law and institutional practice.
* National identity and traditional German cultural content subordinated to European, multicultural and climate priorities in education and cultural policy.
* Education policy emphasises diversity content, critical historical engagement and future-oriented transformation rather than positive national continuity teaching.
* Family and social policy support legal recognition of non-traditional family forms and gender self-identification frameworks.
* Heritage policy incorporates migration narratives rather than unmodified national transmission as the organising frame.
* Residual progressive settlement is extended; pre-progressive family and continuity curriculum are not defended as defaults.

### Expanded Analysis
Primary domains under Green programme are progressive-transformative. Curriculum decolonisation and critical historical engagement replace positive national continuity as the preferred education frame; gender self-identification and non-traditional family recognition redesign status rules; traditional German cultural content is secondary to European, multicultural and climate priorities. The residual baseline is progressive: the party does not seek to restore conventional family law or positive national-history curriculum as the public standard. Domain hierarchy places education framing and family-status design first; heritage is reframed through migration and diversity narratives. Progressive primacy is consistent across high-weight domains.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Curriculum decolonisation, gender self-identification, non-traditional family recognition, and subordination of national continuity to European-multicultural and climate priorities place Die Grünen in the low 21–30 band. Residual progressive settlement is extended, not contested by a continuity programme in primary domains. Contested mid placement would require institutional protection of traditional curriculum or family transmission as baseline; the locked analysis shows progressive redesign of those domains instead.

## Regulation

### Overview
Die Grünen retain and expand the residual green-industrial architecture (emissions trading, renewable expansion, sectoral targets, technology phase-outs) while maintaining ordinary labour protections and adding social-climate conditionality, producing a lower Regulation profile relative to deregulatory parties.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention and expansion of restrictions of residual Energiewende and climate-target architecture; hold to Verbrenner phase-out and emissions trading.
* Investment premiums and directed support for climate-aligned investment.
* Residual Kündigungsschutz, co-determination and standard competition baseline retained.
* Additional social-climate conditionality on industrial and housing policy in programme language.
* No programme for systemic repeal of residual green mandates or Supply Chain residual.

### Expanded Analysis
Die Grünen lock continuation and further expansion of restrictions of residual green-industrial and mandate layers. Technology phase-outs, emissions pricing and directed investment instruments remain or expand. Ordinary labour and competition baselines continue. The institutional pattern is progressive expansion of restrictions of the residual green-industrial settlement rather than its removal, with residual labour protections left in place.

### Regulation Score
Retention and expansion of restrictions of residual green-industrial architecture with ordinary labour baselines produces a Regulation Score of 32.

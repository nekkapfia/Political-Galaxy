# Core 2A: Germany – Bündnis 90/Die Grünen
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 36**

### Overview

Greens prioritise individual right to asylum, legal pathways, and EU solidary distribution over unilateral border closures and third-country outsourcing. Residual enforcement (deportations of rejected cases where law requires) exists but is not the programme centre. Rule-shaper EU posture retained.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Individual right to asylum as cornerstone; legal pathways expanded.
* EU solidary distribution; reject unilateral internal border closures as primary tool.
* Resistance to outsourcing asylum procedures to third countries relative to Union/AfD.
* Integration and participation framing for residents.
* EU membership and treaty reform-from-within, not exit.
* Immigration volume and borders are 2A; membership tests are C3.

### Expanded Analysis

Lower enforcement maximalism than Union/AfD; higher residual sovereign capacity than pure open-border rhetoric. Score mid-30s.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 36

Asylum-right and pathway densification inside EU membership → **36**.

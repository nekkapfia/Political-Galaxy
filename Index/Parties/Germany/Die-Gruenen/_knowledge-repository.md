## 2. Core 1A — Personal Autonomy

**Scores recorded in current Index files:** Endos Score: 27; Xenos Score: 22

### Claims / mechanisms already present in scored documents

- Endos Score: 27**
- Xenos Score: 22**
- Strong defence of criminal hate-speech law (*Volksverhetzung* §130 StGB and related provisions) as protection of democratic culture and human dignity; no narrowing agenda.
- Support for platform content duties against illegal and harmful material, continuing NetzDG logic into DSA-era enforcement, with short removal deadlines and fines for non-compliance remaining part of the regulatory surface.
- Anti-discrimination and equality frameworks treated as organising principles across education, work, culture and public institutions; hostile-environment logic applies to speech that conflicts with institutional norms.
- Gender self-determination / self-identification as a rights priority; institutional consequences follow for speech (misgendering treated as harmful or discriminatory) and for association/access around single-sex spaces.
- Diversity, anti-racism and related educational/institutional programmes treated as positive organisational and state duties rather than optional culture; practice is stronger under Green institutional influence than under SPD governance.
- No programme defence of gender-critical or migration-critical speech against institutional sanction; such speech is frequently framed as potentially harmful or as undermining democratic culture.
- Retention of ordinary direct-harm criminal prohibitions.
- Volksverhetzung* defence, platform regulation, identity-protective institutional norms, gender self-ID secondary speech constraints, and denser diversity/attitude cultures under Green influence place Endos at 27. Heightened application against identity-critical speech produces Xenos 22.

### Enrichment targets / research gaps for this axis

- Manifesto / programme text on speech, protest, hate-speech, data, drug, and lifestyle restrictions.
- Explicit repeal vs retain positions relative to current Strafgesetzbuch / Versammlungsrecht baseline.
- Any Endos/Xenos split justification (religious dress, designated groups).

## 9. Cultural 2B — Pride / National Identity

## 16. Cultural 5B — Radicalism (Pace)

**Scores recorded in current Index files:** Score: 45

### Claims / mechanisms already present in scored documents

- Brief Overview**
- Observable Patterns of Pace and Intensity of Change – Expanded Analysis**
- Climate and energy policy documents frequently use emergency and urgency framing, advocating accelerated transition timelines.
- However, party practice and policy consistently emphasise citizen participation, extended consultation, local democracy, and safeguards against overly centralised or rushed implementation.
- Governing experience (federal coalition since 2021 and multiple state governments) shows both accelerated action under crisis pressure (energy diversification post-2022) and significant extension of review and coalition negotiation processes on structural reforms.
- Opposition to certain infrastructure projects and economic measures often involves support for lengthy legal and participatory review mechanisms.
- Internal culture contains both accelerationist and traditional gradualist/participatory factions, producing inconsistent enforcement of speed.
- Why This Produces a Final Score of 45**

### Enrichment targets / research gaps for this axis

- Platform language on urgency, fast-track, override of deliberation.
- Governing record vs rhetoric; crisis acceleration filter.
- Differentiate from C5A (pace ≠ direction).

## X. 2025 programme and institutional specifics (research expansion)

### Climate / economy (Core 1B, C5A, C5B)
- Grundsatzprogramm and 2025 campaign: Paris Agreement and 1.5°C path as binding reference; transition to 100% renewables; exit fossil gas, oil, coal.
- CO2 price, state investment support, and regulatory bans as instruments; EU lead markets for climate-friendly cement/steel with offtake quotas; CO2 circular economy including capture/storage where unavoidable.
- Hold to 2035 combustion-engine ban and EU fleet limits; stretch penalty timelines for German manufacturers if needed; socially balanced EV purchase/leasing support.
- Climate framed as security risk in foreign-policy language.

### Migration (Core 2A — volume/control; C3 membership separate)
- Individual right to asylum as cornerstone; legal procedure with individual assessment; dignified accommodation.
- Oppose moving asylum procedures to third countries; resist blanket internal-border closures and blanket rejections as contrary to common area of freedom/security/law.
- Diversity council / party resolutions: Germany as diverse migration society; anti-discrimination as progress for all; integration as reciprocal participation.
- Berlin Land Greens examples (context, not federal programme): climate-refugee admission language; resist deportations to Syria/Iran/Afghanistan; critique of 2024–25 federal tightening.
- Youth wing (Grüne Jugend) has demanded higher migrant intake and spending — note as factional signal, not automatically federal programme.

### Identity / equity (C2A, C4B, C2B)
- Strong equity, diversity, anti-discrimination measures; recognition of systemic barriers.
- Support for Selbstbestimmungsgesetz framework historically; critique of rollbacks.
- Most consistently critical / de-nationalising stance among major parties on traditional national identity in prior scoring; European and post-national frames.

### Foreign policy (Core 2B)
- Feminist foreign policy language in youth/party materials; continued climate internationalism.
- Governing record 2021– (Habeck, Baerbock) as implementation evidence for pace and soft-power tools.

### Research still useful
- Federal 2025 programme PDF exact migration numbers vs Land resolutions.
- Selbstbestimmungsgesetz and quota positions in current federal platform.
- Governing-record vs opposition rhetoric on deportations 2021–2025.

# Cultural 2B: Germany – Die Grünen
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 14**

### Overview
Die Grünen maintain the most consistently critical and de-nationalising stance among the major parties. They treat traditional German national identity as largely illegitimate and prioritise historical atonement, European integration and post-national frameworks. Residual baseline of shame- or guilt-oriented framing: national pride is treated as problematic or secondary; critical and pathologising frames predominate in education and cultural institutions.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong suspicion of national pride and traditional German identity.
* Education and cultural policy positions emphasise expanding historical guilt (including colonialism) and moving beyond national frameworks.
* Cultural and historical figures routinely de-nationalised and presented as European or universal.
* Rhetoric frames German history through responsibility, atonement and the need to dissolve older forms of national identity.
* Active support for the institutional prioritisation of atonement memorials and the continued absence of affirmative traditional national statues.
* Local and Land-level actions consistent with critical, post-national cultural policy.
* Residual baseline of shame- or guilt-oriented framing: national pride is treated as problematic or secondary; critical and pathologising frames predominate in education and cultural institutions.

### Expanded Analysis
If Die Grünen held national power they would deepen, not moderate, the existing Guilt orientation. Their programme treats traditional national identity as a problem to be overcome rather than a source of continuity or meaning. The near-absence of traditional national monuments is not an accident they seek to correct; it is a pattern they endorse. De-nationalisation of cultural figures and expansion of atonement narratives (including colonial history) are core elements of their cultural stance. The score therefore sits deep inside the Guilt band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 14
Active commitment to de-emphasising traditional national identity, expanding guilt frames and treating national pride as suspect places Die Grünen firmly in the Guilt band (10–19).

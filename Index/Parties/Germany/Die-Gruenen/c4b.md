# Cultural 4B: Germany – Die Grünen
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 76**

### Overview
Die Grünen treat unequal group outcomes as diagnostic of systemic and intersectional unfairness. Persistent gaps under formal rules are read as proof that opportunity was not equal; equity interventions, targets and institutional transformation are required. Residual baseline of progressive equity-lens framing: gaps between groups are routinely treated as evidence of unfair opportunity structures requiring corrective institutional response.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong structural-racism and intersectional diagnostic language.
* Support for expanded anti-discrimination enforcement, diversity quotas/targets, gender budgeting and representation goals.
* Advocacy of self-ID, decolonisation framing and institutional culture change.
* Critique of pure merit frameworks as reproducing privilege.
* Soft-to-mid mechanisms (targets, positive action, institutional rules) predominate; hard quotas exist in limited domains (gender boards) but are not universal.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks the gap-as-proof diagnostic at high intensity. Remedies centre on proactive equity interventions and institutional redesign. The score is moderated from the extreme high band because primary tools remain soft-to-mid rather than zero-tolerance hard outcome mandates across every core domain. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 76
High diagnostic lock on systemic causes plus consistent advocacy for equity targets and institutional transformation produce a high-band score. Limited reach of hard quotas keeps the score below the 90+ compressed zone.

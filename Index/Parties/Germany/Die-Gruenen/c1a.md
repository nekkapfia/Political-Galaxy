### Overview
The Greens present ecological breakdown and intergenerational injustice as defining moral crises. Core aims are systemic transformation to protect the climate, future generations and the natural world. Organising sources are strongly transcendent: duties to the living world and future persons that regularly override present majorities and short-term economic preference. * Progressive social justice claims operate alongside the ecological core.

### Expanded Analysis
The Greens organise justification through duties that outrank present preference. Climate and ecological limits are moral constraints that legitimate transformation even when costly to living majorities. Intergenerational language treats future persons as independent moral patients. Science establishes the factual basis of an already-primary ethical duty. Progressive social claims reinforce rather than dilute the pattern. The institutional profile is strong transcendent lean within the German spectrum.

The transcendent source is primary and organising. Present democratic will and economic interests are regularly subordinated to ecological and intergenerational claims. The score is 76.

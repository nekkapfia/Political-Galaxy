# Core 1A: Germany – Die Grünen
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview

Die Grünen’s programme produces a restrictive Core 1A profile built from strong defence of *Volksverhetzung* and related hate-speech law, support for platform regulation against illegal and harmful content (NetzDG lineage into DSA enforcement), identity-protective institutional norms, gender self-determination rules with secondary speech effects, and denser diversity/attitude-related institutional cultures where the party holds power. Cumulative weight sits inside the ideological-enforcement range. Heightened intensity against speech critical of protected identity categories The restriction set is scored as a single combined profile under Core 1A rules (no valid stable dual-structure out-group for an Endos/Xenos split).

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Strong defence of criminal hate-speech law (*Volksverhetzung* §130 StGB and related provisions) as protection of democratic culture and human dignity; no narrowing agenda.
* Support for platform content duties against illegal and harmful material, continuing NetzDG logic into DSA-era enforcement, with short removal deadlines and fines for non-compliance remaining part of the regulatory surface.
* Anti-discrimination and equality frameworks treated as organising principles across education, work, culture and public institutions; hostile-environment logic applies to speech that conflicts with institutional norms.
* Gender self-determination / self-identification as a rights priority; institutional consequences follow for speech (misgendering treated as harmful or discriminatory) and for association/access around single-sex spaces.
* Diversity, anti-racism and related educational/institutional programmes treated as positive organisational and state duties rather than optional culture; practice is stronger under Green institutional influence than under SPD governance.
* No programme defence of gender-critical or migration-critical speech against institutional sanction; such speech is frequently framed as potentially harmful or as undermining democratic culture.
* Retention of ordinary direct-harm criminal prohibitions.

### Expanded Analysis

Green programmes treat identity-protective speech rules and institutional anti-discrimination orthodoxy as settled goods to be enforced and extended. *Volksverhetzung* and platform removal duties remain non-negotiable. Gender self-identification is advanced as a rights project whose secondary effects on expression and association are accepted rather than minimised.

Where Greens hold federal or Land power, institutional practice correlates with denser diversity and attitude-related programmes in public bodies, education and culture. Critical speech on race, migration, gender identity and related topics faces elevated risk of being framed as harmful or as creating a hostile environment.

Speech critical of specific protected identity categories attracts heightened criminal, institutional and professional risk relative to other political speech. This is a stable, discretely targetable intensity differential.

The architecture is the densest among major German parties — baseline criminal/regulatory speech law plus institutional orthodoxy plus stronger training cultures where they govern. It is the German analogue of the UK Green densification pole.

### Why this produces Endos 27 / Xenos 22

*Volksverhetzung* defence, platform regulation, identity-protective institutional norms, gender self-ID secondary speech constraints, and denser diversity/attitude cultures under Green influence place Endos at 27. Heightened application against identity-critical speech produces Xenos 22.

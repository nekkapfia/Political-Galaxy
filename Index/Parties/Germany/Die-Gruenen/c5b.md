# Cultural 5B: Germany – Die Grünen
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 45**

### Overview

Die Grünen combine strong urgency framing on climate, energy and ecological transition with a significant institutional preference for consultation, participation and coalition-compatible sequencing. Governing experience at federal and state level has shown both accelerated action under crisis pressure and frequent extension of review and negotiation processes. The net pattern is mid-to-low radicalism.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Frequent emergency and urgency language on climate targets, energy transition and related ecological deadlines.
* Consistent emphasis on public participation, stakeholder consultation and coalition negotiation as legitimate and necessary parts of major change.
* Federal coalition experience (2021–) and multiple state governments demonstrate both rapid measures under external pressure (energy diversification post-2022) and extended deliberative timelines on structural reforms.
* Internal culture contains both accelerationist and traditional participatory/gradualist currents; the latter remain influential.
* Contrast with AfD: markedly higher residual commitment to process and consensus constraints.

### Expanded Analysis

Urgency of desired ecological outcomes does not automatically translate into institutional design that overrides deliberative mechanisms. Die Grünen’s observable practice retains substantial space for consultation and sequencing. This keeps the score in the low-to-mid band (45), below parties that treat process constraints as obstacles to be minimised.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 45

Urgency framing on climate and energy is paired with sustained preference for participatory and coalition-compatible sequencing, placing Die Grünen at 45.

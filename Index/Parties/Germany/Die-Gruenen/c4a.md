# Cultural 4A: Germany – Die Grünen
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
Die Grünen place strong emphasis on reducing socio-economic and migration-related inequalities in education through expanded support, reduced early selection, and greater inclusion. Formal access remains open; the party prioritises active reduction of birth-based quality and pathway differences.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for delaying or softening early tracking in favour of more comprehensive approaches.
* Strong focus on additional resources and support for disadvantaged and migrant-background students.
* Emphasis on all-day schooling and early childhood education as tools for reducing birth-based gaps.
* Preference for structural reduction of quality gradients over acceptance of residual differences.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. Die Grünen operate inside Germany’s early-tracking baseline but seek to soften selection effects and expand early support. Formal access remains universal; the party’s direction is among the strongest gap-closing orientations in the German set. Residual gradients remain, but the programme actively works to reduce their practical weight. This places the score at the lower end of the 21–30 band (or boundary with 11–20). Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Universal schooling plus strong preference for softening early tracking and expanding early support, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the lower 21–30 / upper 11–20 boundary. Score 22.

# Cultural 2A: Germany – Die Grünen
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 18**

### Overview
Die Grünen advocate strong expansion of group-based allocation mechanisms, particularly around gender identity and intersectional approaches. The person is routinely measured as a location in identity categories. Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Strong support for the Selbstbestimmungsgesetz (self-ID by declaration).
* Preference for self-identified gender over biological sex in access and legal status.
* Support for expansive diversity and positive-action policies across institutions.
* Advocacy for further constitutional and institutional recognition of identity categories.
* Opposition to restrictions based on biological sex in most contested contexts.
* Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Expanded Analysis
The party systematically elevates group identity above individual or biological criteria. Self-ID and related frameworks allow self-declared category to determine legal status and access, while broader equity and identity-conscious proposals keep group membership institutionally central. Individual merit remains secondary. The result is severe group collectivism on the unit-of-measure test.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 18
Multi-category allocation goals and systematic prioritisation of identity location over person-unit criteria match the severe group-collectivism band. Score 18.

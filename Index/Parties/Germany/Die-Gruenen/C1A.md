# Cultural 1A: Germany – Die Grünen
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 44 (6M)

### Overview
Die Grünen organise moral legitimacy around climate and ecological limits as empirical threats to human societies and future persons, intergenerational responsibility, and democratic participation. Elevated urgency is present; sacralisation of Nature is not the organising frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Ecological limits and climate duties are framed as empirical requirements to protect human life, civilisation and future human welfare.
* Intergenerational responsibility is treated as a duty to future human persons.
* Social justice and ecological transformation are advanced as interconnected human-welfare requirements.
* Scientific evidence is used to establish the scale of ecological risk.
* Democratic participation remains valued inside the same frame.
* Elevated urgency language appears within the human-necessity framing.

### Expanded Analysis
Climate and ecological limits are framed as empirical requirements on behalf of human survival and future human welfare; intergenerational duty, social justice and democratic participation organise the conversation. This is human-necessity ecology — Mundane — not sacred/inheriting-will ecology. Elevated urgency is lesser residual only. No divine command, Cosmic sacralisation of Nature, or worship-level deification organises legitimacy. The foundation is Mundane with residual elevated ecological urgency.

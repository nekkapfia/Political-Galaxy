# Cultural 2A: Germany – Die Linke
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
Die Linke supports expansive group-based allocation mechanisms, often combining identity and class frames. The individual is regularly subordinated to category location. Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Strong support for positive action and group-based measures.
* Favourable stance toward self-identification and expansive protections organised by identity category.
* View of individual merit as insufficient without accompanying group-based interventions.
* Support for broadening mechanisms that allocate on the basis of group identity.
* Combination of class-based and identity-based approaches to status and opportunity.
* Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Expanded Analysis
The party consistently prioritises group membership over individual characteristics across multiple domains. Positive action, self-ID and structural-category frames treat group location as a primary and legitimate factor in the distribution of rights and opportunities. Individual merit is recognised only within, and subordinated to, group-based remedies. The result is very strong group subordination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Systematic elevation of category over person as the unit of allocation places Die Linke in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 26.

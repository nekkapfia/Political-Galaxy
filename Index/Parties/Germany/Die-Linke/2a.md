### Overview
Die Linke combines internationalist and solidarity framing critical of national border hardening with acceptance or expansion of restrictions of residual EU social and regulatory architecture and scepticism toward NATO.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Favours relatively open migration pathways and internationalist solidarity framing over national border hardening.
* Accepts or expands residual EU social and regulatory architecture.
* Seeks reduced NATO operational commitments while residual EU law primacy and Eurozone membership continue.
* Supports continued EU and euro membership without structural recovery of exclusive national border and monetary autonomy.

# Core 2B: Germany – Die Linke
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview
Die Linke’s foreign-policy orientation emphasises anti-militarism, scepticism toward NATO expeditionary operations, and preference for diplomatic and multilateral restraint relative to mainstream German parties. It does not control the levers of German foreign policy and therefore generates limited direct interference, while its platform still accepts certain soft-power and solidarity instruments.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Consistent opposition to German participation in NATO-led combat or high-intensity expeditionary operations.
* Preference for diplomatic, UN-framed and civilian instruments over military intervention.
* Support for selected solidarity and soft-power programmes (development, human-rights framing) that can still constitute light-to-moderate interference when conditional.
* Residual acceptance of Germany’s broader Western institutional embedding while seeking to reduce its military-interventionist edge.
* Limited ownership of actual foreign-policy decision-making; score reflects platform aims and opposition practice rather than governing record.
* Self-defence of German territory remains out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. Die Linke’s platform aims to reduce German military and hard-power interference while retaining softer solidarity and development instruments. Because the party has not held the decisive foreign-policy portfolios, the score is driven by stated aims and opposition record rather than a long governing pattern of restraint or intervention. Relative to more interventionist mainstream parties the placement is higher; relative to pure non-interventionist platforms it remains mid-range because soft-power and conditional solidarity tools are not rejected wholesale.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48
Platform preference for restraint on military expeditionary action, combined with residual soft-power and solidarity instruments and limited governing ownership of the levers, produces a mid-to-high 40s score.

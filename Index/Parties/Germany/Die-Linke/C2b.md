# Cultural 2B: Germany – Die Linke
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 19**

### Overview
Die Linke maintains one of the most consistently critical stances toward traditional German national identity and history. It treats German nationalism and militarism as deeply suspect and prioritises historical guilt and internationalist frameworks.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Traditional German national identity viewed as largely illegitimate and associated with aggression and authoritarianism.
* Emphasis on atonement, anti-militarism and the need to move beyond national frameworks.
* Cultural and historical figures almost never claimed as sources of national pride; when discussed they are universalised or critically contextualised.
* Some East German nostalgia exists, but framed as socialist rather than national pride.
* Full support for the institutional prioritisation of atonement memorials and the absence of traditional national statues.

### Expanded Analysis
Die Linke’s cultural position is one of active de-emphasis and suspicion of traditional German national identity. Even residual positive references to the GDR are internationalist or class-based rather than national. The party would not restore affirmative national monuments or reverse the de-nationalisation of cultural figures. Its score sits at the upper edge of the Guilt band, reflecting a coherent and consistent rejection of traditional national pride.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 19
Consistent treatment of traditional German national identity as illegitimate, combined with prioritisation of atonement and internationalist frames, places Die Linke at the top of the Guilt band.

# Cultural 2A: Germany – BSW
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 50**

### Overview
BSW occupies a mixed position: it retains formal individual legal personality and shows selective resistance to progressive identity-allocation expansion, while still operating inside residual category frameworks and left-economic group analysis.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Formal individual equality and rights residual as legal baseline.
* Selective criticism of certain progressive identity and self-ID expansions.
* Continued acceptance of core AGG and residual category instruments.
* Class and social-group framing remaining relevant to distributional analysis.
* Not a systematic dismantling programme and not a multi-axis identity-stack regime.

### Expanded Analysis
The party does not organise all allocation around contemporary identity stacks, yet it also does not restore pure person-unit merit as the exclusive rule. Residual category tools remain legitimate and class-group analysis continues to structure parts of its programme. Individual legal personality is the formal default, but group membership retains significant influence. The pattern sits at the boundary of significant and moderate group-influenced allocation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 50
Mixed retention of category tools with only selective resistance to identity expansion places BSW at the upper edge of the significant group-based allocation band. Score 50.

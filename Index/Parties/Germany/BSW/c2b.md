# Cultural 2B: Germany – BSW
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview
The Bündnis Sahra Wagenknecht (BSW) combines left-economic positions with a more affirmative stance toward German cultural continuity and a critique of open-border cosmopolitanism. It is less hostile to traditional national identity than Die Linke or the Greens, yet it does not mount a full challenge to the post-1945 guilt framework. Residual baseline of predominantly critical national framing: traditional pride is limited or heavily qualified; critical and atonement narratives carry substantial institutional weight.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Critique of elite cosmopolitanism and defence of the concrete cultural interests of the existing population.
* More willingness than Die Linke to speak of German cultural continuity and social cohesion.
* Still operates inside the broader post-1945 commemorative and educational architecture; does not campaign to dismantle atonement frameworks.
* Limited engagement with pre-1945 German history as a positive source of identity.
* Focus remains more on present cultural and social cohesion than on restoring traditional national historical pride.
* Residual baseline of predominantly critical national framing: traditional pride is limited or heavily qualified; critical and atonement narratives carry substantial institutional weight.

### Expanded Analysis
BSW occupies a distinctive space: it rejects the most aggressive de-nationalising positions of the progressive left while stopping short of the AfD’s explicit challenge to the guilt culture. Its affirmative language is primarily about the living population and social cohesion rather than about longer historical nationhood or the restoration of traditional national symbols. The score therefore sits near the top of the Balanced band, reflecting a partial but incomplete break with the dominant German pattern.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48
Greater openness to cultural continuity than the progressive left, combined with continued acceptance of the post-1945 commemorative baseline, places BSW at the upper edge of the Balanced band.

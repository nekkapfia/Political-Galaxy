### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Social protection, peace policy and the concrete interests of ordinary citizens are framed as primary political requirements.
* Critique of current economic and foreign-policy arrangements is advanced in terms of fairness and national interest.
* Popular sovereignty and democratic control are cited as central legitimating sources.
* Electoral competition is accepted as the legitimate means of advancing the programme.
* Concrete improvements in living standards and security appear as goals.
* Social-justice and left tradition language is used to critique the existing order and to identify the party’s political lineage.

### Expanded Analysis
Social protection, concrete interests of ordinary citizens, fairness, national interest, popular sovereignty, electoral competition and improvements in living standards organise the moral conversation. Social-justice and left tradition language is present and supplies critique, but the operational grounds of legitimacy are demos will, concrete interests and performance. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law, worship-level deification or sacred Ideal organises authority. The foundation is Mundane with justice-critique language present.

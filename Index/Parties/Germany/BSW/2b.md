# Core 2B: Germany – BSW
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview
The Bündnis Sahra Wagenknecht (BSW) explicitly criticises Ukraine policy, sanctions strategy and German militarisation. It calls for diplomatic settlement and reduction of military support, placing it above the mainstream and close to or slightly below the AfD on the non-intervention scale.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit calls for diplomatic end to the Ukraine conflict and reduction of German military involvement.
* Criticism of EU sanctions as escalatory or economically self-damaging.
* National-social emphasis that prioritises domestic welfare and industrial capacity over external military commitments.
* Rejection of the Zeitenwende framing as open-ended entanglement.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. BSW policy would shrink the German interference toolkit on Ukraine and sanctions. The programme is more focused on de-escalation than on building new expeditionary capacity. Residual acceptance of some international engagement keeps the score out of the highest non-intervention bands.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52
Clear programme to reduce military support and sanctions activism places BSW in the low-to-mid 50s.

# Core 2A: Germany – SPD
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview
The SPD supports continued EU and euro membership with social-policy coordination, incremental asylum reform and European solutions over unilateral national approaches. External pooling of authority continues and is preferred in key domains.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Supports continued EU membership and the euro while pushing for social-policy coordination.
* Favours reform of EU asylum procedures and more effective returns within the existing legal framework.
* Accepts the 2024 Migration Pact framework with emphasis on solidarity mechanisms.
* Supports continued NATO membership and deeper European defence cooperation.
* Supports European coordination on asylum, climate and industrial policy rather than unilateral national approaches.

### Expanded Analysis
The SPD supports continued EU and euro membership, social-policy coordination, reform of asylum procedures and more effective returns within the existing legal framework, and acceptance of the 2024 Migration Pact solidarity mechanisms. NATO and European defence cooperation are retained. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is incremental procedural improvement inside an unchanged European legal and monetary structure. EU membership architecture and legal supremacy constitute primary drag; intensity is moderated by asylum-procedure reform claims but not by structural repatriation of competences.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48
Continued EU and euro membership, acceptance of the Migration Pact and only incremental asylum reform leave European legal authority intact. External pooling of authority is explicit, placing the final set inside the 41–50 Low–Mid band. The score of 48 follows from retention of the residual EU architecture rather than recovery of unilateral control.

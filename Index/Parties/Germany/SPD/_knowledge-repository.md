## 2. Core 1A — Personal Autonomy

**Scores recorded in current Index files:** Endos Score: 48

### Claims / mechanisms already present in scored documents

- Endos Score: 48**
- Xenos Score: N/A**

## X. 2025 programme and institutional specifics (research expansion)

### Migration (Core 2A)
- Steer migration with humanitarian responsibility; solidary distribution across EU under GEAS from 2026; high legal standards for border procedures.
- Reject border closures and blanket rejections at internal borders as contrary to common area of freedom/security/law.
- Reject outsourcing asylum procedures to third countries; keep procedures in-country but accelerate (target decisions within six months via digitisation).
- Continue family reunification for those needing protection; quick consistent deportations for criminals; prefer voluntary returns as more humane.
- Comprehensive migration agreements: legal pathways for training/work plus firm take-back obligations for nationals who must leave.
- Germany as immigration country; expand and fund integration courses; Partizipationsgesetz for equal participation; implement and develop Skilled Immigration Act to bring qualified workers faster.
- Retain dual citizenship law; facilitate IT and qualified specialists.

### Social / economy (Core 1B, C4A, C4B)
- Secure pension level at least 48% long-term; reject pension cuts and raising retirement age from 67.
- Raise national minimum wage toward €15/hour in programme discussion.
- Bürgergeld to support path out of unemployment; more Jobcenter counselling funds; collective agreements to reduce pay gaps.
- Free lunch for daycare and elementary children in some formulations.
- Income tax relief for most households; higher taxes on the very rich.

### Foreign policy (Core 2B)
- Firm support for Ukraine including weapons and equipment; bilateral security agreement; Ukraine able to negotiate at eye level without dictated Russian peace; no Taurus from Bundeswehr stocks in Scholz-era programme caution language.
- NATO commitment; at least 2% GDP defence spending; long-term European Defense Union and competitive European defence industry; modernise Bundeswehr sustainably.
- China as partner, competitor, systemic rival; coordinated European policy; reduce dependence, cooperate on climate/arms control.
- Avoid direct NATO–Russia confrontation.

### Research still useful
- Exact Bürgergeld sanction rules proposed.
- Coalition 2021–2025 record on deportation numbers vs programme.
- Internal SPD tension on Ukraine arms (public debate) for 2B nuance.

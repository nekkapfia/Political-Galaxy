# Core 2B: Germany – SPD
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 38**

### Overview
The SPD as a governing party has participated fully in the Zeitenwende shift: large-scale military support for Ukraine, central involvement in EU sanctions design, and continued commitment to NATO and European defence cooperation. The pattern retains a substantial interference toolkit while remaining short of permanent occupation or multi-country control. Residual baseline is Zeitenwende-era interventionist capacity.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Zeitenwende policy under Scholz: historic increase in military aid and defence spending framed as response to Russian aggression.
* Active role in designing and maintaining successive EU sanctions packages against Russia.
* Continued support for NATO collective defence and European military assistance to Ukraine.
* Development and values-based diplomacy that routinely includes governance and human-rights conditionality.
* Residual baseline of active external pressure instruments post-Zeitenwende; no return to earlier restraint postures as the operative residual.
* Self-defence of German territory and direct collective defence obligations are out of scope or heavily mitigated.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. SPD-led Germany has used military assistance, coordinated sanctions and EU instruments to pressure Russia and support Ukraine’s war effort. Invitation by Ukraine provides partial mitigation; the actions still constitute interference in both Ukrainian and Russian affairs. EU sanctions leadership is a Germany-specific form of external pressure. The residual posture is interventionist and sustained.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 38
Zeitenwende military support plus leading EU sanctions role, with residual active pressure instruments, produce a clear interventionist pattern moderated by invitation and multilateral framing.

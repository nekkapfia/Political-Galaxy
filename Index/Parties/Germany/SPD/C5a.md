# Cultural 5A: Germany – SPD
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 34**

### Overview
The SPD prioritises family individualisation, multicultural integration and inclusion-focused education, framing tradition as requiring adaptation rather than unmodified continuity.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Current positions prioritise welfare modernisation, family individualisation and multicultural integration in law and institutions.
* Education and cultural policy emphasise inclusion metrics, diversity content and future-oriented skills over traditional national narrative teaching as the organising frame.
* Media and institutional guidance frames tradition as requiring adaptation rather than unmodified continuity.
* Family and citizenship policy supports liberalisation of status rules and self-determination frameworks.
* Residual progressive settlement is extended; pre-progressive family and continuity curriculum are not restored as defaults.
* Heritage and welfare-state continuity do not equal preservation of traditional cultural and familial transmission in primary domains.

### Expanded Analysis
Under SPD influence, primary domains prioritise progressive modernisation. Education framing emphasises inclusion and diversity over positive national continuity; family individualisation and liberalised status rules redesign transmission; tradition is framed as needing adaptation. The residual progressive settlement is the operative default rather than a temporary layer awaiting restoration of conventional family or continuity curriculum. Domain hierarchy places education content and family-status design first; welfare-state and material continuity are secondary and do not restore traditional social baselines. Progressive primacy is consistent across high-weight domains, tempered only by residual institutional forms that do not change the transmission pattern in law or schooling.


### Why this produces a Final Score of 34
Inclusion-focused education, family individualisation, multicultural integration, and framing of tradition as requiring adaptation place the SPD in the low 31–40 band. Residual progressive settlement is extended rather than reversed, so contested mid-range placement is not justified without concrete protection of traditional curriculum or family transmission as baseline.

# Cultural 4A: Germany – SPD
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview

Universal practical school access with social-democratic equalisation pressure (e.g. free lunch formulations) and less emphasis on Gymnasium hardening than Union/AfD. Quality gradients remain; hard access barriers are not the residual model.

* Universal schooling access retained.
* Free lunch for daycare and elementary children in some programme formulations.
* Collective and social-equalisation frames in education-adjacent policy.
* Differentiated pathways still exist in the German system residual; SPD does not fully abolish selection.

### Expanded Analysis

Guide rules: universal access + residual selection ≈ quality-gradient band. SPD pulls toward equalisation within that band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28

Access universal; mild equalisation pressure within quality-gradient band → **28**.

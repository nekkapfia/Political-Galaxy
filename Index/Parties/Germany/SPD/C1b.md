# Cultural 1B: Germany – SPD
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 71**

**Orthodoxy: 70, Tribalism: 72**

### Overview
The SPD maintains social-democratic orthodoxy with significant internal factionalism and a record of major policy revision (Agenda 2010), while enforcing a firm cordon against AfD and competing with progressive and conservative alternatives. Orthodoxy is moderated by factionalism and revision history; Tribalism is high against AfD, producing a Moderate-to-Strong band score.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Social-democratic commitments as core identity with historical openness to major programmatic revision.
* Internal factionalism between left and centre reducing pure orthodoxy.
* Firm rejection of cooperation with AfD as illegitimate governing partner.
* Alignment with progressive social and European institutional norms in recent periods.
* Coalition practice and electoral pressure creating ongoing revision space.
* Residual baseline of Volkspartei revision capacity that prevents extreme internal closure.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is moderate-high: social-democratic foundations exist, but the party’s history of major programmatic revision (Agenda 2010) and ongoing factional contest show genuine internal openness relative to more closed parties. Coalition practice and electoral pressure create revision space. Tribalism is high against AfD through firm cordon as illegitimate governing partner; conservative and green frameworks remain rivals within normal competition. Alignment with progressive social and European norms in recent periods raises social-file closure. The institutional pattern is high-mid dogmatism with lower orthodoxy than Greens or AfD and elevated tribalism through cordon practice, calibrated at 71.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 71
Social-democratic foundations combined with residual factional contest and revision history, firm cordon against AfD, and progressive alignment produce moderate-high Orthodoxy with elevated Tribalism. The formula yields 71.

# Core 2A: Germany – SPD
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 40**

### Overview

SPD frames Germany as an immigration country inside EU solidarity: GEAS distribution from 2026, reject internal border closures and third-country outsourcing of asylum procedures, retain family reunification for those needing protection, accelerate in-country decisions, expand legal pathways and skilled immigration, retain dual citizenship. Deportations for criminals and voluntary returns are accepted; blanket rejections are not.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Solidary EU distribution under GEAS from 2026; high legal standards for border procedures.
* Reject border closures and blanket rejections at internal borders.
* Reject outsourcing asylum procedures to third countries; accelerate in-country decisions (target six months).
* Continue family reunification for those needing protection; quick deportations for criminals; prefer voluntary returns.
* Migration agreements: legal pathways for training/work plus take-back obligations.
* Expand integration courses; Partizipationsgesetz; develop Skilled Immigration Act.
* Retain dual citizenship; facilitate IT and qualified specialists.
* EU membership and rule-shaper posture retained.

### Expanded Analysis

Relative to Union/AfD enforcement hardening, SPD maximises legal pathways and rejects safe-third-country outsourcing and internal turn-backs. Residual deportation for criminals and GEAS participation keep the score out of pure open-border territory. One-term realism and rule-shaper context apply.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 40

EU solidarity and immigration-country framing with limited enforcement maximalism → **40**.

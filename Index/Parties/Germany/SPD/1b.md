## Regulation

### Overview
SPD produces a lower-mid Core 1B Regulation profile defined by continuity of residual climate targets and green industrial policy, strong residual labour protections (Kündigungsschutz, co-determination, collective bargaining), and targeted state investment support.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Continuity of residual climate targets and residual green industrial policy with targeted investment incentives.
* Strong residual labour-market protections (Kündigungsschutz) and higher minimum-wage trajectory; residual co-determination and collective-bargaining framework maintained and defended.
* Industrial electricity price and selective energy-cost relief for strategic sectors.
* Bureaucracy reduction present but secondary to transformation and social goals.
* Residual competition-law baseline and residual non-Net-Zero environmental standards continue.
* No high-priority residual green-architecture repeal or residual labour-market deregulation is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The SPD final set retains residual climate and green-industrial steering while adding targeted industrial-policy instruments and investment incentives. Residual Kündigungsschutz, residual co-determination and residual collective-bargaining institutions continue and are defended. Production and investment decisions in energy-intensive and strategic sectors remain shaped by residual state direction, subsidies and conditional relief. Residual competition rules continue. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of green-industrial and labour restrictions combined with targeted industrial-policy direction rather than deregulation.

### Regulation Score

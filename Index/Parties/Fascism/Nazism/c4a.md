# Cultural 4A: Fascism – Nazism
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 90**

### Overview
Nazism instituted a formal hereditary racial classification system that rigidly assigned legal capacity, occupational access, residence rights, and life trajectories on the basis of ancestry. The Nuremberg Laws and subsequent racial legislation created a closed status hierarchy in which individual achievement could not overcome assigned birth category for designated groups. Soft class mobility existed among those classified as Aryan, but the primary architecture was birth-locked assignment of destiny. This places Nazism in the near-absolute social determinism band.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Nuremberg Laws (1935) and follow-on racial legislation defining citizenship, marriage, and legal capacity by hereditary ancestry.
* Systematic exclusion of Jews and other designated groups from occupations, professions, education, and public life on the basis of birth/ancestry.
* Formal dual legal structure that made parentage the decisive gate for status and opportunity.
* Individual merit or conversion offered no legitimate pathway out of the assigned racial category.
* Residual intra-group mobility among those classified as Aryan remained secondary to the hereditary racial lock.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A measures the degree to which birth circumstances assign or constrain life outcomes. Nazism supplies one of the clearest modern cases of formal hereditary assignment. The racial laws did not merely correlate with outcomes; they made ancestry the legal determinant of capacity and pathway access. Designated groups faced near-total closure of legitimate mobility channels. The architecture matches the 90–94 band description: exact occupational and social aspects inherited by birth-related factors, with individual action producing virtually no change in trajectory for those locked out by ancestry. Soft statistical class effects among the favoured population do not offset the formal birth-assignment system that governed the decisive status boundaries.

Equity-style diagnostics of group outcomes belong on Cultural 4B and are not scored here. The determinism score rests solely on the observable institutional assignment of destiny by hereditary category.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 90
Formal hereditary racial classification that assigned legal capacity, occupation, and closed status with almost no residual legitimate exit for designated groups aligns with the 90–94 band (exact occupational and social aspects inherited by birth-related factors; individual action produces virtually no change). Score 90.

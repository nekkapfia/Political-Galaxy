# Cultural 2B: Fascism – Nazism
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 96**

### Overview
Nazism treated the German nation (defined in racial-historical terms) as the ultimate source of legitimacy. National action was celebrated simply because it was national; criticism was treated as betrayal. The score approaches the Glorification pole.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Education, media and ritual organised around the absolute superiority and destiny of the nation.
* Historical narrative presented the nation’s past and mission as near-perfect and sacred.
* Symbols, monuments and commemorations relentlessly celebrated national greatness.
* Any criticism of the nation or its leadership was treated as treason with severe consequences.
* Cultural and historical figures were fully nationalised and subordinated to the regime’s narrative of destiny.

### Expanded Analysis
Under Nazism the nation itself became the highest source of moral and political legitimacy. Achievements were celebrated as national; failures were externalised or denied. The regime saturates public life with affirmative national myth. This produces a score at the upper end of the Glorification band, short of the absolute 100 only because residual internal ideological contestation and the instrumental use of history prevent total perfection claims in every domain.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 96
Nation as ultimate legitimacy, zero tolerance for criticism, and relentless celebratory narrative place Nazism in the Glorification band.

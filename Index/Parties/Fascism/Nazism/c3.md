# Cultural 3: Fascism – Nazism
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 94 | 3B 82**

### Overview
Nazism organised full membership of the core political community through strict ancestral and blood rules. The Nuremberg Laws and subsequent racial legislation created a formal dual structure in which non-group ancestry barred or heavily burdened full membership. Cultural-political transformation was also extreme: trusted membership required deep immersion in Nazi ideology, loyalty demonstration, and severance from prior political, religious, and associational identities. Immigration volume and deportation of non-members remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Reich Citizenship Law and Law for the Protection of German Blood and German Honour (Nuremberg Laws, 1935): defined membership by ancestry (three or four Jewish grandparents = full Jew); Reichsbürger status reserved for those of “German or related blood”.
* Systematic exclusion of Jews (and later Roma and other designated groups) from citizenship, marriage, residence, occupation, and association on pure ancestral grounds.
* Near one-drop logic in practice for full membership of the core in-group.
* Compulsory Hitler Youth, Gleichschaltung of culture and education, and mandatory political-cultural conformity for those not ethnically excluded.
* Loyalty tests, public ritual, and ideological immersion required for trusted status within the Volksgemeinschaft.
* Immigration and deportation policy out of scope for this axis.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is met decisively. Certain ethnicities were permanently barred from full membership solely on the basis of ancestry. This places Nazism in the 90–100 One-Drop / near One-Drop Exclusion band. The 3A score of 94 reflects explicit ancestral purity rules that operated as the primary gatekeeper of membership.

For those not ethnically excluded, 3B remains very high. Full trusted membership demanded dense cultural-political transformation: acceptance of the racial worldview, Führerprinzip, and the approved way of life, together with severance from competing identities. This aligns with High Cultural Demand (80–89). The combination produces one of the highest paired scores on the axis.

### Why this produces 3A 94 / 3B 82
3A aligns with 90–100 (near one-drop ancestral exclusion under Nuremberg racial law). 3B aligns with 80–89 (high cultural-political transformation and loyalty demands for trusted membership).

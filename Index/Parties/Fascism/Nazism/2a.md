### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements

* Explicit repudiation of the Versailles system and all external legal constraints associated with it.
* Withdrawal from the League of Nations and rejection of international judicial or political authority.
* Exclusive national control of borders, population policy and internal security.
* Military and diplomatic action treated as unilateral national prerogatives.

# Cultural 5B: Fascism – Nazism
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 90**

### Overview

Nazism executed one of the most rapid and comprehensive institutional captures in modern European history. The Enabling Act, Gleichschaltung, destruction of independent parties, unions and civil organisations, and construction of the camp system were carried through on compressed timelines with systematic override of deliberative and legal constraints. The early regime period in particular saturates the high-radicalism band.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Enabling Act (March 1933) and Reichstag Fire Decree enabling rule by decree and rapid suspension of core civil and political constraints.
* Gleichschaltung (1933–34): systematic, compressed destruction or absorption of independent parties, trade unions, press and civil-society organisations.
* Rapid construction and expansion of the concentration-camp system for political opponents and expanding categories of designated enemies.
* Legislative and administrative tempo that prioritised immediate institutional monopoly over extended negotiation or pilot processes.
* Education, media and organisational life reoriented toward urgency of national transformation and elimination of intervening structures.
* Observable pattern in the foundational years shows near-constant high-volume transformative action with minimal intervals for deliberation.

### Expanded Analysis

Nazism treated rapid, total institutional transformation as the required mode in its foundational phase. Deliberative and pluralist processes were not merely slowed; they were eliminated as legitimate constraints. Later wartime mobilisation extended controls further. The score of 90 reflects this sustained high-intensity design against delay, placing Nazism at the top of the historical radicalism range short of the absolute theoretical maximum.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 90

Compressed Gleichschaltung, decree-based rule, and systematic destruction of intervening institutions with near-zero tolerance for deliberative sequencing place Nazism at 90.

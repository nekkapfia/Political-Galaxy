# Cultural 1A: Fascism – Nazism
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 44 (6M)

### Overview
Nazism organises moral legitimacy around both the survival and cultural existence of the German people and higher-law claims of racial Nature and Creator’s will.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Transcendent Justifications
* The struggle between races is a law of Nature; the stronger must dominate and not mix with the weaker.
* Allowing racial mixing or the continued presence of the racial threat is a sin against the will of the Creator / against the aristocratic principle of Nature.
* The German / Aryan race is elevated to an Ideal whose purity and survival are treated as non-negotiable higher goods.

#### Mundane Justifications
* The German / Aryan race is the culture-creating race; its preservation is the precondition for high culture and for the continued existence of the people.
* Racial mixing produces physical, intellectual and cultural decline and threatens the survival of the higher race.
* The purity of German blood is a prerequisite for the existence of the German people and the Reich.
* The Jewish race is a parasitic, destructive anti-race whose influence threatens the biological survival, racial purity and cultural existence of the German people.

### Expanded Analysis
Racial struggle as law of Nature, sin against the will of the Creator / aristocratic principle of Nature, and elevation of the Aryan race to an Ideal whose purity is non-negotiable are Transcendent higher-law claims. Culture-creating capacity, survival and cultural existence of the German people, and the framing of racial mixing and the Jewish presence as threats to that survival are Mundane claims that organise the operational justification for preservation. Membership-by-blood rules belong on Cultural 3A/3B (exclusivity), not Foundation. Absolute authority and “word above law” language belong on Cultural 1B (Dogmatism), not Foundation. On C1A both Transcendent Nature/Creator/Ideal claims and Mundane survival/group-capacity claims are present. Operational primacy lies with Mundane survival language, reinforced by secondary Transcendent Nature/Creator and Ideal claims. The foundation is mixed.

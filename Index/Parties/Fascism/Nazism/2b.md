# Core 2B: Fascism – Nazism
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 10**

### Overview
Nazism conducted aggressive territorial expansion, large-scale military occupation, and systematic political, racial and administrative restructuring of other states. Interference in other states’ internal affairs was a core operating mode, not an exception. Residual baseline of expansion and control is total for the period of power.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Annexation of Austria, the Sudetenland and further territories.
* Military occupation regimes across large parts of Europe with direct political control.
* Installation or enforcement of aligned or puppet administrations.
* Ideological and racial restructuring programmes applied to occupied populations and institutions.
* Residual baseline of aggressive expansion and occupation as normal instruments of state policy; no residual non-interference posture during the wartime peak.
* Self-defence claims do not convert aggressive expansion into non-interference.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other nations’ internal arrangements. Nazi expansion and occupation produced sustained, large-scale control over foreign governments, societies and legal orders. Multilateral or alliance cover does not convert this pattern into non-intervention. The residual posture is one of systematic external control; the score is among the lowest recorded because occupation and forced political alignment were normal instruments of state policy.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 10
Aggressive expansion, occupation, systematic political control of other states, and the absence of any residual non-interference baseline place Nazism at the bottom of the observable range.

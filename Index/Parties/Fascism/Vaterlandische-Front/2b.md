# Core 2B: Fascism – Vaterlandische Front
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 64**

### Overview
The Vaterländische Front and the Austrofascist regime under Dollfuss and Schuschnigg were primarily defensive and domestic. The project focused on consolidating an authoritarian Catholic state against both National Socialism and socialism, with minimal external aggression or occupation programmes.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Authoritarian domestic consolidation and suppression of internal opposition.
* Defensive posture toward Nazi Germany; resistance to Anschluss until 1938.
* No programme of external conquest or systematic interference in other states’ internal orders.
* Alignment with Italian interests was tactical and limited in external projection.
* Self-defence of Austrian territory is out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other nations’ internal affairs. Austrofascism’s energy was directed inward and toward regime survival against a more powerful German neighbour. The absence of expansionist or occupation policy raises the score substantially above Italian and German fascism. Residual authoritarian foreign-policy tools keep it out of the highest non-intervention bands.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 64
Primarily defensive and domestic orientation with minimal external interference places the Vaterländische Front in the mid-to-high band.

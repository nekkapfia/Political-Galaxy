# Cultural 4A: Fascism – Vaterlandische Front
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 44**

### Overview
The Austrofascist Vaterländische Front combined corporatist organisation with traditional status elements and political-loyalty filters. Formal mobility channels remained relatively open; hereditary assignment was limited compared with the radical racial regimes. Origin and regime loyalty created differential ease rather than total closure. The system sits in the blocked-access / residual-constraint range.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Corporatist and traditional status structures that preserved certain inherited advantages.
* Political loyalty to the Front and regime as a gate on advancement.
* Formal educational and occupational pathways that remained open relative to totalising hereditary systems.
* Residual class and family advantages in elite pathways.
* Absence of comprehensive racial or multi-generational political classification covering the whole population.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth and inherited position as constraints. Austrofascism retained traditional status residues and used political loyalty as a filter, but it did not institutionalise locked hereditary occupational assignment. Formal mobility continued. The pattern fits the 41–50 band: access to better paths required effort and was shaped by background and loyalty, yet success remained possible and residual birth stigma after achievement was limited. Soft statistical correlation without institutional gates would score lower; the combination of traditional status and political filters supplies the moderate determinism.

Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 44
Traditional status residues and political-loyalty gates, combined with relatively open formal mobility, align with the 41–50 band. Score 44.

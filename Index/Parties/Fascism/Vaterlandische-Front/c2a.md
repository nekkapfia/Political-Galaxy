# Cultural 2A: Fascism – Vaterländische Front
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 38**

### Overview
The Vaterländische Front subordinated individual status to national, corporatist and hierarchical categories while retaining more residual individual and professional ranking than the most radical fascist formations.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* National and corporatist categories structuring political and institutional status.
* Hierarchical and professional differentiation continuing inside the approved frame.
* Independent civil association restricted or absorbed into corporatist structures.
* Individual performance and rank metrics operating inside national-corporatist constraints.
* Less totalising than high-Stalinist or radical legionary models.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant allocation principle is national-corporatist rather than pure individual-meritocratic. Individual ranking by profession and hierarchy remained more substantial than in the most radical fascist cases, but the person as sovereign agent outside the national-corporatist frame was secondary. The pattern is strong group primacy with comparatively greater residual individuation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 38
National-corporatist categories as primary organising units, with relatively substantial residual individual ranking inside the frame, place the Vaterländische Front at the upper edge of the strong group-primacy band. Score 38.

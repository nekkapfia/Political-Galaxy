### Overview
The Vaterländische Front organised Austrian identity around a Catholic-authoritarian national affirmation. National pride was the institutional default; systematic shame frameworks directed at the Austrian nation were rejected. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

* Affirmative national and Catholic historical narrative.
* Symbols and education oriented to Austrian continuity, independence and Christian identity.
* Criticism of the national project constrained by the authoritarian structure.
* Cultural figures and history nationalised within the Ständestaat frame.
* Public ritual and education treated the Austrian nation as a legitimate and positive source of meaning.

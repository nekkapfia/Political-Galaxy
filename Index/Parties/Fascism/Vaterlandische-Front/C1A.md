# Cultural 1A: Fascism – Vaterländische Front
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 82 (32T)
### Overview
The Fatherland Front organises moral legitimacy around Catholic and corporate-state ideology as the authentic moral form of the Austrian community, with competence and material order cited inside that frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Catholic and corporate-state ideology is presented as the authentic moral form of the Austrian community.
* Defence of Christian Austrian identity against liberal and Marxist alternatives is framed as a duty.
* Authoritarian leadership and the Fatherland Front monopoly are described as guardians of the national-Catholic order.

#### Mundane Justifications
* Administrative and economic competence are cited as goods provided under the national-Catholic order.
* Material order and state stability are presented as evidence of the correctness of that order.

### Expanded Analysis
Catholic and corporate-state ideology as the authentic moral form of the Austrian community, defence of Christian Austrian identity as duty, and authoritarian guardianship of correct order organise Transcendent claims with confessional character. Administrative competence, material order and state stability are cited as evidence inside that frame. The confessional-national order organises legitimacy; Mundane competence language is present but secondary. The foundation is Transcendent-organising.

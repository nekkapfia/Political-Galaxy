## Regulation

### Overview
The Iron Guard programme centred on Romanianisation — forced ethnic-national reallocation of commercial and industrial capital — combined with hostility to independent large-scale private capital, state direction, and subordination of economic activity to nationalist political goals.

### Relevant Policies
- Romanianisation as forced transfer of property and businesses from designated groups to the “nation” (confiscatory, not ordinary regulation)
- Hostility to independent large-scale private and foreign capital
- Subordination of economic activity to national-political goals
- Suppression of independent economic organisation of designated groups
- Corporatist and command elements in the proposed economic order
- Residual private activity only under tight political and ethnic-national constraints

### Expanded Analysis
Romanianisation is a confiscatory property-transfer programme, not a tax or ordinary regulatory measure. Independent capital associated with targeted groups was to be eliminated or transferred. State direction and nationalist priority rules would constrain remaining private decision-making. Relative to lower bands: the programme does not abolish money or all private title, but the combination of systematic ethnic-national confiscation and state direction places it in the more severe portion of extensive political control of capital and production.

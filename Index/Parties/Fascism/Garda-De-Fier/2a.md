# Core 2A: Fascism – Garda de Fier
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 77**
**Status: FULLY LOCKED**

### Overview
The Iron Guard demanded full Romanian national independence, rejected Western and Soviet institutional influence and treated foreign capital and minorities as sovereignty risks.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Extreme emphasis on national purity, territorial integrity and independence from foreign political and economic control.
* Hostility to both Western liberal and Soviet institutional influence.
* Support for a strong national state with exclusive internal authority.
* Rejection of external minorities and foreign capital as sovereignty risks.
* Maintained exclusive national authority and rejected pooling of sovereignty with international organisations.

### Expanded Analysis
The Iron Guard’s programme treated Romanian national independence as incompatible with both Western liberal institutional frameworks and Soviet-aligned structures. Foreign capital and minority protections associated with the interwar order were framed as instruments of external leverage rather than neutral legal arrangements. The movement sought a state whose internal authority was exclusive and whose external posture rejected pooling of decision-making with international organisations. On Core 2A the pattern is radical nationalist closure: maximisation of formal independence through elimination of the institutional channels by which external actors could constrain domestic policy.

Radical nationalist maximisation of independence and elimination of external institutional leverage produce a score of 77.

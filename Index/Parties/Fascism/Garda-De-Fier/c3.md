# Cultural 3: Fascism – Garda de Fier (Iron Guard)
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 65 | 3B 80**

### Overview
The Iron Guard (Legion of the Archangel Michael) defined Romanian membership through strong ethno-religious nationalism in which Jews and other designated outsiders were treated as permanently outside the core nation. Ancestral Romanian identity was heavily privileged. Cultural-political demands were extreme: the Legionary “new man”, Orthodox mysticism, loyalty, and martyrdom culture required deep transformation and severance from prior liberal or left identities. Immigration and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Ethno-religious Romanian nationalism with central antisemitism; Jews treated as structurally outside the national community.
* Ancestral Romanian identity as the preferred and largely exclusive basis of full belonging.
* Legionary ideology demanded total cultural-political and religious transformation, loyalty, and rejection of competing identities.
* No formal Nuremberg-style blood statute, but clear ethnic exclusionary practice and doctrine.
* Immigration and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is met through practice and doctrine: certain ethnicities faced permanent barriers to full membership on ethno-religious grounds. The absence of a fully codified one-drop law keeps 3A below the Nazi ceiling, placing it in the Core Ancestry Preference to low Dominant Ancestry range (65).

3B is very high. Trusted membership required immersion in Legionary culture, Orthodox-national mysticism, and demonstrable severance from prior political and cultural identities. This aligns with High Cultural Demand (80–89).

### Why this produces 3A 65 / 3B 80
3A aligns with 60–69 (core ancestry preference and ethnic exclusionary practice). 3B aligns with 80–89 (high cultural-political and religious transformation demands).

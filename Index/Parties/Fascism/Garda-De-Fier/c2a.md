# Cultural 2A: Fascism – Garda de Fier
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview
The Iron Guard subordinated individual status to spiritual-national and legionary categories, with intense collective discipline reducing residual individual ranking relative to other fascist formations.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Legionary and spiritual-national categories as primary units of status and loyalty.
* Intense collective discipline and hierarchical obedience structuring opportunity and recognition.
* Individual differentiation present but tightly bounded by legionary category.
* Independent individual status outside the movement systematically rejected.
* Mystical-national rather than progressive identity logic.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant unit of measure is the legionary-national category rather than the sovereign individual. Individual agency is real only inside intense collective constraints. Relative to more bureaucratic fascist formations, residual intra-frame ranking is weaker. The pattern is very strong group subordination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28
Legionary-national category as primary organising unit with tightly bounded individual differentiation places the Iron Guard in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 28.

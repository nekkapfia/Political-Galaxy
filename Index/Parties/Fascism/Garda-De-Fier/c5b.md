# Cultural 5B: Fascism – Garda de Fier
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 84**

### Overview

The Iron Guard (Garda de Fier) pursued high-intensity, disruptive transformation in its periods of influence and short governing participation. Its style emphasised rapid purification, violent rupture with existing institutional layers, and rejection of gradualist or parliamentary sequencing. The score reflects that intense, short-duration radicalism.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Organisational practice and rhetoric oriented toward immediate, total moral and institutional purification rather than sequenced reform.
* Short periods of governmental participation marked by high-intensity, disruptive actions against existing political and administrative structures.
* Rejection of liberal deliberative and parliamentary tempo as illegitimate delay.
* Use of paramilitary and extra-institutional methods that compressed the interval between decision and enforcement.
* Observable pattern is one of acute, high-radicalism episodes rather than decades-long sustained campaign tempo.

### Expanded Analysis

The Iron Guard’s radicalism is concentrated in intense bursts rather than continuous multi-decade acceleration of the Stalinist or Maoist type. Within those bursts, tolerance for deliberative process was extremely low. The score of 84 places it in the high band, recognising the intensity while distinguishing it from longer-sustained total-transformation systems.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 84

High-intensity, disruptive purification and institutional rupture with near-zero tolerance for deliberative sequencing in its periods of action place the Iron Guard at 84.

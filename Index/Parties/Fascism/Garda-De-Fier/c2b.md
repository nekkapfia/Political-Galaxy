# Cultural 2B: Fascism – Garda de Fier
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 90**

### Overview
The Iron Guard organised Romanian identity around mystical national and religious continuity. National pride, fused with Orthodoxy, was the central organising principle of education, ritual and political mobilisation; systematic shame frameworks were entirely rejected. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Education and ritual celebrated the Romanian nation as sacred, continuous and divinely favoured.
* Symbols and commemorations affirmed national-religious destiny and martyrdom.
* Criticism of the national-religious project was treated as betrayal.
* Cultural and historical narrative subordinated to national-mystical affirmation.
* Public life saturated with affirmative national and religious myth.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
The Iron Guard’s cultural stance saturates public life with affirmative national-religious myth. The nation is treated as a sacred community whose continuity and destiny stand above ordinary political critique. This produces a score at the Glorification threshold: national pride is not merely the default but is sacralised.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 90
Nation treated as sacred and central, with no institutional space for organising shame or critical national narrative, places the Iron Guard at the Glorification boundary.

# Core 1A: Fascism – Garda de Fier
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 18**
**Xenos Score: 8**

### Overview

Garda de Fier (Iron Guard / Legion of the Archangel Michael) during the National Legionary State (September 1940–January 1941) produces a severely restrictive Core 1A profile defined by violent suppression of political opponents, systematic anti-Semitic violence and the Bucharest pogrom, Legionary police operating with impunity, and attempts to monopolise press and associational life. Duration is irrelevant under scoring rules. The final set aligns Endos with extreme restrictions on voluntary political action and Xenos with extreme low autonomy under lethal group-targeted violence. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Violent suppression of political opponents, including assassinations and persecution of officials associated with the prior royal regime.
* Systematic anti-Semitic violence, forced expropriation of persons, and the Bucharest pogrom of January 1941 — organised mass killing and torture of Jews as a designated group.
* Legionary police and parallel power structures operating with substantial impunity against designated enemies.
* Attempts to impose Legionary control over the press, cultural institutions and public associational life.
* Ideology treating Jews and political enemies as existential threats and legitimating lethal violence against them.
* Incomplete consolidation of a stable one-party institutional monopoly before the January 1941 clash with Antonescu.
* Residual ordinary state structures continued alongside Legionary paramilitary violence.
* Ordinary violent-crime prohibitions did not protect designated political or racial targets.

### Expanded Analysis

The Iron Guard final set is high-intensity political and racial violence. Organised opposition speech and association were met with intimidation and murder. Jews faced organised violence, expropriation and pogrom killing. Legionary police extended coercion beyond ordinary legal process.

For Endos — the core population that accommodated or supported the Legionary order — public political space was narrowed by intimidation and parallel police power. A completed speech/association monopoly and camp system of the Nazi type was not institutionalised, but extreme restriction of voluntary political action and association was already present. This aligns with the **10–18** band (severe systematic restrictions on voluntary action and association). Score **18**.

For Xenos — Jews and political enemies of the prior regime — assassination, pogrom violence, torture and expropriation were policy instruments. Control extended to most voluntary aspects of life for those targeted, with lethal outcomes. This aligns with the **7–9** floor band (extreme low autonomy). Score **8**. Systematic extermination as completed genocide architecture (band 5) is not assigned to this short Legionary window alone; the intensity for Xenos remains floor-adjacent.

Framing as national purification or spiritual revolution is discarded. Duration does not raise the score. Only the observable restriction set is scored.

### Why this produces Endos 18 / Xenos 8

Endos aligns with **10–18** (extreme restrictions on voluntary political action, association and security under Legionary police and assassination politics) → **18**. Xenos aligns with **7–9** (extreme low autonomy under organised anti-Semitic pogrom violence, torture and expropriation) → **8**.

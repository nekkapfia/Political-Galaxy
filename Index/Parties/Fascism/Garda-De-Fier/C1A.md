# Cultural 1A: Fascism – Garda de Fier (Iron Guard)
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 86 (36T)
### Overview
The Iron Guard organises moral legitimacy around Orthodox Christian and mystical national resurrection, with legionary sacrifice and purity treated as spiritual requirements.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Orthodox Christian and mystical national resurrection are presented as the binding spiritual-political order of the nation.
* Legionary sacrifice, martyrdom and purity are framed as moral requirements above pragmatic politics.
* Liberal, Jewish and materialist influences are characterised as spiritual contamination.
* The authoritarian national community under legionary ethics is described as the proper form of political life.
* Violence and purification are treated as spiritual necessities.

#### Mundane Justifications
* Material and administrative concerns appear only as subordinate to spiritual-national purity.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodox Christian and mystical national resurrection, legionary sacrifice and purity, and spiritual contamination language organise Transcendent claims with religious-bent character. Violence and purification are treated as spiritual necessities. Material and administrative concerns are subordinated to spiritual-national purity. The spiritual-national order organises legitimacy; Mundane material language is minimal. The foundation is Transcendent-organising.

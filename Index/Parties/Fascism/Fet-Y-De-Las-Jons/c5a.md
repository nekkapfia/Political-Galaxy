# Cultural 5A: Fascism – FET y de las JONS
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 69**

### Overview
FET y de las JONS reversed the Second Republic's secular-liberal reforms while restoring Catholic authority over education, marriage and public morality under authoritarian state control.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Reversed Second Republic secularisation of education and marriage; reimposed Catholic institutional roles in schooling, marriage law and public morality under National Catholicism.
* Repealed or suppressed Republican progressive family and gender reforms; mandated conventional marriage forms and gender roles in law and public policy.
* Reorganised education curricula and public ritual around Spanish historical narrative, imperial memory and Catholic identity as authorised content.
* Banned, censored or placed under party-state control liberal, socialist and anti-clerical cultural organisations and media.
* Replaced Republican constitutional order with authoritarian single-party rule; rebuilt Church–state partnership after Republican disestablishment.
* Conducted state administrative modernisation under the reimposed Catholic social hierarchy rather than under Republican secular-liberal frameworks.
* Residual progressive-Republican settlement was dismantled in primary domains; confessional and conventional family transmission became the operative public standard under authoritarian control.

### Expanded Analysis
FET dismantled the Second Republic's secular-liberal settlement across education, marriage and public morality, then rebuilt Catholic institutional authority in those same domains under single-party rule. Republican progressive family and gender reforms were repealed; authorised curricula and ritual carried Spanish historical and Catholic content; liberal and anti-clerical organisations were removed from public space. Church–state partnership was reconstructed after Republican disestablishment. Domain hierarchy places education content and family-status design first: Catholic educational, familial and moral authority was reinstalled as the operative public arrangement. Administrative modernisation continued under the reimposed clerical social hierarchy, not under secular-liberal frameworks. Authoritarian control structured the restoration; it did not convert confessional and conventional family transmission into mere instruments of a secular party ideology. The residual Republican progressive order was reversed, not conserved.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 69
Concrete reversal of Republican secularisation and reimposition of Catholic roles in education, marriage and public morality place FET in the mid-high band (66–79). Traditional transmission became the operative public standard in primary domains under authoritarian control. Low-band placement would require treating confessional restoration as regime-invented substitution; the locked analysis shows reinstallation of Church and conventional family as institutional baselines, not their extinction.

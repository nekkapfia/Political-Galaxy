# Cultural 1A: Fascism – FET y de las JONS
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 82 (32T)
### Overview
FET organises moral legitimacy around Catholic social order as the authentic moral form of the Spanish community, with competence and material order cited inside that confessional-national frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Catholic social and political order is presented as the authentic moral form of the Spanish community.
* The crusade against communism, atheism and liberal separatism is framed as a historical and moral duty.
* National unity and traditional identity are treated as requirements against regional and class fragmentation.

#### Mundane Justifications
* Administrative and economic competence are cited as goods the regime provides under the national-Catholic order.
* Material order and state strength are presented as evidence of the correctness of that order.

### Expanded Analysis
Catholic social and political order as the authentic moral form of the Spanish community, the crusade against communism and atheism as duty, and national-Catholic unity as requirement organise Transcendent claims with religious-bent character. Administrative competence, material order and state strength are cited as evidence inside that frame. The religious-national order organises legitimacy; competence and material order support it but do not supply an independent Mundane foundation that can override it. The foundation is Transcendent-organising with Mundane competence language present but secondary.

# Core 1A: Fascism – FET y de las JONS
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 22**
**Xenos Score: 12**

### Overview

FET y de las JONS as the governing political force of early Francoism (Nationalist victory through the mid-1940s) produces a severely restrictive Core 1A profile defined by one-party monopoly, comprehensive press control, systematic repression of the defeated Republican side through tribunals, imprisonment, executions and forced labour, restrictions on regional-language expression, and confessional controls on education and public associational life. Cumulative weight sits in the extreme-restriction / upper ideological-enforcement border. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Single-party system under FET y de las JONS; suppression of all independent political parties and free trade unions as independent associational channels.
* Comprehensive press censorship and state control of public information; no legal independent political media.
* Systematic repression of the defeated Republican population through military tribunals, imprisonment and executions.
* Law of Political Responsibilities and related measures imposing political disability, exclusion and ongoing sanction on designated opponents.
* Forced labour battalions and compulsory labour imposed on political prisoners.
* Severe restrictions on public use of regional languages and on autonomist political expression (especially Catalan and Basque).
* Confessional controls on education, marriage and public morality that constrained personal and associational conduct outside Party-state orthodoxy.
* Parallel political-repressive jurisdiction alongside ordinary criminal law for non-political matters.
* No biological-racial extermination programme of the National Socialist type.

### Expanded Analysis

Early Francoism eliminated political pluralism and imposed a durable repressive apparatus on the defeated side of the Civil War. Independent political speech, association and press were closed. Tribunals, prisons and forced labour extended coercion well beyond the fighting. Regional-language and autonomist expression faced systematic restriction. Confessional rules added further constraints on education and public personal conduct.

For Endos — the core population that accepted or accommodated the Nationalist order — public political speech outside the regime was closed; association was channelled through official structures; residual private and religious life continued under political and moral conditions. Placement at 22 reflects severe systematic restriction short of genocidal intensity.

For Xenos — the defeated Republican population, leftists, regional nationalists and other designated opponents — imprisonment, forced labour, execution in the early years, and long-term political disability were routine. That differential pulls Xenos to 12.

Framing as national reconstruction or anti-communism is discarded. Only the observable restriction architecture is scored. Later moderation after the mid-1940s is outside this scoring window.

### Why this produces Endos 22 / Xenos 12

One-party monopoly, press control, political tribunals, forced labour and restrictions on regional and opposition expression place Endos at 22. Systematic, long-term coercion of the defeated and designated opponents produces Xenos 12.

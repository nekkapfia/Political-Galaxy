# Core 2B: Fascism – Fet Y De Las Jons
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 46**

### Overview
The Franco regime after the Civil War was relatively non-expansionist externally. Spain remained non-belligerent in the Second World War as a combatant and focused on regime survival and domestic consolidation. External interference was limited compared with Italian or German fascism.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Victory in the Spanish Civil War with Axis assistance; subsequent focus on internal control.
* Non-belligerence in the Second World War; no major overseas occupation programme.
* Limited external projection after consolidation of power.
* Colonial holdings retained but not expanded through new large-scale conquest in the fascist period.
* Self-defence of Spanish territory is out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other nations’ internal affairs. Francoist Spain after 1939 prioritised regime survival over external transformation of other states. The absence of a sustained expansionist or occupation programme raises the score relative to Nazism and Italian Fascism, while residual authoritarian foreign-policy tools and earlier Civil War internationalisation prevent a very high placement.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 46
Post-Civil-War non-expansionism and limited external projection place FET y de las JONS in the mid band.

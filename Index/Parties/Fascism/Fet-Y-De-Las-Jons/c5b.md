# Cultural 5B: Fascism – FET y de las JONS
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 70**

### Overview

FET y de las JONS and the early Francoist regime combined a radical, high-intensity foundational phase (Civil War mobilisation, rapid suppression of opposition, construction of the single-party framework) with a long subsequent period of authoritarian stabilisation and gradual institutionalisation. The net score reflects that dual character.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Civil War and immediate post-war years: compressed, high-intensity suppression of defeated forces and construction of new authoritarian structures.
* Rapid creation of the single-party framework and centralised controls in the foundational period.
* Later Francoist decades characterised by greater administrative stability, slower policy change, and reduced campaign-style disruption.
* Observable pattern is therefore an early high-radicalism peak followed by extended gradualist-authoritarian governance.
* Education and official culture retained transformational rhetoric while practical tempo slowed.

### Expanded Analysis

Scoring the whole historical trajectory rather than only the Civil War peak produces a mid-high result. The early phase alone would sit higher; the long stabilisation phase pulls the overall placement down to 70. This distinguishes FET/Francoism from regimes that maintained continuous high-intensity transformation for decades.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 70

Early high-intensity institutional rupture followed by prolonged authoritarian stabilisation produces a net score of 70.

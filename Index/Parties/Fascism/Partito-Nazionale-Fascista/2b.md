# Core 2B: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 24**

### Overview
Italian Fascism under the PNF conducted colonial conquest (Ethiopia), annexation (Albania), intervention in the Spanish Civil War, and later participation in Axis occupation policies. The pattern is clearly expansionist and interventionist. Residual baseline of imperial and ideological external projection remains active throughout the period of power.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Invasion and occupation of Ethiopia (1935–36) with subsequent colonial administration.
* Annexation of Albania (1939).
* Military and political intervention in the Spanish Civil War.
* Later Axis-aligned occupation and political control activities.
* Residual baseline of imperial expansion and ideological-military intervention as legitimate state instruments.
* Self-defence of Italian territory is out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures interference in other states’ internal affairs. PNF foreign policy combined colonial conquest, annexation and ideological-military intervention. These actions restructure or subordinate foreign political orders. The residual posture is expansionist rather than restrained. The score sits low, though above Nazism because the geographic and demographic scale of control was smaller.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 24
Colonial conquest, annexation, external military-political intervention, and a residual baseline of imperial projection place Italian Fascism in the low 20s.

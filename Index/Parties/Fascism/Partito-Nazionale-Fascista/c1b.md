### Overview
Italian Fascism under the PNF imposed Party and leader monopoly, suppressed organised internal alternatives, and rejected liberal and Marxist frameworks as anti-national. Corporatist and state structures enforced the line. Both aspects are very high, producing a Strong-band score below the most eliminationist extremes.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Party and Duce primacy; suppression of independent parties and unions.
* OVRA and political controls against organised dissent.
* Education and youth organisations forming the fascist citizen.
* Liberal parliamentarism and Marxism framed as anti-national errors.
* Corporate state as exclusive legitimate organisational form.
* Residual baseline of authoritarian monopoly that left limited residual space for technical administration under political orthodoxy.

# Cultural 2A: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 34**

### Overview
The PNF subordinated individual status to party, national and hierarchical categories while retaining residual individual ranking by performance and rank inside the approved frame.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Party membership and political reliability as significant criteria for advancement and trusted roles.
* National and hierarchical categories organising status and recognition.
* Individual performance and rank differentiation continuing inside the approved frame.
* Independent civil association suppressed or absorbed into party structures.
* Not a multi-axis progressive identity system; category logic is national-party-hierarchical.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant allocation principle is collective-national and party-hierarchical rather than pure individual-meritocratic. Individual ranking by performance remained real inside the frame, but the person as sovereign agent outside party and national categories was secondary. The pattern is strong group primacy with residual intra-frame individuation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 34
Party and national categories as primary organising units, with residual individual ranking inside the frame, place the PNF in the strong group-primacy band. Score 34.

# Cultural 1A: Partito-Nazionale-Fascista
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
Italian Fascism organises moral legitimacy around the State as ethical embodiment of national will, national destiny as political requirement, and performance and strength of the state. Absent Cosmic or religious sacralisation of the Ideal.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* The State is presented as the ethical embodiment of national will and the organising form of political life.
* National destiny, hierarchy and discipline are framed as political requirements of the fascist order.
* Parliamentary liberalism, class struggle and individualist rights are characterised as decadent within the same political frame.
* The power and performance of the state are cited as evidence of the correctness of the fascist order.
* Material recovery and national strength appear as proofs of success.
* Corporatism and mass mobilisation are treated as the formation of subjects for the political order.

### Expanded Analysis
The State as ethical embodiment of national will, national destiny as political requirement, performance, and national strength organise the moral conversation. These are elevated secular political claims without Cosmic, religious, or ontological sacralisation of the Ideal of the kind that clears the Transcendent threshold under the locked lists. No divine command, Cosmic law, or worship-level deification organises legitimacy. Intensity and closure belong on Dogmatism and related axes. Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications. The foundation is Mundane.

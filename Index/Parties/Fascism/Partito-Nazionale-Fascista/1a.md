# Core 1A: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista

**Endos Score: 24**

### Overview

The Partito Nazionale Fascista as governing political force produces a restrictive Core 1A profile defined by criminalisation of dissenting political speech, comprehensive press control, Fascist education and media systems promoting official ideology, OVRA political policing, confino and the Special Tribunal for opponents, and — from 1938 — Racial Laws expanding the Xenos set. Early consolidation is implementation of the same programme. The 1938 racial turn adds targets under the same style of legal disability; it does not create a separate Endos ideology. Scores are set only by band alignment for each group, not by ranking within Fascism. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Criminalisation and penalisation of dissenting political speech and organisation outside Fascist orthodoxy.
* Comprehensive press controls; independent political media eliminated; access to critical political information curtailed.
* Education and media systems used to promote official Fascist ideology and discourage or suppress alternatives (curriculum, youth organisations, state press).
* Sanctions for expressing views that deviate from required public orthodoxy.
* OVRA and political-police monitoring.
* Special Tribunal for the Defence of the State and confino (internal exile) applied to designated opponents — detention and severe restriction of voluntary movement and association for Xenos.
* From 1938, Racial Laws: Jews added as a descent-based Xenos category under marriage restrictions, exclusion from public employment and education, and limits on professional and associational life (same style of legal disability; wider target set).
* Ordinary criminal law continued for non-political matters.
* One-party constitutional form as organigram is out of scope; only personal speech, association, belief-related, and movement restrictions are scored.
* Corporative economic structure is out of scope for Core 1A.

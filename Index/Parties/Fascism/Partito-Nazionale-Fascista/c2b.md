# Cultural 2B: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 90**

### Overview
The PNF organised Italian public life around the glorification of the nation, its Roman past and its fascist destiny. National pride was the central emotional and cultural organising principle. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Education and media relentlessly celebrated national greatness, Roman continuity and fascist achievement.
* Symbols, monuments and rituals (including the cult of the Duce) affirmed the nation as the highest value.
* Criticism of the nation or the regime was treated as disloyalty.
* Cultural figures and history were nationalised and subordinated to the regime’s narrative.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
Italian Fascism produced a near-Glorification score. The nation was mythologised and institutionalised as the primary source of meaning and legitimacy. The score sits at the Glorification/Pride boundary.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 90
Systematic glorification of the nation and zero institutional space for shame-oriented narratives place the PNF at the Glorification threshold.

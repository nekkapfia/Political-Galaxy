## Regulation

### Overview
Italian Fascism organised the economy around corporatist direction, state holding companies in strategic sectors, price and wage controls in key periods, and suppression of independent unions, while formal private title remained widespread under administrative and political constraint.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Corporatist organisation of industry and labour under state oversight (Corporazioni; Charter of Labour)
* IRI and related state holdings — decisive state ownership/control in banking, steel, shipping, telecommunications and other strategic sectors after the early 1930s
* Compulsory membership in fascist syndicates; independent unions and collective bargaining abolished
* Autarky drives and import-substitution campaigns with foreign-exchange and investment controls
* Wage and price controls
* Production campaigns (e.g. Grain Battle) with administrative pressure on agricultural output
* Priority of regime political and military objectives in investment and production
* Formal private ownership retained outside the state-holding core, subject to corporate and regulatory direction

### Expanded Analysis
Italian Fascism organised the economy around corporatist direction, state holding companies in strategic sectors, price and wage controls in key periods, and suppression of independent unions, while formal private title remained widespread under administrative and political constraint. Corporatist or syndicalist instruments directed labour and association. Administrative controls limited firm-level choices in strategic sectors. Independent unions were suppressed. Where confiscation from designated groups was locked, it operated as a confiscatory mechanism rather than ordinary regulation. Residual private title and market activity continued under political constraint but did not restore independent decision rights in the commanding heights. The institutional pattern is extensive state and party direction organised around national-political objectives, short of total extinction of private title.

### Regulation Score
The locked regulation instruments produce a Regulation Score of 22.

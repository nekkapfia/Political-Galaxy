# Cultural 1A: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 82**

### Overview
Italian Fascism under the PNF grounded political legitimacy in the totalitarian state, national destiny, hierarchical order and the rejection of liberal individualism. Ideological and national transcendence organised; residual pragmatic state interest remained visible. The profile is strong transcendent lean.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* The State as ethical and totalitarian embodiment of national will, outranking liberal individual preference.
* National destiny, hierarchy and discipline as moral-political requirements.
* Rejection of parliamentary liberalism, class struggle and individualist rights as decadent.
* Leader and Party as interpreters of national purpose.
* Corporatism and mobilisation as formation of fascist subjects.
* Performance and power of the state as supporting evidence inside the ideological frame.

### Expanded Analysis
PNF Fascism organised justification through state-centred national transcendence. The liberal demos was explicitly secondary to the ethical state and national destiny; individual rights and parliamentary bargaining were decadent residues to be overcome. Corporatism and mass mobilisation aimed to form subjects of the national order rather than to aggregate private preference. Intensity remains below racial-mystical extremes because pragmatic state power and performance retained more visible supporting roles, yet the organising claim is clearly transcendent: the State and national destiny outrank ordinary popular sovereignty. The pattern is strong transcendent lean.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 82
State-national higher-order claims that subordinate liberal demos justification produce strong transcendent lean. The score is 82.

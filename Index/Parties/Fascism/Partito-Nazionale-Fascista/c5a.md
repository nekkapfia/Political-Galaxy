# Cultural 5A: Fascism – Partito Nazionale Fascista
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 37**

### Overview
Italian Fascism abolished liberal institutional arrangements while placing Catholic and Roman cultural forms under party supremacy rather than leaving them as independent authorities.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Dismantled liberal parliamentary institutions; substituted party monopoly and corporatist structures as the framework of public authority.
* Established Opera Nazionale Balilla and successor youth organisations as mandatory state transmission channels alongside or in place of prior plural civic education pathways.
* Restored Catholic Church legal standing in education and marriage through the Lateran Pacts under Fascist state supremacy; Church autonomy remained limited by the party-state.
* Imposed Roman imperial imagery and national-regeneration content in education and public ritual as authorised regime culture.
* Combined conventional family rhetoric with state mobilisation for demographic and political goals under party direction.
* Abolished the pre-Fascist liberal institutional order; Catholic institutional recovery occurred only within Fascist legal and political supremacy.
* Residual liberal order was abolished; traditional Catholic and Roman forms were retained under party supremacy, not as independent baselines.

### Expanded Analysis
Italian Fascism abolished liberal parliamentary institutions and substituted party monopoly, corporatist structures and mandatory youth organisations as the framework of public authority and cultural transmission. The Lateran Pacts restored Catholic legal standing in education and marriage only under Fascist supremacy; Church autonomy remained limited by the party-state. Roman imperial and national-regeneration content was imposed as authorised culture. Conventional family rhetoric coexisted with demographic mobilisation under party direction. Domain hierarchy places education and youth transmission first: operative channels were party and state organisations. The residual liberal institutional order was abolished; Catholic and Roman forms were retained under party supremacy rather than restored as independent traditional authorities. That is abolition of the liberal layer with instrumental retrieval of traditional content, not mid-high autonomous confessional restoration of the FET type.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 37
Abolition of liberal institutional order combined with Catholic and Roman forms retained only under party supremacy, and mandatory youth organisations as transmission channels, place Italian Fascism in the low band (31–40). Mid-high placement would require independent traditional authority as the operative public baseline; Lateran recovery under Fascist supremacy does not meet that test. Floor placement would ignore the real (limited) Catholic institutional recovery and conventional family policy under the regime.

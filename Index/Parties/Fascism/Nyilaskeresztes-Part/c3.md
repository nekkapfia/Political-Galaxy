# Cultural 3: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Párt (Arrow Cross)
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 78 | 3B 74**

### Overview
The Arrow Cross Party defined the Hungarian national community through explicit racial antisemitism and ethno-national criteria. Ancestral and racial barriers to full membership were strong, though not identical in legal form to the German one-drop model. Cultural-political demands were also high: trusted membership required immersion in the party’s Christian-national and racial ideology and rejection of liberal and left identities. Immigration and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit racial antisemitism and ethno-national definition of the Hungarian nation as the basis of political membership.
* Strong ancestral and racial criteria for full belonging; Jews and other designated groups treated as permanently outside the core community.
* Party ideology demanded loyalty, cultural-political conformity, and severance from competing liberal, socialist, or “foreign” identities.
* No formal one-drop statute on the Nuremberg scale, but practice and doctrine produced high ethnic exclusivity.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is met: certain ethnicities faced structural, permanent barriers to full membership on ancestral and racial grounds. This places Arrow Cross in the upper 70s (Dominant Ancestry Expectation to Strong Purity Preference). The 3A score of 78 reflects clear ethnic gatekeeping without reaching the absolute one-drop ceiling of Nazism.

3B is high because full trusted membership required dense ideological and cultural transformation. Partial or superficial alignment was insufficient. The pair is characteristic of radical ethno-national fascist movements that combined blood criteria with intense political-cultural demands.

### Why this produces 3A 78 / 3B 74
3A aligns with 70–79 (dominant ancestry / strong purity preference under explicit racial-national doctrine). 3B aligns with 70–79 (predominantly demanding cultural-political transformation and loyalty).

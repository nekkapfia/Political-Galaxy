# Core 2B: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Part
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 42**

### Overview
The Arrow Cross regime was short-lived, extreme in domestic terror, and operated largely under German occupation context. External projection was limited compared with German or Italian fascism. Placement reflects constrained external interference capacity rather than a sustained independent expansionist programme.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Brief period of power (1944–45) under wartime German dominance.
* Extreme internal violence; limited independent external military projection.
* Alignment with Axis war aims without a comparable independent occupation system.
* Self-defence and purely internal actions are out of scope or scored elsewhere.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B isolates external interference. Arrow Cross domestic crimes are severe but belong primarily to other axes. Independent capacity to restructure foreign internal orders was limited by duration, resources and German control of the wider theatre. The mid-range score records residual Axis alignment without equating the regime to full-scale expansionist systems.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 42
Short duration, limited independent external projection and secondary role within the German war system produce a mid-range score.

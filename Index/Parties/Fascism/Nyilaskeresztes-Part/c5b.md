# Cultural 5B: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Párt
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 86**

### Overview

The Arrow Cross Party, during its brief period of power in 1944–45, implemented rapid, extreme institutional and social measures with minimal deliberative constraint. The intensity of transformation attempted in a short window places it firmly in the high-radicalism band.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Seizure of power and immediate launch of extreme measures against designated populations and remaining institutional constraints.
* Compressed timelines for policy and enforcement actions under wartime and regime-collapse conditions.
* Systematic override of residual legal and administrative sequencing.
* Observable pattern is one of acute, totalising acceleration during the short governing period.
* Limited longer-term governing record; score reflects the intensity of the period of control.

### Expanded Analysis

Like other short-duration extreme regimes, the Arrow Cross demonstrates that high radicalism scores can be produced by intense, compressed transformation even without multi-decade continuity. The near-absence of deliberative process during its rule supports a score in the high 80s.

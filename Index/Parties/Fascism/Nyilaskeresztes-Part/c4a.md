# Cultural 4A: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Part
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview
The Arrow Cross regime combined radical racial-nationalist ideology with exclusionary practice that treated ancestry and national origin as decisive status markers. Designated groups faced severe closure of occupational and legal pathways on the basis of birth/ancestry. Residual mobility among the favoured national population existed, but the overall architecture produced heavy inter-group restriction characteristic of the predominantly deterministic range.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Racial and national-origin criteria that assigned differential legal capacity and occupational access.
* Systematic exclusion of Jews and other designated groups from status pathways on the basis of ancestry.
* Radical nationalist hierarchy that treated birth into the favoured national community as a primary gate.
* Residual intra-group mobility among those classified as within the national community.
* Limited formal channels for exit from designated enemy categories.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores formal and practical birth-constraint. The Arrow Cross programme and brief governing practice treated ancestry as a decisive gate for inclusion in the political and occupational community. This is stronger than soft class correlation and approaches the heavier hereditary-assignment regimes, though it did not fully institutionalise a multi-generational caste system of the Nuremberg or songbun type across the entire population. The pattern fits the 70–79 band: birth locked major status boundaries; intra-group mobility remained possible; crossing out of assigned enemy categories was exceptional. Soft residual variation among the favoured population does not offset the origin-based closure applied to designated groups.

Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72
Ancestry-based exclusion of designated groups from occupational and legal pathways, combined with residual mobility inside the favoured national community, aligns with the 70–79 band. Score 72.

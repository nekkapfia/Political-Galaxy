# Cultural 2B: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Párt
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 88**

### Overview
The Arrow Cross Party elevated Hungarian national identity and destiny as the organising cultural principle. National pride was central to its programme and mobilisation; systematic shame frameworks were rejected. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Affirmative national historical narrative and ritual practice.
* Symbols and education oriented to Hungarian national greatness and continuity.
* Criticism of the national project treated as disloyalty.
* Cultural figures and history nationalised within the party’s radical nationalist frame.
* Public mobilisation organised around national rebirth and destiny.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
Arrow Cross produces a high Pride-band score through consistent elevation of the Hungarian nation as the primary source of meaning and legitimacy. The party’s cultural stance leaves no institutional space for organising national shame.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 88
Clear default of national pride, sacralised national destiny, and rejection of organising shame place the Arrow Cross high in the Pride band.

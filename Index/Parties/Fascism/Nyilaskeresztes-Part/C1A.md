### Overview

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* The Hungarian national-racial community is presented as the political subject requiring purification and authoritarian leadership.
* Eliminationist anti-Semitism and the persecution of designated enemies are framed as historical and political necessities of the national order.
* Liberal democracy and Marxism are characterised as alien to the true national order.
* Social-nationalist economic claims are advanced for the national community.
* Material and social provision are cited as goods for the national community.

# Core 2A: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Párt
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 58**
**Status: FULLY LOCKED**

### Overview
The Arrow Cross combined radical Hungarian nationalist independence claims with wartime practical dependence on Germany during its brief period of power.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Radical nationalist programme emphasising Hungarian independence and ethnic control of the state.
* Rejection of liberal international institutions and minority-protection regimes associated with the interwar order.
* Wartime alignment with Germany that created practical dependence while preserving formal state structures.
* Exclusive internal control of remaining Hungarian territory and security forces during its brief period of power.
* Maintained exclusive national authority and rejected long-term pooling of sovereignty with external organisations.

### Expanded Analysis
The Arrow Cross combined radical Hungarian nationalist claims to independence and ethnic control of the state with rejection of interwar liberal and minority-protection regimes. During its brief period of power it maintained formal state structures while operating under severe wartime dependence on Germany. On Core 2A the pattern is high formal sovereignty ideology constrained in practice by late-war German dominance.

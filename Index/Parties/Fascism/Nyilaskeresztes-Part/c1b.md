# Cultural 1B: Fascism – Nyilaskeresztes Párt
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 90**

**Orthodoxy: 90, Tribalism: 91**

### Overview
The Arrow Cross combined radical national-social orthodoxy with rejection of liberal, conservative-elite and designated minority frameworks as enemies of Hungarian national rebirth. Both aspects are extreme in the wartime radical phase, producing a Strong-band score.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Movement discipline and wartime seizure closing organised internal opposition.
* Radical national-social line as exclusive legitimate programme.
* Violence against political opponents and designated communities.
* Liberal and elite-conservative frameworks treated as anti-national.
* Propaganda and paramilitary organisation enforcing conformity.
* Residual baseline of wartime radical monopoly that eliminated residual space for open contestation of the foundational line.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy and Tribalism are both extreme in the wartime radical phase. Movement discipline and seizure close organised internal opposition; the radical national-social line claims exclusive legitimacy; violence targets political opponents and designated communities; liberal and elite-conservative frameworks are anti-national. Propaganda and paramilitary organisation enforce conformity. Rival moral-political systems lose institutional standing during rule. The institutional pattern is extreme dogmatism at wartime intensity, calibrated in the Strong (86–95) band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 90
Wartime movement monopoly, exclusive national-social line, violence against opponents and designated communities, and systematic anti-national framing of liberal and elite frameworks produce extreme Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 90.

## Regulation

### Overview
The Democratic Unionist Party produces a near-continuity Core 1B Regulation profile. It retains ordinary UK labour protections, residual green/net-zero architecture, residual competition rules and Northern Ireland-specific institutional constraints, with only limited pragmatic industrial and energy support added.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention of ordinary unfair-dismissal, redundancy, national minimum wage, working-time and health-and-safety protections; no high-priority labour-market deregulation is locked.
* Retention of residual Climate Change Act architecture and major delivery instruments (carbon budgets, residual CfD, residual capacity market, residual low-carbon heat and vehicle mandates); no high-priority full repeal is locked.
* Pragmatic support for domestic energy production and industrial capacity in Northern Ireland and UK contexts.
* Retention of residual competition-law baseline (CMA enforcement, merger control).
* Limited planning change with Northern Ireland-specific residual constraints continuing.
* Residual secondary regulatory and bureaucratic baseline continues; no broad bureaucracy-reduction programme is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The DUP final set leaves the residual UK labour-protection architecture in place. Residual green and net-zero delivery instruments remain operative. Residual competition rules continue. Pragmatic domestic energy and industrial support adds limited additional state direction without a comprehensive industrial-steering package. Northern Ireland-specific planning and institutional constraints remain. The institutional pattern is near-continuity with the residual UK economic-regulation baseline: ordinary labour protections and residual green architecture stay in force, with only marginal additional direction rather than either major deregulation or major mandate expansion.

### Regulation Score
Retention of ordinary labour protections, residual green/net-zero architecture, residual competition rules and Northern Ireland-specific constraints, combined with limited pragmatic industrial support, produces a Regulation Score of 58.

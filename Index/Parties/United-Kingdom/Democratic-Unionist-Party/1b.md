### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 58**

The DUP’s non-tax economic policies are largely status-quo oriented. The party does not propose major repeal of net zero mandates, significant labour-market deregulation, or a broad bureaucracy-reduction programme. Pragmatic support for domestic energy and industry produces only marginal additional state direction. Ordinary employment protections, residual green framework rules, general regulatory baseline, and Northern Ireland-specific planning and institutional constraints remain in place. Relative to Reform or Restore Britain there is no substantial deregulatory thrust; relative to Labour or the nationalist left there is no significant expansion of mandates. The net result is near-continuity with current Britain.

# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 50**

### Overview
The DUP’s primary focus is Northern Ireland’s place in the United Kingdom and related constitutional and cultural issues. It has limited bandwidth for initiating UK-wide foreign-policy campaigns and therefore sits higher than the main Westminster interventionist parties. Residual capacity to shape external-interference instruments is constrained by regional priority and scale.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Core agenda is Northern Ireland unionism and domestic institutional questions.
* Limited role in designing UK sanctions or military assistance policy.
* Generally supportive of the UK’s Western alignment and alliance commitments without driving new expeditionary programmes.
* Residual baseline of domestic constitutional priority; external interference is secondary.
* Self-defence remains out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. Regional parties that do not control foreign-policy levers interfere less by default. The DUP’s score reflects that structural position and its consistent domestic focus, while remaining below the highest non-interventionist scores because it does not campaign against the UK’s residual alliance and soft-power architecture.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 50
Limited ownership of UK external-interference tools and primary domestic constitutional focus produce a mid-range score.

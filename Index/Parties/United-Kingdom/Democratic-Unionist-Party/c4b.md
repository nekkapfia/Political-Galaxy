# Cultural 4B: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 32**

### Overview
The DUP prioritises individual, community and traditional equality-of-opportunity frames over progressive equity diagnostics. Group outcome gaps are not treated as automatic proof of systemic unfairness requiring identity-based corrections.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Emphasis on equal treatment, individual responsibility and community cohesion.
* Scepticism toward expansive DEI and identity-preference frameworks.
* Support for basic anti-discrimination protections without soft or hard equity outcome engineering.
* Preference for socio-economic and cultural explanations of disparities where relevant.

### Expanded Analysis
The party sits on the individual-merit side of the opportunity→outcome question. Soft equity infrastructure is not a central commitment; hard quotas are rejected. Residual acceptance of basic equal-treatment law prevents an absolute floor. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 32
Primary emphasis on individual and equal-treatment frameworks, combined with explicit rejection of progressive gap-as-proof diagnostics and identity-based equity interventions, produces a low-mid score on the equity lens.

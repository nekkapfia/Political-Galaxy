# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 38**

### Overview

The DUP has generally prioritised defence of existing constitutional arrangements and negotiated safeguards over compressed transformative agendas. Its governing and opposition practice in Northern Ireland has been shaped by power-sharing rules and a preference for stability-preserving process. The score is low-to-mid.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Strong emphasis on maintaining the Union and resisting rapid constitutional change that would alter Northern Ireland’s status.
* Participation in power-sharing institutions that structurally require negotiation and sequential agreement.
* Limited evidence of systematic design for accelerated, disruptive implementation across multiple policy domains.
* Observable pattern is defensive and process-oriented rather than accelerationist.

### Expanded Analysis

The DUP’s primary orientation is conservation of a preferred constitutional status quo through available institutional channels. That produces a low radicalism score on pace of change, consistent with parties that treat rapid transformation as a threat rather than a method.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 38

Preference for constitutional stability and negotiated power-sharing process over compressed transformative agendas places the DUP at 38.

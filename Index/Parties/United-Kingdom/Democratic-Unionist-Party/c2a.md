# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 62**

### Overview
The DUP maintains a predominantly person-unit approach to allocation in most domestic domains, with limited category tools and a cultural-conservative emphasis on biological sex and traditional institutional forms.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Opposition to self-ID and support for biological-sex criteria in single-sex spaces and services.
* Resistance to expansive DEI and positive-action frameworks in Northern Ireland institutions.
* Formal equality and individual rights as the baseline legal unit.
* Limited residual category instruments operating inside a broader person-default framework.
* Cultural and religious identity more prominent in membership and social policy than in routine employment or educational allocation rules.

### Expanded Analysis
The party treats individual legal personality as the default unit for ordinary allocation while resisting the expansion of progressive category-preference systems. Biological-sex clarification and opposition to self-ID reinforce person- and biology-based criteria in contested domains. Category tools remain secondary and confined. The overall pattern is moderate-to-predominantly individualist.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 62
Person-default residual with confined category tools and active resistance to self-ID expansion place the DUP in the predominantly individualist band. Score 62.

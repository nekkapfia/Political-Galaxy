# Cultural 1A: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 22 (28M)

### Overview
The DUP organises moral legitimacy around the Union as democratic preference and practical security, alongside a Protestant Christian moral grammar that runs through family, education and social-policy positions and community self-understanding, advanced through democratic politics.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* The Union is justified as the democratic and constitutional preference of the pro-Union people of Northern Ireland and as practical security and prosperity.
* Community identity and cultural continuity are defended as the rights and inheritance of a living political community under UK sovereignty.
* Opposition to structures seen as threatening the Union is framed in terms of democratic consent and constitutional principle.
* Electoral mandate and community self-protection are treated as legitimate political claims.
* Practical security and economic stability appear as justificatory grounds.

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Protestant Christian heritage substantially informs moral positions on family, education and social issues and forms part of the community’s self-understanding, even though advanced through democratic politics rather than as independent higher-law override of the demos.

### Expanded Analysis
The Union as democratic preference, community identity, consent, electoral mandate and practical security organise a clear Mundane layer. At the same time Protestant Christian heritage is not ornamental: it runs through family, education, social-policy positions and community self-understanding. Under residual rules this is **substantial-secondary** transcendent weight — reduced relative to full higher-law override, but real and recurrent across domains. Democratic procedure remains the formal organiser; confessional grammar remains a saturating secondary component of the foundation mix. Both sources are present. Operational primacy is Mundane; transcendent residual is substantial-secondary rather than token.

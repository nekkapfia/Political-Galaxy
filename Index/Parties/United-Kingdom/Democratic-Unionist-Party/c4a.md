# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview
The DUP operates inside the Northern Ireland and UK education framework, which retains more selective elements (grammar schools) than most of Great Britain. Formal access remains open; the party has generally defended selective education and traditional pathways while accepting residual quality gradients correlated with family background.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of selective (grammar) education within the Northern Ireland system.
* Preference for traditional academic pathways and high standards.
* Acceptance of residual quality and network gradients as compatible with formal openness.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Limited emphasis on structural equalisation of early resources relative to gap-closing parties.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. Northern Ireland’s retained selective system gives the DUP a structural context in which selection is already institutionalised. The party’s defence of that system accepts differential pathway quality more readily than comprehensive-preferring parties. Formal access remains universal; practical ease and quality continue to correlate with family resources and early performance. This places the score in the upper part of the 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment keeps it well below moderate-determinism ranges. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28
Universal schooling plus defence of selective pathways and acceptance of quality gradients, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the upper 21–30 band. Score 28.

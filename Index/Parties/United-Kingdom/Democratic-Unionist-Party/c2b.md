## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 76**

### Overview
The DUP maintains a strongly affirmative stance toward British national identity, symbols and continuity, especially as these relate to the Union and Northern Ireland’s place within the United Kingdom. Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Robust defence of British symbols, the monarchy and Unionist commemorative traditions.
* Education and cultural positions that treat British identity as legitimate and positive.
* Resistance to de-nationalising or critical reinterpretations of British history in the Northern Irish context.
* National pride functions as a clear emotional and political organising principle within the party’s base and programme.

The DUP’s cultural orientation is one of confident British national affirmation.

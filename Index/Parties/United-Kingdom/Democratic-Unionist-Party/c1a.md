# Cultural 1A: United Kingdom – Democratic Unionist Party
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 41**

### Overview
The DUP grounds political legitimacy in the constitutional Union, the interests and identity of the pro-Union community in Northern Ireland, and a residual Protestant moral-traditional framework. Organising sources are mixed: strongly mundane unionist demos and security claims sit alongside visible traditional-religious moral language that does not, in contemporary practice, function as closed theocratic higher law.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* The Union justified as the democratic and constitutional preference of the pro-Union people of Northern Ireland and as practical security and prosperity.
* Community identity and cultural continuity defended as the rights and inheritance of a living political community under UK sovereignty.
* Traditional moral positions (family, education, social issues) informed by Protestant Christian heritage, typically advanced through democratic politics rather than as non-negotiable sacred law above the demos.
* Opposition to structures seen as advancing Irish unification against unionist consent framed as democratic and constitutional defence.
* Security, order and practical delivery for the unionist community as central mundane sources.
* No organising claim that divine command alone may override clear unionist democratic preference on core constitutional questions.

### Expanded Analysis
The DUP’s moral grammar combines mundane unionist popular sovereignty with residual Christian-traditional colour. The Union is defended primarily as the will and interest of the pro-Union demos and as constitutional order, not as a sacred British-Israelite or covenant destiny independent of consent. Traditional moral language remains audible and shapes social-policy instincts, yet in the contemporary party it operates inside democratic and constitutional politics rather than as theocratic higher law that could bind the community against its clear political preference. Transcendent weight is therefore real but limited—enough to lift the score out of pure demos-populism, not enough to produce a clear transcendent lean. The pattern is mixed, near the boundary of clear mundane.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 41
Mundane unionist demos and security claims organise; residual Protestant traditional sources add modest transcendent weight without becoming closed higher law. The score is 41.

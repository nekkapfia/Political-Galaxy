# Core 2A: United Kingdom – Restore Britain
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview
Restore Britain seeks to end irregular migration, leave the ECHR, scrap the Windsor Framework and impose a hard legal-migration cap to maximise national control over borders and law. External pooling of authority is treated as the target for structural removal.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Commitment to stop all small-boat crossings and deport those who arrive illegally.
* Explicit policy of leaving the ECHR to remove external legal barriers to removal.
* Policy of scrapping the Windsor Framework and eliminating residual internal regulatory differentiation within the UK.
* Target of reducing legal migration significantly below 100,000 net per year.
* Supports national control over borders, law and key policy domains while remaining in NATO and major trade arrangements; post-Brexit pooling of authority is to be actively reduced.

### Expanded Analysis
Restore Britain matches the high-intensity removal pattern on irregular migration, ECHR jurisdiction and Windsor Framework, and adds a harder legal-migration target below 100,000 net. Small-boat crossings are to be stopped and illegal arrivals deported; ECHR exit removes external judicial barriers to removal; scrapping the Windsor Framework eliminates internal regulatory differentiation within the UK. Legal inflows are tightly restricted. NATO and major trade arrangements are retained as ordinary external commitments. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is maximal recovery of border, removal and territorial control within the existing UK state, with remaining constraints confined to standard security and trade frameworks that do not transfer core law-making authority. Primary drag from external pooling of authority is deliberately minimised.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72
Ending irregular settlement, leaving the ECHR, scrapping the Windsor Framework and imposing a hard legal-migration cap maximise unilateral control over borders and law. External pooling of authority that continues is limited to ordinary alliance and trade residual. The final set therefore sits inside the 70–79 Mid–High band. The score of 72 follows from structural recovery of sovereign authority over territory and law rather than incremental adjustment inside external architectures.

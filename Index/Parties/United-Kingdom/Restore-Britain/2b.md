# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Restore Britain
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 62**

### Overview
Restore Britain adopts a stronger sovereignty-first and non-interventionist emphasis than most Westminster parties. It is sceptical of foreign aid as a social-engineering tool and of open-ended external commitments, prioritising domestic capacity and national defence.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Priority on British sovereignty and domestic resilience over expeditionary missions.
* Scepticism of aid conditionality aimed at reshaping foreign societies.
* Support for robust national defence; limited enthusiasm for transformative foreign policy.
* Residual acceptance of limited, threat-linked pressure tools.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. Restore Britain’s programme would further reduce the UK’s role in sanctions-driven or values-export campaigns relative even to Reform. The score sits at the high end of the restrained-nationalist cluster.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 62
Strong non-interventionist and sovereignty-first orientation produces one of the higher scores among UK parties.

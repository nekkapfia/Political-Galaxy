### Overview

Restore Britain’s final restriction set retains ordinary direct-harm criminal law, retains and intensifies antisocial-behaviour / broken-windows enforcement, retains personal drug-possession criminalisation (not repealed), and adds a stated public ban on the burqa and niqab. Speech-liberalisation commitments relative to the Online Safety Act and wide opinion-policing stack remove those layers from the final set; removals grant no upward credit. Lowest Endos band is **60–69**. The dress ban creates a clear Endos/Xenos split at **44–50** for the affected group.

* Core criminal prohibitions on violence, true threats, and direct physical harm — retained.

* Personal possession and use of controlled drugs remain criminal — not repealed → **70–75** (present but not the floor).
* Public ban on the burqa and niqab — stated restriction on religious/identity self-presentation in public → **44–50** for persons for whom the practice is required.
* Online Safety Act platform duties and wide non-violent hate-speech / opinion offences — directed for removal → **not in final set**.
* No mandatory attitude-correction or ideological re-education programme stated for the general population.
* Child-protection and direct-exploitation sexual offences retained (vulnerable-protection; higher than the Endos floor).
* Party-system or constitutional structure out of scope for Core 1A.

### Expanded Analysis

Under pure final-set rules only restrictions that remain are scored. Speech-layer liberalisation uncovers residual and stated order layers; it does not raise the score.

**Endos:** Restore states enforcement of the “little things” — litter, graffiti, vandalism, petty disorder — under broken-windows logic with real consequences for antisocial behaviour. That aligns with **60–69 — Betterment of society restrictions**: laws target disruptive or annoying behaviour beyond direct immediate harm; policing becomes active rather than reactive. Personal drug criminalisation remains and aligns with **70–75**, but under the lowest-band rule it does not set the score once **60–69** ASB enforcement is present. Score **64**.

**Xenos** (persons for whom burqa/niqab is religious or obligatory practice): a public ban restricts self-presentation and public presence on identity and religious criteria. Aligns with **44–50 — Early ideological/identity-based controls**: rules requiring conformity in self-presentation; observable identity-based control of external expression. Score **48**.

Absence of the progressive speech stack produces no upward credit. Each group is scored only on the restrictions that apply to it.

### Overview
Restore Britain maintains complete ethnic openness: no ancestral or blood criterion for British membership. The Exclusion Test is not met. Among major UK parties it articulates the strongest expectation that cultural alignment and commitment to British norms are central to full Endos status. Immigration volume and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit rejection of ethnic or blood criteria; membership remains open to all ethnicities who meet cultural and civic conditions.
* Strong emphasis that cultural alignment, language, and commitment to British norms should be required for full substantive membership.
* Distinction between formal citizenship and real belonging on cultural grounds.
* Birth and descent routes carry no cultural tests; elevated expectations apply primarily to naturalisation and substantive recognition.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Restore Britain
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
Restore Britain advocates a strongly meritocratic and traditionalist approach to education and social advancement, with emphasis on selection, high standards, and resistance to policies that engineer equal outcomes. Formal access remains open; the party accepts greater differentiation based on ability and effort and places less priority on narrowing birth-based quality gradients through redistribution.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong support for grammar schools, academic selection, and traditional educational pathways.
* Opposition to ideological interventions viewed as undermining merit and individual responsibility.
* Emphasis on restoring cultural confidence in British institutions alongside practical skills.
* Acceptance and defence of differential outcomes arising from selection and individual/family agency.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. Restore Britain operates inside the UK’s universal-schooling system. Its distinctive stance is the strongest preference for selection and traditional merit routes among the major UK parties, combined with explicit resistance to equalisation policies. Formal pathways remain open; residual quality and network gradients correlated with birth background are treated as acceptable. This keeps the score inside the 21–30 band: access exists for all, yet the programme does not prioritise reduction of practical friction arising from family resources and early advantages. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Universal schooling plus strong selection preference and acceptance of quality gradients, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the 21–30 band. Score 26.

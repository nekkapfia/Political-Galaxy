## Regulation

### Overview
Restore Britain produces a higher Core 1B Regulation profile defined by removal of residual net-zero targets and green levies, planning liberalisation, expansion of domestic energy production (North Sea, nuclear), and modest reduction in employment regulation and bureaucracy, while retaining ordinary competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Removal of residual net-zero targets and associated green levies and delivery mandates.
* Planning liberalisation and fast-track infrastructure to reduce prior restraint on development and investment decisions.
* Energy production expansion (North Sea, nuclear, domestic) with lower regulatory barriers to voluntary energy production.
* Modest reduction in employment regulation and bureaucracy; ordinary unfair-dismissal and core health-and-safety residual largely continues.
* Retention of residual competition-law baseline and basic non-green environmental standards.
* Residual secondary bureaucratic baseline reduced but not eliminated.

### Expanded Analysis
The Restore Britain final set removes the residual net-zero target architecture and associated green levies and delivery instruments. Planning liberalisation reduces prior restraint on development and investment. Domestic energy production (North Sea, nuclear) faces lower regulatory barriers. Employment regulation and bureaucracy are modestly lightened while ordinary core labour protections continue. Residual competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards remain. The institutional pattern is substantial reduction of residual green-industrial and mandate layers combined with retained ordinary labour and competition baselines and expanded domestic energy production options.

### Regulation Score
Removal of residual net-zero targets and green levies, planning liberalisation, energy production expansion, and modest reduction in employment regulation and bureaucracy, while retaining ordinary competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards, produces a Regulation Score of 65.

### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 65**

Restore Britain’s non-tax policies would produce a significant reduction in state coercion. The combination of net zero mandate and green levy removal, planning liberalisation and fast-track infrastructure, expanded domestic energy production (North Sea, nuclear), and reduced employment regulation and bureaucracy would materially increase voluntary economic decision-making in energy, development and business operations. Ordinary competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards remain. The package represents one of the stronger shifts toward lower coercion, comparable to Reform UK and clearly above the Conservative baseline.

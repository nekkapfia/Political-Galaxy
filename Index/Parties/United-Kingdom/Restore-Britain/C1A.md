# Cultural 1A: United Kingdom – Restore Britain
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)
### Overview
Restore Britain organises moral legitimacy around democratic will, concrete interests of the existing population, competence and stewardship of a specific inherited country. Transcendent higher-law sources do not organise its claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Generational continuity is framed as human stewardship and political responsibility for a specific inherited country.
* Mass immigration and demographic change are criticised as overrides of democratic will and practical social cohesion.
* Progressive institutional orthodoxy is rejected as an elite project detached from popular preference and measurable outcomes.
* National identity and character are defended as the living inheritance of a mortal community.
* Democratic restoration and popular control over institutions are presented as the required political path.
* Quantifiable harms to housing, wages, services and safety are treated as central justificatory evidence.
* Competence and delivery for the existing population appear as core tests of legitimacy.

### Expanded Analysis
Generational continuity as human stewardship, democratic will, concrete harms to housing wages and services, competence and delivery, and national identity as the living inheritance of a mortal community organise the entire moral conversation. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law, worship-level deification, sacred Ideal or extreme sacrifice to a Utopian Ideal appears. The foundation is Mundane: demos will, concrete interests and performance.

# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Green Party
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 44**

### Overview
The Green Party is internationalist on climate, human rights and multilateralism. It is more hesitant on traditional kinetic intervention than Labour or the Conservatives, yet accepts sanctions and normative pressure as legitimate tools, producing a score still inside the broader interventionist range. Residual baseline retains progressive soft-power and conditionality instruments.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong climate and human-rights conditionality instincts in international policy.
* Support for rules-based and multilateral instruments.
* Greater reluctance toward large-scale military intervention than mainstream governing parties.
* Support for Ukraine in principle combined with emphasis on diplomatic and humanitarian tracks.
* Residual baseline of progressive normative pressure: sanctions and values conditionality remain accepted even where kinetic appetite is lower.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. Green policy reduces enthusiasm for kinetic tools while retaining sanctions and normative conditionality. The residual posture is still interventionist on the soft spectrum; the result is a modest upward shift relative to Labour, not a turn to non-intervention.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 44
Retained sanctions and normative pressure tools, residual progressive conditionality instruments, and reduced kinetic appetite place the Greens in the mid-40s.

# Cultural 4B: United Kingdom – Green Party
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 78**

### Overview
The Green Party treats unequal group outcomes as diagnostic of unequal opportunity and systemic unfairness. Persistent gaps under formal rules are read as proof that opportunity was not equal; individual variance is not accepted as a sufficient explanation. Equity interventions, targets and institutional transformation are required. Residual baseline of progressive equity-lens framing: gaps between groups are routinely treated as evidence of unfair opportunity structures requiring corrective institutional response.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit support for affirmative action, parity targets and demographic representation goals in public institutions and employment.
* Framing of racial and gender wealth gaps and outcome disparities as products of structural and historical injustice requiring corrective measures.
* Advocacy for expanded equality duties, diversity monitoring and institutional culture change that prioritises group-outcome considerations.
* Education and cultural policy that treats demographic visibility and anti-discrimination-sensitive rules as standard.
* Critique of pure merit frameworks as reproducing privilege; equal opportunity is expected to yield substantially more equal group outcomes.
* Soft-to-mid mechanisms predominate (targets, positive action, institutional rules) rather than nationwide hard quotas with sanctions.
* Residual baseline of progressive equity-lens framing: gaps between groups are routinely treated as evidence of unfair opportunity structures requiring corrective institutional response.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks the gap-as-proof diagnostic at high intensity. Group outcome disparities are systematically attributed to systemic and intersectional causes; remedies centre on proactive equity interventions. While concrete hard numerical quotas with legal sanctions are not the sole or universal instrument, the combination of targets, positive action, institutional redesign and cultural framing produces a strong equity-lens profile.

The score is moderated from the absolute high band because the primary tools remain soft-to-mid rather than zero-tolerance hard outcome mandates across every core domain. This placement concerns only the opportunity→outcome theory and remedial intensity; it is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 78
High diagnostic lock on systemic causes of group gaps, combined with consistent advocacy for equity targets, positive action and institutional transformation, produces a high-band score. The absence of universal hard quotas with sanctions across all domains keeps the score below the extreme 90+ compressed zone.

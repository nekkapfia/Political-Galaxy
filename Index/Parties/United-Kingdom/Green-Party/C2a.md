# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Green Party
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 18**

### Overview
The Green Party treats group identity as a primary and legitimate basis for the allocation of rights, access and institutional resources. The person is routinely measured through demographic and identity coordinates. Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Strong support for self-identification in gender recognition, allocating access to single-sex spaces, sports and services by self-identified group membership.
* Consistent advocacy for frameworks that treat protected characteristics as a primary basis for rights and resource allocation.
* Opposition to reforms that would restore biological sex or individual merit as the dominant criteria in contested domains.
* Institutional and policy approaches that embed group-based considerations into education, employment and service provision.
* Framing of individualist or merit-based approaches as insufficient without accompanying group measures.
* Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Expanded Analysis
Across law, policy and institutional practice the party elevates group membership above individual characteristics. Self-ID and related frameworks allow self-declared category to determine access in contested domains, while broader identity-conscious approaches treat group location as a routine and necessary factor in allocation. Individual biography and biological criteria are subordinated. The result is severe group collectivism relative to person-unit systems.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 18
Multi-category allocation goals, identity-targeted remedies and systematic discounting of individual characteristics as the primary unit match the severe group-collectivism band. Score 18.

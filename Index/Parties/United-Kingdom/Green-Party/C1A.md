# Cultural 1A: United Kingdom – Green Party
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 44 (6M)

### Overview
The Green Party organises moral legitimacy around planetary boundaries and climate breakdown as empirical threats to human survival and future human welfare, intergenerational responsibility to future persons, and democratic participation. Elevated urgency language is present; sacralisation of Nature as a moral subject is not the organising frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Planetary boundaries and climate breakdown are framed as empirical threats to human life, civilisation and future human welfare requiring urgent action.
* Intergenerational responsibility is treated as a duty to future human persons rather than as a sacred order independent of human welfare.
* Scientific evidence is used to establish the scale of ecological risk to human societies.
* Democratic participation and social justice claims remain present inside the same human-welfare frame.
* Concrete harms to health, environment and future generations appear as evidence of the need for action.
* Elevated urgency and moral-emergency language (“act now”, non-negotiable transformation) appears within the human-necessity frame.

### Expanded Analysis
Planetary boundaries and climate risk are framed as threats to human survival and future human welfare; intergenerational duty is framed as responsibility to future persons; scientific evidence and democratic participation organise the operative conversation. Under the locked source lists this is human-necessity ecology — Mundane (collective self-preservation and concrete interests of present and future humans) — not sacred/inheriting-will ecology. Nature is not treated as a moral subject with intrinsic standing independent of human utility. Elevated urgency language is present as lesser residual only and does not convert the source into Cosmic/Natural law. No divine command, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. The foundation is Mundane with residual elevated ecological urgency.

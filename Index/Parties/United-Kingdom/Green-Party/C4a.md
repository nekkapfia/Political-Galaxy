# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Green Party
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
The Green Party prioritises reducing socio-economic inequality through expanded public services, early intervention, and redistribution, with strong opposition to selective schooling. Formal access is universal; the emphasis is on actively narrowing birth-based quality and resource gaps.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong support for comprehensive education and opposition to selective schooling.
* Emphasis on addressing early disadvantage through well-funded public services and redistribution.
* Scepticism toward market mechanisms and parental-choice frameworks that may reinforce existing advantages.
* Preference for structural reduction of quality gradients over acceptance of residual differences.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks the degree to which birth circumstances constrain outcomes. The Green programme operates inside the UK’s universal-schooling system and seeks to minimise the practical weight of birth-linked quality differences through redistribution and anti-selection policy. Residual gradients remain, but the party’s stated direction is among the strongest gap-closing orientations in the UK set. This places it at the lower end of the 21–30 band (or boundary with 11–20). Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment keeps the score far below moderate-determinism ranges. Equity-lens diagnostics belong on Cultural 4B and are not scored here.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Universal schooling plus strong anti-selection and redistributional gap-closing, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the lower 21–30 / upper 11–20 boundary. Score 22.

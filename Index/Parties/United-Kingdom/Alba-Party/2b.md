# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Alba Party
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 54**

### Overview
Alba’s primary focus is Scottish independence and the constitutional relationship with the rest of the United Kingdom. It has minimal independent capacity to design or implement UK-wide external-interference instruments and therefore sits higher than the main Westminster interventionist parties. Residual foreign-policy bandwidth is limited by scale and priority.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Core programme is Scottish independence and domestic constitutional questions.
* Limited role in designing UK sanctions regimes, military assistance packages or expeditionary doctrine.
* General alignment with progressive and multilateral norms once independence is assumed, without a detailed independent interventionist architecture.
* Residual baseline of domestic priority; external interference is secondary and capacity-constrained.
* Self-defence remains out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures interference in other states’ internal affairs. Regional and independence-focused parties that do not control the UK’s foreign-policy levers generate less direct Core 2B drag by structural position. Alba’s score reflects that limited ownership and its domestic priority, while still sitting below pure non-interventionist platforms because it does not actively campaign against the residual soft-power and alliance instruments of a future independent Scotland.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 54
Limited ownership of UK external-interference tools and primary domestic focus produce a mid-50s score.

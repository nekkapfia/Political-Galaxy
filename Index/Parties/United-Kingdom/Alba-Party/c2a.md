# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Alba Party
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 55**

### Overview
Alba combines a person-unit baseline in ordinary allocation with national and cultural framing that can introduce group considerations at the level of political community and institutional priority.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Formal individual equality and rights as the residual legal baseline.
* Support for biological-sex clarity in contested single-sex domains.
* Limited residual category instruments without a multi-axis identity-stack programme.
* National and cultural continuity framing that can elevate group (national) membership in political and institutional priority setting.
* Not a progressive equity-allocation regime and not a pure person-only libertarian programme.

### Expanded Analysis
The party does not organise routine employment, education or service allocation around progressive identity categories. Individual legal personality remains the default unit. At the same time national and cultural continuity concerns introduce group-level considerations into the definition of political priority and institutional focus. The result is a mixed but still person-leaning pattern, short of high individualism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 55
Person-default residual with limited category tools and national-group framing in political priority place Alba in the upper significant / lower predominantly individualist transition. Score 55.

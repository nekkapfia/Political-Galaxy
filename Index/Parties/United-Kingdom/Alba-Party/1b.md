### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 52**

Alba’s non-tax policies would produce a modest increase in state direction relative to current Britain. Stronger industrial strategy and public investment orientation, expanded employment protections, and continued net zero / Scottish energy prioritisation would increase state influence over investment allocation and labour markets. Planning remains under retained oversight with a Scottish prioritisation layer. The package does not include major public ownership programmes on the scale of the Greens or Workers Party, nor does it propose deregulation of net zero or employment rules. The overall effect is a centre-left interventionist tilt similar to Labour, with an added constitutional-nationalist emphasis.

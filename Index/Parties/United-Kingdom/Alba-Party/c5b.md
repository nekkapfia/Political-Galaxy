# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Alba Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview

Alba prioritises rapid progress toward Scottish independence and criticises what it regards as insufficient urgency in other nationalist formations. At the same time it operates without a governing record and inside the same constitutional constraints as other Scottish parties. The net score is mid-range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Explicit urgency language on independence and criticism of slower or more gradualist approaches within the broader nationalist movement.
* Limited organisational and electoral weight; no national governing record.
* No detailed operational architecture for compressed implementation comparable to the highest-scoring accelerationist platforms.
* Observable pattern is elevated urgency rhetoric within structural constraints that still favour process.

### Expanded Analysis

Alba’s distinctive contribution is higher rhetorical urgency on the independence timeline. That raises it slightly above more gradualist territorial parties but is insufficient, without governing practice or detailed anti-process design, to reach the high-radicalism band. Score 48.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48

Elevated urgency on independence combined with absence of governing record or systematic anti-deliberative design places Alba at 48.

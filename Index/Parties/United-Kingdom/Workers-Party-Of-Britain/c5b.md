# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Workers Party of Britain
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 55**

### Overview

The Workers Party of Britain advocates substantial economic and institutional change and uses urgency language on selected social and geopolitical issues. It lacks a national governing record and has not published a detailed operational design against deliberative constraints comparable to the highest-scoring accelerationist platforms. The score sits in the mid range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Platform and leader statements contain urgency framing on economic restructuring, foreign policy and selected domestic institutions.
* Limited electoral and organisational weight; no governing record at national level.
* No published, detailed day-one implementation architecture of the type that supports scores in the high 70s–80s.
* Observable pattern is elevated transformative ambition without systematic anti-process institutional design.

### Expanded Analysis

Transformative goals and urgency rhetoric raise the score above pure gradualist parties. Absence of governing practice and of a concrete compressed-implementation design keep it below the high-radicalism band. Mid-range placement of 55 is appropriate on current evidence.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 55

Transformative ambition and urgency language without detailed anti-deliberative implementation design place the Workers Party of Britain at 55.

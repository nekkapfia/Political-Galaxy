# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Workers Party Of Britain
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 46**

### Overview
The Workers Party of Britain is critical of Western interventionism, NATO expansion and aspects of Ukraine policy framed as Western overreach. It sits higher than Labour or the Conservatives but does not reach the non-interventionist scores of Reform or Restore Britain.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong criticism of Iraq, Libya and related interventions.
* Scepticism of NATO expansion and of open-ended military support framed as liberal intervention.
* Anti-imperialist framing that reduces support for democracy-export and regime-change tools.
* Operates inside the UK system without a full isolationist programme.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. The party’s programme would reduce UK participation in transformative external campaigns. Residual acceptance of some international engagement and the absence of a governing record keep the score in the mid-to-high 40s.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 46
Criticism of Western interventionism without a pure non-intervention platform produces a mid-to-high 40s placement.

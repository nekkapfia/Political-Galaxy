### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 40**

The Workers Party of Britain’s non-tax policies would produce a substantial increase in state coercion relative to current Britain. Extensive public ownership and planned industrial direction, major expansion of collective labour rights and employment protections, a state-directed green industrial programme, and significant restrictions on capital and trade openness would sharply reduce the scope for voluntary private economic decision-making across production, investment, labour allocation and external economic relations. The package sits well to the left of mainstream centre-left parties and is the most interventionist among the UK parties assessed on this slider, short only of full theoretical command-economy models. Residual market mechanisms would remain in some consumer and small-enterprise sectors, but the dominant mechanical effect is comprehensive state steering of the commanding heights and labour market.

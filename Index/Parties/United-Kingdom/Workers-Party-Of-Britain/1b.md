# Core 1B: United Kingdom – Workers Party of Britain
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Final Score: 49**

## Regulation

### Overview
The Workers Party of Britain produces a lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by reinforcement of residual labour protections, residual green architecture, and preference for substantial public ownership, industrial planning and collective direction of strategic sectors.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Reinforcement of ordinary labour protections and expansion of collective bargaining and trade-union rights.
* Retention of residual green/net-zero architecture; preference for high environmental and industrial standards rather than repeal.
* Support for public ownership of strategic sectors, industrial planning and public-investment direction.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues but is subordinated to industrial-policy priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues; no high-priority labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The Workers Party final set retains residual labour protections and residual green architecture while advancing substantial public-ownership and industrial-planning direction. Collective labour-market institutions are reinforced. Strategic-sector public ownership and planning reduce the scope of private voluntary economic decision-making. Residual competition rules remain but are secondary to industrial-policy priorities. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of labour and green restrictions combined with significant additional public-ownership and planning direction.

### Regulation Score
Retention of residual labour protections and residual green architecture, combined with substantial public-ownership and industrial-planning direction, produces a Regulation Score of 38.

## Taxation

Primary method applies.

* Public-ownership and public-investment preference with limited tax-reduction emphasis; higher public spending sustained by residual and expanded revenue tools.
* Overall estimated net cost relative to residual baseline: ~£40–70 billion (midpoint £55 billion)
* Baseline tax revenue: £997.6 billion (34.4% × £2,900 billion)
* Tax revenue after package: £942.6 billion
* Current GDP: £2,900 billion

**Taxation Score** = (1 − 942.6 / 2900) × 100 = 67.50 → **68**

## Final Score

A = (38 + 68) / 2 = 53.0
L = min(38, 68) = 38
d = |38 − 68| / 100 = 0.30
F = (1 − 0.30) × 53.0 + 0.30 × 38 = 48.5 → **49**

**Final Score: 49**

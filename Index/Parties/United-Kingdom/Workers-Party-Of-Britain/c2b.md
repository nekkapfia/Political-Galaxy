# Cultural 2B: United Kingdom – Workers Party of Britain
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 50**

### Overview
The Workers Party of Britain combines left-economic positions with a more affirmative stance toward British working-class continuity and national interest than the progressive left. It rejects pure post-national cosmopolitanism while remaining distant from traditional conservative patriotism. Residual baseline of mixed or conditional national framing: pride and critical perspectives coexist without a single dominant residual direction.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of the concrete cultural and social interests of the existing British population.
* Critique of elite cosmopolitanism and open-border cultural frames.
* Limited engagement with longer British historical pride; focus is more present-oriented and class-inflected.
* Does not organise around systematic guilt narratives, nor around high traditional national affirmation.

### Expanded Analysis
The party sits near the centre of the Balanced band. It is less hostile to national continuity than the Greens or the most progressive wings of Labour, yet it does not elevate traditional national history or symbols as a primary organising principle. The score reflects this intermediate position.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 50
Partial affirmation of national continuity without strong traditional historical pride or systematic guilt framing places the Workers Party in the Balanced band.

# Cultural 1A: United Kingdom – Workers Party of Britain
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 58**

### Overview
The Workers Party of Britain grounds political legitimacy in working-class interest, socialist economic transformation and opposition to both neoliberal economics and progressive cultural orthodoxy. Organising sources mix demos-centred material justice with residual socialist ideological framing. The profile is mixed, leaning toward transcendent weight where systemic anti-capitalist claims function as binding critique, without reaching closed Party-state historical necessity.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Working-class material interest and social provision framed as primary moral and political requirements, often against elite and market outcomes.
* Capitalist and imperial structures criticised in systemic terms that go beyond ordinary unfairness to claims of structural illegitimacy.
* Opposition to progressive identity orthodoxy framed as defence of working-class cohesion and democratic majority preference.
* Socialist economic direction presented as necessary correction rather than optional preference among equal alternatives.
* Electoral and popular mobilisation treated as means to advance class and national-popular interest.
* No fully closed Leninist higher law monopolising interpretation independent of working-class preference.

### Expanded Analysis
The Workers Party organises justification through material class interest and a residual socialist critique of capitalism as systemically unjust. That systemic critique supplies transcendent-leaning weight: the existing economic order is not merely suboptimal for the living demos but structured so as to require transformation on grounds that outrank ordinary market preference. At the same time the party remains demos- and class-popular in method, rejecting both progressive cultural orthodoxy and pure elite technocracy in the name of working people. It does not operate a closed Party-state orthodoxy of the historical communist type. The balance is therefore mixed with a mild transcendent lean—above Labour’s managerial centre, below dedicated ecological or hard ideological currents. Score adjusted to 58 to reflect systemic justice claims without over-weighting residual Marxist vocabulary relative to operative demos language.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 58
Systemic anti-capitalist justice claims provide organising transcendent weight; demos and working-class interest keep the profile mixed rather than extreme. The score is 58.

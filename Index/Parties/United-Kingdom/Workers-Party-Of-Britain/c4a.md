# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Workers Party Of Britain
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview
The Workers Party of Britain emphasises class-based analysis of educational and occupational opportunity while operating inside the UK’s universal-schooling framework. Formal access remains open; the party treats residual class gradients as structurally significant and prioritises working-class educational and industrial pathways. It does not institute formal hereditary assignment rules.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong class-based analysis of educational and occupational barriers.
* Emphasis on practical and industrial routes alongside academic pathways.
* Critique of elite educational stratification and residual class advantage.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Residual acceptance of quality variation within an open formal framework, combined with class-focused remediation.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. The Workers Party inherits the UK baseline of universal practical access with residual quality and network gradients. Its distinctive contribution is a heavier class-analytic framing that treats those gradients as structurally consequential and prioritises working-class pathways. Formal pathways remain open; practical ease continues to correlate with family resources. This places the score in the upper part of the 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment keeps it well below moderate-determinism ranges. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28
Universal schooling plus strong class-analytic emphasis on residual barriers, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the upper 21–30 band. Score 28.

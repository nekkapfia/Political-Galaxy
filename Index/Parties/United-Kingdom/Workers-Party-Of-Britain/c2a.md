# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Workers Party of Britain
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview
The Workers Party of Britain emphasises class and collective categories as organising units of political and social allocation, subordinating pure individual-merit frameworks to group-based analysis and remedy.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class-based analysis treating economic group location as primary for diagnosis and remedy.
* Support for collective and category-conscious approaches to rights and distribution.
* Formal individual legal personality retained as residual liberal inheritance.
* Rejection of pure person-unit meritocracy as sufficient for justice.
* Limited engagement with contemporary multi-axis identity stacks relative to class framing.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant unit of measure is the class or collective category rather than the sovereign individual. Individual agency operates inside group-structured constraints and remedies. While the party does not adopt the full progressive identity-stack apparatus, its allocation logic remains strongly group-primacy rather than person-unit. The result is very strong group subordination relative to high-individualism systems.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28
Class and collective categories as primary organising units of status and remedy, with individual merit secondary, place the Workers Party in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 28.

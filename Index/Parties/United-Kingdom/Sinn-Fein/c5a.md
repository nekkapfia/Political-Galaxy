# Cultural 5A: United Kingdom – Sinn Féin
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis

### Overview
Sinn Féin promotes Irish language and republican historical narrative while advancing progressive reform on marriage, gender recognition and education content.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Promotion of Irish language and republican historical narrative in education and public culture as national-project continuity.
* Progressive reform positions on marriage, reproductive rights, gender recognition and education content.
* Traditional social structures linked to the older Catholic order treated as objects of reform rather than baselines to conserve.
* Nationalist cultural continuity cultivated in parallel with progressive social transformation in family and status domains.
* Residual progressive settlement on family-status and gender frameworks is extended; older confessional social baselines are not restored.
* Education combines republican narrative emphasis with progressive framing on status and inclusion.

### Expanded Analysis

Domain hierarchy shows progressive primacy in family-status and gender rules, selective continuity in language and republican narrative, and no programme to restore pre-progressive confessional family baselines. The residual progressive settlement on status frameworks is extended. Nationalist cultural continuity and progressive social transformation run in parallel; on high-weight transmission domains the progressive trajectory dominates and determines the overall pattern.

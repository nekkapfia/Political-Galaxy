# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Sinn Féin
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview

Sinn Féin combines transformative constitutional and social goals with the practical experience of power-sharing and negotiated institutions in Northern Ireland. Observable practice shows both urgency on selected agendas and significant residual commitment to process and agreement-based sequencing. The score sits in the mid range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Strong urgency framing on Irish unity and related constitutional change.
* Governing experience in Northern Ireland has required sustained negotiation, power-sharing mechanisms and sequential implementation under the Good Friday and subsequent frameworks.
* Platform contains both accelerationist language on core goals and acceptance of institutional process constraints in day-to-day governance.
* Observable pattern is mixed: higher urgency on constitutional endpoints, more deliberative practice in operational governance.

### Expanded Analysis

Constitutional urgency pulls the score upward relative to pure gradualist parties, while power-sharing experience and process commitments prevent a high-radicalism placement. The mid-range score of 52 records that combination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52

Transformative constitutional urgency tempered by power-sharing and negotiated institutional practice places Sinn Féin at 52.

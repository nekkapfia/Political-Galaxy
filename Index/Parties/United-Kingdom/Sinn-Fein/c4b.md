### Overview
Sinn Féin combines structural readings of group and community disparities with support for equality infrastructure. Soft equity tools and representation commitments are endorsed; hard numerical quotas are not the dominant mechanism. * Residual baseline of mixed or selective equity framing: progressive gap diagnostics coexist with residual individual-responsibility or cultural explanations.

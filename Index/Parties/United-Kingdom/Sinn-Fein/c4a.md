# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Sinn Fein
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
Sinn Féin operates inside the Northern Ireland education framework, which retains selective (grammar) elements, while also emphasising socio-economic opportunity and reduction of barriers associated with community background. Formal access remains open; residual quality gradients and community-linked advantages persist.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Operation within a system that retains selective education alongside comprehensive routes.
* Emphasis on reducing socio-economic and community-linked barriers to educational opportunity.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Residual quality and network gradients correlated with family and community background.
* Focus on equality of opportunity within the existing open formal framework.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. Sinn Féin inherits a Northern Ireland baseline that includes both selective and non-selective pathways. Its emphasis on reducing socio-economic barriers sits alongside residual acceptance of the existing structure. Formal access remains universal; practical ease and quality continue to correlate with family resources and early advantages. This places the score in the mid 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Universal schooling plus residual selective structure and opportunity-focused emphasis, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the mid 21–30 band. Score 26.

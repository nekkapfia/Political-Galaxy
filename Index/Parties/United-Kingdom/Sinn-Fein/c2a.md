# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Sinn Féin
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 36**

### Overview
Sinn Féin operates with formal individual legal personality as a residual layer while supporting equality and rights frameworks that treat group membership (including identity and community categories) as relevant to allocation and institutional design.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Support for equality legislation and institutional diversity frameworks.
* Acceptance of category-conscious remedies and representation metrics.
* Formal individual rights continuing as a liberal residual.
* Community and identity categories carrying weight in Northern Ireland institutional practice.
* No programme of systematic dismantling of positive-action or identity-allocation tools.

### Expanded Analysis
Category membership functions as a recurring and legitimate factor in institutional measurement and remedy. Individual legal personality is not abolished, but it is regularly subordinated to or balanced against group-identity considerations. The pattern is strong group influence on allocation rather than person-unit default.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 36
Routine institutional use of category tools and the absence of a person-only restoration programme place Sinn Féin in the strong group-primacy band. Score 36.

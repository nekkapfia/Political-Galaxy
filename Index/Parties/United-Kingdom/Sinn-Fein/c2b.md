# Cultural 2B: United Kingdom – Sinn Féin
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 72**

### Overview
Sinn Féin strongly affirms Irish national identity, history and symbols. National pride is central; the party’s relationship to British national identity is oppositional rather than affirmative. Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Affirmative framing of Irish history, republican tradition and national continuity.
* British state symbols and narratives treated as external or illegitimate in the Irish context.
* Commemorative practice oriented to Irish national and republican history.
* National pride functions as a core emotional and political organising principle.
* Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Expanded Analysis
Sinn Féin scores highly on national pride with respect to the Irish nation. Its stance toward British national identity is critical by definition of its republican project. For Cultural 2B purposes the relevant nation is the one the party seeks to affirm; that affirmation is strong and consistent, producing an Honour-band score.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 72
Consistent and central affirmation of Irish national identity and history places Sinn Féin in the Honour band.

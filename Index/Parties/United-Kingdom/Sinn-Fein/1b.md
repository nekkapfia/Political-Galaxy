## Regulation

### Overview
Sinn Féin produces a lower-mid Core 1B Regulation profile defined by retention and reinforcement of residual labour protections, residual green architecture, and preference for stronger collective, public-ownership and industrial-policy direction in Northern Ireland and all-Ireland contexts.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention and reinforcement of ordinary labour protections; expansion of collective rights and public-sector employment preference over deregulation.
* Retention of residual green/net-zero architecture and major delivery instruments; preference for high environmental standards rather than repeal.
* Support for public ownership, industrial policy and strategic-sector direction.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues; no high-priority labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The Sinn Féin final set retains residual labour protections and residual green architecture. Collective labour-market institutions and public-ownership preferences are advanced. Industrial-policy and strategic-sector direction is reinforced. Residual competition rules remain. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of labour and green restrictions combined with additional collective, public-ownership and industrial-policy direction rather than deregulation.

### Regulation Score
Retention of residual labour protections and residual green architecture, combined with preference for stronger collective institutions, public ownership and industrial-policy direction, produces a Regulation Score of 47.

### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 46**

Sinn Féin’s non-tax policies would produce a clear increase in state direction relative to current Britain. Significant public ownership and all-Ireland industrial direction, strong expansion of employment and collective rights, green transition mandates, and heavy intervention in housing and land use would substantially increase state control over investment, labour allocation, development and resource use. The package sits further toward the interventionist pole than mainstream centre-left parties (Labour, SNP, Plaid), though short of the full renationalisation and capital-control programme of the Workers Party or the utility-focused public ownership of the Greens. Residual ordinary competition rules remain formally in place, but the dominant mechanical effect is a marked reduction in voluntary private decision-making across key sectors.

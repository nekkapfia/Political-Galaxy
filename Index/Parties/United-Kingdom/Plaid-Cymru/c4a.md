* Emphasis on reducing socio-economic barriers and improving educational opportunity.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Residual acceptance of quality variation within an open formal framework.
* Focus on Welsh institutional control of education rather than structural redesign of mobility mechanisms.

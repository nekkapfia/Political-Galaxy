## Regulation

### Overview
Plaid Cymru produces a mid-to-lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by retention and reinforcement of residual labour protections, residual green architecture, and preference for stronger collective and industrial-policy direction in a Welsh and UK context, without high-priority deregulation.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention and reinforcement of ordinary labour protections (unfair dismissal, minimum wage architecture, working-time residual, health-and-safety residual); expansion of collective rights preferred over deregulation.
* Retention of residual Climate Change Act architecture and major green delivery instruments; preference for high environmental standards rather than repeal.
* Support for industrial policy and public-investment direction in Welsh strategic sectors.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues; no broad bureaucracy-reduction or labour-market liberalisation programme is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The Plaid Cymru final set retains residual UK labour protections and residual green architecture. Collective labour-market institutions are reinforced rather than liberalised. Industrial-policy and public-investment direction is advanced for strategic Welsh sectors. Residual competition rules remain. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of labour and green restrictions combined with additional collective and industrial-policy direction rather than deregulation.

### Regulation Score
Retention of residual labour protections and residual green architecture, combined with preference for stronger collective institutions and industrial-policy direction, produces a Regulation Score of 50.

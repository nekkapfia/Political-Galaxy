### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 49**

Plaid Cymru’s non-tax policies would increase state direction relative to current Britain. Stronger public ownership and Welsh industrial strategy, accelerated green transition mandates, expanded employment protections, and planning rules with community and environmental safeguards would reduce the scope for voluntary economic decision-making in investment, energy, labour markets and development. The package sits on the interventionist side of the centre-left spectrum—more directive than Labour on ownership and green mandates, less totalising than the Greens or Workers Party—with a distinct Welsh prioritisation layer. Residual ordinary competition rules remain, but the dominant mechanical effect is increased state steering.

# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Plaid Cymru
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview
Plaid Cymru focuses on Welsh self-government and has limited direct influence over UK foreign-policy instruments. Like other sub-state nationalists it sits higher on Core 2B than parties that design and implement sanctions and military assistance.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Primary agenda is domestic and constitutional.
* Limited role in initiating or sustaining UK expeditionary or sanctions policy.
* General support for international law and multilateral norms without ownership of the interference toolkit.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. Parties that do not control the relevant state instruments necessarily interfere less. Plaid’s score reflects that structural fact plus residual alignment with broader progressive internationalism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52
Limited control of UK foreign-policy tools and domestic focus place Plaid Cymru in the low-to-mid 50s.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Welsh self-government is justified as the democratic self-determination of the Welsh political community.
* Community, language and cultural continuity are defended as collective goods of a living people.
* Social justice and public services are framed through fairness, equality and evidence-based delivery.
* Environmental and climate concerns are integrated with community and democratic priorities.
* Electoral mandate and practical governing competence are retained as central legitimating sources.
* National interest and the concrete welfare of Welsh communities appear as supporting claims.

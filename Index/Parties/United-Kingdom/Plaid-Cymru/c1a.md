# Cultural 1A: United Kingdom – Plaid Cymru
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 31**

### Overview
Plaid Cymru grounds political legitimacy in Welsh self-determination, community wellbeing and progressive social and environmental goals delivered through democratic means. Independence or maximal autonomy is framed as the right of the Welsh people to govern themselves, not as sacred national destiny. Organising sources are predominantly mundane—demos, community goods, fairness and competence—with modest progressive and ecological colour that does not reach Green-level transcendent intensity. Residual baseline of strongly mundane justification: legitimacy is organised through demotic procedure, welfare outcomes or pragmatic performance rather than higher-order ideological or sacred sources.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Welsh self-government justified as democratic self-determination of the Welsh political community.
* Community, language and cultural continuity defended as collective goods of a living people, primarily through democratic and policy means rather than sacred obligation.
* Social justice and public services framed through fairness, equality and evidence-based delivery.
* Environmental and climate concerns present and morally coloured, but typically integrated with community and democratic priorities rather than elevated as strict demos-overrides.
* Criticism of Westminster and centralisation cast as democratic deficit and neglect of Welsh interests, not as breach of higher law.
* Electoral mandate and practical delivery retained as central legitimating sources.
* Residual baseline of strongly mundane justification: legitimacy is organised through demotic procedure, welfare outcomes or pragmatic performance rather than higher-order ideological or sacred sources.

### Expanded Analysis
Scored as an individual party, Plaid organises justification through the democratic claims of the Welsh demos and through community-centred social-democratic and cultural goals. National and linguistic continuity matter, yet they are advanced as the preferences and goods of a living political community rather than as transcendent duties independent of consent. Ecological language appears but does not organise the whole programme at the intensity of dedicated green parties; it remains one moral colour among others rather than a binding higher law that regularly overrides majorities. Progressive fairness and competence complete a predominantly mundane profile. There is no organising role for divine command, revolutionary historical necessity, or nation-as-sacred-substance. The pattern is clear mundane lean with slightly more community and progressive colour than the purest independence-first currents, hence a modest step above Alba and in the same band as the SNP.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 31
Democratic self-determination, community goods and progressive fairness as organising sources, without sacred or strong ecological higher law, produce a clear mundane lean. Treated as a unique party, the score is 31.

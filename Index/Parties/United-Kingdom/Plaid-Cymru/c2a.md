# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Plaid Cymru
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 46**

### Overview
Plaid Cymru maintains formal individual legal personality while supporting category-conscious equality and diversity frameworks, producing a mixed but group-influenced allocation pattern.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Support for progressive equality and diversity instruments within Welsh institutional competence.
* Acceptance of protected-characteristic frameworks as relevant to representation and resource decisions.
* Formal individual rights residual continuing alongside category tools.
* National and linguistic identity more central to Cultural 3 / Core 2A questions than to routine person-vs-category allocation in employment.
* Limited evidence of systematic dismantling of category-preference machinery.

### Expanded Analysis
The party does not organise all allocation around multi-axis identity stacks, yet it does not restore pure person-unit merit as the exclusive public rule either. Category tools remain available and institutionally legitimate. Individual legal personality is the formal baseline, but group membership regularly influences measurement and priority. The result sits in the significant group-based allocation range.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 46
Continued institutional legitimacy of category tools without a person-only restoration programme places Plaid Cymru in the significant group-based allocation band. Score 46.

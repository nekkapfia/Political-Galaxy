# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Plaid Cymru
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 45**

### Overview

Plaid Cymru advocates substantial constitutional and policy change for Wales while operating inside devolution and UK-wide institutional constraints. Its observable preference is for sequenced, negotiated progress rather than compressed, anti-process acceleration. The score is low-to-mid.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Urgency language on Welsh self-government and selected progressive policy agendas.
* Practical positioning emphasises building support, negotiation and incremental constitutional advance rather than unilateral rapid rupture.
* Limited experience of unconstrained executive power; operates inside frameworks that favour consultation and sequencing.
* Observable pattern is transformative goals pursued through largely deliberative means.

### Expanded Analysis

Like other territorial parties with strong end-goals but constrained methods, Plaid Cymru does not design systematically against deliberative process. The score of 45 reflects that gradualist method preference.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 45

Transformative constitutional goals pursued primarily through negotiated and sequenced means place Plaid Cymru at 45.

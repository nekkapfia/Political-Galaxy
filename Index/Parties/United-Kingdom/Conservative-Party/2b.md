# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Conservative Party
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 40**

### Overview
Recent Conservative governments conducted or sustained major external interventions (Iraq legacy, Libya, Afghanistan), led post-2014 and post-2022 Ukraine support and sanctions, and treated sanctions and alliance pressure as primary tools. The interference toolkit remains fully available.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Leadership role in Ukraine military and sanctions support after 2022.
* Historical record of Iraq 2003, Libya 2011 and Afghanistan commitments.
* Extensive use of financial and trade sanctions as foreign-policy instruments.
* Aid and diplomatic practice carrying governance and human-rights conditions.
* Self-defence (including Falklands) is out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. Conservative foreign policy has repeatedly used military force, occupation-era stabilisation, sanctions and conditional diplomacy. Post-2011 caution on new large ground wars raises the score relative to the 2001–2011 peak, yet the retained toolkit and Ukraine posture keep the party in the interventionist band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 40
Sustained sanctions and Ukraine support, combined with a residual expeditionary tradition, produce a low-to-mid 40s score.

# Cultural 3: United Kingdom – Conservative Party
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 0 | 3B 38**

### Overview
The Conservative Party maintains complete ethnic openness. No ancestral or blood criterion exists for British membership. The Exclusion Test is not met. The party supports the existing framework of language requirements, the Life in the UK test, and citizenship ceremonies as conditions of naturalisation, treating cultural expectations as light-to-moderate rather than transformative. Immigration volume and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention and defence of the Life in the UK test, English language requirements, and citizenship ceremonies for naturalisation.
* Emphasis on contribution and integration as part of substantive membership without demanding deep cultural conversion.
* No ethnic or blood criteria for membership; birth and descent routes remain culturally unrestricted.
* Opposition to lowering existing cultural and knowledge thresholds, without significant further elevation.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is not met. 3A is 0. 3B is light-to-moderate because cultural requirements exist and are defended, but remain limited in depth and concentrated on the naturalisation route. Birth and descent routes carry zero cultural expectations, pulling the overall system score down. This aligns with Light Demand (30–39).

# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Conservative Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 62**

### Overview

The Conservative Party has demonstrated selective but real capacity for accelerated delivery when a priority is clearly set (Brexit completion under Johnson, COVID emergency measures, certain education and welfare reforms). At the same time it has frequently accommodated gradualist constraints, internal resistance, and extended sequencing in other domains. The net pattern is mid-range: higher than pure process parties, lower than platforms that treat rapid total transformation as the default mode.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Brexit delivery (2019–2020) pursued through compressed legislative timelines and controversial procedural mechanisms that prioritised speed over extended consultation.
* COVID-19 response involved rapid deployment of emergency powers, accelerated procurement, and compressed vaccine rollout — evidence of institutional capacity for high-intensity action when prioritised.
* Education policy (academisation, some curriculum and accountability reforms) showed periods of relatively rapid structural change alongside later slowing.
* Frequent internal and parliamentary resistance has produced dilution, delay, or reversal of other structural ambitions (planning reform, net-zero sequencing, some welfare changes).
* Platform and governing practice under recent leadership have mixed “bold action” language with explicit emphasis on stability and careful sequencing after periods of disruption.
* Contrast with Restore Britain / Reform UK: Conservatives retain greater residual acceptance of deliberative and institutional constraints.

### Expanded Analysis

The Conservative record shows that accelerated mechanisms are available and have been used effectively in selected high-priority domains. They have not become the sustained, cross-domain operating mode characteristic of the high-radicalism band. The score therefore sits in the mid range (60–69), reflecting real capacity without the systematic override of process that defines scores in the 70s and 80s. This matches the prior full Britain analysis calibration of 62.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 62

Selective but demonstrated capacity for compressed implementation on priority issues, combined with frequent retention of gradualist constraints elsewhere, places the Conservative Party at 62.

# Cultural 1B: United Kingdom – Conservative Party
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 66**

**Orthodoxy: 64, Tribalism: 68**

### Overview
The Conservative Party combines residual factional openness with rejection of Labour and Reform as governing alternatives in different domains. Internal ideological diversity lowers orthodoxy relative to more disciplined parties, producing a Moderate-to-Strong band score at the lower edge.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Persistent factional contest (One Nation, Thatcherite, national-conservative, residual liberal) limiting pure orthodoxy.
* Leadership discipline in office without elimination of organised internal currents.
* Rejection of Labour economic and progressive cultural frameworks as illegitimate governing programmes.
* Ambivalent boundary with Reform: competition plus partial policy absorption.
* Institutional continuity and electoral pragmatism as sources of openness to revision.
* Residual baseline of broad-church conservative practice that prevents extreme internal closure.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is moderate: the party’s long factional structure (One Nation, Thatcherite, national-conservative, residual liberal) means foundational principles are routinely contested in public. Leadership in office imposes discipline without extinguishing organised internal currents. There is no single closed ideological text enforced as non-negotiable higher law. Tribalism is moderate-high against Labour progressive frameworks, treated as illegitimate governing programmes on economy and culture, and variable toward Reform—competition combined with partial absorption. Institutional continuity and electoral pragmatism further open revision space. The institutional pattern is mid-range dogmatism characteristic of a long-governing broad-church party, calibrated at 66.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 66
Residual factional contest, leadership discipline without elimination of currents, rejection of Labour progressive frameworks, and electoral pragmatism produce moderate Orthodoxy with moderate-high Tribalism. The formula yields 66.

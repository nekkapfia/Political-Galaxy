## Regulation

### Overview
The Conservative Party produces a mid-to-upper Core 1B Regulation profile defined by planning system simplification, a lighter overall regulatory approach, and limited sectoral support with reduced state direction, while retaining residual net-zero architecture and ordinary employment protections.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Planning system simplification and reduction of red tape to reduce prior restraint on development and investment.
* Lighter overall regulatory approach with modest reduction in compliance burden across sectors.
* Sectoral support with lighter state direction in favoured sectors.
* Retention of residual Climate Change Act architecture and major green delivery instruments; no high-priority full repeal is locked.
* Retention of ordinary employment protections and residual labour-market rules; no high-priority labour-market deregulation is locked.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues.

### Expanded Analysis
The Conservative final set simplifies planning and reduces residual bureaucratic burden. Sectoral support is pursued with lighter state direction. Residual net-zero architecture and major green delivery instruments remain operative. Ordinary employment protections continue. Residual competition rules remain. The institutional pattern is modest reduction of residual bureaucratic and planning restraints combined with continuity of residual green architecture and ordinary labour protections.

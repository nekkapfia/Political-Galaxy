# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Conservative Party
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
The Conservative Party operates inside the UK’s universal-schooling system and accepts residual quality gradients as compatible with formal openness. It has favoured parental choice, academies, free schools, and (in principle) grammar-school expansion, while resisting policies that penalise private education or high-aspiration families. Formal access remains open; birth effects operate mainly through quality and network differences rather than locked assignment.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for grammar-school expansion in principle, though limited delivery in office.
* Emphasis on academies and free schools as mechanisms for improvement through autonomy and competition.
* Resistance to policies perceived as penalising private education or high-aspiration families.
* Acceptance of existing quality gradients while seeking to raise the floor through competition and targeted interventions.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules in education or occupation.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores the degree to which birth circumstances constrain life outcomes. Under the Conservative approach, universal practical access to schooling is preserved; remaining birth effects are quality gradients (better schools, early starts, networks) and stratified further/higher education. The party’s preference for selection and choice accepts differential pathway quality rather than prioritising equalisation of early resources. This keeps the score inside the 21–30 band: formal access exists for all, yet practical ease and quality remain patterned by family resources and early advantages. Soft statistical correlation without formal locked assignment does not push the score higher. Equity-lens diagnostics belong on Cultural 4B and are not scored here.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Universal schooling plus acceptance of quality gradients and selective mechanisms, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the 21–30 band (access exists but is not equally frictionless). Score 26.

# Cultural 4B: United Kingdom – Conservative Party
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 44**

### Overview
The Conservative Party retains the core Equality Act and Public Sector Equality Duty framework while actively contesting expansive systemic-oppression diagnostics and identity-based outcome engineering. Gaps are treated as compatible with equal opportunity when individual, cultural, family and socio-economic factors are given weight; equity interventions are accepted only in limited soft form. Residual baseline of limited equity-lens application: progressive gap-as-proof diagnostics are present in some domains but not dominant residual institutional practice.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention of the Equality Act 2010 and the Public Sector Equality Duty (“due regard”) as baseline legal architecture.
* Commissioning and defence of the Sewell Report / Commission on Race and Ethnic Disparities (2021), which rejected systemic-racism primacy and emphasised family, culture, socio-economic position and individual agency.
* Explicit opposition, under recent leadership, to race-based or identity-based preferences beyond narrow positive action; colour-blind merit language.
* Criticism of institutional DEI programmes and “woke” capture as divisive and empirically weak.
* Support for transparency tools (gender pay-gap reporting) without converting them into binding outcome mandates.
* No programme of hard quotas or mandatory group-parity targets in core domains.
* Residual baseline of limited equity-lens application: progressive gap-as-proof diagnostics are present in some domains but not dominant residual institutional practice.

### Expanded Analysis
The party’s observable framework is contested mid-low. It maintains soft equity infrastructure (PSED procedural duty, positive-action permissions, transparency reporting) inherited from prior legislation, yet systematically challenges the stronger gap-as-proof diagnostic. The Sewell Report constitutes a clear institutional push-back against the claim that persistent group gaps under formal equality of opportunity automatically prove unfair process. Leadership rhetoric and platform language prioritise individual merit, personal responsibility and socio-economic explanations over intersectional oppression narratives.

Hard equity mechanisms (binding quotas, sanctioned outcome targets) are absent from the programme. Soft mechanisms remain present but are framed as limited and reviewable rather than as the default corrective logic. This placement concerns only the opportunity→outcome diagnostic and remedial intensity; it is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 44
Retention of soft procedural equity tools alongside active contestation of systemic-oppression primacy, rejection of hard quotas, and elevation of individual, cultural and socio-economic explanations produce a mid-low contested score. The architecture sits above pure merit/libertarian positions but well below systems that lock the gap-as-proof diagnostic as primary.

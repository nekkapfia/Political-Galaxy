### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Individual liberty and civil rights are justified through constitutional principle, personal agency and protection from arbitrary state power.
* Fairness and opportunity are framed as practical and moral requirements of a decent society, often tied to evidence and outcomes.
* International cooperation and openness are presented as pragmatic and values-based policies.
* Institutional reform is justified by competence, accountability and democratic quality.
* Consent, procedure and constitutional form are treated as the proper operationalisation of rights.
* Evidence-based policy and delivery capacity appear as supporting legitimating sources.
* Electoral mandate remains a central source of authority.

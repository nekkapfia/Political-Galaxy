# Cultural 5A: United Kingdom – Liberal Democrats
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 29**

### Overview
The Liberal Democrats prioritise individual autonomy and progressive institutional reform over protection of inherited national or familial baselines.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Platform prioritises constitutional reform, progressive equality frameworks and individual autonomy over protection of inherited national or familial baselines.
* Education and cultural policy favour future-oriented and inclusive content rather than unmodified national-narrative continuity.
* Family and status rules oriented toward liberal individual rights rather than conventional transmission of traditional norms.
* Heritage and traditional cultural transmission receive no distinctive institutional priority relative to autonomy-centred reform.
* Residual progressive settlement in education content, family-status rules and public-sector equality duties retained and expanded.
* No programme for reversal of residual progressive curriculum framing or parental-authority restoration against progressive residual.

### Expanded Analysis
The Liberal Democrats treat individual autonomy and progressive institutional reform as primary goods of cultural policy. Constitutional reform, equality frameworks and future-oriented curriculum change take precedence over protection of inherited national or familial baselines. Heritage and traditional transmission receive no distinctive institutional priority. Domain hierarchy places education content and family-status design first: both are oriented toward progressive inclusion and autonomy rather than continuity as default. The residual progressive settlement is extended rather than reversed. Traditional forms survive only insofar as they do not obstruct autonomy-centred reform.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 29
Explicit progressive orientation on education inclusion and family diversity, expansion of residual progressive settlement in transmission institutions, and absence of any programme to restore traditional curriculum or family baselines place the Liberal Democrats in the low 21–30 band. Higher placement would require institutional protection of traditional transmission as a policy good in primary domains.

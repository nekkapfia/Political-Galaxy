# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Liberal Democrats
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
The Liberal Democrats have consistently supported comprehensive education, additional funding for disadvantaged schools, and widening participation in higher education. Formal access is universal; the party’s emphasis is on reducing birth-based quality and resource gaps within the existing open framework.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Long-standing opposition to grammar schools and support for comprehensive systems.
* Emphasis on additional funding for schools in disadvantaged areas and pupil-premium-style mechanisms.
* Support for widening participation in higher education.
* Focus on improving the quality and resources available to lower birth groups within the comprehensive framework.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint on life outcomes. The Liberal Democrat approach preserves universal practical access to schooling and seeks to narrow quality gradients through targeted funding and anti-selection preference. Residual differences in school quality, early advantages, and elite pathway access remain, but the programme actively works to reduce their practical weight. This places the party at the lower end of the 21–30 band (or the boundary with 11–20): access is universal and friction is actively reduced, yet systematic quality differences correlated with birth background have not been eliminated. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Universal schooling plus consistent anti-selection and gap-closing investment, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the lower 21–30 / upper 11–20 boundary. Score 22.

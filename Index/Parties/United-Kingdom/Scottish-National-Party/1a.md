# Core 1A: United Kingdom – Scottish National Party
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Score: 25**

### Overview

The SNP’s governing programme produces a restrictive Core 1A profile built from the Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021, retention of the UK Online Safety Act framework, institutional measures treating critical speech as potentially stirring up hatred, gender self-identification rules that generate secondary speech and association constraints, and enforcement emphasis on identity-hostile expression. The denser Scottish hate-crime framework is the decisive instrument. Cumulative weight sits inside the **19–27 — Ideological reprogramming / thought control** and upper **28–36** border. No Endos/Xenos split applies: heightened risk attaches to categories of expression (content-triggered), not to a stable, closed identity group with a dual legal structure.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021 — stirring-up hatred offences with a lower threshold than residual English provisions; covers race, religion, sexual orientation, transgender identity and other characteristics.
* Retention and application of Online Safety Act-style platform duties for illegal and priority content affecting Scottish users and regulated services.
* Institutional and public-sector rules treating criticism of gender identity, immigration or related topics as potential hate incidents or hostile-environment material.
* Gender self-identification policy and guidance generating secondary constraints on speech (misgendering) and association around single-sex spaces and services.
* Residual Public Order Act and communications offences remaining available.
* No programme to repeal the Scottish hate-crime architecture or to dismantle institutional orthodoxy layers.
* Ordinary direct-harm criminal prohibitions retained.
* Immigration and foreign-national status rules are Core 2A and are not scored here.
* Constitutional structure and independence programme are out of scope for Core 1A.

### Expanded Analysis

The SNP final set is defined by active criminal and institutional control of speech framed as hatred or identity-hostility, reinforced by platform duties and self-identification secondary effects. Mandatory or quasi-mandatory workplace and public-sector orthodoxy on protected characteristics operates as attitude and expression control. This aligns with **19–27 — Ideological reprogramming / thought control** where training and sanctions target deviant expression and beliefs, and with **28–36** where criminal speech law is extensive.

The differential that appears around identity-critical speech is content-triggered: anyone who utters the covered criticism faces the heightened institutional and criminal risk. There is no stable, discretely targetable out-group that lives under a separate legal dual structure analogous to Racial Laws or religious-dress bans. Under stability and non-overlap requirements the split is invalid. The score is the single more restrictive regime the system has locked in.

Framing as inclusion or safety is discarded. Only observable restrictions are scored.

### Why this produces a Score of 25

Scottish hate-crime offences with lowered threshold, Online Safety Act retention, institutional orthodoxy measures, self-identification speech and association constraints, and residual public-order offences place the regime inside **19–27** / border **28–36** at **25**. No Endos/Xenos split.

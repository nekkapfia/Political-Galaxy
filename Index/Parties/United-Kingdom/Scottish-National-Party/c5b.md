# Cultural 5B: United Kingdom – Scottish National Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 50**

### Overview

The SNP combines urgency language around independence and selected policy agendas with the practical constraints of governing inside the UK constitutional and Scottish institutional framework. Observable practice shows both accelerated pushes on priority items and substantial residual sequencing and consultation. The net score is mid-range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Strong urgency framing on independence referendums and related constitutional goals.
* Governing record in Scotland shows periods of relatively rapid legislative action on priority social and institutional files alongside extended consultation and legal constraints on others.
* Operates inside a devolution settlement and UK-wide legal framework that structurally limits pure compressed transformation.
* Internal culture contains both accelerationist (especially on independence) and more gradualist governing currents.
* Observable pattern is selective urgency within significant process constraints.

### Expanded Analysis

Independence-related urgency raises the score above pure gradualist parties, but the SNP has not adopted a systematic design against deliberative and legal sequencing comparable to high-radicalism platforms. The mid-range placement of 50 reflects that balance.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 50

Urgency on constitutional goals combined with substantial residual process and legal constraints produces a mid-range score of 50.

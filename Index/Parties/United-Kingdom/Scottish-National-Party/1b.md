## Regulation

### Overview
The Scottish National Party produces a mid-to-lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by retention and reinforcement of residual labour protections, residual green architecture, and preference for stronger collective, industrial-policy and public-investment direction, with independence as the primary structural objective.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Primary objective of Scottish independence; residual UK economic-regulation architecture continues during any transitional phase until replaced.
* Retention and reinforcement of ordinary labour protections; expansion of collective rights preferred over deregulation.
* Retention of residual green/net-zero architecture and major delivery instruments; preference for high environmental and industrial standards rather than repeal.
* Support for industrial policy, public investment and strategic-sector direction.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues during transition.
* No high-priority full labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
During any transitional independence process the residual UK labour-protection and residual green architecture continue. Post-independence the preferred settlement reinforces collective labour institutions, high environmental standards and industrial-policy direction rather than a deregulatory break. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of labour and green restrictions combined with additional collective and industrial-policy direction.

### Regulation Score
Retention of residual labour protections and residual green architecture, combined with preference for stronger collective institutions and industrial-policy direction, produces a Regulation Score of 49.

### Regulation Score & Justification

**Regulation Score: 50**

The SNP’s non-tax policies would increase state direction relative to current Britain. Expanded public ownership and Scottish industrial strategy, strong net zero and green industrial mandates, and expanded employment and collective protections would increase state influence over investment, energy allocation and labour markets. Planning remains under retained oversight with Scottish prioritisation. The package is clearly interventionist within a centre-left frame—comparable to or slightly more directive than Labour on ownership and green policy—with constitutional-nationalist prioritisation layered on top. No major deregulatory programme is proposed; residual ordinary competition and baseline environmental rules remain, but the dominant effect is increased state steering of production and investment.

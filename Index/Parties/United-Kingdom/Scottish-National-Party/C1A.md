* Progressive social policy is justified through rights, dignity and evolving secular norms.
* National identity is treated as the political community entitled to democratic self-government.
* Electoral mandate and the capacity to deliver better outcomes are retained as central legitimating sources.
* Competence and practical governing capacity appear as supporting claims.

# Core 2A: United Kingdom – Scottish National Party
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview
The Scottish National Party prioritises Scottish independence and the creation of a fully sovereign Scottish state, with EU accession as the preferred external framework after independence. The pattern is high formal sovereignty through new statehood, immediately followed by deliberate re-pooling of authority through European membership.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Primary objective is Scottish independence and the establishment of a fully sovereign Scottish state ending Westminster control over Scottish affairs.
* Supports an independent Scotland rejoining the European Union as the preferred post-independence external framework.
* UK external constraints (ECHR/Human Rights Act architecture, residual assimilated law, Trade and Cooperation Agreement obligations, Windsor Framework residual where relevant) continue during any transitional independence process until replaced by the new state’s arrangements.
* No high-priority programme of external isolation or non-EU alternative framework is locked; European pooling of authority is the intended post-independence pattern.
* NATO membership and multilateral treaty residual continue unless explicitly altered.

### Expanded Analysis
SNP’s final set removes Westminster control through Scottish independence and the creation of a new sovereign state. During any transitional phase the continuing UK external architecture (ECHR jurisdiction, residual assimilated law, TCA constraints, and Windsor Framework residual where applicable) remains in force until the new state replaces it. Once independence is achieved the preferred external framework is European Union membership, which immediately re-pools significant authority over law, borders, trade, monetary policy and institutional decision-making. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is therefore high formal sovereignty of new statehood offset by deliberate re-pooling through EU accession. Transitional external constraints and post-independence European pooling of authority form the locked final set; neither is omitted. Primary drag arises from the re-pooling step rather than from incomplete formal independence.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52
Independence produces high formal sovereignty. Immediate re-pooling through EU accession constitutes primary drag that places the final set inside the 51–59 Mid–Lower band. Transitional UK external constraints and post-independence European pooling of authority are explicit in the locked analysis. The score of 52 follows from high formal sovereignty immediately followed by deliberate external pooling of core authority rather than sustained high closure.

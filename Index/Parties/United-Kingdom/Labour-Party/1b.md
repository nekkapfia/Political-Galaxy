## Regulation

### Overview
Labour produces a mid-range Core 1B Regulation profile defined by expansion of residual labour protections (day-one unfair dismissal, strengthened collective bargaining), retention and reinforcement of residual green architecture through public ownership and green industrial strategy, and modest planning acceleration with retained oversight.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Day-one unfair dismissal rights and restrictions on zero-hours contracts and fire-and-rehire practices.
* Strengthened collective bargaining and expanded flexible working and parental leave entitlements.
* Great British Energy (public ownership) and National Wealth Fund as instruments of green industrial strategy and public investment.
* Borrowing for green investment and retention of residual Climate Change Act architecture and major delivery instruments.
* Planning reform to accelerate housebuilding with retained oversight.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues; no high-priority labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The Labour final set expands residual labour protections through day-one unfair dismissal rights, restrictions on zero-hours and fire-and-rehire practices, and strengthened collective bargaining. Residual green architecture is retained and reinforced through public ownership (Great British Energy) and a green industrial strategy with public investment direction. Planning is modestly accelerated while residual oversight remains. Residual competition rules continue. The institutional pattern is expansion of residual labour protections combined with reinforcement of residual green-industrial direction through public ownership and investment instruments rather than deregulation.

### Regulation Score
Expansion of residual labour protections, retention and reinforcement of residual green architecture through public ownership and green industrial strategy, and modest planning acceleration with retained oversight produce a Regulation Score of 52.

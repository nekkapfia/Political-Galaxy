# Cultural 3: United Kingdom – Reform UK
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 0 | 3B 48**

### Overview
Reform UK maintains complete ethnic openness: no ancestral or blood criterion for British membership. The Exclusion Test is not met. The party elevates cultural alignment, language, and commitment to British norms as meaningful conditions of full substantive membership, distinguishing formal status from real belonging. Immigration volume and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit rejection of ethnic or blood criteria for British membership; naturalisation and citizenship remain open to all ethnicities.
* Support for stronger integration and cultural expectations attached to naturalisation and substantive recognition as British.
* Emphasis that cultural alignment and commitment to British values should matter for full Endos status.
* Birth and descent routes continue to carry no cultural tests; requirements concentrate on the naturalisation pathway.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is not met. 3A is 0. 3B is moderate because Reform treats cultural alignment as a significant condition of full membership while stopping short of demanding total cultural transformation or severance of prior identities. Formal citizenship is not regarded as automatically sufficient for substantive belonging. This aligns with Light-to-Moderate to Moderate Demand (40–49).

### Why this produces 3A 0 / 3B 48
3A aligns with 0 (no ethnic bar). 3B aligns with 40–49 (elevated cultural expectations for substantive membership without transformative conversion).

# Core 2B: United Kingdom – Reform UK
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 60**

### Overview
Reform UK prioritises British national interest, criticises open-ended nation-building and liberal interventionism, and seeks to reorient aid and foreign spending. It supports Ukraine in a narrower frame while emphasising capacity limits and European responsibility.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit criticism of “forever wars” and nation-building projects.
* Preference for aid and diplomatic effort directed to clear national-interest outcomes.
* Support for Ukraine tempered by demands for burden-sharing and limits on British exposure.
* Residual willingness to use sanctions against clear threats.
* Self-defence of UK territory remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. Reform’s programme would shrink the transformative and open-ended elements of UK foreign policy while retaining selective pressure tools. One-term realistic outcome is a measurable reduction in expeditionary and democracy-export activity relative to Labour or Conservative baselines, producing a clearly higher score.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 60
National-interest framing and scepticism of open-ended intervention place Reform UK in the low 60s.

# Core 2A: United Kingdom – Reform UK
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 70**

### Overview
Reform UK seeks to end irregular migration, leave the ECHR, scrap the Windsor Framework and cap legal migration in order to restore unilateral national control over borders and law. Residual external pooling of authority is treated as the target for structural removal.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Commitment to stop small-boat crossings and deport those who arrive illegally.
* Explicit policy of leaving the ECHR to remove residual external legal barriers to removal; residual jurisdiction is to be ended.
* Policy of scrapping the Windsor Framework and restoring a single UK internal market and regulatory space; residual Northern Ireland external layer is to be removed.
* Target of limiting net legal migration to approximately 100,000 per year.
* Supports continued NATO membership and existing major trade agreements outside the EU legal order; residual post-Brexit assimilated law is to be actively diverged from where it constrains residual sovereign capacity.

### Expanded Analysis
Reform UK’s platform removes the three dominant residual external constraints on UK territorial and legal control: irregular settlement with low removal capacity, residual ECHR barriers to deportation, and the residual Windsor Framework’s internal regulatory border. Small-boat crossings are to be stopped and those who arrive illegally deported; the ECHR is to be left so that residual removal decisions sit under domestic authority; the Windsor Framework is to be scrapped so that Great Britain and Northern Ireland form a single internal market and regulatory space. Legal migration is capped at approximately 100,000 net per year. NATO membership and existing major trade agreements outside the EU legal order are retained. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is systematic recovery of residual unilateral control over borders, removals and internal territorial integrity, with residual constraints limited to ordinary alliance and trade commitments that do not transfer core residual law-making authority. Primary drag from residual external pooling of authority is deliberately reduced.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 70
Ending irregular settlement, leaving the ECHR, scrapping the Windsor Framework and capping legal migration reduce residual foreign legal interference and restore residual unilateral border control. Residual external pooling of authority that continues is limited to ordinary alliance and trade residual. The final set therefore sits in the mid–high band. The score of 70 reflects structural recovery of residual sovereign authority over territory and law rather than incremental operational adjustment inside residual external architectures.

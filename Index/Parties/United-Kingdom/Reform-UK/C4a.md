# Cultural 4A: United Kingdom – Reform UK
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
Reform UK places greater emphasis on traditional meritocratic selection, skills-based pathways, and resistance to policies that prioritise group-based outcomes over individual achievement. Formal access remains open; the party accepts differential pathway quality arising from selection and family effort more readily than gap-closing parties.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for expanding selective education and grammar schools.
* Scepticism toward diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives that may dilute merit-based selection.
* Emphasis on practical skills, apprenticeships, and traditional routes to advancement.
* Acceptance of residual quality gradients as compatible with formal openness and individual agency.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. Reform UK operates inside the same universal-schooling baseline as other major UK parties. Its distinctive stance is stronger preference for selection and traditional merit routes, which accepts rather than prioritises the reduction of birth-linked quality differences. Formal pathways remain open; practical ease and quality continue to correlate with family resources and early advantages. This keeps the score inside the 21–30 band: access exists for all, yet friction and quality differences remain consequential. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Universal schooling plus preference for selection and acceptance of quality gradients, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the 21–30 band. Score 26.

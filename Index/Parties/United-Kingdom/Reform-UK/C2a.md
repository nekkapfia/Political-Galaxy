# Cultural 2A: United Kingdom – Reform UK
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 82**

### Overview
Reform UK’s programme treats the individual as the sole legitimate unit of public allocation. Explicit commitments to dismantle category-based preference machinery restore person-level merit as the default rule across employment, education, contracting and institutional practice.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Manifesto commitment to replace or repeal the Equality Act’s positive-action provisions on the grounds that they require discrimination by group membership.
* Explicit policy to scrap all DEI roles, quotas and targets across the civil service, police, NHS, schools and public bodies.
* Mandate for single-sex spaces and facilities based on biological sex rather than self-identified gender.
* Ban on transgender ideology in primary and secondary schools; requirement that schools maintain single-sex facilities.
* Government contracts and public appointments to be scored on value and performance, not diversity targets.
* Local-government record of actively removing DEI structures where Reform holds power.

### Expanded Analysis
The party’s platform systematically removes institutional mechanisms that allocate opportunity or status according to group membership. By treating positive action and DEI infrastructure as illegitimate and by restoring biological sex as the criterion for single-sex provision, the programme makes individual characteristics the exclusive public evaluative unit. Residual equality-of-treatment rules remain, but category-preference tools are eliminated. The overall pattern is high individualism with only the narrowest residual exceptions.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 82
Explicit dismantling of positive-action architecture and DEI machinery, combined with restoration of person- and biology-based criteria in core domains, places Reform UK in the high-individualism band (80–89). Score 82.

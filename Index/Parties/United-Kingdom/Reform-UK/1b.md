## Regulation

### Overview
Reform UK produces a higher Core 1B Regulation profile defined by removal of residual net-zero targets and green levies, planning liberalisation, reduction of retained EU-derived regulation, and modest reduction in employment regulation and bureaucracy, while retaining ordinary competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Removal of residual net-zero targets and associated green levies and delivery mandates (Climate Change Act architecture and major instruments directed for repeal or radical reduction).
* Planning liberalisation and fast-track infrastructure to reduce prior restraint on development and investment decisions.
* Reduction in retained EU-derived regulation and associated compliance requirements.
* Modest reduction in employment regulation and bureaucracy; ordinary unfair-dismissal and core health-and-safety residual largely continues.
* Retention of residual competition-law baseline (CMA enforcement, merger control) and basic non-green environmental standards.
* Residual secondary bureaucratic baseline reduced but not eliminated.

### Expanded Analysis
The Reform UK final set removes the residual net-zero target architecture and associated green levies and delivery instruments. Planning liberalisation reduces prior restraint on development and investment. Retained EU-derived regulatory layers are reduced. Employment regulation and bureaucracy are modestly lightened while ordinary core labour protections continue. Residual competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards remain. The institutional pattern is substantial reduction of residual green-industrial and mandate layers combined with retained ordinary labour and competition baselines.

### Regulation Score
Removal of residual net-zero targets and green levies, planning liberalisation, reduction of retained EU-derived regulation, and modest reduction in employment regulation and bureaucracy, while retaining ordinary competition rules and basic non-green environmental standards, produces a Regulation Score of 66.

## Taxation

Primary method applies.

* Raising personal income tax thresholds and fuel duty reductions: £8–14 billion cost
* Abolition or major reduction of green levies: £6–10 billion cost
* Business rates and SME reliefs: £4–7 billion cost

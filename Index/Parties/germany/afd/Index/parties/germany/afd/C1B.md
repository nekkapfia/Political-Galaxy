# Cultural 1B: Germany – AfD
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 84**

### Overview

AfD exhibits high internal discipline on migration, EU, and national-identity red lines, repeated internal purges aimed at ideological coherence, and consistent treatment of the post-2015 immigration consensus and much of the established party system as illegitimate rather than merely mistaken. Rival frameworks are often framed as threats to demos continuity, not as legitimate alternates within a shared constitutional game.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Strong internal discipline and rejection of deviation from core party line on migration and sovereignty.
* Clear rejection of the post-2015 immigration consensus and much of the established party system.
* Internal purges and faction management to maintain coherence.
* Strong opposition to supranational (especially EU) frameworks as illegitimate overrides.
* Verfassungsschutz conflict and “firewalls” from other parties reinforce mutual illegitimacy framing (external tribalism evidence).

### Expanded Analysis

Orthodoxy is high on the issues that define the party; tribalism is high in the sense that rivals are often denied full legitimacy rather than engaged as empirical competitors only. Limited federal governing record means discipline is observed in opposition, Land practice, and congress control rather than cabinet collective responsibility. Score remains high-band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 84

Sustained orthodoxy mechanisms plus illegitimacy attribution to rival frameworks place AfD at **84** on Dogmatism.

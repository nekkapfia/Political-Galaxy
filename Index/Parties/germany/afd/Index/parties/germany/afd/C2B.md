# Cultural 2B: Germany – AfD
## Pride / National Identity Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview

AfD is the main parliamentary vehicle for affirmative national pride and rejection of guilt-first framing of German identity. It criticises the “cult of guilt,” claims cultural and historical figures as German, and promotes Leitkultur-adjacent continuity. Even so, much rhetoric remains reactive within a political culture that treats strong national pride as extreme, which caps confident affirmative scoring.

### Relevant Policies / Elements

* Explicit critique of guilt-centred remembrance culture; call for pride in German culture and achievements.
* Rejection of traditional German national identity as inherently illegitimate.
* Leitkultur and German language as state language; cultural continuity against multiculturalism.
* Patriotic cultural policy supporting customs, traditions, and positive historical memory (programme language).
* Family and demographic continuity framed nationally (also C5A-relevant).

### Expanded Analysis

C2B measures affirmative pride versus critical or post-national frames. AfD is clearly on the affirmative side among major parties. Reactive posture and opposition status temper the score: the party campaigns against de-nationalisation more than it administers a stable high-pride institutional settlement. Mid-band affirmative.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52

Strong anti-guilt and Leitkultur mechanisms raise the score; reactive rather than fully institutionalised affirmative pride keeps it mid-band. **52**.

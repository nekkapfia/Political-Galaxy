# Core 1A: Germany – Die Linke
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 32**  
**Xenos Score: 27**

### Overview

Die Linke’s programme produces a restrictive Core 1A profile built from strong anti-racism and anti-discrimination commitments, defence of hate-speech enforcement against right-wing and racist expression, support for platform regulation, full alignment with LGBTIQA* and self-identification agendas, and identity-protective framing of migration-related speech. Limited governing reach means less nationwide training infrastructure than the Greens, but the normative direction is clearly densifying. Heightened intensity on specific identity categories justifies a limited Endos/Xenos split.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Strong programmatic defence of criminal hate-speech law and its use against racist, right-wing extremist and related group-directed expression; no narrowing agenda.
* Support for platform regulation against illegal and hateful content in the NetzDG–DSA framework.
* Explicit opposition to racism, antimuslim racism, antisemitism, homophobia and related forms of group-directed hostility as core programme commitments.
* Full support for LGBTIQA* rights including self-identification positions; opposition to “rolling back” queer rights (explicit contrast with BSW).
* Migration-related speech heavily framed through anti-racism and equal rights independent of origin; critical speech on migration/Islam faces elevated normative and mobilisation pressure.
* Anti-discrimination and minority-protection frameworks treated as organising principles; institutional pressure where the party has local or cultural influence.
* Limited state capacity for nationwide mandatory attitude-correction systems, but clear normative support for diversity and anti-discrimination institutional cultures.
* Retention of ordinary direct-harm criminal prohibitions.

### Expanded Analysis

Die Linke is identity-protective and anti-discrimination maximalist on paper. Programmes elevate anti-racism (including antimuslim racism), queer rights and minority protection as non-negotiable. Hate-speech enforcement and platform duties against right-wing and racist expression are affirmed. Gender self-identification and related institutional agendas are supported; the party positions itself against any rollback associated with BSW.

Limited governing reach means fewer embedded nationwide training empires than under Green federal/Land power. Densification therefore runs more through norms, mobilisation and local institutional practice than through administrative systems. That still leaves a dense final set: criminal/regulatory baseline plus strong identity-protective speech politics.

Speech critical of specific protected identity categories — especially racialised groups, Muslims, and queer/self-ID frameworks — attracts heightened political and mobilisation-backed scrutiny. This is a stable, discretely targetable intensity differential, sufficient for a limited Endos/Xenos split.

Die Linke belongs on the progressive densification side of the German field, not the resistance side. It is denser than SPD on identity-protective intensity and lighter than Greens on administrative embedding.

### Why this produces Endos 32 / Xenos 27

Hate-speech defence, platform regulation, strong anti-racism/antimuslim-racism and queer/self-ID commitments, and identity-protective framing of migration speech place Endos at 32. Heightened intensity on those identity categories produces Xenos 27.

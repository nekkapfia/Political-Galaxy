# Core 2B: France – La France Insoumise
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 46**

### Overview
La France Insoumise is critical of NATO, of Western military intervention and of aspects of Ukraine policy framed as escalation. It favours multipolar and anti-imperialist framings that reduce support for the mainstream interference toolkit. Residual baseline still accepts selected solidarity and diplomatic instruments.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Criticism of NATO expansion and of Western military interventions.
* Preference for diplomacy and multipolar accommodation over sanctions-led pressure in several theatres.
* Anti-imperialist orientation that limits enthusiasm for democracy-export tools.
* Residual baseline of selective external engagement: multipolar and solidarity framings remain, while the heaviest kinetic and sanctions instruments are resisted.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. LFI’s programme would reduce French participation in NATO-centric and sanctions-heavy campaigns. It does not reach pure isolationism; residual multipolar and solidarity tools keep the score below the highest non-interventionist placements, producing a mid-to-high 40s result.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 46
Anti-interventionist and multipolar framing, residual selective engagement instruments, and absence of a pure isolationist programme place LFI in the mid-to-high 40s.

# Cultural 1A: La-France-Insoumise
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
La France Insoumise organises moral legitimacy around popular sovereignty, concrete interests of ordinary citizens, systemic critique, and electoral mobilisation. These are secular this-worldly claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Popular sovereignty and the concrete interests of ordinary citizens are cited as central sources of legitimacy.
* Electoral mobilisation and popular assemblies are treated as legitimate expressions of the people’s will.
* Concrete improvements in living standards and public services appear as goals.
* Systemic injustice and the illegitimacy of current structures are presented as requiring transformative change within a secular frame.
* Anti-imperialism and opposition to financial power are framed as political and moral claims.
* Ecological and social justice claims are advanced inside the same secular transformative frame.
* Historical left and republican traditions are invoked as continuity and inheritance for present political duty.

### Expanded Analysis
Popular sovereignty, concrete interests, electoral mobilisation, systemic critique, and delivery goals organise the moral conversation. These are secular this-worldly claims. No divine command, Cosmic law, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. (Ecological claims here are not framed as binding planetary higher-law independent of demos will in the manner of the Green parties.) Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications. The foundation is Mundane.

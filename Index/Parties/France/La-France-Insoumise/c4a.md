# Cultural 4A: France – La France Insoumise
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 24**

### Overview
La France Insoumise emphasises structural reduction of class barriers in education and opportunity while operating inside the French universal-schooling and selective higher-education system. Formal access remains open; residual quality gradients correlated with birth background are treated as consequential.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong class-analytic critique of educational and occupational stratification.
* Emphasis on expanding public education and reducing elite pathway barriers.
* Preference for structural remediation of socio-economic gaps.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Residual quality gradients within an open formal framework.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. LFI inherits the French baseline of universal schooling plus a selective higher-education tier. Its distinctive contribution is a heavier class-analytic framing that treats residual gradients as structurally significant. Formal pathways remain open; practical ease continues to correlate with family resources. This places the score in the mid 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 24
Universal schooling plus class-analytic gap-closing emphasis, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the mid 21–30 band. Score 24.

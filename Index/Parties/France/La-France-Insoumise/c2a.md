# Cultural 2A: France – La France Insoumise
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
La France Insoumise emphasises collective and category-based frameworks for justice and allocation, subordinating pure individual-merit approaches to group analysis and remedy.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Strong support for collective and category-conscious approaches to rights and distribution.
* Acceptance of identity and social-group frameworks as organising units of political claim.
* Formal individual legal personality retained as residual.
* Rejection of pure person-unit meritocracy as sufficient.
* Alignment with expansive equality and anti-discrimination instruments that elevate group location.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant unit of measure is the collective or identity category rather than the sovereign individual. Individual agency operates inside group-structured constraints and remedies. The pattern is very strong group subordination relative to high-individualism systems.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Systematic elevation of category and collective over person as the unit of allocation places La France Insoumise in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 26.

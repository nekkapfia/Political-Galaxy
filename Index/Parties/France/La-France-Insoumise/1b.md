## Regulation

### Overview
La France Insoumise proposes extensive public ownership in energy and key services, strong labour protections, ecological planning, price controls, and significant state direction of investment.

### Relevant Policies
- Extensive public ownership in energy and strategic services
- Strong labour protections and collective rights
- Ecological planning and green industrial direction
- Price controls and state intervention in key markets
- Expanded public investment and planning tools

### Expanded Analysis
Extensive public ownership in energy and strategic services is the primary restrictive mechanism. Price controls, ecological planning and expanded public investment tools further reduce independent decision-making in key markets. Strong labour protections increase rigidity. The package sits at the upper edge of the 28–38 band / lower 39–49 boundary.

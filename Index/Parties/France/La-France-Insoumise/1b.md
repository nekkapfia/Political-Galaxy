## Regulation

### Overview
La France Insoumise produces a lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by extensive public ownership in energy and strategic services, strong residual and expanded labour protections, ecological planning and green industrial direction, price controls, and significant state direction of investment.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Extensive public ownership in energy and strategic services.
* Strong residual and expanded labour protections and collective rights under residual Code du travail architecture.
* Ecological planning and green industrial direction; residual SNBC architecture retained and intensified.
* Price controls and state intervention in key markets.
* Expanded public investment and planning tools directing capital and production.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues but is subordinated to public-ownership and industrial-policy priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues and is deepened; no high-priority labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The LFI final set advances extensive public ownership in energy and strategic services. Residual Code du travail labour protections are retained and expanded through stronger collective rights. Residual SNBC architecture is intensified through ecological planning and green industrial direction. Price controls and expanded public investment tools further reduce independent decision-making in key markets. Residual competition rules remain but are secondary to public-ownership and planning priorities. The institutional pattern is substantial expansion of residual green-industrial and public-ownership direction combined with expanded labour protections, significantly increasing state control over investment, production and resource allocation.

### Regulation Score
Extensive public ownership in energy and strategic services, strong residual and expanded labour protections, ecological planning and green industrial direction, price controls, and expanded public investment tools produce a Regulation Score of 38.

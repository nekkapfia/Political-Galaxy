# Cultural 5A: France – La France Insoumise
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview
LFI drives progressive reform on gender, family diversity and education content while treating traditional hierarchical and religiously grounded norms as requiring reform.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Platform and parliamentary positions supporting progressive reform of family-status rules, including recognition of diverse family forms beyond conventional marriage-centred transmission.
* Support for gender self-identification frameworks and related institutional recognition in law and public administration.
* Education and cultural policy positions favouring inclusive and critical reframing of national historical narratives rather than unmodified continuity teaching as the organising curriculum frame.
* Traditional hierarchical and religiously grounded social norms treated as objects of legislative and cultural reform rather than as baselines requiring protection.
* Multicultural and inclusive frameworks preferred in public institutions when they conflict with inherited confessional or customary authority.
* Residual republican laïcité and institutional form continue, but pre-progressive family and education content is not defended as the operative default.

### Expanded Analysis
Primary domains under LFI’s programme run progressive. Family-status and gender-recognition positions treat inherited conventional transmission as open to redesign; education content is oriented toward inclusive and critical narrative rather than chronological national continuity as the protected frame; confessional and hierarchical norms are reform targets. The residual baseline in contemporary France is already a post-1960s progressive settlement on many family and cultural questions, with republican laïcité as an institutional red line. LFI does not seek to reverse that settlement back toward pre-progressive family or curriculum defaults; it pushes further along the progressive axis. Material heritage and republican symbols may be retained, but they are secondary under the Cultural 5A domain hierarchy. Progressive primacy in education framing, family-status rules and institutional transmission of norms is consistent and unsoftened by any programme to restore conventional family law or national-continuity curriculum as the baseline.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28
Concrete progressive positions on family status, gender recognition and education narrative, combined with treatment of traditional hierarchical and religious norms as reform material and no defence of pre-progressive baselines in primary domains, place LFI in the low 21–30 band. Residual republican form does not outweigh progressive primacy in the high-weight transmission domains. Adjacent higher bands would require selective institutional protection of traditional family or curriculum continuity that the locked analysis does not show.

# Cultural 2A: France – Renaissance
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview
Renaissance retains formal individual legal personality while supporting and operating within category-conscious equality and diversity frameworks, producing a mixed but group-influenced allocation pattern.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Support for parity, diversity and equality instruments within French institutional practice.
* Acceptance of protected-characteristic and representation frameworks as relevant to allocation.
* Formal individual rights residual continuing alongside category tools.
* Limited evidence of systematic dismantling of category-preference machinery.
* Centrist managerial approach that treats category tools as available policy instruments.

### Expanded Analysis
Category membership remains a legitimate and recurring factor in institutional measurement and representation. Individual legal personality is the formal baseline, but it is regularly balanced against group-conscious rules. The pattern is significant group-based allocation rather than pure person-unit default.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48
Continued institutional legitimacy of category tools without a person-only restoration programme places Renaissance in the significant group-based allocation band. Score 48.

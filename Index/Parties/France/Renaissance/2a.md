# Core 2A: France – Renaissance
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 41**

### Overview
Renaissance prioritises further EU integration in defence, fiscal coordination and migration, advancing the primacy of EU law and common European border-management mechanisms. External pooling of authority is actively expanded.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong support for further EU integration, including in defence, fiscal coordination and migration.
* Favours European strategic autonomy while remaining inside NATO.
* Accepts and advances the primacy of EU law and institutions in areas of shared competence.
* Supports common European asylum and border-management mechanisms.
* Supports European industrial and regulatory policy as the primary framework, limiting purely national approaches; euro and ECB monetary framework continue.

### Expanded Analysis
Renaissance supports further EU integration in defence, fiscal coordination and migration, advances the primacy of EU law in shared competences, and favours common European asylum and border-management mechanisms. European industrial and regulatory policy is treated as the primary framework. The euro and ECB constraints continue. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is deliberate construction of European-level authority at the expense of unilateral national control. EU membership architecture and legal supremacy constitute primary drag and are actively expanded rather than resisted. National residual capacity is exercised inside a densifying external framework.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 41
Further EU integration in defence, fiscal and migration policy with EU-law primacy emphasises European authority over national unilateralism. External pooling of authority is explicit and preferred, placing the final set inside the 41–50 Low–Mid band. Constraints that continue and are expanded are locked. The score of 41 follows from prioritisation of international integration rather than recovery of unilateral control.

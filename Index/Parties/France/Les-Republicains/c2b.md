# Cultural 2B: France – Les Républicains
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 64**

### Overview
Les Républicains maintain a broadly positive national self-image and defend traditional French symbols and continuity, while operating inside a political culture that contains substantial critical content. Residual baseline of mixed or conditional national framing: pride and critical perspectives coexist without a single dominant residual direction.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of republican symbols, the French language and core national commemorations.
* Education and cultural rhetoric that still give space to national achievements alongside critical material.
* Opposition to the most aggressive de-nationalising or iconoclastic projects.
* Patriotism is frequent but usually paired with republican and democratic qualifications.
* Residual baseline of mixed or conditional national framing: pride and critical perspectives coexist without a single dominant residual direction.

### Expanded Analysis
LR affirms national continuity and resists radical de-nationalisation, yet does not make national pride the sole organising principle of education and culture. The score sits in the Admiration band: strongly positive self-image with critical perspectives still present and tolerated.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 64
Broadly affirmative stance toward French national symbols and continuity, without authoritarian enforcement of pride, places Les Républicains in the Admiration band.

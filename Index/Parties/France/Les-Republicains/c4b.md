# Cultural 4B: France – Les Républicains
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 40**

### Overview
Les Républicains retain residual soft equity tools while prioritising individual merit, republican universalism and equal treatment. Expansive identity-equity engineering is resisted.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for core anti-discrimination law within a republican equal-treatment frame.
* Emphasis on individual opportunity, education and merit.
* Scepticism toward expansive DEI and identity-preference frameworks.
* Soft equity tools, where present, remain limited and secondary.

### Expanded Analysis
The party sits in the contested mid-low zone. A weak-to-moderate gap diagnostic coexists with dominant individual-merit and universalist language. Hard quotas are not central. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 40

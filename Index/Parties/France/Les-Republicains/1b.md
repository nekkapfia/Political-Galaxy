## Regulation

### Overview
Les Républicains favour lower regulatory burden, labour-market flexibility, nuclear support and a more cautious approach to additional climate mandates, while retaining the social-market floor.

### Relevant Policies
- Reduction of regulatory and bureaucratic burden on enterprise
- Labour-market flexibility and lower social charges on hiring
- Support for nuclear and domestic energy; cautious climate-mandate expansion
- Lighter industrial direction than left parties

### Expanded Analysis
Lower regulatory and bureaucratic burden plus labour-market flexibility are the main liberalising moves. Nuclear preference and caution on further climate mandates limit additional coercive layers. The residual social-market floor remains, so the package is a modest shift toward freer enterprise rather than a structural break. Score sits at the upper end of the social-market range.

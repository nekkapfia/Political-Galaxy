# Core 2A: France – Les Républicains
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 51**

### Overview
Les Républicains support EU membership with stronger border controls, asylum reform and intergovernmental methods, resisting further federalisation in sensitive national domains. External pooling of authority continues but is contested on operational intensity.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Supports EU membership combined with stronger national and European external border controls.
* Favours reform of EU asylum and migration rules to increase returns and reduce irregular inflows.
* Defends French institutional and cultural specificities within the European framework.
* Supports continued NATO membership alongside retention of France’s independent nuclear deterrent.
* Supports intergovernmental decision-making methods and resists further federalisation in sensitive national domains; euro framework continues.

### Expanded Analysis
Les Républicains operate inside the existing European architecture while attempting to shift its practical centre of gravity toward national and intergovernmental control. Stronger external border enforcement and asylum reform aimed at higher returns are the principal sovereignty-relevant demands; further federalisation in sensitive domains is resisted; France’s independent nuclear deterrent is retained alongside NATO membership. The party does not propose exit from the EU or the euro. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is enforcement and preference-defence within continued membership—tighter operational control without recovery of formal competences. Primary drag remains the residual EU membership and legal-supremacy architecture; intensity is moderated by intergovernmental preference and border-control demands.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 51
Stronger borders and intergovernmental preference inside continued EU membership assert national priorities without removing European legal constraints. External pooling of authority continues and is explicit, placing the final set inside the 51–59 Mid–Lower band. The score of 51 follows from operational recovery attempts inside a retained external framework rather than structural exit.

# Cultural 2A: France – Les Républicains
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 60**

### Overview
Les Républicains maintain formal individual legal personality and republican equality rhetoric while accepting residual category instruments and showing only partial resistance to progressive identity expansion.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Formal equality and individual rights as the residual legal baseline.
* Acceptance of some parity and diversity instruments within the existing French framework.
* Selective resistance to the most expansive self-ID and identity-preference proposals.
* Residual category tools remaining institutionally legitimate.
* Not a systematic dismantling programme and not a multi-axis progressive identity regime.

### Expanded Analysis
The party operates inside the existing French equality architecture. Individual legal personality remains the formal default, but category tools continue to operate and are not the object of a comprehensive removal programme. The pattern is moderate-to-predominantly individualist with residual group-based allocation still present.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 60
Person-default residual with retained category tools and only partial rollback of identity expansion place Les Républicains at the boundary of the moderate and predominantly individualist bands. Score 60.

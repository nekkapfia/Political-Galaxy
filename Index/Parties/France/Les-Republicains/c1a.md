# Cultural 1A: France – Les Républicains
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
Les Républicains present themselves as the party of order, controlled immigration, economic competence and defence of French republican institutions. Core aims are responsible government prioritising security, national cohesion and fiscal seriousness. Organising sources are clearly mundane: citizen interest, institutional stewardship and competence.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Immigration and security justified by protection of the concrete interests and safety of French citizens and preservation of social cohesion.
* Institutional continuity and governing competence presented as practical necessities of a functioning republic.
* National identity and republican order defended as the historical framework that has served France, primarily as practical inheritance rather than sacred higher law.
* Residual Gaullist sovereignty language secondary to order and competence.
* Elite and European constraints criticised when they undermine national control capacity.
* Electoral mandate and delivery as central legitimating sources.

### Expanded Analysis
Les Républicains organise justification through citizen interest, order and institutional pragmatism. Republican and Gaullist references supply historical colour but do not elevate a transcendent duty above democratic and practical governing requirements. Immigration and security trade-offs are resolved by reference to living citizens’ safety and cohesion. The pattern is clear mundane lean, above pure national-populism only insofar as institutional and competence language remains thicker.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Mundane sources—citizen interest, order, competence, institutional pragmatism—are primary. Transcendent sources are limited. The score is 26.

# Cultural 5A: France – Les Républicains
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 54**

### Overview
Les Républicains defend republican heritage and relatively conservative family and education positions while accepting key progressive social reforms with reluctance rather than enthusiasm.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of republican heritage, national continuity language and relatively conservative positions on family and education content relative to the left and centre.
* Acceptance of key progressive social reforms (post-1960s family and status settlement) with reluctance rather than a programme of full reversal.
* Public ritual and republican symbols retained as continuity anchors.
* Critique of rapid progressive cultural change in education and status rules without a full restorationist programme across family law and curriculum design.
* Residual progressive settlement remains largely in place; traditionalist pressure is selective rather than systematic institutional redesign.
* Education and family positions more continuity-oriented than PS/Renaissance/LFI, but short of RN-style systematic national-continuity baseline demands.

### Expanded Analysis
Les Républicains sit in the contested mid-range because primary domains are mixed. They defend republican heritage and push more conservative family and education language than the progressive parties, while accepting the broad post-1960s status settlement rather than dismantling it. The residual baseline is still largely progressive on family-status and inclusive education frameworks; LR does not reimpose pre-progressive conventional transmission as the legal default across the board. Domain hierarchy shows selective continuity pressure in education narrative and family rhetoric, with republican ritual as secondary support, but progressive residual frameworks are not systematically reversed. That produces more preservationist signal than the left without the institutional baseline restoration characteristic of mid-high traditionalist programmes.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 54
Relatively conservative family and education positions plus republican heritage defence, combined with reluctant acceptance of key progressive social reforms and no full restorationist programme, place LR in the contested mid band (51–59). Mid-high placement would require traditional transmission to be institutionalised as the protected default in curriculum and family-status law; the locked analysis shows selective pressure inside a progressive residual settlement.

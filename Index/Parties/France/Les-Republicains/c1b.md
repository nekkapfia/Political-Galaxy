# Cultural 1B: France – Les Républicains
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 68**

**Orthodoxy: 66, Tribalism: 70**

### Overview
Les Républicains retain residual Gaullist and conservative foundations with real internal contest and leadership turnover, while elevating Tribalism against both LFI left-populism and RN national-populism. Orthodoxy is moderated by factional structure; the net score sits in the Moderate-to-Strong band.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Residual Gaullist-conservative and centre-right foundations as identity markers.
* Real internal factional contest and leadership turnover limiting total orthodoxy.
* Rejection of LFI economic radicalism and of RN as governing partner (cordon practice).
* Willingness to revise tactical positions under electoral pressure.
* Alignment with republican institutional norms against extremes.
* Residual baseline of broad-church centre-right practice that prevents extreme internal closure.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is moderate-to-high rather than extreme: residual Gaullist-conservative foundations exist, but real factional contest, leadership turnover and tactical revision under pressure prevent the closed discipline characteristic of higher-scoring parties. Tribalism is elevated against dual out-groups—LFI left-populism and RN national-populism—through both rhetorical framing and practical cordon behaviour. Alignment with republican institutional norms reinforces boundaries without producing state-party monopoly. The institutional pattern is Moderate-to-Strong dogmatism typical of mainstream centre-right parties with residual pluralist structure, calibrated at 68.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 68
Residual conservative foundations combined with real internal contest, dual rejection of left-populist and national-populist extremes, and cordon practice produce moderate Orthodoxy with elevated Tribalism. The formula yields 68.

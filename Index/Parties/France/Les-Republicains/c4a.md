# Cultural 4A: France – Les Républicains
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 24**

### Overview
Les Républicains operate inside the French universal-schooling and selective higher-education system and generally defend standards, merit, and residual selective pathways while accepting quality gradients correlated with family background and early performance. Formal access remains open.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of academic standards and selective higher-education pathways (classes préparatoires, grandes écoles).
* Preference for merit and performance as the primary basis for advancement.
* Acceptance of residual quality gradients as compatible with formal openness.
* Targeted rather than structural approaches to socio-economic gaps.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. LR inherits the French baseline and leans toward preservation of selective and merit-based elements. Formal access remains universal; practical ease and quality continue to correlate with family resources and early performance. This places the score in the mid 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 24
Universal schooling plus defence of selective/merit pathways and residual quality gradients, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the mid 21–30 band. Score 24.

## Regulation

### Overview
Rassemblement National combines economic nationalism, preference for domestic industry and energy, and scepticism toward further EU regulatory layers, with a mixed labour stance.

### Relevant Policies
- Economic nationalism and preference for domestic production
- Selective reduction of EU-derived regulatory burdens
- Support for nuclear and domestic energy security
- Mixed labour stance (protections for domestic workers; resistance to some liberalisations)

### Expanded Analysis
Economic nationalism and preference for domestic production introduce selective industrial direction. Scepticism toward further EU regulatory layers offers limited relief from external rules. Mixed labour stance retains protections for domestic workers while resisting some liberalisations. The package is neither broad deregulation nor heavy ownership-based control; it sits in the mid social-market range.

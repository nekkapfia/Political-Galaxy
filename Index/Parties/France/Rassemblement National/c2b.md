# Cultural 2B: France – Rassemblement National
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 80**

### Overview
Rassemblement National treats French national identity, history and symbols as central and legitimate. National pride is the clear default; systematic shame frameworks are rejected. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit affirmation of French history, culture and continuity as organising principles.
* Education and cultural positions that prioritise transmission of national pride and shared culture.
* Defence of traditional national symbols and resistance to their critical reinterpretation or removal.
* Rhetoric presents the nation as a primary source of meaning and political legitimacy.
* Criticism of the nation’s past is secondary to affirmative continuity.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
RN’s programme would shift curriculum, commemorative practice and public symbolism firmly toward affirmative national continuity. The score sits at the top of the Honour band / lower Pride band: national pride is the clear default and shapes institutional priorities, without reaching the extreme Glorification pole that treats all national action as inherently justified.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 80
Clear default of national pride, rejection of organising shame narratives, and affirmative treatment of French historical continuity place RN at the Honour/Pride boundary.

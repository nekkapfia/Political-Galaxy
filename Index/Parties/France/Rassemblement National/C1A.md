# Cultural 1A: France – Rassemblement National
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)
### Overview
Rassemblement National organises moral legitimacy around national preference, concrete interests of French citizens, sovereignty as practical necessity and electoral mandate. Transcendent higher-law sources do not organise its claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* National preference, secure borders and the concrete interests of French citizens are presented as primary political goods.
* Mass immigration is criticised as an override of popular will and a source of pressure on social cohesion and services.
* National identity and cultural continuity are defended as the inheritance of a living political community.
* Sovereignty and the capacity of the state to control its territory are framed as practical necessities.
* Electoral mandate and direct popular preference are treated as the proper sources of authority.
* Concrete harms to security, housing and services appear as evidence.

### Expanded Analysis
National preference, secure borders, concrete interests of French citizens, national identity as inheritance of a living community, sovereignty as practical necessity, electoral mandate and direct popular preference organise legitimacy. Concrete harms to security, housing and services are used as evidence. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law, worship-level deification or sacred Ideal appears. The foundation is Mundane: demos will, concrete interests and national self-preservation.

# Core 2B: France – Rassemblement National
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 58**

### Overview
The Rassemblement National prioritises French national interest, criticises open-ended Ukraine commitments and EU foreign-policy overreach, and focuses on borders and domestic capacity. It is the clearest major French alternative to the mainstream intervention toolkit.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Calls for limits on French military and financial exposure in Ukraine.
* Scepticism of EU sanctions strategy and of using the EU as a transformative external actor.
* Priority on immigration control, energy and domestic industrial capacity.
* Residual acceptance of limited, threat-linked national defence tools.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. RN policy would shrink French participation in open-ended assistance and sanctions campaigns. One-term realistic outcome is a clear upward move relative to Renaissance or PS baselines.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 58
National-interest framing and explicit desire to reduce external military and sanctions exposure place the RN well above the French mainstream.

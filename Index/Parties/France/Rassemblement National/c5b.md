# Cultural 5B: France – Rassemblement National
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 55**

### Overview

The Rassemblement National advocates rapid shifts on immigration, identity and selected institutional issues and has moved toward more explicit accelerationist language in recent cycles. It remains constrained by French institutional design and by the need to construct broader electoral majorities. The score sits in the mid range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Platform demands for substantial and relatively rapid change on immigration control, national preference and related identity policies.
* Rhetoric that treats existing legal and European constraints as obstacles to be overcome or compressed.
* Practical positioning still operates inside electoral, constitutional and EU frameworks that impose sequencing.
* No national governing record of sustained compressed implementation.
* Observable pattern is elevated urgency on core issues within significant residual process constraints.

### Expanded Analysis

RN’s core issues are framed with higher tempo language than mainstream centre-right or centre-left parties. That raises the score into the mid range. It does not yet match the detailed anti-process operational design of the highest-scoring accelerationist platforms. Score 55.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 55

Elevated urgency on immigration and identity issues within French institutional constraints places the Rassemblement National at 55.

## Regulation

### Overview
Rassemblement National produces a mid-range Core 1B Regulation profile defined by economic nationalism and preference for domestic industry and energy, selective reduction of EU-derived regulatory burdens, support for nuclear and domestic energy security, and a mixed labour stance that retains residual Code du travail protections while resisting further liberalisation.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Economic nationalism and preference for domestic production and strategic-sector direction.
* Selective reduction of residual EU-derived regulatory burdens; no high-priority full dismantling of residual SNBC / C3IV green architecture is locked.
* Support for nuclear and domestic energy security as residual industrial-policy priority.
* Retention of residual Code du travail ordinary working-time, dismissal and collective-bargaining protections; mixed stance that resists further liberalisation while protecting domestic workers.
* Residual competition-law baseline (Autorité de la concurrence) continues.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues; no broad bureaucracy-reduction or residual green-architecture repeal is locked as primary programme.

### Expanded Analysis
The RN final set advances economic nationalism and domestic industrial preference. Residual EU-derived regulatory layers are selectively reduced. Nuclear and domestic energy security are prioritised. Residual Code du travail ordinary labour protections continue; further liberalisation is resisted. Residual SNBC carbon-budget and C3IV green-industry instruments remain operative because no high-priority full repeal is locked. Residual competition rules continue. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of labour protections and residual green architecture combined with selective industrial-nationalist direction and limited relief from external regulatory layers.

### Regulation Score
Economic nationalism and domestic industrial preference, selective reduction of residual EU-derived burdens, retention of residual Code du travail protections, and continuity of residual SNBC / C3IV architecture produce a Regulation Score of 53.

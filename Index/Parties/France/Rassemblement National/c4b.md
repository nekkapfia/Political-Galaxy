# Cultural 4B: France – Rassemblement National
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
Rassemblement National rejects the progressive equity lens. Group outcome gaps are not treated as automatic proof of systemic unfairness requiring identity-based corrections; national preference and individual/cultural frames predominate.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Opposition to expansive DEI, identity-preference policies and systemic-oppression diagnostics.
* Emphasis on national community, equal treatment for citizens, and individual responsibility.
* Critique of institutional practices that adjust outcomes by group identity.
* Soft and hard equity tools are rejected or heavily constrained.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks the opposite of the gap-as-proof diagnostic. Gaps are explained by individual, cultural and national-preference factors rather than progressive systemic unfairness. Residual acceptance of basic equal-treatment principles prevents an absolute floor. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Rejection of the progressive gap-as-proof diagnostic and equity interventions, combined with national and individual framing, produces a low score. Residual equal-treatment principles prevent an absolute floor.

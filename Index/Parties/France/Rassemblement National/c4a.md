### Overview
The Rassemblement National operates inside the French universal-schooling and selective higher-education system and emphasises national educational standards, merit, and residual selective pathways while accepting quality gradients correlated with family background and early performance. Formal access remains open.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Emphasis on academic standards and traditional educational pathways.
* Preference for merit and performance as the primary basis for advancement.
* Acceptance of residual quality gradients as compatible with formal openness.
* Limited emphasis on structural equalisation of early resources relative to gap-closing parties.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.

Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

## Foundation Analysis

### Overview
The Parti Socialiste grounds political legitimacy in social justice, republican equality and competent social-democratic government. Organising sources are mixed: fairness and equality claims carry moral weight alongside strongly mundane emphasis on delivery, institutions and electoral viability. ### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Social justice and reduction of inequality framed as moral and republican requirements of a decent society.
* Public services and redistribution justified through fairness, solidarity and practical cohesion.
* Republican universalism and secularism as institutional and moral framework rather than sacred higher law.
* Evidence, competence and governing responsibility as central mundane sources.
* Progressive social positions advanced through rights and evolving consensus.
* Electoral mandate and institutional continuity as constraints on pure justice maximalism.

### Expanded Analysis
The PS organises justification through a blend of republican social justice and governing pragmatism. Equality and solidarity are moralised beyond pure preference, yet remain embedded in institutional republicanism and electoral delivery rather than elevated as closed systemic higher law of the hard-left type. The pattern is mixed, near the centre of the French spectrum.

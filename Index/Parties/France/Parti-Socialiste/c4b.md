# Cultural 4B: France – Parti Socialiste
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 66**

### Overview
The Parti Socialiste supports equality infrastructure and treats persistent group disparities as partly structural. Soft equity tools predominate alongside individual-opportunity and republican language. Residual baseline of mixed or selective equity framing: progressive gap diagnostics coexist with residual individual-responsibility or cultural explanations.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for equality duties, positive action and diversity measures within existing legal frames.
* Recognition of structural barriers alongside education and opportunity remedies.
* Soft mechanisms predominate; hard quotas are not the central instrument.
* Balance between structural diagnosis and individual/republican opportunity framing.
* Residual baseline of mixed or selective equity framing: progressive gap diagnostics coexist with residual individual-responsibility or cultural explanations.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks a moderated gap-as-proof diagnostic sufficient to justify soft equity infrastructure. Individual-opportunity language remains substantial, producing a mid-high contested profile. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 66
Support for soft equity tools and partial structural diagnosis of group disparities, combined with retained individual-opportunity and republican emphasis and the absence of hard quotas as the primary instrument, produce a mid-high contested score.

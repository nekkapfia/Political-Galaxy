## Regulation

### Overview
The Parti Socialiste produces a mid-to-lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by retention and reinforcement of residual Code du travail labour protections, retention of residual SNBC / C3IV green architecture, and preference for stronger collective institutions and selective industrial and public-investment direction.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Retention and reinforcement of residual Code du travail ordinary working-time, dismissal and collective-bargaining protections; expansion of collective rights preferred over deregulation.
* Retention of residual SNBC carbon-budget architecture and residual C3IV green-industry instruments; preference for high environmental standards rather than repeal.
* Support for selective industrial policy and public-investment direction in strategic sectors.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues; no high-priority labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The PS final set retains residual Code du travail labour protections and residual SNBC / C3IV green architecture. Collective labour-market institutions are reinforced rather than liberalised. Selective industrial policy and public-investment direction are advanced. Residual competition rules remain. The institutional pattern is residual continuity of labour protections and residual green architecture combined with additional collective and industrial-policy direction rather than deregulation.

### Regulation Score
Retention of residual Code du travail labour protections and residual SNBC / C3IV green architecture, combined with preference for stronger collective institutions and selective industrial-policy direction, produces a Regulation Score of 48.

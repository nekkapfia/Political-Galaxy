# Core 2B: France – Parti Socialiste
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 38**

### Overview
The Parti Socialiste as a mainstream centre-left party accepts the standard European toolkit of sanctions, military assistance to allies or partners under attack, and values-based conditionality. Residual baseline is alignment with the post-Cold-War liberal-intervention and multilateral-pressure repertoire.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for EU and French sanctions regimes in major crises.
* Acceptance of military assistance to partners under attack (including Ukraine) within multilateral frames.
* Values-based and governance conditionality in development and diplomatic policy.
* Residual baseline of mainstream European interventionist instruments; no programme-level rejection of the soft-to-hard pressure toolkit.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. PS policy sits inside the mainstream French and European pattern: sanctions, conditional aid and military assistance to defenders are treated as legitimate. Invitation mitigation applies to Ukraine support; the residual posture remains interventionist rather than restrained.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 38
Mainstream acceptance of sanctions, military assistance and values conditionality, with residual alignment to the liberal-intervention toolkit, places the Parti Socialiste in the high 30s.

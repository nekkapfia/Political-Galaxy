# Cultural 2A: France – Parti Socialiste
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 34**

### Overview
The Parti Socialiste supports equality and diversity frameworks that treat group membership as a significant factor in institutional allocation and remedy, while retaining formal individual legal personality as a residual layer.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Support for positive action, parity and diversity instruments.
* Acceptance of category-conscious representation and equality metrics.
* Formal individual rights continuing as liberal residual.
* Institutional practice that regularly incorporates group membership into allocation decisions.
* No programme of systematic restoration of pure person-unit rules.

### Expanded Analysis
Category membership functions as a recurring and legitimate factor in institutional measurement and remedy. Individual legal personality is not abolished, but it is regularly subordinated to or balanced against group-identity considerations. The pattern is strong group influence on allocation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 34
Routine institutional use of category tools and the absence of a person-only restoration programme place the Parti Socialiste in the strong group-primacy band. Score 34.

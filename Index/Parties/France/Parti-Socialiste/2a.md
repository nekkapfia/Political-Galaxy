# Core 2A: France – Parti Socialiste
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 43**

### Overview
The Parti Socialiste supports deeper EU integration on social, environmental and fiscal policy, treating European coordination as the primary level of decision-making. External pooling of authority is preferred.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Supports EU membership and further integration on social, environmental and economic governance.
* Favours common European approaches to migration, climate and fiscal coordination.
* Accepts the primacy of EU law in areas of conferred competence.
* Supports continued NATO membership and European defence cooperation initiatives.
* Supports European coordination mechanisms on migration, climate and fiscal policy in preference to unilateral national measures; euro framework continues.

### Expanded Analysis
The Parti Socialiste supports deeper EU integration on social, environmental and fiscal policy, common European approaches to migration and climate, and primacy of EU law in areas of conferred competence. NATO and European defence cooperation are retained. The euro and ECB constraints continue. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is European coordination as the primary level of decision-making, with national unilateralism subordinated. EU membership architecture and legal supremacy constitute primary drag and are densified rather than resisted. National residual capacity is exercised inside an expanding external framework.

# Cultural 2A: France – Europe Écologie Les Verts
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 18**

### Overview
EELV treats group identity as a primary and legitimate basis for the allocation of rights, access and institutional resources. The person is routinely measured through demographic and identity coordinates. Residual baseline of group unit of measure: significant residual institutional practice organises rights, opportunities or moral standing around group membership rather than the individual alone.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Strong support for self-identification and identity-recognition frameworks.
* Consistent advocacy for diversity, parity and category-conscious allocation instruments.
* Opposition to reforms that would restore pure person-unit or biological-sex criteria as dominant.
* Institutional approaches that embed group-based considerations into education, employment and representation.
* Framing of individualist approaches as insufficient without accompanying group measures.

### Expanded Analysis
Across policy and institutional practice the party elevates group membership above individual characteristics. Identity frameworks allow category location to determine access and status, while broader diversity approaches treat group membership as routine and necessary. Individual biography is subordinated. The result is severe group collectivism on the unit-of-measure test.

Multi-category allocation goals and systematic prioritisation of identity location over person-unit criteria match the severe group-collectivism band. Score 18.

# Core 2B: France – Europe Écologie Les Verts
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 44**

### Overview
EELV is values-based and internationalist on climate and human rights. It accepts normative pressure and multilateral instruments while remaining more hesitant on pure kinetic intervention, producing a score inside the broader progressive interventionist range. Residual baseline retains soft-power and conditionality tools.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong climate and human-rights conditionality in external policy.
* Support for multilateral and EU normative power.
* Greater reluctance toward traditional military intervention than centrist governing parties.
* Residual baseline of progressive normative pressure: sanctions, conditionality and multilateral leverage remain accepted instruments even where kinetic appetite is lower.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures external interference. Green policy retains sanctions and normative tools while reducing kinetic appetite. The residual posture is still interventionist on the soft-to-grey spectrum; the result is a modest upward shift relative to more kinetic centrist parties, not a non-intervention stance.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 44
Retained normative pressure tools, residual conditionality instruments, and reduced but not eliminated kinetic emphasis place EELV in the mid-40s.

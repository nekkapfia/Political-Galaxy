## Regulation

### Overview
EELV prioritises strong environmental and net zero mandates, green industrial transformation, expanded public steering of energy and transport, and enhanced labour protections.

### Relevant Policies
- Strong environmental and net zero mandates; densified climate-regulatory architecture
- Public steering and investment in energy, transport and green industry
- Restrictions on high-emission sectors
- Expanded labour and social protections

### Expanded Analysis
Strong net-zero mandates and densified climate-regulatory architecture are the dominant restrictive elements. Public steering of energy, transport and green industry, plus restrictions on high-emission sectors, constrain voluntary decisions in those domains. Expanded labour protections add rigidity. The package sits in the upper 39–49 range, driven by environmental and industrial direction rather than classical public ownership.

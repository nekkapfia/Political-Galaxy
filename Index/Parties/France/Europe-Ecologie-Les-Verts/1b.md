## Regulation

### Overview
Europe Écologie Les Verts produces a lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by intensification of residual green architecture (SNBC / C3IV), ecological planning and green industrial direction, expanded residual labour protections, and preference for public and cooperative ownership in strategic environmental sectors.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Intensification of residual SNBC carbon-budget architecture and residual C3IV green-industry instruments; strong environmental mandates and compliance requirements.
* Ecological planning and green industrial direction of investment and production.
* Expanded residual labour protections and collective rights under residual Code du travail architecture.
* Preference for public and cooperative ownership in strategic environmental and utility sectors.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues but is subordinated to green-industrial and ownership priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues and is deepened; no high-priority residual green-architecture repeal or labour-market deregulation is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The EELV final set intensifies residual SNBC and C3IV green architecture through ecological planning and strong environmental mandates. Residual Code du travail labour protections are expanded. Public and cooperative ownership preferences in strategic environmental sectors reduce the scope of private voluntary decision-making. Residual competition rules remain but are secondary to green-industrial and ownership priorities. The institutional pattern is substantial expansion of residual green-industrial direction combined with expanded labour protections and ownership preferences.

### Regulation Score
Intensification of residual SNBC / C3IV architecture, ecological planning and green industrial direction, expanded residual labour protections, and preference for public and cooperative ownership produce a Regulation Score of 40.

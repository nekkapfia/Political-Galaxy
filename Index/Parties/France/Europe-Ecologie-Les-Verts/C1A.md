# Cultural 1A: France – Europe Écologie Les Verts
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 44 (6M)

### Overview
EELV organises moral legitimacy around ecological limits as empirical threats to human societies and future persons, intergenerational responsibility, and democratic participation. Elevated urgency is present; sacralisation of Nature is not the organising frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Ecological limits and planetary boundaries are framed as empirical constraints required to protect human life, civilisation and future human welfare.
* Intergenerational responsibility and the interests of future persons are treated as human-welfare claims.
* Climate and biodiversity crises are framed as failures that produce concrete harm to human communities.
* Scientific evidence is used to establish the scale of ecological risk.
* Social justice and democratic participation remain present inside the same frame.
* Elevated urgency language appears within the human-necessity framing.

### Expanded Analysis
Ecological limits are framed as empirical constraints on behalf of human survival and future human welfare; intergenerational duty and scientific evidence organise the conversation alongside democratic participation. This is human-necessity ecology — Mundane — not sacred/inheriting-will ecology. Nature is not treated as a moral subject with independent standing. Elevated urgency is lesser residual only. No divine command, Cosmic sacralisation of Nature, or worship-level deification organises legitimacy. The foundation is Mundane with residual elevated ecological urgency.

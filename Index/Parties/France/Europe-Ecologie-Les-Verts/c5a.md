# Cultural 5A: France – Europe Écologie Les Verts
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
EELV subordinates inherited cultural forms to ecological and social transformation agendas, prioritising progressive gender, family-status and education reform over unmodified traditional baselines.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Affirmative platform commitments to progressive gender recognition and family-status reform, treating conventional transmission rules as requiring redesign.
* Education and cultural policy positions subordinating traditional national narratives when they conflict with equity and climate goals.
* Unmodified traditional curriculum or family-law baselines receive little institutional priority in platform content.
* Inherited cultural forms treated as obstacles to ecological and social transformation agendas where they conflict with those goals.
* Residual progressive settlement is extended rather than reversed; pre-progressive family and continuity curriculum are not defended.
* Material heritage may be valued environmentally; that does not restore traditional social transmission as the public default.

### Expanded Analysis
Primary domains under EELV’s programme are transformative relative to inherited traditional baselines. Gender and family-status reform are affirmative; education content is ranked by equity and climate criteria rather than by unmodified national continuity; traditional forms yield when they conflict with those agendas. The residual baseline is progressive: the party does not seek to restore conventional family law or continuity curriculum as the operative standard. Domain hierarchy places education framing and family-status design first; environmental valuation of heritage is secondary and does not equal social-traditional preservation. Progressive primacy is consistent across high-weight domains.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Affirmative progressive gender and family-status commitments, subordination of traditional narratives to equity and climate goals, and absence of protection for unmodified traditional baselines in primary domains place EELV in the low 21–30 band. Residual progressive settlement is extended, not contested by a continuity programme, so contested mid-range placement is not justified.

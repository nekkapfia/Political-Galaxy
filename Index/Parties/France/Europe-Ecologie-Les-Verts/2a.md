# Core 2A: France – Europe Écologie Les Verts
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 39**

### Overview
Europe Écologie Les Verts prioritises further EU integration and federalising tendencies on climate, energy and migration, with European rules taking priority over national unilateral measures. External pooling of authority is actively expanded in the domains the party treats as decisive.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong support for further EU integration and federalising tendencies in climate, energy and migration policy.
* Favours common European asylum and border management approaches over purely national control.
* Supports the primacy of EU law and the expansion of qualified-majority decision-making.
* Supports strengthening European human-rights and environmental frameworks with direct effect on national policy.
* Supports European climate and migration rules taking priority over conflicting national unilateral measures; euro and ECB framework continue.

### Expanded Analysis
Europe Écologie Les Verts supports further EU integration and federalising tendencies on climate, energy and migration. Common European asylum and border management is preferred to national control; EU law primacy and qualified-majority expansion are affirmed; European climate and migration rules are to take priority over conflicting national measures. The euro and ECB constraints continue. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is systematic pooling of authority in the domains the party treats as decisive, with residual national capacity exercised inside an expanding external framework. Primary drag arises from the densification of European-level authority rather than from residual national closure.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 39
Federalising integration on climate, energy and migration with European rules over national measures prioritises international integration over unilateral sovereign decision-making. External pooling of authority is explicit and preferred, placing the final set inside the 31–40 Low band. The score of 39 follows from systematic expansion of European authority rather than recovery of unilateral control.

# Cultural 4A: France – Europe Ecologie Les Verts
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
EELV prioritises reduction of socio-economic and territorial inequalities in education and opportunity. Formal access remains universal within the French system; the party emphasises early support and gap-closing rather than expansion of selective pathways. Residual quality gradients correlated with birth background persist.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Emphasis on reducing educational and territorial inequalities through public investment.
* Preference for comprehensive and inclusive approaches over reinforcement of selective hierarchies.
* Support for early intervention aimed at narrowing birth-linked resource gaps.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Residual acceptance of quality variation within an open formal framework.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. EELV operates inside France’s universal-schooling system, which includes a selective higher-education tier (classes préparatoires, grandes écoles). The party’s direction is gap-closing and anti-reinforcement of early advantage. Formal access remains open; practical ease continues to correlate with family resources. This places the score at the lower end of the 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Universal schooling plus gap-closing emphasis, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the lower 21–30 band. Score 22.

# Cultural 5B: France – Europe Écologie Les Verts
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview

EELV combines strong urgency framing on climate and ecological transition with a preference for participatory, consultative and legally sequenced processes characteristic of Green parties elsewhere. French institutional constraints reinforce rather than counteract that deliberative tilt. The net score is low-to-mid.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Frequent emergency and urgency language on climate targets and ecological deadlines.
* Consistent emphasis on public participation, legal safeguards and extended consultation as legitimate parts of major change.
* Governing experience at local and occasional national junior-partner level has shown both rapid symbolic measures and substantial residual process commitments.
* Observable pattern parallels other Green parties: urgency of ends does not produce systematic design against deliberative delay.

### Expanded Analysis

As with the UK and US Greens, outcome urgency is not equivalent to institutional radicalism on pace. EELV retains significant commitment to process. Score 48.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 48

Urgency on ecological outcomes paired with sustained preference for participatory and sequenced processes places EELV at 48.

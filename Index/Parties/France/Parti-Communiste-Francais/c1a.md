# Cultural 1A: France – Parti Communiste Français
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 56**

### Overview
The PCF grounds political legitimacy in working-class interest, anti-capitalist critique and republican social transformation pursued through democratic means. Residual communist ideological framing supplies transcendent-leaning weight; contemporary practice remains constrained by electoral and demos realities. The profile is mixed with mild transcendent lean.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Capitalist structures criticised as systemically unjust rather than merely inefficient or unfair in distribution.
* Working-class and popular interest as primary moral constituency.
* Public ownership and planning defended as necessary correctives, not optional preferences among equal alternatives.
* Republican and democratic means retained as legitimate constraints on pure ideological maximalism.
* Progressive social alliances framed through solidarity and equality.
* No fully closed Party-state orthodoxy independent of French popular preference.

### Expanded Analysis
The PCF retains systemic anti-capitalist critique that functions as a higher-order moral claim relative to pure market preference: the existing economic order is structured so as to require transformation on grounds that outrank ordinary voluntary exchange. At the same time the party operates inside republican democratic politics and accepts electoral constraint. Working-class interest is both demos claim and ideological constituency. The result is mixed with mild transcendent lean—above the PS managerial-social profile, below historical Party-state communism—consistent with a mass communist party that has adapted to pluralist competition without fully abandoning systemic justice language.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 56
Systemic justice claims provide organising transcendent weight moderated by democratic and republican constraints. The score is 56.

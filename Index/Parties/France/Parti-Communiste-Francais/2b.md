# Core 2B: France – Parti Communiste Francais
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 42**

### Overview
The PCF combines a historical internationalist tradition with contemporary criticism of Western military intervention and NATO expansion. It sits slightly above the governing mainstream on the non-intervention scale.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Criticism of Western military interventions and of NATO-centric escalation.
* Residual internationalist and anti-imperialist framing.
* Limited contemporary capacity to implement alternative foreign-policy instruments.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. PCF opposition to key Western intervention tools raises the score relative to Renaissance or PS, while the absence of a governing non-intervention record keeps it out of the higher nationalist band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 42
Criticism of Western interventionism without control of state instruments produces a low-to-mid 40s score.

# Cultural 4A: France – Parti Communiste Français
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
The PCF emphasises class-based analysis of educational and occupational barriers while operating inside the French universal-schooling system. Formal access remains open; the party treats residual class gradients as structurally significant and prioritises working-class educational and industrial pathways.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong class-analytic critique of educational and occupational stratification.
* Emphasis on public education and industrial/vocational routes for working-class advancement.
* Preference for structural remediation of socio-economic gaps.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Residual quality gradients within an open formal framework.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth constraint versus mobility. The PCF inherits the French baseline and supplies a heavier class-analytic framing than most mainstream parties. Formal pathways remain open; practical ease continues to correlate with family resources. This places the score in the mid-to-upper 21–30 band. Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment precludes higher scores. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26

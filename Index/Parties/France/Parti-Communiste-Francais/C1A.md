# Cultural 1A: France – Parti Communiste Français
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)
### Overview
The PCF organises moral legitimacy around working-class material interest, social protection and delivery, with communist tradition language supplying identity and critique.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Working-class material interest and social protection are framed as primary moral and political requirements.
* Capitalist structures are criticised for producing inequality and social degradation.
* Public services and redistribution are presented as necessary corrections.
* Electoral competition and popular mobilisation are accepted as legitimate means.
* Concrete improvements in living standards and social security are cited as goals and evidence.
* Historical communist and labour traditions are invoked as sources of continuity and identity.
* Anti-imperialism and opposition to financial globalisation appear as moral-political claims.

### Expanded Analysis
Working-class material interest, social protection, public services, electoral competition and concrete improvements in living standards organise the operational moral conversation. Historical communist tradition and anti-imperialism are present and supply identity and critique. The constituency and delivery claims are Mundane and carry primary weight; the tradition and anti-imperial claims add purpose language without establishing divine, Cosmic or worship-level sources. The foundation is Mundane with residual ideological-tradition language.

# Core 2A: France – Parti Communiste Français
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 52**
**Status: FULLY LOCKED**

### Overview
The PCF seeks a more independent French defence posture, renegotiated EU economic rules and expanded national control over strategic sectors while remaining inside EU membership. Residual external pooling of authority through EU and euro architecture continues; intensity is moderated by renegotiation claims rather than structural exit.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Supports a more independent French defence and foreign-policy orientation with reduced NATO operational residual constraints.
* Supports renegotiation of residual EU rules that constrain public ownership, industrial policy and social spending.
* Favours national control over strategic sectors and public services.
* Supports continued French membership of the EU with renegotiated economic and social rules; residual EU supremacy architecture continues.
* Supports multilateral frameworks that expand national social-policy space and reduce military deployments; residual euro framework continues.

### Expanded Analysis
The PCF supports a more independent French defence posture with reduced residual NATO operational constraints, renegotiation of residual EU rules that limit public ownership and industrial policy, and national control over strategic sectors. Residual EU membership is retained; residual multilateral frameworks that expand social-policy space are favoured over those that constrain it. The programme expands residual national economic and defence autonomy without a formal exit agenda. Residual euro and ECB constraints continue. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is recovery of selected residual economic and defence policy space inside continued residual European membership. Residual EU architecture remains primary drag; intensity of residual external pooling of authority is moderated by renegotiation claims rather than structural removal of residual supremacy.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52
More independent residual defence policy and renegotiation of residual EU economic constraints inside continued membership recover selected residual autonomy without ending residual European legal integration. Residual external pooling of authority continues and is explicit. The final residual set therefore sits inside the 51–59 Mid–Lower band. The score of 52 reflects partial residual recovery offset by residual EU membership architecture rather than structural exit from residual external supremacy.

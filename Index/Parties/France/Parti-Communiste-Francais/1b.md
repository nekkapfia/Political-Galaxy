## Regulation

### Overview
The Parti Communiste Français produces a lower Core 1B Regulation profile defined by extensive public ownership, strong residual and expanded labour protections, industrial planning and public-investment direction, and retention and intensification of residual green architecture.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Extensive public ownership of strategic sectors and utilities.
* Strong residual and expanded labour protections and collective rights under residual Code du travail architecture.
* Industrial planning and public-investment direction of capital and production.
* Retention and intensification of residual SNBC architecture and residual green-industry instruments.
* Residual competition-law baseline continues but is subordinated to public-ownership and planning priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues and is deepened; no high-priority labour-market deregulation or residual green-architecture repeal is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The PCF final set advances extensive public ownership of strategic sectors and utilities. Residual Code du travail labour protections are retained and expanded through stronger collective rights. Industrial planning and public-investment direction reduce the scope of private voluntary decision-making. Residual SNBC architecture is intensified. Residual competition rules remain but are secondary to public-ownership and planning priorities. The institutional pattern is substantial expansion of residual public-ownership and industrial-planning direction combined with expanded labour protections and residual green architecture.

### Regulation Score
Extensive public ownership, strong residual and expanded labour protections, industrial planning and public-investment direction, and retention and intensification of residual SNBC architecture produce a Regulation Score of 32.

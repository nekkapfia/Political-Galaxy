# Core 1A: Communism – Stalinism
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 9**
**Xenos Score: 5**

### Overview

Stalinism as a governing political force produces a near-floor Core 1A profile defined by total one-party control of speech and association, mass political terror, the Gulag system, forced collectivisation, internal passport and movement controls, mandatory ideological conformity, and designated enemy categories subject to execution, deportation and lethal deprivation. Cumulative weight sits in the floor / extreme-restriction bands. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Total monopoly of political expression, press, education and association under Party-state control; no legal independent political speech or organisation.
* Great Terror and mass operations: arrest, execution and imprisonment of designated political, class and national categories on quota and list systems.
* Gulag forced-labour camp system as a standing instrument of political repression and economic coercion.
* Forced collectivisation and dekulakisation enforced through deportation, confiscation, movement bans, political labelling and famine conditions used as coercion (collective ownership structure itself is out of scope for Core 1A).
* Internal passport regime, propiska and related movement controls restricting residence, travel and choice of workplace.
* Mandatory ideological conformity in public life, culture, science and education; deviation treated as political crime.
* Deportations of entire national groups (e.g. Chechens, Crimean Tatars, Volga Germans and others) and intensified repression of designated Xenos populations.
* Near-elimination of residual private political or associational space that had partially survived under early Leninism.
* Article 58 and related political-criminal provisions enabling broad prosecution of non-violent expression and association.
* Ordinary violent-crime prohibitions exist but do not define placement.

### Expanded Analysis

Stalinism completed and radicalised the Leninist restrictive architecture. Political speech and association outside Party control were impossible. Mass terror, the Gulag and movement controls extended coercion deep into daily life for the general population. Collectivisation campaigns used deportation, movement bans and famine as instruments of coercion and killed large numbers; the property-system change itself is out of scope for Core 1A.

For Endos — accepted Soviet citizens not swept into mass operations — public expression was limited to prescribed ideology; surveillance, denunciation risk and administrative controls constrained association, movement and occupational choice. Placement at 9 reflects extreme, systematic multi-domain restriction approaching the floor without requiring absolute physiological control.

For Xenos — kulaks, designated nationalities, political opponents, “enemies of the people” and related categories — policy included execution, deportation, camp destruction through labour and engineered deprivation. That differential places Xenos at the upper floor.

Framing as industrialisation necessity or wartime security is discarded. Only the observable restriction architecture is scored.

### Why this produces Endos 9 / Xenos 5

Total speech and association monopoly, Great Terror, Gulag, deportation and famine coercion under collectivisation campaigns, passport and movement controls, and mandatory ideology place Endos at 9. Mass operations, national deportations and lethal enemy-category policies produce Xenos 5.

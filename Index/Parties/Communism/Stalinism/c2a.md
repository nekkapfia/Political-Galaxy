# Cultural 2A: Communism – Stalinism
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 14**

### Overview
Stalinism subordinated individual merit and status to class origin, political reliability and party-defined categories, with residual performance channels operating only inside those constraints.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class-background and political-reliability gates on promotion, education access and trusted roles.
* Party and state categories organising status; individual performance metrics existing inside those constraints.
* Collective and cadre systems mediating goods, housing and recognition.
* Independent civil association suppressed; status-bearing groups confined to approved structures.
* Residual formal equality rhetoric operating inside a category-structured system.

### Expanded Analysis
Persons were measured primarily through political and class categories. Individual agency operated only within tightly constrained category structures. The dominant unit of allocation was the collective-political category rather than the sovereign individual. The pattern is severe group collectivism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 14
Political and class categories as primary organising units of status, with only residual individual ranking inside constraints, place Stalinism in the severe group-collectivism band. Score 14.

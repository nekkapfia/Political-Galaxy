# Cultural 1B: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 97**

**Orthodoxy: 98, Tribalism: 97**

### Overview
The Khmer Rouge combined Year Zero elimination of prior social and moral frameworks with total internal closure around the Party line and Angkar. Deviation and residual alternative identities were treated as enemies to be destroyed. Both aspects reach the absolute extreme, producing one of the highest scores in the entire set.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Year Zero destruction of existing institutions, culture, religion and social forms as residual baselines to be erased.
* Absolute Party (Angkar) monopoly; no legal or organised internal opposition.
* Eliminationist violence and terror against deviation, class enemies and residual alternative identities.
* Forced re-education and total organisational control of population.
* Liberal, traditional, religious and competing socialist frameworks treated as enemies to be liquidated rather than debated.
* Residual baseline of pre-revolutionary society deliberately abolished; no recognised process of internal revision of the foundational line.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is absolute: Angkar monopoly and Year Zero practice left no residual institutional space for internal challenge or empirical revision of the line. Eliminationist mechanisms (terror, forced labour, re-education, liquidation of residual identities) enforced closure at the highest intensity observed in the set. Tribalism is equally absolute: prior moral, religious, cultural and competing political frameworks were not merely illegitimate but targets for destruction. Education and propaganda presented the Party line as the sole remaining truth after the erasure of alternatives. The institutional pattern is pure extreme dogmatism with no meaningful residual openness on either aspect. This is the calibration ceiling for the Total/Extreme band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 97
Year Zero elimination of residual baselines, absolute Angkar monopoly, eliminationist terror against deviation and alternative identities, and total rejection of all competing frameworks as enemies produce the highest combined Orthodoxy and Tribalism in the scored set. The formula yields 97.

# Cultural 4A: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 82**

### Overview
The Khmer Rouge regime treated class origin, urban background, and educational status as decisive markers of destiny. Large categories of the population were labelled enemies on the basis of birth and prior social position; formal schooling and professional specialisations were abolished as class-enemy markers; and individual action produced essentially no legitimate trajectory change out of the assigned category. Life outcomes were subordinated to Angkar classification in a system of near-total constraint.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Class and political labelling that designated city people, the educated, former regime personnel, and certain ethnic groups as enemies subject to execution or lethal deprivation.
* Forced evacuation of cities and abolition of formal schooling and professional specialisations treated as markers of hostile class origin.
* Near-total closure of legitimate mobility channels out of the assigned origin category.
* Individual merit, effort, or re-education offered no reliable pathway to restored status for those classified as class enemies.
* Forced labour and collective discipline under military-style control that further locked life chances to Angkar assignment.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores the degree to which birth and inherited social position determine life outcomes. The Khmer Rouge operated a radical class-origin assignment system. Urban origin, education, and prior occupational status functioned as hereditary-style markers that assigned individuals to enemy categories with almost no residual legitimate exit. The destruction of formal schooling and professional pathways eliminated the main channels through which individual action might otherwise have altered trajectory. This matches the high social determinism bands (80–89): birth/origin determined broad class membership as the standard rule, intra-class options were minimal, and crossing out of the assigned category was exceptional to the point of near-impossibility. Soft residual variation among those not labelled enemies does not offset the formal origin-based lock that governed the decisive status boundaries.

Equity-lens diagnostics belong on Cultural 4B and are not scored here.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 82
Class-origin labelling that assigned large population categories to closed enemy status, combined with abolition of schooling and professional pathways and near-total absence of legitimate exit, aligns with the 80–89 band (heavy restrictions both intra-class and inter-class; crossing major class boundaries rare). Score 82.

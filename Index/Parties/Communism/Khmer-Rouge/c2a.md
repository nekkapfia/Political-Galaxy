# Cultural 2A: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 10**

### Overview
Under the Khmer Rouge, class origin, political reliability and registration status functioned as primary categorical units for advancement, access and survival, approaching near-total categorical assignment of fate.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class-background and political-reliability gates on virtually all significant status and survival outcomes.
* Collective and party-defined categories organising housing, work, movement and life chances.
* Individual exam or performance channels minimal or absent inside totalising category constraints.
* Near-elimination of independent individual status outside assigned group location.
* High-Stalinist and Khmer practice approaching categorical assignment of fate rather than residual individual ranking.

### Expanded Analysis
The system measured persons almost exclusively through political and class categories. Individual agency had negligible independent effect on major outcomes. The unit of allocation was the category; the person as sovereign agent was effectively erased. This is near-floor absolute group collectivism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 10
Near-total categorical assignment of fate and the effective erasure of independent individual status place the Khmer Rouge at the absolute group-collectivism floor. Score 10.

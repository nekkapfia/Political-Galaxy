# Cultural 5B: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 96**

### Overview

The Khmer Rouge implemented one of the most extreme compressed transformations in modern history. Year Zero, the forced evacuation of cities, the rapid abolition of money, markets, formal education and independent religion, and the imposition of total Angkar command were executed with minimal tolerance for deliberation, sequencing or gradual adaptation. The system saturates the high end of the radicalism scale.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Year Zero declaration and immediate attempt to reset society to a radical agrarian baseline.
* Forced evacuation of Phnom Penh and other cities within days of capture (April 1975), driving the urban population into rural collectives under military discipline with no right of return.
* Rapid abolition of money, markets, private property in practice, formal schooling and independent religious organisation.
* Construction of a total command system (Angkar) that replaced prior institutional layers with near-immediate effect.
* Mass campaigns of social destruction, forced labour mobilisation and elimination of designated categories conducted as continuous, high-intensity operations rather than sequenced reform.
* Zero institutional space for pilot testing, extended consultation or gradualist feedback mechanisms.
* Observable pattern is sustained total acceleration across the entire period of control, not a single crisis spike.

### Expanded Analysis

The Khmer Rouge converted accelerated, total transformation into the sole operating mode. Deliberative or stability-preserving processes were not merely weakened; they were eliminated. This matches the compressed high-end zone (90–100) more closely than almost any other modern regime. The score reflects pace and intensity of institutional and social change, not the substantive content of the agrarian or political programme (scored on other axes).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 96

Near-total, immediate and sustained transformation of society, economy and institutions with zero tolerance for deliberation or gradual sequencing places the Khmer Rouge at 96.

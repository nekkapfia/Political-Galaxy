# Core 2A: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 85**

### Overview
The Khmer Rouge constructed an extreme sovereign and autarkic Cambodian state under Angkar direction, rejecting external institutional authority and reducing external connection points to near-zero controlled residual.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Exclusive Angkar control of territory, borders, law, military command and economic planning.
* Rejection of external institutional authority and residual external frameworks.
* Autarkic industrial and agricultural mobilisation under complete national direction.
* Forced evacuation and residual territorial control as instruments of institutional and population closure.
* External connection points reduced to minimal controlled residual.

### Expanded Analysis
The Khmer Rouge imposed exclusive national control of territory, borders, law, military command and economic planning under Angkar. External institutional authority was rejected. Autarkic industrial and agricultural mobilisation continued under complete national direction. Forced evacuation and territorial control intensified institutional and population closure. External connection points were reduced to minimal controlled residual. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is extreme national closure with autarkic residual rather than external pooling of authority.

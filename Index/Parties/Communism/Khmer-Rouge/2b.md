# Core 2B: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 56**

### Overview
The Khmer Rouge regime’s defining pattern was internal. External military action (border conflict with Vietnam and earlier regional activity) existed but was secondary to the domestic programme. Placement reflects limited sustained interference in other states’ internal political orders relative to expansionist communist systems.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Primary focus on internal transformation and demographic engineering inside Cambodia.
* Border conflict with Vietnam culminating in Vietnamese invasion and regime overthrow.
* Earlier regional revolutionary links and cross-border activity of more limited scale.
* No multi-state occupation or permanent satellite system.
* Self-defence and purely internal actions are out of scope or scored on other axes.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B isolates interference in other nations’ internal arrangements. The Khmer Rouge committed extreme internal crimes; those belong primarily to other axes. External interference, while real, did not reach the scale or duration of Soviet, Chinese or Cuban overseas campaigns. The mid-range score records residual external conflict without treating internal totalitarianism as automatic Core 2B drag.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 56
Limited external projection relative to expansionist peers, combined with real but secondary border conflict, places the regime in the mid band.

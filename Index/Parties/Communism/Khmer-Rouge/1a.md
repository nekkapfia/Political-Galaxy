# Core 1A: Communism – Khmer Rouge
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Score: 4**

### Overview

The Khmer Rouge as a governing political force produces a floor Core 1A profile defined by near-total abolition of private and urban life, forced evacuation of cities, elimination of independent religion and education, forced labour under military discipline, and mass killing of designated categories (intellectuals, former officials, ethnic Vietnamese, Cham, and others). Multi-domain coercion was so comprehensive that both Endos and Xenos sit at the practical floor of 4; the optional split is not applied.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Forced evacuation of Phnom Penh and other cities; urban population driven into rural collectives under military control with no right of return.
* Near-total subordination of subsistence and daily work to Angkar command through forced labour and military discipline (abolition of money/markets as economic system is out of scope for Core 1A).
* Elimination of independent political expression, association, press and civil organisation — total monopoly under the Party/Angkar with no residual legal channel for dissent.
* Systematic destruction of independent religious practice, particularly Buddhism, including targeting and killing of monks and closure of religious life.
* Abolition of formal schooling, independent cultural institutions and most professional specialisations as class-enemy markers.
* Forced labour under military-style discipline with extreme working hours, minimal food, and collective punishment for failure to meet quotas.
* Class and political labelling that designated large categories of the population (city people, educated, former regime personnel) as enemies subject to execution or lethal deprivation.
* Near-total control of movement, residence, speech, association, family separation practices and daily conduct.
* Mass executions, organised torture centres (including S-21), and death through overwork and starvation as standing instruments of policy.
* Ordinary interpersonal crime categories are irrelevant to placement given the scale of system coercion.

### Expanded Analysis

The Khmer Rouge attempted the most radical reconstruction of society in the Communist set, eliminating residual private, economic and cultural spheres almost entirely. Political expression and association outside absolute Party control were impossible. Designated categories faced systematic extermination; the general population under Angkar lived under forced labour, movement bans and terror.

Because repression was comprehensive across the whole population under control, Endos is already at the practical floor. Additional targeting of intellectuals, former Lon Nol personnel, ethnic Vietnamese, Cham and others is real, yet under the Score 4 Floor and identical-criteria rule both scores are held at 4 and the optional split is not applied. Raising Endos above the floor would be inconsistent with the observed destruction of personal autonomy for people not in special enemy categories.

Framing as pure revolution, anti-urbanism or agrarian utopia is discarded. Only the observable restriction architecture is scored.

### Why this produces Endos 4 / Xenos 4

Near-total multi-domain coercion — forced evacuation, abolition of private and urban life, speech and association monopoly, forced labour, and mass killing — saturates the practical floor for the population under Angkar control. Split not applied; both scores lock at 4.

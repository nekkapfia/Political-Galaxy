# Cultural 1A: Khmer-Rouge
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 42 (8M)

### Overview
The Khmer Rouge organises moral legitimacy around Year Zero and a pure classless agrarian society as this-worldly utopian project, extreme mobilisation, and the interests of the base people. Sacralising rhetoric around Angkar appears but the Ideal remains secular.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Angkar is presented with sacralising language (omniscient centre, mother-and-father, all-seeing) that elevates it beyond ordinary organisational authority.

#### Mundane Justifications
* Year Zero requires the total erasure of prior society so that a pure, classless, agrarian society can begin — a this-worldly utopian end-state.
* Extreme sacrifice, total mobilisation and renunciation of ordinary comfort are required as the path to that secular Ideal.
* Class enemies, urban “new people,” intellectuals and designated contaminants are subject to elimination as political necessity for the pure society.
* Material production targets appear as instruments of the revolutionary transformation and as proofs of correct line.
* The concrete interests of the “base people” / poor peasantry are cited as the constituency the pure society is to serve.

### Expanded Analysis
Year Zero, extreme sacrifice, class-enemy elimination, production proofs, and base-people interest organise the bulk of the moral conversation as secular this-worldly utopian and political claims. Sacralising language around Angkar is present and supplies a limited Transcendent colour, but the Ideal itself remains a secular agrarian communist end-state — not divine command, Cosmic law, or a sacralised people as ontological Ideal. Under the locked lists, secular millenarianism does not automatically clear the Transcendent threshold; intensity and closure belong primarily on Dogmatism, Radicalism, and related axes. Both sources appear; Mundane secular-utopian and constituency claims carry operational weight, with residual Angkar sacralisation. The foundation is Mundane-leaning mixed.

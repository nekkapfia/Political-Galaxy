# Cultural 3: Communism – Castroism
## Endos Prerequisites Analysis
**Ethnic / descent (3A): 0**
**Cultural / integration (3B): 58**

### Overview

Castroism defines full membership of the core political community through revolutionary-political and ideological criteria, not through ethnic or blood rules. Any ethnicity can in principle become Endos. Substantive belonging requires demonstrated loyalty to the socialist state, participation in approved structures, and absence of counter-revolutionary designation.

### Membership Conditions

* No formal ethnic, racial or ancestral bar to citizenship or full political membership. Multi-racial revolutionary leadership and official anti-racism ideology accompany the absence of blood rules. The 3A Exclusion Test is not met → **3A = 0**.
* Citizenship and formal status remain distinct from substantive Endos standing: political reliability, CDR participation and ideological conformity condition access to trust, advancement and sometimes goods.
* Counter-revolutionary labelling places persons outside the core in-group while they remain under the system — a political-cultural, not ethnic, boundary.
* Emigration and “exile” categories reinforce a membership frame in which leaving the revolutionary community is treated as political exit.
* Religious practice and independent civic identity are tolerated only within limits that do not challenge revolutionary cultural primacy.
* Immigration and naturalisation policy is secondary to the internal political definition of who counts as a full member of the socialist nation.
* Birth in Cuba plus ordinary non-opposition may carry relatively low cultural-test burden for basic membership; full trust and advancement carry higher political-cultural demand. Totality weighting across routes keeps 3B elevated but not at transformative conversion levels.

### Expanded Analysis

The decisive Endos gate is cultural-political: alignment with the revolution and the Party’s moral community. Ethnic zero is a positive finding under the Exclusion Test — Castroism does not permanently bar any ethnicity from full membership on blood grounds. The 3B score reflects the density of ideological and participatory requirements that separate formal residents or citizens from trusted members of the core in-group, weighted by the fact that majority birth routes do not impose a formal cultural examination comparable to naturalisation tests in some other systems.

Deportation and exit-control practices belong primarily on Core 2A / Core 1A; they are noted only insofar as they mark political exit from the Endos community.

### Why this produces 3A 0 / 3B 58

No ethnicity is permanently excluded on ethnic grounds → 3A = 0. Revolutionary loyalty, mass-organisation participation and counter-revolutionary exclusion as conditions of substantive belonging, moderated by low formal cultural tests on majority birth routes → 3B = 58.

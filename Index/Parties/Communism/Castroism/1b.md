# Core 1B: Communism – Castroism
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Final Score: 33**
**Regulation Score: 20**
**Taxation Score: 70**

## Regulation

### Overview
Castroism organised the Cuban economy around near-total state ownership of industry and large agriculture, central planning, administrative labour direction, and high state claim on surplus through enterprise remittances, with residual private activity confined to limited self-employment and small plots in later periods.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Dominant state ownership of industry, land (after agrarian reform) and services
* Central planning of investment, output and material allocation
* Administrative labour allocation and restrictions on independent employment
* Long-duration rationing of key consumer goods as a standing distribution system
* Restricted residual private and cooperative activity (size caps, negative lists, oversight; periodic tightening)
* Priority of political and social objectives in resource allocation
* Limited openings that did not restore independent decision-making in the commanding heights

### Expanded Analysis
Castroism organised the Cuban economy around near-total state ownership of industry and large agriculture, central planning, administrative labour direction, and high state claim on surplus through enterprise remittances, with residual private activity confined to limited self-employment and small plots in later periods. The institutional pattern is extensive state control of ownership, investment, labour allocation and surplus claim. Residual private activity, where it exists at all, is confined to the margins stated in the policy set. Ownership, forced labour and confiscatory mechanisms are Regulation; they are not mixed into the Taxation algebra.

### Regulation Score
The locked regulation instruments produce a Regulation Score of 20.

## Final Score

A = (20 + 70) / 2 = 45.0
L = min(20, 70) = 20
d = |20 − 70| / 100 = 0.5
F = (1 − 0.5) × 45.0 + 0.5 × 20 = 32.500 → **33**

**Final Score: 33**

### Why this produces a Final Score of 33
Regulation at 20 reflects the locked ownership, planning, labour-direction and extraction instruments. Taxation at 70 is the pure algebraic inverse of the stated effective fiscal claim under the Alternative Method. The A/L/d combination with strict ceiling produces Final Score 33.

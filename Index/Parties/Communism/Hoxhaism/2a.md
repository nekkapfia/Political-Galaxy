# Core 2A: Communism – Hoxhaism
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 88**

### Overview
Hoxhaism constructed an extreme sovereign and largely autarkic Albanian state, rejecting external institutional authority and alliance subordination after the breaks with Yugoslavia, the Soviet Union and China.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Exclusive Albanian control of territory, borders, law, military command and economic planning under Party direction.
* Rejection of external institutional authority after successive breaks with Yugoslav, Soviet and Chinese frameworks.
* Autarkic industrial and military mobilisation under complete national direction.
* Bunkerisation and militarised society as instruments of territorial and institutional closure.
* External connection points reduced to minimal controlled trade and diplomatic residual.

### Expanded Analysis
Hoxhaism imposed exclusive national control of territory, borders, law, military command and economic planning. External institutional authority was rejected after successive breaks with Yugoslav, Soviet and Chinese frameworks. Autarkic industrial and military mobilisation continued under complete national direction. Bunkerisation intensified territorial and institutional closure. External connection points were reduced to minimal controlled trade and diplomatic residual. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is extreme national closure with autarkic residual rather than external pooling of authority.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 88
Exclusive national control, rejection of external institutional authority, autarkic mobilisation and minimal controlled connection points place the final set inside the 80–89 High – Strict closure band at 88. The score follows from extreme national closure without external pooling of core authority.

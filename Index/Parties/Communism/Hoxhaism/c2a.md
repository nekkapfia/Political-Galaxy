# Cultural 2A: Communism – Hoxhaism
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
Hoxhaism subordinated individual status to class origin, political reliability and party-defined categories under conditions of extreme institutional closure.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class-background and political-reliability gates on promotion, education and trusted roles.
* Party and state categories organising virtually all significant status outcomes.
* Individual performance channels minimal and strictly inside political constraints.
* Independent civil association eliminated outside approved structures.
* Totalising category logic approaching high-Stalinist intensity.

### Expanded Analysis
Persons were measured almost exclusively through political and class categories. Individual agency had minimal independent effect on major outcomes. The unit of allocation was the category; residual individual ranking existed only inside tightly controlled structures. The pattern is very strong group subordination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Political and class categories as near-total organising units of status place Hoxhaism in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 22.

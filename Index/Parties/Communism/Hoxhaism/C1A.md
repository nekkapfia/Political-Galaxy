# Cultural 1A: Hoxhaism
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
Hoxhaism organises moral legitimacy around anti-revisionist orthodoxy, continuous revolution, purity against contamination, and material provision as duty of the socialist state. These are secular this-worldly claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Continuous revolution and anti-revisionism are described as political obligations of the correct line.
* Class enemies are subject to political and social elimination as a requirement of purity within the secular project.
* Autarky and ideological isolation are framed as political necessities against external contamination.
* Material provision for the population is cited as a duty of the socialist state.
* State performance is presented as evidence of correct orthodoxy.

### Expanded Analysis
Anti-revisionism, continuous revolution, purity, material provision, and state performance organise the moral conversation. These are secular political claims. No divine command, Cosmic law, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. Closure and orthodoxy intensity belong on Dogmatism. Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications. The foundation is Mundane.

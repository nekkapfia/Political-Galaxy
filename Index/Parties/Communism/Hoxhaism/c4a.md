# Cultural 4A: Communism – Hoxhaism
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 58**

### Overview
Hoxhaist Albania combined tight political control with class-origin and political-reliability gates on education and advancement. Formal mobility channels existed inside a highly constrained system; individual effort could improve status, but origin and loyalty markers created routine headwinds. The pattern sits at the upper end of the noticeable-shift / lower end of the moderate-determinism range.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Class-origin and political-reliability criteria that conditioned access to higher education, Party membership, and responsible posts.
* Formal educational and occupational pathways that remained open in principle but were filtered by background and loyalty assessments.
* Periodic campaigns against “hostile” class elements that reinforced origin-based barriers.
* Residual capacity for individuals from acceptable origins to rise through competence and political conformity.
* Highly controlled society in which independent pathways outside state and Party structures were minimal.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A tracks birth and inherited social position as constraints on mobility. Under Hoxhaism, class origin and family political standing mattered for life chances, but the system retained formal channels (schools, Party structures, industrial hierarchies) through which some individuals advanced. It did not institutionalise a comprehensive hereditary caste of the songbun or Nuremberg type. The overall pattern is consistent with the upper 51–59 / lower 60–69 range: both inter-class and intra-class mobility were realistic yet still restricted by origin markers and political gates. Soft statistical correlation alone would score lower; the institutional background checks and origin filters supply the additional determinism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 58
Origin and reliability gates on education and advancement, combined with residual formal mobility channels inside a tightly controlled system, align with the upper 51–59 band (both directions of mobility realistic but still restricted). Score 58.

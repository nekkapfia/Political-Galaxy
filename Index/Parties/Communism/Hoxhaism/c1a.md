### Expanded Analysis
Hoxhaism organises justification through closed ideological transcendence and permanent purity struggle. The living population cannot authorise revision through preference or evidence; the correct line defines legitimate politics. Anti-revisionism functions as higher-order duty against both Soviet and Western deviation. Autarky and isolation are moral requirements of purity, not mere strategic choices. Mundane sources—welfare, ordinary legality, demos preference—exist only inside the authorised frame and cannot override it. The pattern is extreme transcendent lean, among the highest in the communist cluster.

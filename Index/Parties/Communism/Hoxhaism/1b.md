## Regulation

### Overview
Hoxhaism organised the Albanian economy around near-total state and cooperative ownership, central planning, administrative labour direction, extreme autarky, and high extraction through compulsory delivery and enterprise remittances, with residual private activity confined to the narrowest personal plots.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Near-total state ownership of productive capital
* Extreme central planning of production, investment and allocation
* Administrative labour direction
* Constitutional and practical isolation from foreign capital and residual market forms
* Minimal private plots and private activity under tight constraint
* Priority of political and autarkic objectives over household consumption
* No significant independent enterprise sector at any stage

### Expanded Analysis
Hoxhaism organised the Albanian economy around near-total state and cooperative ownership, central planning, administrative labour direction, extreme autarky, and high extraction through compulsory delivery and enterprise remittances, with residual private activity confined to the narrowest personal plots. The institutional pattern is extensive state control of ownership, investment, labour allocation and surplus claim. Residual private activity, where it exists at all, is confined to the margins stated in the policy set. Ownership, forced labour and confiscatory mechanisms are Regulation; they are not mixed into the Taxation algebra.

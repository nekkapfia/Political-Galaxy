# Cultural 1B: Communism – Hoxhaism
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 95**

**Orthodoxy: 96, Tribalism: 95**

### Overview
Hoxhaism combines extreme internal closure around anti-revisionist Marxism-Leninism with comprehensive rejection of external socialist and liberal frameworks. Deviation from the pure line is treated as betrayal; rival systems are illegitimate. Both aspects approach totalitarian intensity under conditions of deliberate isolation, placing Hoxhaism near the top of the Strong band.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Party monopoly and suppression of internal factional opposition.
* Anti-revisionist purity as closed doctrine; engagement with Soviet, Chinese or Western alternatives treated as contamination.
* Surveillance, political police and isolation mechanisms protecting the line.
* Education and media monopoly excluding competing frameworks.
* Self-reliance ideology framing external models as hostile or decadent.
* Residual baseline of purity enforcement that left no recognised internal process for empirical revision of foundational principles.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is near-total: the anti-revisionist line is not open to legitimate internal empirical challenge. Party monopoly, surveillance and isolation mechanisms protect purity; engagement with Soviet, Chinese or Western alternatives is contamination rather than debate. Tribalism is equally extreme: rival socialist currents and liberal systems are illegitimate, not competing schools within a shared frame. Education and media monopoly exclude alternative platforms. Public dissent is career- and liberty-threatening. Mechanisms approach totalitarian intensity under conditions of deliberate isolation. The institutional pattern is extreme dogmatism on both aspects, among the highest in the communist set and calibrated directly against the Strong (86–95) upper anchors.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 95
Complete Party monopoly, anti-revisionist purity as non-negotiable doctrine, surveillance/isolation machinery, media and education monopoly, and the treatment of all external frameworks as contamination produce near-ceiling Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 95, matching the calibrated Hoxhaism position alongside Stalinism at the upper end of the Strong band.

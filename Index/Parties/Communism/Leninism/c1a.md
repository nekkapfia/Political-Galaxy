### Overview
Leninism grounds political legitimacy in the vanguard Party as bearer of historical consciousness, proletarian dictatorship, and revolutionary necessity that overrides ordinary democratic and liberal constraints. Ideological transcendence organises; demos preference is legitimate only when aligned with correct line. Residual baseline of transcendent moral justification: higher-order ideological, sacred or civilisational sources organise legitimacy and subordinate pure demotic or welfare claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Vanguard Party as exclusive interpreter of historical and class truth.
* Proletarian dictatorship as necessary stage overriding formal democratic equality.
* Revolutionary violence and repression justified by historical necessity.
* Spontaneous mass preference treated as insufficient without Party leadership.
* International revolutionary duty beyond single national demos.
* Mundane welfare subordinated to seizure and defence of power for historical ends.

### Expanded Analysis
Leninism organises justification through vanguard ideological transcendence. The working class in itself does not authorise the line; the Party as class-for-itself does. Liberal and demos constraints are explicitly secondary. The pattern is strong transcendent lean, foundational for later higher-scoring Party-state variants.

# Cultural 1A: Leninism
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
Leninism organises moral legitimacy around the historical mission of the proletariat, revolutionary seizure as political obligation, and the concrete interests of the working masses. These are secular this-worldly claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Seizure and defence of state power are framed as the indispensable means of advancing the historical mission of the proletariat.
* The concrete interests of the proletariat and the working masses are presented as the constituency the project serves.
* Class enemies and opposing political forces are subject to suppression as requirements of revolutionary survival and the historical project.
* State power and organisational capacity are cited as instruments and as evidence of the historical mission’s progress.
* Internationalist duty is described as extending the same historical project beyond the single national demos.

### Expanded Analysis
The historical mission of the proletariat, revolutionary seizure, constituency interest, and state capacity organise the moral conversation. These are secular historical and political claims. No divine command, Cosmic law, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. Intensity and exclusive authority belong on Dogmatism and related axes. Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications. The foundation is Mundane.

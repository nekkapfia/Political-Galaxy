# Cultural 1B: Communism – Leninism
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 90**

**Orthodoxy: 91, Tribalism: 89**

### Overview
Leninism establishes vanguard-party monopoly and democratic centralism as the core institutional pattern of internal closure, while treating liberal, bourgeois and competing socialist currents as class enemies or deviations. Both Orthodoxy and Tribalism are very high, placing Leninism solidly in the Strong band below the isolationist or eliminationist peaks.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Vanguard party monopoly and democratic centralism closing legitimate factional opposition after decisions.
* Suppression of organised political alternatives outside the Party.
* Cheka/security and censorship mechanisms protecting the line.
* Education and agitation framing Marxism-Leninism as sole scientific truth.
* Liberal, bourgeois and competing socialist currents treated as class enemies or deviations.
* Residual baseline of Party discipline and security organs that made public challenge career- and liberty-threatening.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is very high: vanguard monopoly and democratic centralism close legitimate factional opposition after decisions; security and censorship protect the line; education frames Marxism-Leninism as sole scientific truth. Public challenge is labelled opportunism or counter-revolution. Tribalism is very high: liberal, bourgeois and competing socialist currents are class enemies or deviations, not legitimate rivals within a shared debate frame. Mechanisms include Party monopoly and security organs without always reaching the full Stalinist terror intensity or the deliberate isolation of Hoxhaism. The institutional pattern is strong dogmatism calibrated below the most extreme communist variants yet still firmly in the Strong (86–95) band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 90
Vanguard monopoly, democratic centralism, security/censorship apparatus, educational monopoly of the scientific-truth claim, and systematic treatment of external currents as class enemies produce very high Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 90, the calibrated Leninism position within the Strong band.

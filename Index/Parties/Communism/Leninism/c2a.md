# Cultural 2A: Communism – Leninism
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 22**

### Overview
Leninism subordinates individual status to class position, party reliability and revolutionary category, while retaining residual performance and technical channels inside those constraints.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class origin and political-reliability criteria structuring access to education, advancement and political trust.
* Party and soviet institutions mediating status and opportunity.
* Individual technical or examination channels existing inside revolutionary-category constraints.
* Independent civil association suppressed outside Party-approved structures.
* Formal equality rhetoric operating inside a category-structured system.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant allocation principle is collective-political rather than individual-meritocratic. Individual agency operates within class-and-party category structures. The person as sovereign agent is secondary to revolutionary location. The pattern is very strong group subordination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 22
Class and party-reliability categories as primary organising units of status place Leninism in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 22.

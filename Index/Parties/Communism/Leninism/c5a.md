# Cultural 5A: Communism – Leninism
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 14**

### Overview
Leninism is a revolutionary doctrine of radical break with existing traditional, religious and bourgeois social forms. It treats inherited traditions as obstacles to be dismantled in the transition to socialist modernity, not as baselines to be preserved.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit programme of transforming “backward” traditional social structures through revolutionary institutional change rather than protecting them as defaults.
* Hostility to religion and customary authority as competing sources of legitimacy, expressed in doctrine and converted in early Soviet practice into anti-religious decrees and cultural policy.
* Reorganisation of family, education and cultural transmission under Party direction rather than inherited traditional mechanisms (church, customary law, pre-revolutionary curricula).
* Cultural policy subordinated to revolutionary modernisation goals in education content and public institutions.
* No durable institutional protection for unmodified pre-revolutionary traditional norms as an autonomous baseline in law, schooling or family status.
* Residual pre-revolutionary continuity treated as temporary lag to be superseded, not as a protected institutional default.
* Domain hierarchy: education, family and religious authority reorganised under Party direction.

### Expanded Analysis
On Cultural 5A the decisive pattern is doctrinal and early-practical: inherited traditional transmission is an obstacle on the road to socialist modernity. Primary domains—education content, family organisation and the legitimacy of religious/customary authority—are to be reorganised under Party direction. Early Soviet practice converted that doctrine into anti-religious decrees, family-law rupture and suppression of customary authority, so the residual baseline is not protected continuity but transitional lag. There is no programme in which pre-revolutionary family forms, confessional education or customary law remain the operative public standard. Domain hierarchy therefore runs uniformly transformative. As doctrine-plus-early-practice rather than a fully sustained Year Zero campaign across every residual sphere for decades, the pattern sits in the near-absolute transformative band rather than the absolute floor.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 14
Education and cultural transmission under Party direction, hostility to religion and custom as rival legitimacies, and the absence of durable protection for pre-revolutionary norms as baseline place Leninism in the 11–20 band. The residual pre-revolutionary order is not conserved. Placement above the floor reflects document type—doctrine and early practice, not fully realised permanent prohibition of every private residual under continuous monitoring—while remaining clearly transformative on primary domains.

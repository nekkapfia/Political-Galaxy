# Cultural 1B: Communism – Maoism
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 93**

**Orthodoxy: 94, Tribalism: 93**

### Overview
Maoism combines continuous-revolution orthodoxy with rejection of revisionist and bourgeois frameworks. Struggle campaigns enforce internal closure; external systems are illegitimate class enemies. Both aspects are extreme during mobilisation phases, producing a Strong-band score near the upper end of the communist cluster.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Party leadership and mass campaigns against revisionism and right deviation.
* Cultural Revolution-type mechanisms pathologising and punishing deviation from the correct line.
* Education and cultural control reproducing Mao Zedong Thought as closed authority.
* Liberal, Soviet-revisionist and traditional frameworks treated as enemies of continuous revolution.
* Public dissent framed as counter-revolutionary or revisionist betrayal.
* Residual baseline of continuous-revolution doctrine that treated foundational political principles as non-negotiable even when limited technical debate was permitted.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is extreme during mobilisation phases: continuous revolution and struggle against revisionism enforce closure through campaigns rather than open internal empirical revision. Cultural Revolution-type mechanisms pathologised and punished deviation. Tribalism is extreme: liberal, Soviet-revisionist and traditional frameworks are class enemies of continuous revolution. Education and cultural control reproduce Mao Zedong Thought as closed authority. Limited technical debate may exist where it does not touch the foundational political line. The institutional pattern is very high dogmatism with recurrent mobilisation against mundane or revisionist openness, calibrated within the Strong (86–95) band alongside Castroism and below the purest isolationist or Year-Zero peaks.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 93
Mass campaigns against revisionism, Cultural Revolution-style pathologisation of deviation, educational monopoly of Mao Zedong Thought, and systematic enemy designation of external frameworks produce extreme Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 93.

# Cultural 1A: Maoism
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
Maoism organises moral legitimacy around continuous revolution, class struggle as permanent political requirement, and the concrete interests of the peasantry and masses. These are secular this-worldly claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Continuous revolution and Cultural Revolution logic are presented as permanent political requirements of socialist society.
* Class struggle is framed as a permanent feature of socialist society requiring ongoing vigilance.
* The concrete interests of the peasantry and the masses are cited as the constituency the project serves.
* Material production and mass campaigns are invoked as proofs of correct revolutionary line.
* Revolutionary purity is presented as superior to mere administrative stability within the same secular project.

### Expanded Analysis
Continuous revolution, class struggle, mass interest, and production proofs organise the moral conversation. These are secular political and historical claims. No divine command, Cosmic law, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. Pace and intensity of rupture belong on Radicalism; closure on Dogmatism. Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications. The foundation is Mundane.

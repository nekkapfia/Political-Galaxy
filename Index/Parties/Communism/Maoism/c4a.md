# Cultural 4A: Communism – Maoism
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 64**

### Overview
Maoism, especially in its radical phases, made class background (chengfen) and political reliability major determinants of educational and occupational opportunity. “Bad class origin” (landlords, rich peasants, rightists, and related categories) created lasting barriers; individual merit was frequently subordinated to origin and political labels. Formal mobility channels existed but were heavily filtered by hereditary and political markers, producing gated advancement in the moderate-to-predominantly deterministic range.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Class-background classification (chengfen) that assigned families to categories with differential access to education, Party membership, and desirable posts.
* Cultural Revolution practices that treated bad class origin as a decisive and often multi-generational barrier.
* Formal educational and occupational pathways that remained open in principle but were conditioned by origin and political reliability.
* Periodic campaigns that closed or reversed mobility for those with stigmatised family backgrounds.
* Residual capacity for individuals from “good” or neutral class origins to advance through competence and political conformity.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A measures birth and inherited social position as constraints on life outcomes. Maoist China institutionalised class origin as a formal marker that shaped life chances, particularly in education and cadre selection. During the Cultural Revolution the weight of hereditary class labels intensified, reducing the scope for individual action to overcome assigned status. Outside the most radical periods, formal mobility channels (schools, factories, Party structures) continued to operate, so the system never fully matched pure hereditary caste. The overall pattern sits in the 60–69 band: inter-class movement was achievable with significant effort but remained uncommon and heavily shaped by early (including hereditary) advantages. Soft statistical correlation without institutional origin gates would score lower; the formal chengfen system supplies the additional determinism.

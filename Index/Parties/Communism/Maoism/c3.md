# Cultural 3: Communism – Maoism
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 0 | 3B 74**

### Overview
Maoism defined membership by class position, political line, and continuous revolutionary transformation rather than by ethnicity. The Exclusion Test is not met. Trusted membership required intensive ideological remoulding, participation in class struggle, and cultural transformation, reaching extreme intensity during the Cultural Revolution. Immigration and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Class origin and political reliability as the primary criteria for full membership of the revolutionary community.
* Continuous revolution and ideological remoulding (including Cultural Revolution practices) as demands for cultural-political transformation.
* Ethnicity secondary to class and political line; no formal ancestral blood bar to membership.
* Severance from “bourgeois”, traditional, and revisionist identities required for trusted status.
* Immigration and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is not met. Maoism did not permanently exclude any ethnicity from membership on blood grounds. 3A is 0.

3B is high. Full trusted membership demanded demonstrable political-cultural transformation and ongoing loyalty. The Cultural Revolution period intensified these demands. This aligns with Predominantly Demanding to High Cultural Demand (70–79).

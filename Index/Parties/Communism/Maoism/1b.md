# Core 1B: Communism – Maoism
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Final Score: 26**
**Regulation Score: 14**
**Taxation Score: 75**

## Regulation

### Overview
Maoism organised the Chinese economy around collectivisation, central planning, administrative labour direction, Great Leap mobilisation, and high procurement claim on agricultural output, with residual private plots and limited household activity at the margins.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Collectivisation of agriculture and formation of people’s communes
* High forced extraction from agriculture (procurement quotas; price scissors)
* Administrative mass labour mobilisation (Great Leap campaigns, backyard steel, water conservancy)
* Hukou system restricting labour mobility and rural-urban movement
* Priority of accumulation and political goals over household consumption
* Detailed planning targets for industry and agriculture
* Residual household plots periodically restricted or suppressed
* Cultural Revolution disruption of ordinary managerial decision-making (political struggle replacing enterprise autonomy)
* State ownership of industry and monopoly of key distribution channels
* Famine (GLF) as mechanical outcome of extraction and mobilisation intensity

### Expanded Analysis
Maoism organised the Chinese economy around collectivisation, central planning, administrative labour direction, Great Leap mobilisation, and high procurement claim on agricultural output, with residual private plots and limited household activity at the margins. The institutional pattern is extensive state control of ownership, investment, labour allocation and surplus claim. Residual private activity, where it exists at all, is confined to the margins stated in the policy set. Ownership, forced labour and confiscatory mechanisms are Regulation; they are not mixed into the Taxation algebra.

### Regulation Score
The locked regulation instruments produce a Regulation Score of 14.

## Taxation

Ownership, forced labour and confiscation remain Regulation only.

**Taxation Score: 75**

## Final Score

A = (14 + 75) / 2 = 44.5
L = min(14, 75) = 14
d = |14 − 75| / 100 = 0.61
F = (1 − 0.61) × 44.5 + 0.61 × 14 = 25.895 → **26**

**Final Score: 26**

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Regulation at 14 reflects the locked ownership, planning, labour-direction and extraction instruments. Taxation at 75 is the pure algebraic inverse of the stated effective fiscal claim under the Alternative Method. The A/L/d combination with strict ceiling produces Final Score 26.

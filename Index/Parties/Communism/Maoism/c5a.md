# Cultural 5A: Communism – Maoism
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 9**

### Overview
Maoism subordinated inherited family, religious and national-continuity transmission to party control through campaigns against the Four Olds and continuous revolutionary mobilisation, with later selective promotion of traditional culture only under Party leadership.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Cultural Revolution and related campaigns targeting the Four Olds (old ideas, culture, customs, habits) as objects of struggle and destruction.
* Red Guard and mass mobilisation against traditional religious sites, ancestral practices and cultural elites as carriers of pre-revolutionary continuity.
* Education and political study reorganised around Maoist texts and class struggle rather than inherited classical or confessional curricula.
* Family and lineage authority attacked as feudal residue; loyalty redirected to Party and revolution.
* Later selective rehabilitation of “excellent traditional Chinese culture” only under Party control and in service of socialist modernisation — not as autonomous baseline.
* Residual pre-revolutionary traditional order was the target of continuous revolution; elevating it as preferred public order remained illegitimate.
* Domain hierarchy: education content and family/lineage authority redesigned against inherited baselines.

### Expanded Analysis
Maoism's Cultural 5A pattern is transformative destruction of inherited traditional transmission, with only later instrumental retrieval under Party sovereignty. Primary domains were reorganised against the Four Olds: education became political study around revolutionary texts; family and lineage authority were attacked as feudal; religious and customary sites were targets of mobilisation. The residual pre-revolutionary order was not a protected baseline—it was the problem continuous revolution sought to overcome. Later selective promotion of traditional culture did not restore autonomous traditional authority; it remained under Party leadership and modernisation goals. Domain hierarchy places education and family/lineage transmission first: both were reconstructed against inherited continuity. Instrumental post-Mao cultural retrieval does not convert the long-run pattern into mid-range preservation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 9
Campaigns against the Four Olds, attack on family/lineage and religious authority, and education reorganised around revolutionary transmission place Maoism in the floor band (0–10). Later Party-controlled cultural retrieval does not restore autonomous traditional baselines, so upward movement into the contested mid-range is not justified.

# Core 2B: Communism – Juche
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 54**

### Overview
Juche ideology emphasises extreme self-reliance and has produced a state with limited large-scale external military projection relative to Soviet or Chinese patterns. Border conflicts and selective support activities exist, but the dominant external posture is defensive isolation rather than systematic interference in multiple foreign internal orders.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Doctrine of self-reliance constrains open-ended foreign entanglement.
* Historical border conflicts and limited conventional operations remain narrower than Soviet-style sphere control.
* Selective support for foreign revolutionary or aligned groups has occurred but has not been the primary mode of state action.
* No multi-country occupation system comparable to Stalinist Eastern Europe.
* Self-defence of North Korean territory is out of scope for Core 2B.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. Juche’s practical record shows more isolation and regime survival focus than transformative external control. Residual support activities and regional tensions prevent a very high score, yet the absence of sustained occupation or systematic regime-change campaigns keeps the placement in the mid range. Internal repression is scored elsewhere.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 54
Limited external projection combined with residual support activities and regional friction produces a mid-range non-intervention score.

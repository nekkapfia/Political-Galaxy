# Cultural 4A: Communism – Juche
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 62**

### Overview
The Juche system in North Korea operates a formal hereditary political classification (songbun) that assigns citizens to core, wavering, or hostile classes largely on the basis of family background and the political reliability of ancestors. This multi-generational birth-based status system constrains education, occupation, residence, and life chances. Individual effort can produce limited movement within strata, but crossing major songbun boundaries is heavily restricted. The architecture is a clear case of moderate-to-predominantly deterministic social structure.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Songbun hereditary classification system that assigns political and social status primarily by family background and ancestral political reliability.
* Differential access to education, employment, housing, and Party advancement according to songbun category.
* Multi-generational transmission of status: children inherit the consequences of parents’ and grandparents’ classifications.
* Formal mobility channels that exist but are filtered by songbun; hostile-class origin creates lasting barriers.
* Residual intra-stratum movement through competence and political conformity for those in core or wavering categories.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores the degree to which birth circumstances determine life outcomes. Songbun is a formal hereditary status system: family political background assigns individuals to ranked categories that shape nearly every major pathway. This is stronger than soft statistical class correlation; it is institutionalised birth-based assignment. The system retains some formal channels for advancement, so it does not reach the near-absolute bands reserved for total hereditary occupational locks or racial caste systems with almost no residual agency. It fits the 60–69 band: inter-class (or inter-songbun) movement is possible but hard; once inside a category, early and inherited advantages continue to shape further rise. The multi-generational character of the classification supplies additional weight relative to purely individual political-reliability gates.

Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 62
Formal hereditary songbun classification that constrains education, occupation, and residence on the basis of family background, combined with limited residual mobility within strata, aligns with the 60–69 band. Score 62.

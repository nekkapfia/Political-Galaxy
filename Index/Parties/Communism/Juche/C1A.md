# Cultural 1A: Juche
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 88 (38T)

### Overview
Juche organises moral legitimacy around the sacralised Leader and lineage as objects of worship, with national destiny elevated above individual interest and performance cited inside that frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Transcendent Justifications
* The Leader and the lineage are treated as objects of worship and sacralised centres of the nation’s existence (eternal leaders, ritual devotion, ontological status beyond ordinary political authority).
* Juche self-reliance is described as the binding ideological and moral framework of the nation under the sacralised leadership.
* National and revolutionary destiny are presented as superior to individual or factional interest within the sacralised order.

#### Mundane Justifications
* Performance and welfare claims appear as proofs of correct leadership and as goods provided to the population.

### Expanded Analysis
The Leader and lineage are treated as objects of worship — the worship threshold for deification is met. That is a clear Transcendent source. National destiny is elevated inside that sacralised frame. Performance and welfare claims appear as proofs inside the frame and do not override it. Mundane sources are present but secondary; they do not organise independent legitimacy. The foundation is strongly Transcendent on genuine deification grounds.

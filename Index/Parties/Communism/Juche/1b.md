# Core 1B: Communism – Juche
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Final Score: 18**
**Regulation Score: 16**
**Taxation Score: 20**

## Regulation

### Overview
Juche organised the North Korean economy around near-total state ownership, central planning, administrative labour direction, military-first priority, and extreme state claim on surplus through enterprise remittances after formal tax abolition, with residual private activity confined to informal margins and limited market activity in later periods.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Near-total state and military ownership of productive capital
* Songun / military-first allocation as a standing priority rule over civilian consumption
* Extreme labour extraction and mass mobilisation (speed battles, campaign labour)
* Administrative control of labour allocation and mobility
* Political classification systems affecting access to economic opportunity and rations
* Residual informal markets under surveillance and periodic crackdown
* Severe restrictions on independent private enterprise of any scale
* Famine (Arduous March) as mechanical outcome of extraction and priority rules

### Expanded Analysis
Juche organised the North Korean economy around near-total state ownership, central planning, administrative labour direction, military-first priority, and extreme state claim on surplus through enterprise remittances after formal tax abolition, with residual private activity confined to informal margins and limited market activity in later periods. The institutional pattern is extensive state control of ownership, investment, labour allocation and surplus claim. Residual private activity, where it exists at all, is confined to the margins stated in the policy set. Ownership, forced labour and confiscatory mechanisms are Regulation; they are not mixed into the Taxation algebra.

### Regulation Score
The locked regulation instruments produce a Regulation Score of 16.

## Final Score

A = (16 + 20) / 2 = 18.0
L = min(16, 20) = 16
d = |16 − 20| / 100 = 0.04
F = (1 − 0.04) × 18.0 + 0.04 × 16 = 17.920 → **18**

**Final Score: 18**

### Why this produces a Final Score of 18
Regulation at 16 reflects the locked ownership, planning, labour-direction and extraction instruments. Taxation at 20 is the pure algebraic inverse of the stated effective fiscal claim under the Alternative Method. The A/L/d combination with strict ceiling produces Final Score 18.

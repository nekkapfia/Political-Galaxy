## Regulation

### Overview
Juche realises a militarised total command economy. The state and party exercise extreme control over production, investment, labour and foreign-currency earnings. Formal central planning is combined with mass mobilisation and extensive informal extraction mechanisms; residual private/market activity remains subordinate.

### Relevant Policies
- Near-total state and military control of formal productive assets
- Central planning and mass mobilisation of labour
- Administrative wage setting at nominal or near-zero levels in the formal sector
- Extensive forced or highly discounted labour extraction (domestic and overseas)
- Mandatory political-fund contributions and quasi-taxation of informal market activity
- Priority allocation to military, heavy industry and state construction projects
- Maintain only a tightly controlled residual informal sector

### Expanded Analysis
Under Juche the formal economy is organised around state and military objectives with extreme direction of labour and resources. Overseas worker remittances and informal-market activity are themselves subject to heavy extraction. Consumption is subordinated to regime priorities. The residual informal sector, while economically significant for household survival, does not alter the dominant mechanical character of comprehensive state control over production, investment and labour allocation.

The package exhibits extreme state and military control over production, investment and labour.

### Final Score
The decisive mechanical effects are near-total state/military ownership and control, extreme labour extraction, mass mobilisation, and subordination of residual market activity. This produces a Regulation Score of 20.

# Core 2A: Communism – Juche
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 88**

### Overview
Juche organises the state around absolute political, economic and military self-reliance, with exclusive national control of all core sovereign functions and minimal external engagement. External institutional authority is rejected.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Doctrinal and practical insistence on self-reliance in politics, economy and defence.
* Exclusive national control of territory, borders, law, military command and nuclear forces.
* Maintains exclusive national authority with no external judicial, political or military body recognised as binding.
* Minimal and tightly controlled external economic engagement.
* Systematically minimises foreign political, economic and cultural influence as a security requirement.

### Expanded Analysis
Juche organises the state around absolute political, economic and military self-reliance. Territory, borders, law, military command and nuclear forces remain under exclusive national control; no external judicial, political or military body is recognised as binding; external economic engagement is minimal and tightly controlled; foreign influence is systematically minimised as a security requirement. The institutional pattern on Core 2A is near-total formal and practical independence, with isolation accepted as the price of that posture. Primary drag from external pooling of authority is near-absent; controlled connection points are minimal and subordinated to national security requirements.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 88
Absolute self-reliance and exclusive control of all core sovereign functions with no external authority recognised as binding place the final set inside the 80–89 High – Strict closure with minimal controlled connection points band. The score of 88 follows from maximal national control without external pooling of core authority.

# Cultural 1A: Communism – Juche
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 91**

### Overview
Juche grounds political legitimacy in the absolute authority of the leader, the self-reliance of the Korean nation as ideological subject, and a closed revolutionary-moral system that subordinates all ordinary preference to correct line. Mundane sources are extinguished as independent organisers. The profile is extreme transcendent lean. Residual baseline of transcendent moral justification: higher-order ideological, sacred or civilisational sources organise legitimacy and subordinate pure demotic or welfare claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Leader and Party as infallible interpreters of historical and national destiny.
* Juche self-reliance as binding ideological-moral framework, not optional policy.
* Mass mobilisation and ideological education as formation of correct subjects.
* Dissent framed as moral-political betrayal rather than legitimate preference.
* National and revolutionary destiny outrank individual or factional interest.
* Performance and welfare claims exist only inside the authorised ideological frame.
* Residual baseline of transcendent moral justification: higher-order ideological, sacred or civilisational sources organise legitimacy and subordinate pure demotic or welfare claims.

### Expanded Analysis
Juche organises justification through closed ideological and leader-centric transcendence. The demos does not authorise the line; the line defines the legitimate demos and the meaning of national self-reliance. Ordinary popular preference, market outcomes and liberal rights have no independent standing where they conflict with the authorised path. Performance and welfare language appears only as validation of correct leadership, never as a licence to set the line aside. Mass mobilisation and ideological education aim to form subjects who internalise the higher order rather than to aggregate pre-existing preference. The institutional pattern is extreme transcendent lean, among the highest in the set.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 91
Leader authority and Juche ideology as binding higher-order sources that extinguish independent demos justification produce extreme transcendent lean. The score is 91.

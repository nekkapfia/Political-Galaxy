# Cultural 2A: Communism – Juche
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 20**

### Overview
Juche subordinates individual status to political reliability, collective duty and state-defined categories, with the person measured primarily through loyalty and collective location.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Political reliability and loyalty gates on advancement and trusted roles.
* Collective and state-mediated channels organising status and opportunity.
* Individual performance metrics operating inside political-category constraints.
* Independent civil association tightly controlled or suppressed.
* Residual formal rhetoric of popular sovereignty operating inside a category-structured system.

### Expanded Analysis
Persons are measured primarily through political and collective categories. Individual agency exists only within tightly bounded loyalty and collective structures. The dominant unit of allocation is the political-collective category rather than the sovereign individual. The pattern is severe-to-very-strong group subordination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 20
Political-loyalty and collective categories as primary organising units of status place Juche at the boundary of severe and very strong group-subordination bands. Score 20.

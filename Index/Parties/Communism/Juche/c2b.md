# Cultural 2B: Communism – Juche
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 80**

### Overview
Juche elevates the Korean nation and its leadership as the central source of meaning and legitimacy. National (and leader-centric) pride is the organising principle of education, media and ritual; systematic shame frameworks are rejected. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Education and media relentlessly celebrate national achievement, continuity and the leadership’s role.
* Symbols, monuments and mass rituals affirm the nation and its unique path.
* Criticism of the national project or leadership is treated as betrayal.
* Historical narrative is fully nationalised and subordinated to the Juche frame.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
North Korea under Juche produces a high Honour/Pride score. The nation (fused with the leadership) is the primary emotional and cultural organising principle. The score sits at the top of the Honour band.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 80
Nation and leadership treated as the central source of legitimacy, with zero institutional space for organising shame, place Juche at the Honour/Pride boundary.

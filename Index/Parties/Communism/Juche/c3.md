# Cultural 3: Communism – Juche
## Ethnic and Cultural Exclusivity Analysis
**Score: 3A 0 | 3B 80**

### Overview
Juche ideology contains strong ethnic-national rhetoric and emphasis on the distinctiveness of the Korean nation. However, under the strict Exclusion Test, no ethnicity is permanently barred from membership of the core political community solely on ethnic grounds; formal criteria remain political loyalty and ideological immersion. Trusted membership demands extreme cultural-political transformation, leader loyalty, and severance from external identities. Immigration and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Formal membership of the core political community defined by political loyalty to the leadership and acceptance of Juche ideology, not by blood quantum.
* Strong ethnic-national rhetoric and “pure Korean” cultural emphasis exist, but do not create a permanent ethnic bar equivalent to racial legislation.
* Extreme demands for ideological immersion, leader worship, cultural isolation, and rejection of external political and cultural identities.
* Naturalisation and incorporation, where they occur, are governed by political reliability rather than ancestral exclusion.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
The Exclusion Test is not met. Despite intense ethnic-national rhetoric, the operating criterion for full trusted membership is political-cultural loyalty rather than permanent ethnic exclusion. Individuals of non-Korean background have faced extreme practical barriers, but the system does not codify a blood bar that permanently excludes named ethnicities from membership in the manner of racial law. Under the authoritative scoring rule, 3A is therefore 0.

3B is very high. Trusted membership requires dense transformation: total ideological immersion, demonstrated loyalty, cultural isolation from external influences, and severance from rival identities. This aligns with High Cultural Demand (80–89).

### Why this produces 3A 0 / 3B 80
3A aligns with 0 (Exclusion Test not met; no permanent ethnic bar). 3B aligns with 80–89 (high cultural-political transformation and loyalty demands).

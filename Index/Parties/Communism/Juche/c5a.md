# Cultural 5A: Communism – Juche
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 11**

### Overview
Juche subordinated inherited family, religious and national-continuity transmission to party and leader-centred institutional control; elevating pre-Juche Korean tradition as a preferred order remained incompatible with the regime.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Mandatory ideological study and leader-worship practices in schools, workplaces and public life as the core transmission system.
* Education content organised around Juche ideology and leader authority rather than pre-revolutionary Korean classical, confessional or customary curricula as public baseline.
* Independent religious authority suppressed; residual religious practice tightly restricted and subordinated to regime control.
* Family and social organisation structured through party, workplace and neighbourhood organisational controls; songbun hereditary status classifying loyalty.
* Pre-Juche Korean cultural forms permitted only when aligned with regime narrative; elevating the pre-Juche order as preferable prohibited.
* Residual pre-revolutionary traditional continuity was not restored as autonomous institutional baseline; operative transmission was Juche and leader cult.
* Domain hierarchy: education and ideological transmission primary; family and religious authority subordinated to regime.

### Expanded Analysis
Juche replaces inherited traditional transmission with leader-centred ideological transmission. Primary domains—education and public ideological practice—are organised around mandatory study and leader authority. Independent religious authority is suppressed; family and social life are structured through party and neighbourhood controls. Pre-Juche Korean tradition is not the public baseline: where traditional forms appear, they are aligned with regime narrative. Elevating the pre-Juche order as preferred remains illegitimate. Domain hierarchy places education and ideological transmission first; residual traditional culture is not a protected autonomous default. Regime-manufactured ritual (including mandatory leader imagery and organisational controls) is substitution, not preservation of the inherited baseline.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 11
Mandatory leader-centred ideological transmission, suppression of independent religious authority, and prohibition of elevating pre-Juche tradition as preferred place Juche in the low 11–20 band. Higher placement would require autonomous traditional transmission as institutional baseline; the locked analysis shows Party and leader control of primary domains.

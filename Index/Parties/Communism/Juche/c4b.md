# Cultural 4B: Communism – Juche
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 62**

### Overview
Juche combines structural and self-reliance diagnostics with hierarchical and political-reliability criteria. Gaps are treated as partly structural and partly as failures of collective discipline and loyalty.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Political reliability and collective self-reliance as organisers of outcomes.
* Gaps under formal rules treated as residual structural or loyalty failures.
* Corrective mechanisms include political education and hierarchical control.
* Individual variance heavily constrained.
* Contested structural and hierarchical diagnostic.

### Expanded Analysis
The framework locks a moderated structural gap-as-proof diagnostic within a self-reliance and hierarchical frame. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 62
Structural gap diagnostic moderated by hierarchical self-reliance and political-reliability elements produces a mid-high contested score. Gaps are treated as partly structural and partly failures of collective discipline.

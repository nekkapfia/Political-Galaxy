# Cultural 5B: Communism – Juche
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 70**

### Overview

Juche combines long periods of apparent stagnation with recurring campaign-style mobilisations and rapid institutional directives from the centre. When the leadership prioritises a goal, implementation is typically compressed and leaves little room for deliberative or gradualist sequencing. The overall pattern is mid-high radicalism rather than pure gradualism.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Recurring mass mobilisations and speed campaigns (construction, production, political study) conducted as short, intense drives.
* Central directives on institutional and social organisation implemented with limited intermediate consultation or pilot stages.
* Education and propaganda systems that frame rapid collective effort and obedience to tempo as core virtues.
* Capacity for abrupt policy shifts (economic, security, diplomatic) executed on compressed timelines when ordered from the top.
* Long intervals of low outward change coexist with an institutional design that remains oriented toward rapid compliance rather than deliberative sequencing.
* Observable pattern is therefore mixed but weighted toward the system’s demonstrated ability and preference for accelerated directives.

### Expanded Analysis

Juche does not display the continuous high-volume campaign tempo of peak Maoism or Stalinism, yet it retains a clear design against deliberative delay. When the centre moves, implementation is rapid and comprehensive. This keeps the score in the mid-high band (70–79) rather than the gradualist range occupied by consensus-oriented systems.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 70

Recurring campaign mobilisations and central capacity for compressed institutional directives, set against long stagnation intervals, place Juche at 70.

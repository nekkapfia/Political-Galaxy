# Cultural 1B: Communism – Juche
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 96**

**Orthodoxy: 97, Tribalism: 96**

### Overview
Juche combines total internal closure around leader thought and the Juche idea with comprehensive rejection of external moral-political frameworks. Loyalty to the leader and Party defines the boundary of legitimate thought. Both Orthodoxy and Tribalism sit at the extreme end, producing a Total/Extreme band score.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Leader thought and Juche idea as supreme closed guidance.
* No legal organised internal opposition or factional challenge to the line.
* Surveillance and organisational control extending into daily life.
* Education and media monopoly reproducing the official ideology.
* External systems (liberal, imperialist, revisionist) framed as enemy or illegitimate.
* Residual baseline of total organisational capture leaving no residual space for open internal contestation of foundations.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is extreme: leader thought and the Juche idea function as supreme closed guidance. No legal organised internal opposition exists; surveillance and organisational control extend into daily life; education and media monopoly reproduce official ideology. Tribalism is extreme: liberal, imperialist and revisionist systems are framed as enemy or illegitimate rather than as debatable alternatives. The institutional pattern combines total organisational monopoly with continuous ideological reproduction and external enemy designation. Intensity remains consistently high across the post-establishment period; limited technical or managerial discussion does not open foundational political principles to challenge. This places Juche in the Total/Extreme (96–100) calibration cluster with Khmer Rouge and Nazism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 96
Supreme status of leader thought, absence of legal internal opposition, pervasive surveillance, media/education monopoly and systematic framing of external frameworks as enemy produce ceiling-level Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula therefore yields 96, the calibrated Juche position at the extreme end of the scale.

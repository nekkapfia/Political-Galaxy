# Cultural 1B: Communism – Marxism
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 60**

**Orthodoxy: 58, Tribalism: 62**

### Overview
End-state Marxism presents doctrinal closure around historical materialism and class emancipation while dissolving the standing Party-state enforcement machinery once the state withers. Orthodoxy is therefore moderate (doctrinal rather than institutional); Tribalism remains elevated in theory against capitalist and liberal frameworks. The result is a mid-range score distinctly lower than any Leninist or later Party-state variant.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Historical materialism and class emancipation as closed theoretical foundations in doctrine.
* Anticipated free association of producers reducing institutional punishment of internal dissent once the state withers.
* Rejection of capitalist and liberal frameworks as expressions of class domination (tribalism in theory).
* Absence of residual democratic-centralist enforcement apparatus, Party monopoly, security organs or media monopoly in the pure end-state model.
* No institutional mechanism for punishing deviation comparable to governing communist regimes.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is moderate: historical materialism and class emancipation are closed theoretical foundations in doctrine, but the pure end-state model dissolves standing Party monopoly, security organs and media monopoly. Without those institutions, enforcement of closure is doctrinal rather than state-total. Tribalism remains elevated in theory against capitalist and liberal frameworks as expressions of class domination, yet lacks the full state apparatus of rejection characteristic of Leninist regimes. Anticipated free association of producers reduces institutional punishment of internal dissent once the state withers. The institutional pattern is mid-range dogmatism relative to Party-state regimes—doctrinal closure without totalitarian machinery—and correctly sits in the Noticeable (56–65) band as the calibrated end-state Marxism anchor.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 60
Doctrinal closure without residual Party-state enforcement machinery produces only moderate Orthodoxy; theoretical rejection of liberal and capitalist frameworks produces elevated but non-institutional Tribalism. The formula therefore yields 60, the calibrated distinction between pure doctrine and governing communist systems.

# Cultural 1A: Marxism
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
Marxism organises moral legitimacy around historical materialism, the classless end-state as historical resolution of exploitation, and the concrete interests of the producing class. These are secular this-worldly claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Historical materialism is presented as the scientific account of history and the necessity of class emancipation.
* The classless and stateless end-state is described as the historical resolution of exploitation.
* The concrete interests of the producing class are the stated ground of the project.
* Emancipation of the associated producers is framed as the ethical and political horizon of the project.
* Coordination among free producers is anticipated after the withering of coercive higher authority.

### Expanded Analysis
Historical materialism, class emancipation, the classless end-state, and the interests of the producing class organise the moral conversation. These are secular historical and ethical claims about this-worldly emancipation. No divine command, Cosmic law, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications. The foundation is Mundane.

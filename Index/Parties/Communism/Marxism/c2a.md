# Cultural 2A: Communism – Marxism
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 34**

### Overview
As doctrine, Marxism treats class location as the primary analytical and eventual organising unit of status and historical agency, while residual individual and technical channels are acknowledged inside the class frame.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class position as the fundamental unit of historical and social analysis.
* Collective and class-based remedies treated as the proper response to structured inequality.
* Individual technical or moral qualities recognised but secondary to class location.
* Doctrinal end-state oriented toward classless society, but transitional practice organised by class category.
* Distinct from both liberal person-unit systems and the most totalising regime implementations.

### Expanded Analysis
The doctrinal unit of measure is the class category rather than the sovereign individual. Individual agency is real but interpreted through, and ultimately subordinated to, class location. Relative to high-Stalinist or Khmer practice, doctrinal Marxism retains more residual space for individual differentiation inside the class frame; relative to liberal individualism it remains strongly group-primacy. The score reflects doctrine rather than any single regime.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 34
Class category as primary analytical and transitional organising unit, with residual individual differentiation inside the frame, places doctrinal Marxism in the strong group-primacy band. Score 34.

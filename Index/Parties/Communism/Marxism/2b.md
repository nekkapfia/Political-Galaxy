# Core 2B: Communism – Marxism
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 86**

### Overview
Pure doctrinal Marxism, as a body of theory without a state apparatus, has no institutional capacity to interfere in the internal affairs of other states. The programme contains no mechanism for occupation, sanctions, conditional aid, or regime-change operations. High placement follows directly from the absence of any state capable of external interference. Residual baseline is theoretical internationalism without mechanical instruments of interference.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* No state, army, or foreign-policy bureaucracy attached to pure theory.
* No programme of cross-border military intervention, occupation, or proxy control.
* No sanctions architecture, conditional aid, or democracy-export instruments.
* Revolutionary internationalism remains theoretical; it does not generate observable mechanical interference by a governing authority.
* Residual baseline of pure doctrine: internationalist language exists but is not operationalised through any residual state or party apparatus capable of external control.
* Self-defence is inapplicable in the absence of a territorial state.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures observable interference in other states’ internal affairs. End-state or pure theoretical Marxism possesses none of the instruments that lower scores for governing communist systems. There is no residual army, security service, aid conditionality, or occupation capacity. Theoretical internationalism does not itself constitute mechanical interference. The score therefore sits near the top of the real-world range for any entity that lacks a state.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 86
Complete absence of state capacity and residual instruments of external interference, combined with purely theoretical internationalism, places pure Marxism in the high non-intervention band.

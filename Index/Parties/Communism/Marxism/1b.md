## Regulation

### Overview
Theoretical Marxism (higher phase of communism) abolishes private ownership of the means of production, commodity production, market exchange and money. Production and distribution are organised entirely through social planning. There is no residual sphere of private economic decision-making under any circumstances.

### Relevant Policies
- Complete abolition of private property in the means of production
- Abolition of commodity production and market exchange
- Abolition of money
- Social ownership and planned allocation of the entire social product
- Distribution according to need
- No independent private production, investment, contracting or labour allocation possible
- No residual private economic activity of any kind

### Expanded Analysis
In the pure theoretical model every economically significant decision is made socially. Independent agents do not enter voluntary contracts, own productive assets, or retain a privately controlled share of output. Personal consumption occurs only as receipt of an allocated bundle. Relative to the floor of the scale: this is the theoretical endpoint — complete and total collective economic control with no private economic activity possible under any circumstances. No historical regime fully instantiated this pure model; the score applies to the theoretical system as specified.

# Cultural 4A: Communism – Marxism
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 30**

### Overview
Theoretical Marxism analyses class position as a primary determinant of life outcomes and consciousness, but it does not itself institute formal hereditary assignment rules or locked occupational castes. As a theoretical programme rather than an implemented regime with institutional birth-assignment mechanisms, it produces only moderate residual determinism under the formal-assignment reading of Cultural 4A. Soft structural analysis of class is not equivalent to locked institutional rules that assign destiny by parentage.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Theoretical emphasis on class position as shaping life chances and historical agency.
* Absence of any implemented hereditary status system, racial law, or formal origin-classification bureaucracy.
* No institutional mechanism that legally assigns occupation or closed status by birth.
* Analytical claim that economic class structures constrain individual trajectories, without corresponding formal birth-assignment rules.
* Residual implication that overcoming class constraints requires collective rather than purely individual action.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores observable institutional patterns that make birth circumstances assign or constrain life outcomes. Theoretical Marxism supplies a structural analysis of class determination but does not create the formal locked rules that drive high scores (hereditary occupational assignment, racial legal dual structures, songbun-style classification, etc.). Under the scoring rules, soft structural correlation and theoretical diagnosis of class constraint belong mainly in the lower bands unless translated into institutional birth-assignment mechanisms. The 21–30 / low 31–40 range captures a programme that treats birth/class as consequential while still leaving formal pathways open in principle. Implemented Marxist-Leninist regimes that added origin gates score higher precisely because they institutionalised what the theory only analysed.

Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 30
Theoretical class-determination analysis without implemented formal hereditary or origin-assignment institutions aligns with the upper 21–30 band (access exists but is treated as structured by class background). Score 30.

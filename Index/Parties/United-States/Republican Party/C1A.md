# Cultural 1A: United States – Republican Party
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 16 (34M)

### Overview
The Republican Party organises moral legitimacy around national interest, citizen preference, competence, popular sovereignty and constitutional form, with traditional values and religious liberty recurrent and prominent in substantial currents.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* National interest, secure borders and the concrete preferences of American citizens are presented as primary political goods.
* Economic growth, competence and institutional continuity are cited as central tests of government.
* Popular sovereignty and electoral mandate are treated as the proper sources of authority.
* Constitutional form and limited government are framed as protections of liberty and order.
* Concrete harms to security, prosperity and institutional integrity appear as evidence.

#### Transcendent Justifications
* Traditional values and religious liberty are recurrent and prominent in substantial party currents (including the religious right) and form a real secondary component of justificatory language, advanced through democratic and constitutional means rather than as independent higher-law override.

### Expanded Analysis
National interest, citizen preference, competence, popular sovereignty, electoral mandate and constitutional form organise the primary Mundane layer. Traditional values and religious liberty are recurrent across substantial currents and are not marginal vocabulary. Under residual rules this is **moderate-to-substantial secondary** transcendent weight — reduced relative to full higher-law override, scaled by prevalence and centrality. Both sources are present. Operational primacy is Mundane; transcendent residual is real secondary rather than token.

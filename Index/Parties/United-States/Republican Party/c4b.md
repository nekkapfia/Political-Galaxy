# Cultural 4B: United States – Republican Party
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 30**

### Overview
The Republican Party prioritises individual merit and equal treatment over progressive equity diagnostics. Group outcome gaps are not treated as automatic proof of systemic unfairness requiring identity-based corrections.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Opposition to disparate-impact over-extension, DEI programmes and race-based preferences.
* Emphasis on colour-blind selection, individual responsibility and equal opportunity.
* Critique of institutional practices that treat group gaps as proof of unfair process.
* Support for basic anti-discrimination law protecting individuals while rejecting group-outcome engineering.

### Expanded Analysis
The party sits on the individual-merit side of the opportunity→outcome question. Soft and hard equity tools are opposed or constrained. Residual acceptance of core equal-treatment principles prevents an absolute floor. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 30
Primary emphasis on individual merit and colour-blind frameworks, combined with opposition to progressive equity interventions and disparate-impact over-extension, produces a low score on the equity lens.

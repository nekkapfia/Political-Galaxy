# Cultural 2A: United States – Republican Party
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 66**

### Overview
The Republican Party maintains formal individual legal personality as the default unit while contesting the expansion of category-preference tools. Residual group-based instruments continue to exist in law and institutions, but the party’s direction is toward their constraint.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Formal equality and individual rights baseline in constitutional and statutory rhetoric.
* Political and legal contest over affirmative action, DEI mandates and disparate-impact rules.
* Support for biological-sex criteria and opposition to expansive self-ID in contested domains.
* Some residual category instruments remaining in federal and state practice.
* Emphasis on merit and colour-blind / person-unit standards in recent platform and litigation positions.

### Expanded Analysis
The party treats individual legal personality as the proper default and has moved to constrain category-preference machinery through litigation, executive action and platform commitments. Affirmative action and DEI expansion are contested rather than defended. Residual group tools have not been fully eliminated, so the pattern remains predominantly individualist with confined group-based policies rather than pure high individualism.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 66
Person-default residual combined with active constraint of category tools places the Republican Party in the predominantly individualist band. Score 66.

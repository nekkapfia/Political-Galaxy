# Cultural 5B: United States – Republican Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 52**

### Overview

The Republican Party has demonstrated selective capacity for accelerated action, particularly under unified government and in the Trump eras on regulatory, immigration and certain institutional files. It remains constrained by the same constitutional architecture as the Democrats. The net score is mid-range, slightly above the Democratic baseline on recent evidence of willingness to compress process on priority items.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Periods of rapid regulatory rollback, executive action and legislative pushes when holding the presidency and congressional majorities.
* Explicit criticism of administrative-state delay, judicial constraints and extended process on selected issues (immigration enforcement, deregulation).
* Simultaneous operation inside separation of powers, federalism and internal coalition constraints that limit sustained cross-domain acceleration.
* Observable pattern is higher willingness than the Democratic Party to treat certain process layers as obstacles, still short of systematic high-radicalism design.

### Expanded Analysis

Recent Republican practice shows clearer accelerationist episodes than the median Democratic record, especially on regulatory and immigration tempo. Structural US constraints prevent a high-radicalism score. Placement at 52 records the modest upward difference.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 52

Selective accelerationist practice on priority files inside persistent constitutional constraints places the Republican Party at 52.

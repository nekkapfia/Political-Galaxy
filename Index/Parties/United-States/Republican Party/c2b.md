# Cultural 2B: United States – Republican Party
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 74**

### Overview
The Republican Party maintains a strongly affirmative stance toward American national identity, founding myths and symbols. National pride is the clear default within the party’s cultural programme. Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Defence of traditional national symbols, the founding narrative and core commemorations.
* Education and cultural rhetoric that prioritise American achievement and continuity.
* Opposition to critical race theory-style curriculum and large-scale statue removal or critical reinterpretation campaigns.
* Rhetoric of patriotism is frequent and largely unqualified by systematic shame frames.
* Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Expanded Analysis
Republicans produce an Honour-band score. They treat the American nation and its founding as legitimate and positive sources of meaning. Critical perspectives exist in the wider culture but are resisted rather than institutionalised by the party. The score reflects consistent national affirmation.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 74
Strong, consistent affirmation of American national identity and founding narrative places the Republican Party in the Honour band.

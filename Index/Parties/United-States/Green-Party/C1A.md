# Cultural 1A: United States – Green Party
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 42 (8M)

### Overview
The US Green Party organises moral legitimacy around ecological limits as threats to human and ecological welfare, intergenerational responsibility, and systemic transformation framed in human-welfare and justice terms. Elevated urgency is present; sacralisation of Nature is not the dominant organising frame.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Ecological limits and climate duties are framed as requirements to prevent human and societal harm and to protect future persons.
* Intergenerational responsibility is treated as a duty to future human persons.
* Economic arrangements are criticised for destructive impact on ecosystems and on vulnerable human populations.
* Systemic transformation is framed as required by the scale of ecological and social harm.
* Scientific evidence is used to establish the scale of ecological necessity.
* Social justice claims appear inside the same transformative human-welfare frame.
* Elevated urgency and moral-emergency language appears within that frame.

### Expanded Analysis
Ecological limits are framed primarily as threats to human welfare, vulnerable populations and future persons; systemic transformation is justified by the scale of that harm. This is human-necessity ecology — Mundane — not sacred/inheriting-will ecology as the dominant source. Elevated urgency is lesser residual only. No divine command, Cosmic sacralisation of Nature as moral subject, or worship-level deification organises legitimacy. The foundation is Mundane with residual elevated ecological urgency.

# Cultural 1A: United States – Libertarian Party
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)
### Overview
The Libertarian Party organises moral legitimacy around individual liberty and rights operationalised through consent and voluntary order, limited government and competence. Transcendent higher-law sources do not organise its claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Individual liberty, personal agency and non-aggression are justified through consent and the rejection of coercive interference.
* Rights are operationalised through voluntary order, private property and freedom of association.
* Limited government and free markets are framed as the institutional conditions of prosperity and agency.
* Competence and voluntary cooperation are treated as superior to central direction.
* Electoral participation is accepted as a means of reducing coercive state power.
* Personal responsibility and non-interference appear as core moral claims.

### Expanded Analysis
Individual liberty, personal agency, non-aggression, rights operationalised through consent and voluntary order, limited government, competence, electoral participation as means of reducing coercion, and personal responsibility organise the entire moral conversation. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law or worship-level deification appears. The foundation is Mundane: rights-as-procedure, consent and competence.

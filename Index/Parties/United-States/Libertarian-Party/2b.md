# Core 2B: United States – Libertarian Party
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 84**

### Overview
The Libertarian Party programme locks military force to defence of US territory and people, rejects regime-change and ideological intervention, and opposes foreign aid used for social engineering. It is the clearest major-party non-interventionist platform in the set. Residual baseline is explicit rejection of the principal interference instruments.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit rejection of undeclared wars and regime-change operations.
* Call to end foreign aid deployed for social or political engineering abroad.
* Sanctions treated as exceptional and threat-linked, not as routine ideological pressure.
* Military force limited to direct defence of the United States.
* Residual baseline of non-intervention: the programme systematically removes open-ended military assistance, transformative aid and routine secondary sanctions rather than treating them as available tools.
* Self-defence of US territory is out of scope for Core 2B scoring of interference.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other nations’ internal affairs. Libertarian policy removes the principal tools that lower other parties’ scores: open-ended military assistance, transformative aid, and routine secondary sanctions. Alliance membership without interference practice does not itself lower the score. The residual posture is one of the highest non-intervention baselines in the set.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 84
Programme-level rejection of regime-change, social-engineering aid and routine external pressure, with a residual non-intervention baseline, places the Libertarian Party in the high non-intervention band.

# Core 1B: United States – Libertarian Party
## Economic Autonomy Analysis
**Final Score: 80**
**Regulation Score: 76**
**Taxation Score: 84**

## Regulation

### Overview
The US Libertarian Party produces a high Core 1B Regulation profile defined by systematic repeal or radical reduction of residual federal labour mandates, occupational licensing, energy and green-industrial controls, price and production mandates, and extensive bureaucratic interference with voluntary exchange, while residual competition rules limited to force and fraud continue and ordinary property rights are maximised.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Opposition to all government interference with private property, including confiscation, nationalisation and coercive eminent domain.
* Repeal of occupational and other licensing laws that infringe the right to earn a living through free exchange.
* Opposition to price controls (wages, rents, interest), production mandates, profit limits and civil asset forfeiture.
* Opposition to government control of energy pricing, allocation and production; end to government energy subsidies.
* Employment and compensation agreements outside the scope of government; no government-mandated benefits or social engineering in private employment; free choice whether to bargain through a union without compulsory arbitration or forced bargaining.
* Environment addressed through clear definition and enforcement of individual rights and responsibilities regarding land, water, air and wildlife, with restitution where damages are proven, rather than expansive green-mandate architecture.
* Residual FLSA, NLRA, OSHA and extensive federal regulatory layers targeted for substantial repeal or radical reduction; residual IRA/EPA-style green-industrial mandates targeted for removal.
* Competition residual retained only insofar as it protects against force and fraud, not as industrial steering.

### Expanded Analysis
The Libertarian programme treats voluntary exchange and private property as the default organising principles of economic life. Residual federal labour, licensing, energy and environmental-mandate layers are locked for removal or deep reduction. Price, wage and production controls are rejected. Green-industrial steering is replaced by property-rights and restitution logic. Labour relations are returned to private agreement without mandated benefits or compulsory union structures. The residual competition architecture is narrowed to force-and-fraud protection. The institutional pattern is comprehensive deregulatory liberalisation of residual US labour, licensing, energy and green-mandate baselines, maximising the domain of unconstrained private allocation.

### Regulation Score
Locked repeal direction on licensing, labour mandates, energy controls, green-industrial architecture and price/production mandates produces a Regulation Score of 76.

## Taxation

Primary method applies.

* Repeal of the income tax, abolition of the IRS, opposition to any tax increase and direction toward voluntary funding of remaining public functions produce a substantially lower residual tax-to-GDP ratio than lower-rate reform packages.
* Estimated tax revenue after package: $4,000 billion
* Current GDP: $25,000 billion

**Taxation Score** = (1 − 4000 / 25000) × 100 = 84.00 → **84**

Ownership and deregulation content remain Regulation only.

**Taxation Score: 84**

## Final Score

A = (76 + 84) / 2 = 80.0
L = min(76, 84) = 76
d = |76 − 84| / 100 = 0.08
F = (1 − 0.08) × 80.0 + 0.08 × 76 = 73.60 + 6.08 = 79.68 → **80**

**Final Score: 80**

### Why this produces a Final Score of 80
Regulation at 76 reflects locked deregulatory repeal of residual labour, licensing, energy and green-mandate layers. Taxation at 84 is the pure algebraic inverse of the stated lower post-package tax claim under the Primary Method. The A/L/d combination with strict ceiling produces Final Score 80.

# Cultural 4B: United States – Libertarian Party
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 12**

### Overview
The Libertarian Party rejects the progressive equity lens entirely. Group outcome gaps are treated as compatible with equal opportunity and individual variance; state group preference and outcome equalization are opposed. Residual baseline of rejection or absence of progressive equity lens: group outcome gaps are not treated as presumptive evidence of unfair opportunity; essentialist, hierarchical or pure individual-responsibility frames predominate.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Platform rights-and-discrimination plank: government must not abridge individual rights by sex, race, ethnicity or similar categories.
* Explicit opposition to government affirmative action and group privileges or preferences.
* Equal treatment under law distinguished from equal outcomes; private association retained.
* Allocation of status in the public sphere tracks individual conduct and consent, not demographic coordinates.
* Residual baseline of rejection or absence of progressive equity lens: group outcome gaps are not treated as presumptive evidence of unfair opportunity; essentialist, hierarchical or pure individual-responsibility frames predominate.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks the opposite of the gap-as-proof diagnostic. Hard and soft equity tools are rejected. Individual merit and equal treatment are the sole legitimate public framework. This produces a very low score. The placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 12
Consistent rejection of gap-as-proof diagnostics and all state group-preference or outcome-equalization mechanisms aligns with the very-low equity-lens bands. Individual variance is treated as compatible with equal opportunity.

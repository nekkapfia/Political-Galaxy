# Cultural 2A: United States – Libertarian Party
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 88**

### Overview
The Libertarian Party treats the individual as the sole public rights and allocation unit. Government is barred from using group identity as a basis for granting or withholding status, opportunity or rights.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Platform rights-and-discrimination plank: government must not abridge individual rights on the basis of sex, race, ethnicity, creed, orientation or similar categories.
* Explicit opposition to government affirmative action and all group privileges or preferences by the state.
* Equal treatment under law distinguished from equal outcomes; private association retained.
* Allocation of status in the public sphere tracks individual conduct and consent, not demographic coordinates.
* Rejection of state-enforced category preference in hiring, contracting, education and licensing.

### Expanded Analysis
The operative unit of moral and institutional measurement is the person. Group identity is treated as irrelevant to what the state may grant or withhold. Core public domains operate on individual merit and formal equality without routine group-preference machinery. Residual private association is protected, but the public allocation rule is person-only. This is high individualism with minimal group exceptions.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 88
Person-unit rights plank, rejection of state group preference, and equal-treatment-not-equal-outcomes framing place the Libertarian Party in the high-individualism band. Score 88.

# Cultural 1B: United States – Libertarian Party
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 53**

**Orthodoxy: 52, Tribalism: 55**

### Overview
The Libertarian Party maintains doctrinal foundations around non-aggression and individual liberty while treating internal challenge as legitimate and operating without state-party enforcement machinery. Orthodoxy is moderate (doctrinal); Tribalism is moderate (argumentative rather than institutional), producing a Transitional band score—the lowest in the scored set.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Non-aggression principle and individual liberty as clear doctrinal foundations.
* Culture of internal debate (anarchist versus minarchist currents, foreign-policy disputes) treating challenge as legitimate.
* Rejection of progressive administrative orthodoxy and authoritarian-right or theocratic frameworks as coercive.
* Small-party culture with limited institutional enforcement machinery.
* Residual baseline of classical-liberal openness under doctrinal constraint; no residual state-party monopoly or career-threatening discipline for internal dissent.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is moderate: non-aggression and individual liberty are clear doctrinal foundations, but the party’s culture of internal debate (anarchist versus minarchist currents, foreign-policy disputes) treats challenge as legitimate rather than pathologised. There is no state-party enforcement machinery; closure is doctrinal and organisational only. Tribalism is moderate: both progressive administrative orthodoxy and authoritarian-right or theocratic frameworks are rejected as coercive, yet the rejection operates through argument and platform clarity rather than institutional exclusion from a wider public sphere the party does not control. Small-party scale limits the social reach of any orthodoxy it maintains. The institutional pattern is mid-range dogmatism—lower than mass parties with institutional capture capacity—consistent with classical-liberal openness under doctrinal constraint, calibrated in the Transitional (48–55) band as the set’s low anchor.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 53
Doctrinal non-aggression and liberty foundations without residual enforcement machinery, legitimate internal debate culture, and argumentative rather than institutional rejection of external frameworks produce only moderate Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 53.

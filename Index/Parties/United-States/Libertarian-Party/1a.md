# Core 1A: United States – Libertarian Party
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Score: 82**

### Overview
The US Libertarian Party produces a high Core 1A profile built from absolute defence of freedom of expression, repeal of all victimless-crime laws including full drug legalisation, opposition to government censorship and platform collusion, and a universal individual-rights framing that rejects motive-based penalty enhancements and identity-based speech restrictions. Residual ordinary direct-harm criminal law continues. No Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Full freedom of expression; opposition to all government restrictions on speech, art, media or other expression; opposition to government censorship in any form.
* Opposition to government regulation, control of technology, or collusion with communications media that restricts lawful speech.
* Language perceived as offensive is not a cause for legal action; speech that is not a threat of aggression or violence is not itself aggression or violence.
* Repeal of all laws creating crimes without victims, including manufacture, sale, possession and use of drugs for medicinal or recreational purposes, prostitution and consensual adult sexual services, and gambling.
* Explicit end to the War on Drugs as a violation of personal freedom.
* Freedom of individuals to determine health care, medicines and treatments under informed consent; opposition to mandates that restrict voluntary bodily choices.
* Consenting adults free to choose personal relationships; government has no authority to define, promote, license or restrict personal relationships.
* Private organisations retain the right to set their own association standards; individuals free to respond with ostracism, boycotts and market solutions.
* Retention of ordinary direct-harm criminal prohibitions limited to force, fraud, or deliberate credible threats of imminent force.

### Expanded Analysis
Under the US residual baseline the First Amendment is already comparatively strong. The Libertarian programme locks further removal of residual drug criminalisation, victimless-crime offences and any remaining content-based or platform-pressure expansions. Expression that does not initiate force or fraud is treated as outside legitimate criminal law; offensive content is not a basis for state action. Drug possession, sale and use for adults are removed from the criminal code as a central programme commitment. Association and personal relationship rules are left to private choice. The framing is universal individual rights rather than differential protection or restriction of designated groups, so no stable Endos/Xenos intensity differential is locked. The institutional pattern is systematic liberalisation of residual personal-autonomy constraints—speech, drugs, victimless offences and bodily choice—while ordinary force-and-fraud criminal law remains.

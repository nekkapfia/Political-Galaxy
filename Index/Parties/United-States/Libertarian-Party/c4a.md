# Cultural 4A: United States – Libertarian Party
## Social Determinism Analysis
**Score: 16**

### Overview
The Libertarian Party maximises formal openness and individual agency while minimising state-directed equalisation of educational and occupational pathways. Formal access is treated as the primary requirement; residual quality gradients arising from family resources and voluntary networks are accepted as outside the proper scope of state correction. This produces the lowest score among the modern democratic parties in the set.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Strong preference for formal openness and minimal state direction of educational and occupational pathways.
* Opposition to state-directed equalisation or redistribution aimed at narrowing quality gradients.
* Acceptance of residual family and network advantages as compatible with individual liberty.
* No formal hereditary or origin-assignment rules.
* Emphasis on voluntary association and market mechanisms over structural remediation.

### Expanded Analysis
Cultural 4A scores birth-constraint versus mobility. The Libertarian programme is the purest formal-openness case among the modern parties: it preserves universal formal access while rejecting state action to compress residual quality gradients. Practical ease continues to correlate with family resources, but the programme treats those differences as non-actionable by the state. This places the score in the upper 11–20 band (universal access – only quality differs). Soft statistical correlation without locked assignment and without state equalisation machinery keeps the score at the low end of the modern range. Cultural 4B equity diagnostics are out of scope.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 16
Near-universal formal access plus explicit rejection of state equalisation of quality gradients, without formal birth-assignment rules, aligns with the upper 11–20 band. Score 16.

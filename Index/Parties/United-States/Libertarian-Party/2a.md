### Overview
The Libertarian Party prioritises maximal national sovereignty through non-intervention, treaty minimisation, open but lawful borders in some formulations, and rejection of residual alliance and international-organisation constraints that limit domestic freedom of action.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Non-interventionist foreign policy; opposition to residual NATO operational constraints and foreign wars.
* Minimisation or exit from treaties and international organisations that constrain domestic policy.
* Border policy varies by platform cycle; residual formal sovereignty maximised.
* Constitutional supremacy and rejection of external legal expansion of restrictions.

### Expanded Analysis

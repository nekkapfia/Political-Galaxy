# Cultural 5B: United States – Libertarian Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 40**

### Overview

The Libertarian Party prioritises radical reduction in the scope of state action. On the specific dimension of pace of *state-driven* institutional change, this produces a low radicalism score: the party does not seek accelerated state transformation so much as accelerated retreat of the state. Its methods are electoral and legal rather than compressed executive redesign of institutions.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Platform aims at rapid reduction of government size, regulation and expenditure.
* Methods emphasise electoral persuasion, legal challenge and legislative repeal rather than construction of new high-tempo state machinery.
* Limited electoral weight and no governing record of implementing compressed institutional programmes.
* Observable pattern on state-driven pace of change is therefore low: the party is not designing accelerated state transformation capacity.

### Expanded Analysis

Cultural 5B measures pace and intensity of change pursued through institutional and political systems. A party whose core aim is to shrink the state’s transformative capacity scores low on that dimension even if its desired end-state is radical. Score 40.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 40

Preference for rapid reduction of state capacity rather than accelerated state-driven institutional transformation places the Libertarian Party at 40.

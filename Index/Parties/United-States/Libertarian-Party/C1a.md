# Cultural 1A: United States – Libertarian Party
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 21**

### Overview
The Libertarian Party grounds political legitimacy in individual liberty, non-aggression, voluntary exchange and minimal state coercion. Organising sources are strongly mundane: personal agency, consent and observable outcomes of free interaction. Natural-rights vocabulary appears but is operationalised through consent and non-interference rather than higher-law overrides of voluntary order. Residual baseline of strongly mundane justification: legitimacy is organised through demotic procedure, welfare outcomes or pragmatic performance rather than higher-order ideological or sacred sources.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Individual liberty and property justified through personal agency, consent and non-aggression.
* Market outcomes treated as legitimate results of voluntary interaction rather than subjects of higher distributive justice.
* State coercion limited to narrow protection of rights against force and fraud.
* Natural-rights language present but tied to consent and non-interference, not to duties that bind free individuals against their clear voluntary choices.
* Opposition to both progressive and conservative moral enforcement through state power.
* Empirical consequences of liberty versus coercion as central evidence.
* Residual baseline of strongly mundane justification: legitimacy is organised through demotic procedure, welfare outcomes or pragmatic performance rather than higher-order ideological or sacred sources.

### Expanded Analysis
Libertarians organise justification through agency, consent and voluntary order. Natural-rights talk is real yet functions as a description of the moral space of free individuals rather than as transcendent authority that could impose comprehensive moral ends on unwilling persons. Distributive justice claims that would override voluntary exchange are rejected. Cultural enforcement from left or right is resisted on non-aggression and consent grounds. Hard trade-offs resolve by reference to liberty and voluntary outcomes, not by higher-order duties independent of consent. The pattern is strong mundane lean.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 21
Mundane sources—consent, agency, voluntary outcomes—organise. Natural-rights vocabulary does not install demos-binding higher law. The score is 21.

# Core 2B: United States – Democratic Party
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 30**

### Overview
The Democratic Party in recent platforms retains a high-intervention baseline: strong Ukraine support, broad sanctions use, democracy and human-rights conditionality, alliance leadership, and residual expeditionary capacity. It sits among the more interventionist major Western parties.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Large-scale military and financial support for Ukraine.
* Extensive use of primary and secondary sanctions architectures.
* Democracy, human-rights and governance conditionality as standing diplomatic and aid tools.
* Leadership role in NATO and other alliance pressure mechanisms.
* Self-defence of US territory and forces is out of scope or heavily mitigated.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. Democratic foreign-policy practice treats sanctions, military assistance to partners, and normative conditionality as normal instruments. Invitation mitigation applies to Ukraine support; the overall toolkit remains highly active and global in reach, producing one of the lower scores among democratic parties.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 30
Broad sanctions, major military assistance programmes and systemic conditionality place the Democratic Party in the low 30s.

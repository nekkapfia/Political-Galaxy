# Cultural 5B: United States – Democratic Party
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 50**

### Overview

The Democratic Party operates inside the US separation-of-powers, federal and judicial system that structurally limits compressed transformation. It has shown selective acceleration on priority items under unified government and crisis conditions, while retaining substantial commitment to process, consultation and incrementalism. The net score is mid-range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Periods of relatively rapid legislative action when holding the presidency and congressional majorities (e.g., early Obama, early Biden on selected files).
* Simultaneous structural constraints: filibuster, committee processes, federalism, judicial review and internal coalition management that routinely extend timelines.
* Platform and practice mix urgency language on climate, social policy and institutional reform with acceptance of deliberative and legal sequencing.
* Observable pattern is selective acceleration inside a high-constraint institutional environment.

### Expanded Analysis

US institutional design itself exerts a strong gradualist pull. The Democratic Party does not systematically design against that pull in the manner of high-radicalism platforms. Mid-range score of 50 is appropriate.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 50

Selective acceleration capacity inside persistent constitutional and process constraints places the Democratic Party at 50.

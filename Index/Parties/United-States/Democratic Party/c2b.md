The Democratic Party, particularly its progressive wing, treats traditional American national identity as heavily qualified. ### Expanded Analysis
The Democratic Party’s cultural orientation sits in the Negative band. Critical perspectives predominate in the educational and cultural domains the party influences. Traditional national pride is limited and heavily qualified. The party does not seek total erasure, but neither does it treat traditional national identity as the default source of collective meaning.

Predominance of critical and qualified frames over affirmative traditional national pride places the Democratic Party in the Negative band.

The Democratic Party treats persistent group outcome gaps as evidence of systemic and historical unfairness. Equity interventions, disparate-impact logic and institutional DEI measures are supported; pure individual-variance accounts are insufficient. Residual baseline of progressive equity-lens framing: gaps between groups are routinely treated as evidence of unfair opportunity structures requiring corrective institutional response.

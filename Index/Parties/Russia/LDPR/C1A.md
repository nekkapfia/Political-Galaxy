# Cultural 1A: Russia – LDPR
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
LDPR organises moral legitimacy around Russian national interest, great-power status, internal order and electoral mobilisation. Transcendent higher-law sources do not organise its claims.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Russian national interest and the restoration of great-power status are presented as the primary political goods.
* A strong state, internal order and decisive leadership are justified by the need for security and cohesion of the population.
* Nationalist identity and the defence of the Russian people are framed as the natural political community whose preferences matter.
* Liberal international norms and external constraints are rejected as obstacles to sovereignty and national will.
* Electoral mobilisation and direct appeal to popular sentiment are treated as legitimate and preferred methods of politics.
* Practical power, territorial integrity and the capacity to act independently are repeatedly cited as measures of success.

### Expanded Analysis
Russian national interest, great-power status, strong state and internal order for security and cohesion, nationalist identity as the political community whose preferences matter, rejection of external constraints as obstacles to sovereignty, electoral mobilisation and practical power as measures of success organise legitimacy. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law or worship-level deification appears. The foundation is Mundane: national interest, order and demos mobilisation.

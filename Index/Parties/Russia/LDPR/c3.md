### Overview
The Liberal Democratic Party of Russia has long employed ethno-nationalist rhetoric emphasising Russian cultural and ethnic identity. Under the strict Exclusion Test, however, no ethnicity is permanently barred from membership of the Russian political community solely on ethnic grounds; formal criteria remain open to naturalisation and civic incorporation. Cultural expectations for full belonging are elevated. Immigration and deportation remain out of scope.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Ethno-nationalist rhetoric and emphasis on Russian cultural identity in party programme and leadership statements.
* No formal permanent ethnic bar to Russian citizenship or membership; naturalisation remains legally available across ethnic groups.
* Elevated expectations of cultural alignment, language, and loyalty to Russian state and cultural norms for substantive belonging.
* Immigration volume and deportation out of scope.

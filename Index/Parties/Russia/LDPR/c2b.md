# Cultural 2B: Russia – LDPR
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 84**

### Overview
The LDPR elevates Russian national identity, power and historical continuity as central. National pride is the clear default; the party’s rhetoric is among the most affirmatively nationalist in the contemporary Russian system. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit affirmation of Russian historical greatness and great-power status.
* Defence of traditional national symbols and opposition to critical reinterpretation of the national past.
* Education and cultural positions oriented to positive national narrative and civilisational uniqueness.
* Criticism of the nation’s past is secondary or rejected as unpatriotic.
* Public rhetoric consistently presents the Russian nation as a primary source of meaning and legitimacy.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
LDPR sits high in the Pride band. Its programme treats the Russian nation as a primary source of legitimacy and meaning, with little institutional space for organising shame. Affirmative historical narrative and great-power identity are central rather than secondary.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 84
Locked preference for affirmative national narrative and rejection of organising shame place LDPR high in the Pride band.

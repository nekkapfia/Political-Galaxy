# Cultural 1A: Russia – KPRF
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
The KPRF organises moral legitimacy around social protection, working-people interest and electoral competition, with Marxist-Leninist and Soviet inheritance language supplying identity and critique.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Social protection, opposition to oligarchic inequality and defence of working-people living standards are presented as central moral claims.
* Post-Soviet capitalism is criticised for producing unfair outcomes, social degradation and loss of sovereignty for ordinary citizens.
* Participation in elections and parliamentary work is accepted as a legitimate means of advancing the party’s programme.
* Patriotic and anti-Western positions are combined with social-justice language as joint grounds for political mobilisation.
* Marxist-Leninist concepts and the defence of socialist values appear as part of the party’s identity and critique of the current order.
* Soviet industrial, scientific and geopolitical achievements are cited as a positive inheritance and source of national pride.

### Expanded Analysis
Social protection, opposition to oligarchic inequality, working-people living standards, critique of post-Soviet outcomes for ordinary citizens, electoral participation and patriotic mobilisation organise the operational moral conversation. Marxist-Leninist concepts and Soviet achievements supply identity, critique and inheritance language. Constituency and delivery claims are Mundane and carry primary weight; ideological-tradition language is present without establishing divine, Cosmic or worship-level sources. The foundation is Mundane with residual ideological-tradition language.

# Core 2B: Russia – KPRF
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 26**

### Overview
The KPRF is the principal communist opposition. It is critical of some Kremlin economic policies but generally supports strong state power, great-power status and resistance to Western encroachment. It does not advance a non-intervention programme.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Support for Russian great-power posture and for resistance to NATO expansion.
* Residual internationalist and anti-Western framing that does not translate into restraint on Russian external control tools.
* Operation inside a political system whose foreign policy is highly interventionist.
* Self-defence remains out of scope.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B scores interference in other states’ internal affairs. KPRF opposition is not organised around reducing Russian military or political control abroad. The score therefore remains low, reflecting the broader system rather than a distinct non-interventionist alternative.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 26
Absence of a programme to shrink Russian external interference, inside a high-interference state, produces a low score.

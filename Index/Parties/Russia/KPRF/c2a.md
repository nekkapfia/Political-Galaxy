# Cultural 2A: Russia – KPRF
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 30**

### Overview
The KPRF emphasises class and collective categories as organising units of political and social allocation, subordinating pure individual-merit frameworks to group-based analysis rooted in the communist tradition.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Class-based analysis treating economic and social group location as primary for diagnosis and remedy.
* Support for collective and category-conscious approaches to rights and distribution.
* Formal individual legal personality retained as residual.
* Rejection of pure person-unit meritocracy as sufficient.
* Continuity with historical communist category logic rather than contemporary progressive identity stacks.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant unit of measure is the class or collective category rather than the sovereign individual. Individual agency operates inside group-structured constraints and remedies inherited from the communist tradition. The pattern is strong group primacy on the unit-of-measure test.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 30
Class and collective categories as primary organising units of status and remedy place the KPRF in the strong group-primacy band. Score 30.

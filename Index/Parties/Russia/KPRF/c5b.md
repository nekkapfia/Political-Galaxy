# Cultural 5B: Russia – KPRF
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 48**

### Overview

The Communist Party of the Russian Federation retains transformative language drawn from the Soviet legacy while functioning as a systemic opposition party inside the contemporary Russian framework. Its observable pattern on pace is low-to-mid: residual ideological urgency without current capacity for independent compressed implementation.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Platform and symbolism continue to reference rapid socialist transformation and Soviet-era mobilisation models.
* Practical politics are constrained by opposition status and the managed party system.
* No recent governing record of executing high-intensity institutional campaigns at national level.
* Observable pattern is therefore closer to residual ideological radicalism than to operational high radicalism on pace.

### Expanded Analysis

Historical and symbolic association with high-tempo Soviet methods raises the score modestly above pure gradualist parties. Current operational constraints keep it in the low-to-mid band. Score 48.

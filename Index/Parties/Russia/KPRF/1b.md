## Regulation

### Overview
KPRF produces a very low Core 1B Regulation profile defined by preference for extensive public ownership, residual and expanded state direction of strategic sectors, residual labour controls reinforced by collective and planning preference, and residual industrial-planning direction.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Preference for extensive public ownership of strategic sectors and major industry.
* Residual and expanded state direction of investment and production.
* Residual labour controls reinforced by collective and planning preference.
* Residual industrial-planning and public-investment direction.
* Residual competition residual subordinated to public-ownership and planning priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory baseline continues and is deepened; no high-priority residual state-economic control liberalisation is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The KPRF final set advances extensive public ownership of strategic sectors. Residual state direction of investment and production is retained and expanded. Residual labour controls are reinforced by collective and planning preference. Residual private economic activity is further subordinated to residual public-ownership and planning priorities. The institutional pattern is residual multi-domain state-economic control intensified by public-ownership and planning preference.

### Regulation Score
Preference for extensive public ownership, residual and expanded state direction, residual labour controls reinforced by collective preference, and residual industrial-planning direction produce a Regulation Score of 18.

## Taxation

Primary method applies.

Coherent tax-revenue and GDP figures are available. SOE ownership and industrial-policy direction remain Regulation territory only.

* Government tax / mandatory contribution revenue: **₽68–72 trillion under higher-tax package** (midpoint **₽70 trillion** used for calculation)
* **GDP**: **₽180 trillion**
* Tax-to-GDP = 70 / 180 = **38.89%**
* Fiscal system revenues (CBR 2024 order of magnitude) support a mid-30s tax-to-GDP band including social contributions

**Taxation Score** = (1 − 70 / 180) × 100 = **61.11** → **62**

**Taxation Score: 62**

## Final Score

A = (18 + 62) / 2 = 40.0
L = min(18, 62) = 18
d = |18 − 62| / 100 = 0.44
F = (1 − 0.44) × 40.0 + 0.44 × 18 = 30.320 → **31**

**Final Score: 31**

# Cultural 4B: Russia – KPRF
## Equity Lens Analysis
**Score: 60**

### Overview
The KPRF retains a structural (class and systemic) gap diagnostic. Unequal outcomes under formal equality are read as evidence of residual capitalist or oligarchic unfairness requiring collective and state-oriented correction.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Structural and class-based diagnostic language.
* Support for equality measures and collective remedies.
* Critique of pure individual-merit accounts that ignore structural constraints.
* Soft-to-mid mechanisms with transformative structural claims.

### Expanded Analysis
The party locks a moderated structural gap-as-proof diagnostic. This placement is independent of Cultural 2A (unit of measure) and Cultural 3 (membership gates).

### Why this produces a Final Score of 60
Structural gap diagnostic plus support for collective and state-oriented remedies produce a mid-high contested score. Pure individual-merit accounts of disparities are treated as insufficient within the party framework.

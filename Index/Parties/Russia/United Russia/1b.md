## Regulation

### Overview
United Russia produces a low Core 1B Regulation profile defined by residual extensive state ownership and direction of strategic sectors, residual industrial policy and sanctions-adaptation planning, residual labour controls, and residual price/quantity interventions in key markets.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Residual extensive state ownership and direction of strategic sectors (energy, defence, major industry).
* Residual industrial policy and sanctions-adaptation planning directing investment and production.
* Residual labour controls and residual workplace discipline architecture.
* Residual price and quantity interventions in key markets.
* Residual competition residual is subordinated to state-direction priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory and administrative baseline continues and is deepened; no high-priority residual state-economic control liberalisation is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The United Russia final set retains residual extensive state ownership and direction of strategic sectors. Residual industrial policy and sanctions-adaptation planning shape investment and production. Residual labour controls continue. Residual price and quantity interventions operate in key markets. Residual private economic activity exists but is heavily conditioned by residual state direction and administrative permission. The institutional pattern is residual multi-domain state-economic control with limited residual private decision-making under continuous direction.

### Regulation Score
Residual extensive state ownership and direction of strategic sectors, residual industrial policy and sanctions-adaptation planning, residual labour controls, and residual price/quantity interventions produce a Regulation Score of 22.

## Taxation

Primary method applies.

Coherent tax-revenue and GDP figures are available. SOE ownership and industrial-policy direction remain Regulation territory only.

* Government tax / mandatory contribution revenue: **₽60–65 trillion** (midpoint **₽62.5 trillion** used for calculation)
* **GDP**: **₽180 trillion**
* Tax-to-GDP = 62.5 / 180 = **34.72%**
* Fiscal system revenues (CBR 2024 order of magnitude) support a mid-30s tax-to-GDP band including social contributions

**Taxation Score** = (1 − 62.5 / 180) × 100 = **65.28** → **66**

**Taxation Score: 66**

## Final Score

A = (22 + 66) / 2 = 44.0
L = min(22, 66) = 22
d = |22 − 66| / 100 = 0.44
F = (1 − 0.44) × 44.0 + 0.44 × 22 = 34.320 → **35**

**Final Score: 35**

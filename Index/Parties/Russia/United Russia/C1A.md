# Cultural 1A: Russia – United Russia
## Foundation Analysis
Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
United Russia organises moral legitimacy around stability, order, performance delivery and national strength as practical power. Patriotic vocabulary reinforces cohesion but does not function as binding higher law.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Stability, order and continuity are presented as primary goods for the Russian state and population.
* Improvements in security, living standards and national strength are cited as central evidence of successful leadership.
* Sovereignty and great-power status are framed as practical capacities that must be defended and expanded.
* Managed popular support and electoral participation are treated as legitimate expressions of public backing.
* Patriotic education and traditional values are invoked to reinforce social cohesion and regime continuity.
* Opposition is characterised as a threat to stability, national interest and the normal functioning of the state.

### Expanded Analysis
Stability, order, continuity, improvements in security and living standards, national strength, sovereignty as practical capacity, managed popular support and opposition characterised as threat to stability and national interest organise legitimacy. Patriotic and traditional vocabulary reinforces cohesion but does not function as binding higher law. No divine command, Cosmic or Natural law or worship-level deification organises authority. The foundation is Mundane: performance, order and national self-preservation.

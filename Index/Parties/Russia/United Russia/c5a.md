# Cultural 5A: Russia – United Russia
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 62**

### Overview
United Russia backs traditional family values, patriotic education and selected religious-cultural forms under state direction while rejecting Western progressive gender and cultural agendas.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* State support for traditional family values and demographic policy through law and programmes.
* Patriotic education and Russian historical narrative emphasised in school curricula and public commemorations.
* Critique of Western progressive gender and cultural agendas as alien to Russian institutional baselines.
* Institutional protection of selected traditional cultural and religious forms under state and Party direction.
* Administrative modernisation that does not adopt Western progressive social transformation on family and gender.
* Residual Soviet and post-Soviet institutional forms continue; pre-revolutionary autonomous traditional baselines are not fully restored, but Western progressive settlement is rejected as the alternative.

### Expanded Analysis
United Russia uses state capacity to underwrite traditional family values, patriotic education and selected religious-cultural protection while rejecting Western progressive gender and cultural agendas. Primary domains are continuity-oriented relative to Western progressive models: family policy and demographic programmes support conventional structures; curricula emphasise patriotic and historical narrative; progressive gender frameworks are treated as alien. Transmission is under state and Party direction rather than autonomous pre-revolutionary confessional or customary authority. Domain hierarchy prioritises education content and family-status design as state traditionalist projects. The residual baseline is state-directed national-traditional continuity, not Western progressive settlement and not fully autonomous pre-Soviet traditional order. That yields mid-band preservation relative to progressive Western parties, with authoritarian modernisation limiting pure traditional-autonomy readings.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 62
State support for traditional family values, patriotic education, selected religious-cultural protection, and rejection of Western progressive gender agendas place United Russia in the mid band (60–69). Traditional transmission is an explicit state project in primary domains. Higher mid-high placement is limited by Party-directed rather than autonomous traditional authority; lower placement would ignore the systematic rejection of progressive Western frameworks in education and family policy.

# Cultural 2A: Russia – United Russia
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 44**

### Overview
United Russia maintains formal individual legal personality as a residual layer while operating inside a system where political reliability, loyalty and state-defined categories significantly structure advancement and access.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Formal individual rights and equality rhetoric as residual baseline.
* Political loyalty and reliability functioning as recurring criteria for trusted roles and advancement.
* State and party-mediated channels influencing access to status and opportunity.
* Limited independent civil association; status-bearing groups largely inside approved structures.
* Not a progressive multi-axis identity-stack regime; category logic is political-loyalty and state-defined rather than contemporary identity politics.

### Expanded Analysis
The dominant allocation principle in core political and institutional domains is mediated by loyalty and state-defined categories rather than pure individual merit. Individual agency operates inside those constraints. Formal individual legal personality remains, but it does not displace category-structured advancement as the practical organising unit. The pattern is significant group-based allocation of a political-reliability type.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 44
Political-loyalty and state-category mediation of status, with residual individual rights, places United Russia in the significant group-based allocation band. Score 44.

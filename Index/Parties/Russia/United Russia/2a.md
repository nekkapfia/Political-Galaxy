# Core 2A: Russia – United Russia
## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 77**
**Status: FULLY LOCKED**

### Overview
United Russia prioritises sovereignty and non-interference, rejects selected Western legal mechanisms and maintains exclusive national control over military command, borders and strategic policy. Residual external Western institutional reach is systematically reduced; residual Russian-led structures remain instrumental.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Explicit doctrine of sovereignty and non-interference as core foreign-policy principles.
* Rejection of ICC jurisdiction, selected human-rights mechanisms, and Western sanctions regimes as illegitimate residual external constraints.
* Support for Eurasian Economic Union and CSTO as Russian-led structures rather than residual constraints on core authority.
* Tight control of borders, migration, and domestic political space.
* Maintenance of full formal and practical control over military command and nuclear forces; residual external command limitation is rejected.

### Expanded Analysis
United Russia maintains an explicit doctrine of sovereignty and non-interference, rejects ICC jurisdiction and selected Western human-rights and sanctions mechanisms as residual illegitimate constraints, and keeps exclusive residual control of military command, nuclear forces, borders and domestic political space. Residual Eurasian Economic Union and CSTO structures operate under Russian leadership as instruments rather than residual external constraints. On Core 2A the pattern is exclusive residual national control of core functions combined with systematic reduction of residual Western institutional reach. Residual constraints that continue are ordinary non-transferring forms that do not impose residual external legal supremacy.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 77
Exclusive residual control of military, borders and political space and rejection of selected residual Western legal mechanisms minimise residual external constraint. Residual Russian-led regional architecture remains instrumental. The score of 77 follows from near-complete retention of residual sovereign authority over core residual domains.

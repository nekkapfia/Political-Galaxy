# Cultural 5B: Russia – United Russia
## Radicalism (Pace of Change) Analysis
**Score: 55**

### Overview

United Russia, as the dominant party of the Putin system, operates inside a centralised framework capable of relatively rapid policy and personnel shifts when directed from the top. At the same time much of its routine governance is administrative and stabilising rather than continuously campaign-driven. The net score is mid-range.

### Observable Pace and Intensity

* Capacity for compressed implementation of central directives on security, foreign policy and selected domestic files.
* Party and administrative structures designed to transmit tempo from the centre with limited intermediate veto.
* Routine governance often emphasises continuity and controlled management rather than permanent high-intensity transformation.
* Observable pattern is selective high tempo under central direction, not continuous radical acceleration across all domains.

### Expanded Analysis

The system can move quickly; it does not treat continuous disruptive transformation as the default mode in the manner of peak Stalinism or Maoism. Mid-range score of 55 reflects that selective capacity.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 55

Central capacity for compressed directives combined with substantial routine stabilising governance places United Russia at 55.

# Cultural 2B: Russia – United Russia
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 82**

### Overview
United Russia promotes a strongly affirmative view of Russian national history, continuity and great-power identity. National pride is the clear institutional default; systematic shame frameworks are rejected. Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Education and media emphasise Russian historical continuity, victory and civilisational uniqueness.
* Symbols, monuments and commemorations (especially wartime) affirm the nation as central.
* Critical frames toward the Soviet or imperial past are secondary to affirmative continuity.
* Rhetoric presents Russia as a primary source of meaning and international standing.
* Residual baseline of strong national affirmation: symbols, education and commemorations treat national continuity and achievement as the default frame rather than an object of systematic critique.

### Expanded Analysis
United Russia produces a solid Pride-band score. The party and the state it leads treat Russian national identity as legitimate and central. Organising shame narratives are absent; affirmative historical narrative predominates.

Clear default of national pride and rejection of organising shame narratives place United Russia in the Pride band.

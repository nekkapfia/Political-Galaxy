# Cultural 1A: Russia – United Russia
## Foundation Analysis
**Score: 13**

### Overview
United Russia grounds political legitimacy in stability, national strength, performance delivery and the concrete interests of the Russian state and population under existing leadership. Organising sources are overwhelmingly mundane: order, performance, sovereignty as practical power, and managed popular support. Ideological and civilisational language operates as reinforcement rather than independent higher law.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements
* Stability, order and continuity as primary goods for the living population and state.
* Performance legitimacy—security, living standards, national strength—as central mundane sources.
* Sovereignty framed as practical capacity and great-power status rather than sacred mission independent of regime interest.
* Managed popular support and electoral form as legitimating procedures.
* Residual patriotic and traditional vocabulary secondary to performance and order.
* Opposition framed as threat to stability and national interest rather than as violation of higher sacred law.

### Expanded Analysis
United Russia organises justification through performance, order and state interest. Civilisational or traditional language, where used, reinforces regime continuity and national strength rather than installing transcendent authority that could bind the leadership against its pragmatic calculations. The pattern is extreme mundane lean.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 13
Transcendent sources are marginal. Moral authority derives from stability, performance and state interest. The score is 13.

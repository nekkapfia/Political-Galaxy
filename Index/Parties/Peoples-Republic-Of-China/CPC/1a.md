# Core 1A: People’s Republic of China – Communist Party of China
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 12**  
**Xenos Score: 8**

### Overview

The Communist Party of China, scored as the ruling political entity, produces a severely restrictive Core 1A profile defined by comprehensive censorship of political and identity-sensitive expression, mandatory ideological education and attitude alignment, criminal and administrative sanctions for dissenting speech and organisation, pervasive surveillance enabling enforcement, and sharply differential treatment of designated out-groups (notably Uyghurs, Tibetans, and other targeted populations). Cumulative weight sits in the extreme-restrictions / upper floor range. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Comprehensive state and platform censorship of political speech, historical contestation, and content deemed harmful to Party authority or social stability; Great Firewall and domestic platform duties enforce removal and pre-emptive filtering.
* Criminal and administrative offences covering subversion, separatism, picking quarrels, spreading rumours, and related non-violent expression; thresholds are low and enforcement is political.
* Mandatory ideological education and “Xi Jinping Thought” study requirements across Party, state, education, and many workplace settings — programmes designed to correct incorrect beliefs and ensure alignment with official ideology.
* Social Credit, grid management, and mass surveillance systems that make expression and association continuously observable and sanctionable.
* Severe restrictions on religious expression and organisation outside state-supervised structures; additional controls on online and private religious practice.
* Designated out-group policies in Xinjiang and Tibet: mass detention, forced political re-education, movement controls, family separation practices, and intensive cultural/religious suppression — markedly worse than Endos baseline.
* No independent civil society, independent media, or legal protection for dissenting political speech; association outside Party-led structures is tightly constrained or prohibited.
* Retention of ordinary violent-crime prohibitions; these do not define placement.

### Expanded Analysis

The CPC’s final restriction set is not a European-style hate-speech densification layer on a liberal baseline. It is a full authoritarian speech, belief, and association architecture. Censorship is comprehensive rather than category-limited. Ideological study requirements meet the 19–27 band language on mandatory programmes aimed at correcting beliefs, but they operate inside a wider system that already criminalises organised dissent and blocks information at scale.

For the core population (Endos), daily expression is constrained by censorship, surveillance, and administrative risk; private criticism exists in limited forms but public and organised political speech does not. Placement in the low teens reflects severe, systematic restriction of voluntary expression, association, and belief-related conduct without requiring the absolute theoretical floor.

For designated Xenos populations — particularly Uyghurs in Xinjiang and Tibetans under intensified security regimes — the architecture adds mass detention, forced re-education, movement bans, and cultural/religious suppression. These are physical and totalising controls that pull Xenos into the upper floor / extreme-restriction bands.

Framing as “stability,” “sinicisation,” or “deradicalisation” is discarded. Only the observable restrictions are scored.

### Why this produces Endos 12 / Xenos 8

Comprehensive censorship, criminalisation of non-violent political expression, mandatory ideological alignment programmes, pervasive surveillance-backed enforcement, and association bans place Endos at 12 (10–18 extreme restrictions band, upper portion). Mass detention, forced re-education, movement controls and intensified cultural/religious suppression for designated out-groups place Xenos at 8 (upper floor).

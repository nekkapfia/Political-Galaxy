# Cultural 2A: People’s Republic of China – CPC
## Individuality Analysis
**Score: 24**

### Overview
The CPC subordinates individual merit and status to political reliability, party-defined categories and collective goals. Rights, opportunities and advancement are routinely mediated by group and loyalty criteria.

### Allocation Mechanisms
* Political reliability and party-defined categories as recurring criteria for advancement and trusted roles.
* Hukou and related registration systems historically and residually shaping access to education, welfare and urban status.
* Collective and party-mediated channels organising status; individual performance channels operating inside those constraints.
* Suppression or tight control of independent civil association outside Party-approved structures.
* Residual market and examination channels framed as instruments within, not alternatives to, political-category order.

### Expanded Analysis
The system measures persons through political and collective categories. Individual agency operates only within category-structured constraints. Examination and performance metrics exist, but they do not displace political-reliability and collective-category logic as the organising unit of high-status allocation. The result is very strong group subordination.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 24
Political and collective categories as primary organising units of status and opportunity place the CPC in the very strong group-subordination band. Score 24.

# Core 2B: Peoples Republic of China – CPC
## International Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 28**

### Overview
The CPC under the People’s Republic maintains a mixed but clearly interventionist pattern: large-scale economic and infrastructural influence networks, assertive territorial and maritime claims backed by force posture, support for selected foreign regimes and parties, and systematic soft-power and United Front work abroad. Residual non-interference rhetoric coexists with extensive practical instruments of external influence.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Belt and Road / infrastructure and lending networks that create structural economic leverage over recipient states’ internal policy space.
* Assertive South China Sea and territorial postures with associated military and paramilitary presence.
* Support for selected foreign governments, parties and movements aligned with Chinese strategic interests.
* United Front and soft-power / influence work targeting diaspora communities, elites and institutions in other states.
* Residual formal “non-interference” doctrine that does not eliminate the practical architecture of economic, political and grey-zone leverage.
* Self-defence of Chinese territory and immediate maritime claims is partially mitigated but still generates external pressure on neighbouring states’ internal and external arrangements; pure territorial defence is out of scope only where it remains strictly internal.

### Expanded Analysis
Core 2B measures interference in other nations’ internal affairs. The CPC combines formal non-interference language with a dense practical toolkit of economic structural leverage, influence operations, and selective political support. Belt and Road and related lending create long-term policy constraints on recipients. United Front and soft-power work systematically targets foreign internal opinion and elite networks. Maritime and territorial assertiveness generates ongoing pressure on neighbouring states. The residual non-interference claim therefore does not raise the score into the mid-range; the observable instruments pull it into the high 20s.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 28
Structural economic leverage networks, United Front / influence operations, selective regime and party support, and assertive maritime posture produce a clear interventionist pattern only partially offset by residual non-interference rhetoric, placing the CPC in the high 20s.

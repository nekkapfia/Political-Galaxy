# Cultural 5A: Peoples Republic of China – CPC
## Tradition vs Progress Analysis
**Score: 38**

### Overview
The CPC combined revolutionary dismantling of pre-1949 traditional land, family and religious structures with later selective promotion of traditional culture only under Party control and in service of socialist modernisation.

### Cultural 5A Relevant Policies / Elements
* Historical revolutionary dismantling of traditional land, family and religious structures in the Maoist period as foundational institutional break.
* Contemporary selective promotion of “excellent traditional Chinese culture” only under Party leadership and in service of socialist modernisation.
* Technological, economic and social modernisation treated as core legitimacy claims in state policy, education content and Party doctrine.
* Family and demographic policy remains instrumentally managed rather than grounded in pre-revolutionary traditional baselines as autonomous standards.
* Traditional forms permitted or promoted only when aligned with Party authority; not restored as an autonomous institutional baseline in education, family law or religious authority.
* Residual pre-1949 traditional order was systematically broken; contemporary residual is Party-authorised cultural retrieval inside modernisation, not inherited autonomous continuity.

### Expanded Analysis
The CPC’s Cultural 5A record is defined by foundational transformative destruction of pre-1949 traditional land, family and religious structures, followed by selective rehabilitation of traditional culture only under Party control and for modernisation goals. Primary domains remain Party-directed: education content serves socialist modernisation and authorised traditional retrieval; family and demographic policy is instrumental; religious and customary authority are not autonomous baselines. The residual pre-revolutionary order was broken as a project; what continues is selective cultural content under Party sovereignty. Domain hierarchy places institutional design and education under modernisation primacy, with traditional forms as permitted instruments rather than protected inherited defaults. That is transformative long-run pattern with controlled cultural retrieval—low band relative to autonomous traditional preservation, above pure Year Zero floor because selective traditional content is actively used.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 38
Revolutionary dismantling of pre-1949 traditional structures, contemporary promotion of traditional culture only under Party control and for socialist modernisation, and instrumental family/demographic policy place the CPC in the low 31–40 band. Traditional forms are not autonomous baselines; residual continuity is authorised retrieval inside a transformative modernisation order. Higher contested placement would require inherited traditional transmission to operate as protected public default independent of Party modernisation goals.

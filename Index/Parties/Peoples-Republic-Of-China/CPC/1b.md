## Regulation

### Overview
The CPC combines dominant party-state control of strategic sectors, extensive industrial policy and planning guidance, residual private and foreign enterprise under political oversight, and comprehensive administrative regulation.

### Relevant Policies
- Dominant state/party control of strategic sectors (energy, finance, telecoms, defence, major industry)
- Industrial policy, five-year plans and guidance targets
- Residual private and foreign enterprise under political and regulatory oversight
- Extensive administrative licensing and party committees in major firms
- Labour allocation and mobility constraints (hukou and related systems)

### Expanded Analysis
Dominant party-state control of strategic sectors, industrial policy and five-year guidance targets, and extensive administrative-political oversight of enterprise are the core restrictive mechanisms. Hukou and related labour-mobility constraints add further direction. Residual private and foreign enterprise under oversight does not restore independent decision-making in the commanding heights. Lower 28–38 range.

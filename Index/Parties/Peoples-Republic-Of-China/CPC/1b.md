## Regulation

### Overview
The CPC produces a very low Core 1B Regulation profile defined by residual extensive state and Party direction of strategic sectors, residual industrial policy and planning, residual labour controls, residual dual-track private sector under continuous political and administrative direction, and residual price/quantity interventions.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Residual extensive state and Party ownership and direction of strategic sectors (energy, finance, major industry, technology).
* Residual industrial policy, five-year planning and industrial-steering instruments directing investment and production.
* Residual labour controls and residual workplace political reliability architecture.
* Residual dual-track private sector that exists under continuous residual political and administrative direction and permission.
* Residual price and quantity interventions in key markets.
* Residual competition residual is subordinated to residual Party and state-direction priorities.
* Residual secondary regulatory and administrative baseline continues and is deepened; no high-priority residual state-economic control liberalisation is locked.

### Expanded Analysis
The CPC final set retains residual extensive state and Party ownership and direction of strategic sectors. Residual industrial policy and planning instruments shape investment and production. Residual labour controls and residual workplace political reliability continue. Residual private economic activity exists in a dual-track form but is heavily conditioned by residual political and administrative direction. Residual price and quantity interventions operate in key markets. The institutional pattern is residual multi-domain state and Party economic control with residual private decision-making subordinated to continuous direction and permission.

### Regulation Score
Residual extensive state and Party ownership and direction of strategic sectors, residual industrial policy and planning, residual labour controls, residual dual-track private sector under continuous direction, and residual price/quantity interventions produce a Regulation Score of 12.

## Taxation

Primary method applies.

Coherent tax-revenue and GDP figures are available. Non-tax SOE ownership and industrial-policy direction remain Regulation territory only.

* **Government tax revenue**: **¥35–40 trillion** (midpoint **¥37.5 trillion** used for calculation)
* **GDP**: **¥180 trillion**
* Tax-to-GDP = 37.5 / 180 = **20.83%** (OECD Asia-Pacific 2024: 19.5%)

**Taxation Score** = (1 − 37.5 / 180) × 100 = **79.17** → **80** (beneficial rounding)

**Taxation Score: 80**

## Final Score

A = (12 + 80) / 2 = 46.0
L = min(12, 80) = 12
d = |12 − 80| / 100 = 0.68
F = (1 − 0.68) × 46.0 + 0.68 × 12 = 22.880 → **23**

**Final Score: 23**

# Cultural 2B: Peoples Republic of China – CPC
## National Pride Analysis
**Score: 78**

### Overview
The Communist Party of China promotes a strongly affirmative view of Chinese national revival, civilisational continuity and the party’s role in national rejuvenation. National pride is the clear institutional default. Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Education and media emphasise Chinese civilisational continuity, the “century of humiliation” overcome, and national rejuvenation.
* Symbols, monuments and rituals affirm the Chinese nation and the party’s leadership of it.
* Critical frames are directed at the “century of humiliation” and at external forces, not at the Chinese nation as such.
* Traditional cultural figures are increasingly re-nationalised within a patriotic frame.
* Organising shame narratives toward the Chinese nation itself are absent.
* Residual baseline of positive national framing: traditional symbols and continuity retain institutional space, while critical content is present but secondary.

### Expanded Analysis
The CPC produces a high Honour-band score. Chinese national identity is treated as legitimate and central; the party’s narrative is one of recovery and rise. While the party retains ideological control over the content of history, the emotional stance toward the nation is strongly affirmative.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 78
Clear default of national pride, re-nationalisation of cultural figures, and rejection of organising national shame place the CPC in the Honour band.

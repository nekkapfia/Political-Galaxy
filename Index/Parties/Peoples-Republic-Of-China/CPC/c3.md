### Overview
The Communist Party of China defines full trusted membership of the core political community by political loyalty to the Party and acceptance of the dominant political culture with Chinese characteristics. Ethnic minorities are citizens within a unitary framework; no permanent ethnic bar to citizenship or Party membership exists on blood grounds. High cultural-political transformation is required for trusted and leading positions. Immigration and deportation remain out of scope.

* Ethnic minorities hold citizenship; no formal ancestral blood bar to membership of the state or Party.
* Dense expectations of ideological conformity, political study, and cultural alignment for Party members and leading cadres.
* Immigration and deportation out of scope.

# Cultural 1A: People's Republic of China – CPC

Final Score: 0 (50M)

### Overview
The CPC organises moral legitimacy around continuous improvements in living standards, growth and stability as core proofs of legitimacy, the concrete interests of the Chinese people, national strength, and secular historical-mission and civilizational-continuity language under Party interpretation.

### Moral Justification – Relevant Elements

#### Mundane Justifications
* Continuous improvements in living standards, poverty elimination, economic growth and social stability are cited as core proofs of the system’s legitimacy.
* The concrete interests of the Chinese people and national strength appear as central legitimating claims.
* Popular support is measured and cultivated through state-managed consultation, performance feedback and patriotic education rather than competitive multiparty mandate.
* Socialism with Chinese characteristics and the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation are presented as the historical mission of the Party and the people — a secular this-worldly historical project.
* Cultural confidence, opposition to historical nihilism, and the inheritance of Chinese civilization are invoked as supports of national cohesion within a secular civilizational-continuity frame.
* Marxist theory and traditional Chinese thought are fused under Party interpretation as sources of purpose and continuity without sacralisation as divine or Cosmic higher law.

### Expanded Analysis
Performance, living standards, concrete interests of the Chinese people, national strength, and secular historical-mission and civilizational-continuity language organise the moral conversation. Under the locked lists, historical mission and civilizational inheritance without sacralisation are Mundane (or at most soft civilisational residual). No divine command, Cosmic law, worship-level deification, or sacralised Ideal organises legitimacy. Transcendent sources are entirely absent from the moral justifications as properly defined. The foundation is Mundane.

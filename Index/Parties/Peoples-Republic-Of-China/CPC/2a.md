## National Sovereignty Analysis
**Score: 87**

### Overview
The CPC maintains exclusive control of territory, borders, law and military command, treating international engagement as selective and subordinate to Party-state authority.

### National Sovereignty Relevant Policies / Elements
* Absolute formal and practical control of territory, borders, internal security and the People’s Liberation Army.
* Rejection of external judicial or political authority over Chinese domestic governance.
* Participation in international organisations and treaties is selective and subject to Party-state veto on core interests.
* Tight control of data, technology, capital and migration flows when they are deemed to affect security or stability.
* Belt and Road and other external projects are instruments of Chinese influence, not sources of external constraint on China.

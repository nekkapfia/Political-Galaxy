# Cultural 1B: Peoples Republic of China – CPC
## Dogmatism Analysis
**Score: 94**

**Orthodoxy: 95, Tribalism: 93**

### Overview
The CPC maintains near-total internal closure around Party leadership and ideological guidance, and rejects liberal-democratic and rival socialist frameworks as illegitimate for China. Both Orthodoxy and Tribalism are extreme, producing a Strong-band score near the top of the set.

### Relevant Policies / Elements
* Party monopoly of political power; no legal organised opposition.
* Democratic centralism and discipline making public line-deviation career- and liberty-threatening.
* Education, media and organisational control reproducing official ideology.
* Categories of historical nihilism and related offences closing alternative historical-moral frames.
* Liberal-democratic systems framed as unsuitable and hostile.
* Residual baseline of Party-state monopoly and surveillance systems extending conformity pressure beyond formal Party members.

### Expanded Analysis
Orthodoxy is extreme: Party leadership and ideological guidance are closed to legitimate internal overthrow. Democratic centralism, discipline systems, education and media monopoly, and categories such as historical nihilism make public line-deviation career- and liberty-threatening across major domains. Foundational principles are not provisional preferences open to open empirical challenge comparable to multiparty contestation. Tribalism is extreme: liberal-democratic systems are framed as unsuitable and hostile rather than as legitimate rival moral-political communities for China. Surveillance and administrative systems extend conformity pressure beyond formal Party members. Mechanisms include legal, organisational and technological enforcement at state scale. The institutional pattern is near-ceiling dogmatism, among the highest in the set, short only of theoretical absolute, calibrated in the Strong (86–95) upper range.

### Why this produces a Final Score of 94
Party monopoly, democratic centralism, education/media monopoly, historical-nihilism categories, and systematic framing of liberal-democratic systems as hostile produce extreme Orthodoxy and Tribalism. The formula yields 94.

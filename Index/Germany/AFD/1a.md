# Core 1A: Germany – Alternative für Deutschland
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 56**  
**Xenos Score: 48**

### Overview

Alternative für Deutschland produces a mid-range Core 1A profile for Endos and a lower score where programme priorities impose identity or religious-presentation controls on a designated practice group. The final set retains Germany’s criminal Volksverhetzung and order baseline (not a full speech-code repeal party in practice), emphasises opposition to progressive institutional orthodoxy, and advances stricter rules on Islamic religious self-presentation and parallel-society symbols in public and school settings. Immigration and foreign-national status are Core 2A and are not scored here. A limited Endos/Xenos split applies where dress/presentation rules fall differentially on a stable practice group.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Retention of core criminal law against violence and true threats; no programme to abolish ordinary direct-harm offences.
* Volksverhetzung and related hate-speech statutes — AfD criticises selective enforcement and “opinion crime” practice but does not present a completed repeal-and-replace of the entire speech code as a single locked final set; residual criminal speech law remains a live baseline risk.
* Explicit opposition to progressive DEI / attitude-correction institutional programmes — those densification layers are **out** of an AfD final set.
* Stricter laïcité-analogue / public-order rules on Islamic religious self-presentation (headscarf, burqa/niqab-type restrictions in schools and public employment / selected public contexts) as programme priority → **44–50** for affected practice group.
* Personal drug-possession criminalisation retained unless explicitly liberalised (not a core liberalisation brand).
* Order and security politics; association controls aimed at designated Islamist organisations where already framed as security/constitutional-order measures.
* Immigration, asylum and foreign-citizen status → Core 2A only.
* Party-system structure out of scope for Core 1A.

### Expanded Analysis

**Endos** (population not under distinctive religious-dress disability): AfD removes or refuses progressive mandatory orthodoxy training, keeps ordinary order and drug paternalism, and operates inside a Germany that still has Volksverhetzung on the books. Without treating AfD as a full speech-code abolition party, Endos sits in **51–59 — Paternalism / self-betterment** with residual speech-statute weight. Score **56**.

**Xenos** (persons for whom targeted Islamic dress/presentation is religious or obligatory): school and public-presentation bans restrict self-presentation on religious-identity criteria. Aligns with **44–50 — Early ideological/identity-based controls**. Score **48**.

Core 2A migration enforcement is excluded. Progressive training densification is excluded from the final set by opposition. Scoring is band-text only, not relative placement against CDU or RN.

### Why this produces Endos 56 / Xenos 48

Endos aligns with **51–59** (order and drug paternalism; progressive reprogramming out; no full speech-code abolition locked) → **56**. Xenos aligns with **44–50** (Islamic dress/self-presentation controls) → **48**.

# Core 1A: Russia – United Russia
## Personal Autonomy Analysis
**Endos Score: 22**  
**Xenos Score: 14**

### Overview

United Russia as the dominant governing party of the Russian Federation produces a severely restrictive Core 1A profile defined by criminalisation of dissenting political speech, foreign-agent and undesirable-organisation regimes constraining association, wartime and security-era speech bans (including on characterisation of the Ukraine conflict), pressure on independent media and protest, and designated out-groups (organised political opposition, labelled foreign agents, and related categories) under heightened administrative and criminal coercion. Cumulative weight sits in the **19–27 / 10–18** region. A clear Endos/Xenos split applies.

### Core 1A Relevant Policies / Elements

* Criminal and administrative penalties for discrediting the armed forces, spreading “fake” information about the military, and related wartime speech offences — dissenting speech on core security topics criminalised.
* Foreign-agent and undesirable-organisation laws restricting association, funding, publication and public activity for designated groups and individuals.
* Pressure on independent media, protest coordination and opposition organisation — independent political expression and association heavily constrained.
* Retention of ordinary direct-harm criminal law.
* Broad extremism and public-order tools used against political dissent beyond direct violence.
* Designated political Xenos (organised opposition, foreign-agent labelled persons and groups) face heightened registration, speech, association and often criminal exposure.
* No progressive Western-style DEI attitude-correction stack as the primary instrument; restriction is classical authoritarian speech/association monopoly politics.
* Immigration and foreign-citizen status rules partly Core 2A; domestic speech/association rules remain Core 1A.

### Expanded Analysis

**Endos** (population not labelled foreign agent or organised opposition target): public political speech critical of the war, leadership, or security policy is criminalised or administratively risky; independent media and protest space is narrow; association outside approved channels is constrained. Education and media promote official lines and suppress alternatives. This aligns with **19–27 — Ideological reprogramming / thought control** and the upper edge of **10–18** where protest and movement controls intensify. Score **22**.

**Xenos** (organised opposition, foreign-agent designees, undesirable organisations and their affiliates): registration burdens, funding bans, publication limits, criminal exposure, and organisational destruction. Aligns with **10–18 — Extreme restrictions on voluntary actions, movement and association** (detention risk, severe association bans). Score **14**.

Framing as national security or anti-extremism is discarded. Only the observable restriction sets on each group are scored.

### Why this produces Endos 22 / Xenos 14

Endos aligns with **19–27** (criminalised dissenting speech on security/politics; media and association pressure; official ideology promotion) → **22**. Xenos aligns with **10–18** (foreign-agent/undesirable regime; organisational and personal coercion of designated opponents) → **14**.
